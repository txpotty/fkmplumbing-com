    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Chula Vista

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it detects vapors or overheating.     Tank Leaks and Pressure Problems in Chula Vista

    Even gas water heaters can develop tank leaks, often due to corrosion from hard water or high water pressure.     Pilot Light Repair in Chula Vista

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Gas Valve and Thermostat Repair in Chula Vista, CA

    Issues with the gas valve or thermostat can lead to inconsistent water temperatures or a complete lack of hot water. Our team can test these crucial components for proper operation, checking for electrical continuity and gas flow. If a part is defective, we will replace it with a quality, manufacturer-approved component so your water heater heats evenly and safely.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Chula Vista, CA

    The gas control valve regulates temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something fails. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or listed universal model. We leak test and verify temperature control afterward.
