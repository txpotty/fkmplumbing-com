    Size depends on how many fixtures run at once and your incoming water temperature. Many homes need a unit rated around 7 to 10 gallons per minute.

    Tankless water heaters benefit from annual flushing to remove mineral buildup, especially in areas with hard water. This helps maintain efficiency and prolong the unit's lifespan. We offer maintenance services to keep your system running optimally.

    Absolutely. When properly sized, a tankless water heater can provide an endless supply of hot water, even for large families. It is crucial to select a unit with a high enough GPM (gallons per minute) rating to meet your peak demand.

    Yes, tankless water heaters are very energy efficient in almost any climate. Intense sun breaks down exposed plastic pipe, sprinkler parts, and exterior fittings, so they wear out faster than indoor plumbing. Since they only heat water on demand, they avoid the standby heat loss typical of traditional tanks, which can be substantial even in milder seasons.

    Yes, gas tankless water heaters require specific venting materials and configurations different from traditional tank heaters. Many modern units use PVC or polypropylene for venting, and proper slope and termination are critical for safe and efficient operation. Electric units do not require venting.
