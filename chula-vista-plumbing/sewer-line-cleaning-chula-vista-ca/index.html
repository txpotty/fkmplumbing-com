    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Line Cleaning in Chula Vista

    Sewer line cleaning in Chula Vista usually costs $200 to $500 with a cable machine, or $400 to $900 with hydro jetting. Adding a recorded camera inspection usually runs $150 to $350.

    FKM Plumbing uses a range of professional-grade equipment, including powerful drain snakes and augers for smaller clogs, and hydro jetting machines for tougher blockages like grease buildup and tree roots. We also use sewer cameras for precise diagnosis before any work begins.

    Hydro jetting is generally safe for most pipe materials, but it is crucial to have a professional assess your sewer line's condition first. We always perform a camera inspection to confirm your pipes can handle the high-pressure water without damage, especially for very old or severely corroded lines.

    The duration of sewer line cleaning varies depending on the severity and accessibility of the blockage. A straightforward clog might take 1 to 2 hours, while more complex issues involving extensive root removal or multiple access points could take 3 to 4 hours or more. We will provide an expected timeline after our initial inspection.

    Lines with roots or past clogs often need cleaning every 12 to 24 months. Newer PVC lines without issues may go many years without service.

    We firmly advise against chemical drain cleaners for sewer line clogs. These products often contain harsh chemicals that can corrode pipes, especially older ones, and are usually ineffective against severe blockages like tree roots. They can also pose a health risk and make professional cleaning more difficult.

    During our diagnostic camera inspection, if we discover that your sewer line is broken, collapsed, or severely damaged, we will inform you immediately. In such cases, cleaning might only be a temporary fix, and we would discuss options for sewer line repair or replacement, outlining all available solutions and costs.

    Root treatment products can slow regrowth after a mechanical cleaning, but they do not remove a root mass. Cleaning first is the key step.
