    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Chula Vista

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are often required for high-hazard applications in Chula Vista. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. FKM Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Chula Vista, CA

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Chula Vista, CA, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also make sure of proper clearance and drainage.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Chula Vista, CA

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested. We carry proven brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.
