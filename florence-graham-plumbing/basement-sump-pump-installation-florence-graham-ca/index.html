    Common signs you might need a new sump pump include unusual noises during operation, visible rust or corrosion on the unit, frequent cycling on and off, or a pump that fails to turn on during heavy rain.     Swapping a pump in an existing pit typically takes two to three hours. A new pit with slab cutting and concrete patching typically takes most of a day.

    Usually, standard home insurance policies do not cover a new sump pump installation or repairs unless the damage was caused by a sudden, accidental event like a burst pipe, rather than gradual wear and tear or groundwater seepage.
