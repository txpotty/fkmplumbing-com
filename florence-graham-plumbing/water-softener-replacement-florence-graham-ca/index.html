    Salt-free conditioners reduce scale buildup but do not remove hardness minerals. A true softener removes them, which is better for soap and fixtures.

    Spots on dishes, scale on faucets, dry skin and increased soap use can point to a failing softener. A softener that no longer uses salt or keeps flooding the brine tank may also need replacement.

    The right size depends on your household's daily water usage and the hardness of your water in grains per gallon. Our technicians will perform a water test and calculate the correct softening capacity in grains per day for your Florence-Graham home, so the system performs well.

    Salt-based softeners remove hardness minerals, calcium and magnesium, through ion exchange, truly softening the water.
