    Sediment Flushing & Cleaning in Redondo Beach, CA

    Over time, minerals and sediment settle at the bottom of hot water tanks, reducing efficiency and possibly leading to premature failure. Our maintenance includes a thorough flushing of the tank to remove these deposits. This process helps your tank heat water more efficiently and quietly, a vital step for any hot water tank in Redondo Beach, CA, where mineral content in the water is a common concern.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Redondo Beach

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure spike every heating cycle. High street pressure is not unusual in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Redondo Beach

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. We test each element with a meter, check the thermostats and look for scorched wires at the terminal block. If an element has failed we replace it with the matching wattage and voltage. Plenty of families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Redondo Beach

    A properly functioning temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to ensure it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will suggest immediate replacement to keep your Redondo Beach home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Electric Hot Water Tank Element Check in Redondo Beach

    Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout. We also check the thermostat settings and wiring connections to confirm they are functioning correctly and safely. This preventive check can identify failing elements before they stop heating altogether, avoiding unexpected cold showers for your family in Redondo Beach.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Redondo Beach, CA

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.
