    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Redondo Beach, CA

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a quick spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with real information.

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a particularly challenging issue.     Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Redondo Beach, CA

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California install softeners or scale filters. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe rarely lasts because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and ends repeat leaks.
