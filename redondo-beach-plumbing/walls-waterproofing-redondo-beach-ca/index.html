    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Redondo Beach, CA

    Sump pumps are a critical part of many interior waterproofing systems, particularly in homes that see heavy rainfall. We install high-capacity sump pumps designed to handle large volumes of water, keeping your basement dry even during flash floods. Our services include primary sump pump installation, battery backup sump pump systems for power outages, and routine maintenance or repair of existing units. We make sure the discharge line is routed properly so water does not circulate back toward the foundation.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Redondo Beach, CA

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to confirm the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test confirms the pan is watertight before tile.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Redondo Beach, CA

    Basement wall sealing is a critical part of walls waterproofing. Hot inland summers push water heaters and outdoor plumbing hard, while coastal salt air can corrode exposed metal fittings. We apply high-quality sealants to porous concrete and masonry walls to prevent moisture from wicking through and causing dampness, mold, or musty odors. This service can be performed alone or as part of a larger waterproofing system, offering a drier, healthier environment in your Redondo Beach, CA basement.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Redondo Beach, CA
