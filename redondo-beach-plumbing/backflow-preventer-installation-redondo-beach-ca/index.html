    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Redondo Beach, CA

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Redondo Beach, CA, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.

    Pool and Spa Fill Backflow Protection in Redondo Beach, CA

    An auto-fill line or hose connection on a pool or spa creates a straight path for chlorinated water to reach the supply. We install the correct device on pool fill lines and check that hose bibbs have vacuum breakers. This simple step protects your household water and keeps you in line with local code. We can add the work when a pool is being built or remodeled.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Redondo Beach

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Redondo Beach

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Redondo Beach. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. FKM Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with trusted brands like Watts and Febco.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer Installation in Redondo Beach

    The cost for backflow preventer installation in Redondo Beach, CA can range from approximately $300 to $1,500, depending on the type of device required, such as a DCVA or an RPZ, the complexity of the installation and any changes needed to existing plumbing.     Backflow preventers are installed at cross-connection points where non-potable water could enter the potable water supply. Common locations include irrigation system connections, fire sprinkler lines, boiler systems and connections to auxiliary water supplies, often just after the main water meter or at the point of hazard.

    Most municipalities require annual testing of backflow preventers by a licensed, certified backflow tester. This confirms the device is working correctly and continues to protect your potable water supply from contamination. We can also assist with scheduling these annual tests.

    Yes, in many areas a permit is required for backflow preventer installation, especially for systems connected to the municipal water supply. FKM Plumbing will help you navigate the permitting process in Redondo Beach and make sure all required inspections are scheduled and passed.

    Most residential backflow preventer installations can be finished within 2 to 4 hours. More complex commercial systems or installations requiring significant pipe changes might take longer. We always aim to complete the work promptly while keeping our quality standards high.

    In Redondo Beach, most backflow preventers must be tested once a year by a certified backflow tester to confirm they are working correctly and providing adequate protection. This is a separate service from installation but is essential for ongoing compliance and safety.
