    Softener Loop Installation in Redondo Beach, CA

    Metered Softener Replacement in Redondo Beach

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly exhausted. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Redondo Beach

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water around the clock. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but prevent hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Redondo Beach

    A standard single tank softener replacement usually costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can add to the cost.

    Most standard residential water softener replacements can be completed within 3 to 5 hours. This includes disconnecting the old unit, installing the new one, making plumbing connections and testing the system. More complex installations might take a bit longer.

    We install systems with Fleck, Clack and Kinetico valves, among others. These brands have good parts availability and reliable performance.

    Common signs include a return of hard water symptoms like scale buildup on fixtures, dry skin and hair, dingy laundry or heavy soap use. Other signs are a clear drop in water pressure, leaks from the unit, or a softener over 10 to 15 years old that needs frequent repairs.

    Salt-based softeners remove hardness minerals, calcium and magnesium, through ion exchange, effectively softening the water. Salt-free conditioners do not remove minerals but alter their crystal structure so they do not stick to surfaces. That prevents scale buildup without actually softening the water.

    Spots on dishes, scale on faucets, dry skin and increased soap use can signal a failing softener. A softener that no longer uses salt or keeps flooding the brine tank may also need replacement.

    Many systems include 5 to 10 years on the valve and longer coverage on tanks. We also provide a warranty on our labor.

    New water softeners usually come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years on different components, such as the control valve and resin tank. FKM Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work for your peace of mind. Specific warranty details will be provided with your quote.
