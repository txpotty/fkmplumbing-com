    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in San Diego

    The cost for garbage disposal replacement in San Diego, CA usually ranges from $200 to $600. This includes the new unit itself, which can vary from $100 for a basic 1/3 HP model to over $300 for a high-end 1 HP unit, plus labor costs. Factors like the disposal's horsepower, brand, and any necessary plumbing modifications can affect the final price.

    A continuous feed disposal operates when you flip a wall switch, allowing you to add food waste while it's running. A batch feed disposal only operates when a special stopper is inserted into the drain, making it generally safer as it prevents accidental activation while hands are near the opening.

    Common signs include frequent clogs, loud grinding noises without processing food, persistent leaks from the unit's housing, or if the disposal simply stops turning on. If you notice these issues, it's probably time for a replacement.

    Most replacements take about an hour. Upgrades with new electrical or drain changes may take a bit longer.

    If your disposal is old, frequently leaking, or making grinding noises without grinding food, replacement is usually more cost-effective and provides a longer-lasting solution. Minor issues like a simple jam can usually be repaired.

    For light use in a single-person household, 1/3 HP might suffice. Most homeowners choose 1/2 HP or 3/4 HP for general use, which handles common food waste well. For large families, frequent cooking, or tougher waste like small bones, a 1 HP unit offers the best performance and durability.

    Most disposals last 8 to 12 years. Hard water, heavy use and grease can shorten that life.

    To extend the life of your new disposal, avoid putting fibrous foods like celery, corn husks, or onion skins down it. Also, do not dispose of grease, coffee grounds, or non-food items. Always run cold water before, during, and after use, and grind small amounts of citrus peels or ice cubes periodically to clean the unit.
