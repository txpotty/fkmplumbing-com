    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in San Diego

    The cost of water softener replacement in San Diego, CA can vary widely, typically ranging from $1,500 to $4,000. This depends on the type and size of the new system, labor costs and any needed plumbing changes. We provide clear, upfront pricing after assessing your needs.

    Requirements vary by city. Simple replacements may not require one, but new plumbing connections may. We check local rules on your behalf.

    Spots on dishes, scale on faucets, dry skin and increased soap use can point to a failing softener. A softener that no longer uses salt or keeps flooding the brine tank may also need replacement.

    Salt-based softeners remove hardness minerals, calcium and magnesium, through ion exchange, truly softening the water. Salt-free conditioners do not remove minerals but alter their crystal structure so they do not stick to surfaces. That prevents scale buildup without actually softening the water.

    New water softeners usually come with two types of warranties. There is a manufacturer's warranty on the equipment itself, which can range from 5 to 10 years or more for tanks and control valves, and an installation warranty from FKM Plumbing. We back our work with a solid warranty for your peace of mind.

    Common signs include a return of hard water symptoms like scale buildup on fixtures, dry skin and hair, dingy laundry or excessive soap use. Other signs are a noticeable drop in water pressure, leaks from the unit, or a softener over 10 to 15 years old that needs frequent repairs.

    The right size depends on your household's daily water usage and the hardness of your water in grains per gallon. Our technicians will perform a water test and calculate the appropriate softening capacity in grains per day for your San Diego home, so the system performs well.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is usually not required for a direct like-for-like water softener replacement if no significant changes to the existing plumbing are made.
