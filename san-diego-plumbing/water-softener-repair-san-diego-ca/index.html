    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in San Diego

    A softener using far too much salt or water often has settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units may regenerate on a schedule regardless of use. We retest hardness, reprogram the valve or upgrade the head to a metered control. Proper settings can significantly cut salt and water use.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in San Diego, CA

    A salt bridge, a crust of salt that forms in the brine tank, can prevent your water softener from regenerating properly. This leads to hard water throughout your home. Our team can safely break up and remove salt bridges, clean the brine tank, and advise on best practices to prevent future occurrences. We also repair leaks or damage to the brine tank itself, frequent in older systems.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in San Diego, CA

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, regulating the flow of water and starting regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water, regenerate too often, or leak. We diagnose issues like stuck pistons, worn out seals, motor failures, or programming errors. Our plumbers carry a range of replacement parts for popular brands like Fleck, Clack, and Autotrol, allowing a quick and effective repair to restore proper operation.

    Resin Bed Replacement in San Diego

    Over time, the resin beads in your water softener can become fouled or degrade, losing their ability to exchange ions and soften water. This is a frequent issue, especially with older systems or those exposed to high levels of chlorine. FKM Plumbing provides professional resin bed replacement services, carefully removing old resin and installing new, high-quality media to restore your softener's efficiency and capacity in San Diego.
