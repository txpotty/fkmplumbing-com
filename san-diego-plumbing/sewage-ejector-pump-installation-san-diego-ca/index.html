    The cost for sewage ejector pump installation in San Diego usually ranges from $2,500 to $5,000.     A sump pump removes groundwater or seepage, while an ejector pump handles sewage and wastewater from fixtures. Ejector pumps are designed to pass solids and require a sealed, vented basin.

    Avoid flushing wipes, paper towels, feminine products, grease and cat litter. These items clog pumps and cause premature failure.

    We mainly install high-quality, reputable brands such as Liberty Pumps and Zoeller. These manufacturers are known for their durability, reliability and efficient performance, providing a long-lasting solution for your home. We can discuss the best brand and model for your needs.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from 1 to 5 years depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor.

    Signs that you might need a new sewage ejector pump include unusual noises from the pump or pit, frequent cycling on and off, slow drainage from basement fixtures or a sewage odor coming from the area. If you notice any of these issues, it is best to have a professional inspect your system to prevent a complete failure and costly backups.

    Common signs that you might need a new sewage ejector pump include the pump running constantly, not turning on at all, making unusual grinding or loud noises, or foul odors or sewage backing up into your basement fixtures. These symptoms point to a failing unit that requires immediate attention.
