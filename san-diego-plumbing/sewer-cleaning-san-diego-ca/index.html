    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in San Diego, CA

    Grease from kitchens cools and coats the inside of sewer pipes, and minerals in the water bond with it to form a tough crust. Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California add softeners or scale filters. Over years the pipe opening shrinks until flow slows to a trickle. A cable can punch a hole through, but jetting cleans the walls back to full size. We choose the method based on pipe material and condition. Cleaning before the line fully closes spares you money and stress.
