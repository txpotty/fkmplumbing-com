    Crawl Space Encapsulation in San Diego

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in San Diego, CA

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already penetrated the foundation walls. This typically involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and works well where exterior access is limited.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in San Diego

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most reliable method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Expansive clay and hillside soils can shift over time, putting stress on slab plumbing and separating older sewer joints. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also added to divert water away.
