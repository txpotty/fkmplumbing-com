    Sewer Camera Inspection in San Diego

    Every sewer repair starts with a look inside the pipe. We run a self-leveling camera from your cleanout or a pulled toilet and record the full length of the line. A sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact location and depth of every problem on the surface. You get a copy of the video and a plain-English summary of what we found. This step alone often saves homeowners from paying for repairs they do not need.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in San Diego, CA

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is well suited for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in San Diego.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in San Diego

    Before any repair, we use high-resolution video camera inspection to precisely locate and assess sewer line damage. This involves inserting a flexible rod with a camera into your sewer line, allowing us to see blockages, cracks, root intrusion or pipe collapses in real time. This non-invasive method prevents unnecessary digging and helps us provide a clear diagnosis and repair plan. It's key for understanding issues in older homes in San Diego and identifying problems like bellied pipes or collapsed sections.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in San Diego

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to repeat clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it will hold.

    Root Intrusion Repair in San Diego, CA

    Tree and shrub roots go right for the moisture and nutrients leaking from old sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense mats that catch paper and waste. We cut the roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro-jetter, then repair or seal the joint so they cannot come right back. For lines with many rooted joints, we will show you when lining or replacement makes more sense than repeated cleaning.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in San Diego, CA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Expansive clay and hillside soils can shift over time, putting stress on slab plumbing and cracking older sewer joints. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in San Diego

    When trenchless repair isn't possible due to severe damage or specific pipe configurations, traditional dig-and-replace methods are necessary. This involves excavating a trench to access and replace the damaged section of the sewer line. While more intrusive, it is sometimes the only way to address severely collapsed pipes, significant offsets or certain pipe materials common in older Southern California properties. Our team makes sure of careful excavation and efficient replacement, minimizing the impact on your property in San Diego.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in San Diego, CA

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive option. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from major disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an effective and popular choice for protecting property appearance in San Diego.
