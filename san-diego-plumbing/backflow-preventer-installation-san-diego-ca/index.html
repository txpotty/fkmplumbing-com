    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in San Diego

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in San Diego. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. FKM Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with trusted brands like Watts and Febco.
