    Slab Leak Reroute in Hawthorne

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is easiest. Reroutes end future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Hawthorne

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method bypasses the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Hawthorne with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Hawthorne, CA

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a load-bearing wall or in a difficult-to-access area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method. This allows our plumbers to reach the damaged pipe from outside your home, avoiding disruption to your interior floors and finishes. Tunneling spares the interior but calls for skilled excavation and shoring to protect structural integrity throughout the repair process in Hawthorne, CA.
