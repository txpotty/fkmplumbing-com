    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Hawthorne

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, especially in closed-loop systems. Code here requires seismic strapping on water heaters and water-efficient fixtures, and inspectors check both closely on remodels. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to support proper system operation and prevent early wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Hawthorne.
