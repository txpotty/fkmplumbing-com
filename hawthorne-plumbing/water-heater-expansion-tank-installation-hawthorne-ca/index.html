    Replacing Old Expansion Tanks in Hawthorne

    Expansion tanks do not last forever, and an old, waterlogged or corroded tank offers no protection. If your existing tank in Hawthorne is showing signs of failure, such as a dripping relief valve or fluctuating water pressure, it is time for a new one.     Preventing Water Heater Damage in Hawthorne

    Thermal expansion is a common cause of premature water heater failure in Hawthorne homes. Without an expansion tank, constant pressure swings stress the water heater's tank, connections and internal components, leading to leaks, reduced efficiency and a shorter lifespan. Our installation service solves this issue, providing a buffer that protects your investment. This small addition can save you thousands in future water heater repair or replacement costs.

    Expansion Tank Sizing for Large Water Heaters in Hawthorne

    A standard 2 gallon tank works for many 40 and 50 gallon heaters at moderate pressure. Larger heaters, higher incoming pressure or high-capacity systems may need a 4.5 gallon tank or larger. We use manufacturer sizing charts that factor in heater volume, supply pressure and temperature setting. Correct sizing keeps the tank from over-flexing and failing early.

    Commercial and Multi-Unit Expansion Tanks in Hawthorne

    Apartment buildings, restaurants and offices with large commercial water heaters or multiple tanks need larger expansion tanks sized for total system volume. We calculate requirements for the entire hot water system, including any storage tanks and recirculation loops. Larger tanks are floor-mounted with proper supports. We schedule work to limit hot water interruption for tenants or customers.

    New Expansion Tank Installation in Hawthorne

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve and no expansion tank, the water heater is probably seeing a pressure spike every heating cycle. We install a new tank on the cold water supply near the heater using a tee and short nipple. The air side is precharged to match your static pressure, usually between 50 and 70 psi. We then run the heater and watch the gauge to confirm pressure stays stable.

    Expansion Tanks With Tankless Water Heaters in Hawthorne, CA

    Tankless water heaters hold very little water, so thermal expansion is minimal in most homes. However, homes with recirculation loops, storage buffer tanks or certain closed system setups may still benefit from an expansion tank. We evaluate your system and only recommend a tank when it serves a real purpose. This keeps you from paying for parts you do not need.
