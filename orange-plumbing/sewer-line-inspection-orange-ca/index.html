    Cast Iron and Clay Pipe Condition Assessment in Orange

    Belly and Offset Detection in Orange, CA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds water and waste. Offsets happen when pipe sections shift out of alignment, often because of soil movement. Expansive clay and hillside soils can shift over time, putting stress on slab plumbing and separating older sewer joints. The camera shows these defects clearly, and we mark their location for targeted repair.

    Detecting Pipe Damage and Corrosion in Orange, CA

    Sewer lines can suffer from various forms of damage, including cracks, breaks, corrosion and sagging sections due to shifting soil. Our camera inspections provide a detailed visual assessment of the pipe's interior, identifying these structural issues. We can determine the severity and exact location of damage, helping you decide between spot repair, relining or full pipe replacement. Early detection is key to preventing catastrophic failures.

    Root Intrusion Inspection in Orange, CA

    Roots from shade trees, ornamental trees and thirsty shrubs seek out moisture in sewer joints. Over time they can fill the pipe and cause recurring backups. Our camera shows exactly where roots enter and how much of the pipe they block. We then recommend cleaning, jetting or a long-term repair like lining.
