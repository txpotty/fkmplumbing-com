    Tank Water Heater Flushing in Orange

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clean. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs more quietly.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Orange, CA

    Gas water heater maintenance involves crucial safety inspections in Orange, CA. We check for gas leaks, proper venting of exhaust gases, and the function of the pilot light or igniter. We also clean the burner assembly and inspect the thermocouple. These checks are essential to prevent carbon monoxide exposure and keep your gas-fired appliance running safely.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Orange

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Orange, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's significantly depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing expensive tank leaks.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Orange

    Tankless units are particularly sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures steady.
