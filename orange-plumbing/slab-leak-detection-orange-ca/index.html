    Most slab leak detection services can be completed within 1 to 3 hours, depending on the severity and location of the leak. Our goal is always to be as efficient and non-invasive as possible, providing a fast diagnosis so repair work can begin promptly.

    Common pipe materials prone to slab leaks include older copper pipes vulnerable to corrosion from hard water or soil acidity, and sometimes PEX or CPVC if they were improperly installed or exposed to extreme temperatures.     Many homeowner insurance policies cover the cost of water damage from a sudden or accidental slab leak, and sometimes the cost of accessing the leak for repair. However, coverage varies greatly, so it's essential to check your specific policy with your insurance provider.
