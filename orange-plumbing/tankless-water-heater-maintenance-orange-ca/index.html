    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Orange

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is shielded where it runs outdoors.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Orange

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system thoroughly. Restored flow means steadier temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Recirculation and Combi System Service in Orange, CA

    Many tankless units now include built-in recirculation pumps or buffer tanks to deliver faster hot water. These systems move more water through the exchanger and scale up sooner. We flush the recirculation loop, check pump operation and verify crossover or dedicated return line settings. Correct schedules can cut wasted energy while still giving you quick hot water.

    Navien Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Orange

    Navien tankless water heaters often feature condensing technology, making them highly efficient but also requiring specific maintenance. Our tune-ups include checking the condensate trap for blockages, cleaning the secondary heat exchanger and verifying the operation of the circulator pump. We also ensure a proper air-fuel mixture and calibrate sensors for peak efficiency in Orange homes.

    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Orange

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading correctly and the burner breathing properly.

    Tankless Water Heater Venting System Check in Orange

    Proper ventilation is critical for the safe operation of gas-fired tankless water heaters. Our maintenance service in Orange includes a thorough inspection of the venting system for blockages, proper slope and secure connections. We confirm that exhaust gases are safely directed outside and that no carbon monoxide leaks are present, which is vital for the health and safety of your household.
