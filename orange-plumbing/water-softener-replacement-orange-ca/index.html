    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Orange

    A standard single tank softener replacement typically costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can add to the cost.

    We install systems with Fleck, Clack and Kinetico valves, among others. These brands have good parts availability and reliable performance.

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had heavy mineral buildup restricting flow. However, a water softener's main job is to remove hardness minerals, not to boost pressure. If low water pressure is your main concern, we can look into other causes.

    Most softeners last 10 to 15 years. Resin and valves wear out sooner with very hard or chlorinated water.

    New water softeners usually come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years on different components, such as the control valve and resin tank. FKM Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work for your peace of mind. Exact warranty details will be provided with your quote.

    FKM Plumbing installs a wide range of reputable water softener brands, including Fleck, Autotrol, Culligan, Pelican and Watts. We select brands known for their reliability, efficiency and long-term performance, so you get a quality system that meets your needs.

    Municipal water across much of Southern California is hard, leaving white scale on faucets, showerheads, and inside water heater tanks. Hard water contains dissolved minerals like calcium and magnesium. These minerals can lead to scale buildup in pipes, reduced efficiency of water heaters and appliances, spots on dishes, and dry skin and hair. A working water softener is essential for combating these issues in Orange homes.

    To extend the life of your new water softener, use the correct type of salt, if applicable, and keep the brine tank filled. Regular maintenance, such as cleaning the brine tank and periodic inspections by a professional, can also help protect its longevity and efficiency.
