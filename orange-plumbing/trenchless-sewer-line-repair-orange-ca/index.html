    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Orange, CA

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is well suited for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Southern California homeowners.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Orange, CA

    Sewer lines in Orange, CA can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or misalign. Expansive clay and hillside soils can shift over time, putting stress on slab plumbing and cracking older sewer joints. Tree root intrusion is another major culprit, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also add to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Orange

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while breaking apart the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is highly resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Orange homes. This method is well suited when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.
