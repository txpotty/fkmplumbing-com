    Pilot Light Repair in Orange

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Burner Assembly and Venting System Repair in Orange

    A worn burner assembly can result in poor heating or unusual noises. We closely inspect the burner for blockages, corrosion or wear. We also check the venting system for proper draft and obstructions, which is vital for safe operation and preventing carbon monoxide buildup in your Orange home. Proper venting is a key safety consideration.

    Water Heater Tank Leaks and Pressure Issues in Orange, CA

    While gas water heaters are sturdy, tank leaks or pressure problems can occur. We can often fix small fitting leaks or replace relief valves if pressure is too high. If the tank itself is failing due to rust or old age, a replacement might be the more economical long-term solution. We give you an honest assessment of the repair options for your Orange, CA home.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Orange, CA

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame signals a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats more quickly and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes dropping from the flue.
