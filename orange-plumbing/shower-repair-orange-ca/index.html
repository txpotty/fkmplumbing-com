    Clogged Shower Drain Solutions in Orange, CA

    Slow or standing water in your shower is a clear sign of a clogged drain. These clogs are typically caused by a buildup of hair, soap scum, and sometimes mineral deposits. Our team uses professional drain snakes and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the most stubborn blockages without damaging your pipes. We also offer advice on preventative measures to keep your shower drains flowing freely.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Orange, CA

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This brings back full flow to the showerhead.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Orange, CA

    Many homes in Orange feature Moen or Delta shower fixtures, known for their quality but still subject to wear and tear. Our plumbers are very experienced in repairing and sourcing parts for these popular brands. Whether it is a leaky single-handle cartridge or a diverter issue, we have the specialized knowledge to fix your Moen or Delta shower correctly.

    Showerhead and Arm Repair in Orange

    Dripping showerheads, leaking arm connections and broken handheld hoses are frequent problems. We clean or replace showerheads, reseal shower arms and install new handheld hoses and holders. We can also add a slide bar for a handheld shower. Hard water clogs nozzles, so we recommend heads with flexible rubber nozzles that are easy to clean.

    Shower Drain Repair in Orange

    Slow shower drains are typically clogged with hair and soap scum, often hardened by mineral deposits. We clear the drain with augers and check the drain assembly for leaks. Loose or cracked drain bodies can let water seep under the pan. We repair or replace drain components as needed.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Orange, CA

    Weak shower pressure is often caused by mineral buildup in the showerhead or flow restrictor. It can also come from a clogged cartridge or a failing pressure regulator. We clean or replace the showerhead, check the cartridge and test whole-home pressure if needed. Most low-pressure issues are fixed fast.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Orange

    If you are experiencing inconsistent water temperature, low water pressure, or difficulty turning your shower on or off, the shower valve may be failing. A faulty mixing valve can lead to scalding or freezing water, which is a safety hazard. We can replace old or broken shower valves with new, reliable models, including pressure-balancing and thermostatic options for improved safety and comfort. This often involves accessing the valve behind the shower wall.

    Shower Faucet Leak Fixes in Orange

    Beyond the shower head, leaks can occur at the faucet handles or behind the wall. These leaks can be harder to spot but can cause serious damage if left unaddressed. We use advanced diagnostic tools to pinpoint the exact location of the leak and provide precise repairs, often involving O-ring replacement or tightening connections, preventing costly water damage to your home's structure.
