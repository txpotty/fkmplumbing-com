    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Orange, CA

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is vital for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it changes the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's badly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, saving you from a full tank replacement.

    Sediment Flushing & Cleaning in Orange, CA

    Over time, minerals and sediment settle at the bottom of hot water tanks, reducing efficiency and possibly leading to premature failure. Our maintenance includes a thorough flushing of the tank to remove these deposits. This process helps your tank heat water more efficiently and quietly, a vital step for any hot water tank in Orange, CA, where mineral content in the water is a frequent concern.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Orange

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause noisy popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Orange

    The cost for hot water tank maintenance in Orange, CA usually ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the tank type, age and specific services required like anode rod replacement. We provide a detailed quote before any work begins.

    Routine hot water tank maintenance, such as flushing and anode rod replacement, usually does not require a permit in Orange, CA. However, if major repairs or a full replacement are needed, a permit may be required to confirm compliance with local building codes.

    Watch for slower recovery, rusty or cloudy hot water, noises, moisture around the base or a relief valve that drips. Any of these means it is time for an inspection visit.

    The anode rod, also known as a sacrificial rod, is a metal rod inside your hot water tank designed to corrode before the tank itself. It protects the tank lining from rust and corrosion, which counts most when water carries a lot of minerals, and it extends the lifespan of your unit.

    Most flushes and inspections take 45 minutes to 90 minutes. Heavily scaled tanks or anode replacements in tight closets can take a bit longer.
