    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Bakersfield

    Tree roots are a common culprit for sewer line damage in Southern California, drawn to the moisture and nutrients inside pipes.     Root Intrusion Repair in Bakersfield, CA

    Tree and shrub roots go straight for the moisture and nutrients leaking from old sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense mats that catch paper and waste. We cut the roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro-jetter, then repair or seal the joint so they cannot come right back. For lines with many rooted joints, we will show you when lining or replacement makes more sense than ongoing cleaning.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Bakersfield, CA

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is well suited for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Bakersfield.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Bakersfield, CA

    While often overlooked, the sewer vent pipe is crucial for proper drainage and preventing sewer gas buildup. Issues with vent pipes, such as blockages from animal nests or damage from strong winds, can lead to slow drains, gurgling noises and foul odors. FKM Plumbing diagnoses and repairs vent pipe problems, ensuring your entire drainage system works correctly and safely and maintaining good indoor air quality.
