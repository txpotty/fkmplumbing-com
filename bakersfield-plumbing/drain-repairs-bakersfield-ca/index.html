    Minor clogs can sometimes be resolved with plungers or enzymatic drain cleaners. However, for stubborn blockages, recurring issues or suspected pipe damage, it is best to call a professional. DIY attempts with harsh chemical cleaners can sometimes damage pipes or hide a larger problem.

    Minor drain repairs, such as clearing a simple clog, usually do not require a permit in Bakersfield.     You should call a professional if your DIY efforts with a plunger or basic snake haven't cleared the clog, or if you have recurring clogs, foul odors or multiple slow drains. These issues often point to a deeper problem that requires specialized tools and expertise to resolve effectively.
