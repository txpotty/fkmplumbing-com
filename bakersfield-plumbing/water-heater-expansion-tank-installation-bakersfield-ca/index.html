    Expansion Tank Sizing for Large Water Heaters in Bakersfield

    A standard 2 gallon tank works for many 40 and 50 gallon heaters at moderate pressure. Larger heaters, higher incoming pressure or high-capacity systems may need a 4.5 gallon tank or larger. We use manufacturer sizing charts that factor in heater volume, supply pressure and temperature setting. Correct sizing keeps the tank from over-flexing and wearing out early.

    Residential Expansion Tank Installation in Bakersfield

    For homeowners in Bakersfield, a properly installed water heater expansion tank is important, especially with so many closed plumbing systems.     New Expansion Tank Installation in Bakersfield

    We install potable-rated thermal expansion tanks from brands such as Watts, Amtrol and A.O. Smith. Potable tanks use a butyl diaphragm and a lined interior so water never touches bare steel.     Expansion Tank Replacement in Bakersfield, CA

    Expansion tanks wear out over time as the internal bladder or diaphragm fails and the tank fills with water. A waterlogged tank feels heavy, and tapping it produces a dull sound all the way up instead of a hollow top. Pressing the air valve and getting water instead of air is a sure sign it has failed.
