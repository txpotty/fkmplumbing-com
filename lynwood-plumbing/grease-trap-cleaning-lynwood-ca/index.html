    Indoor Grease Trap Cleaning in Lynwood

    Small hydromechanical traps under sinks fill quickly in busy kitchens and often need cleaning every few weeks. We remove the lid, pump or hand-scoop the grease and solids, and scrape the baffles. We check the gasket, flow control fitting and inlet for damage. Then we reassemble the unit and confirm water flows through properly. This keeps odors down and prevents slow sink drainage.

    Outdoor Grease Interceptor Pumping in Lynwood, CA

    Large in-ground interceptors require a vacuum truck to fully pump out grease, water and settled solids. We open each compartment, pump it down and use high-pressure water to break up hardened grease on the walls. We inspect inlet and outlet tees, baffles and risers while the tank is empty. Warm weather can make grease odors stronger, so we also check lid seals. A complete pump-out brings back full retention capacity.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Cleaning in Lynwood

    For larger establishments, outdoor grease interceptors are critical components of the plumbing system. These large tanks collect significant amounts of FOG and sediment. Our team has the specialized equipment and experience to effectively clean and maintain these large interceptors in Lynwood, ensuring they function correctly and comply with environmental regulations. Proper cleaning prevents overflow into the public sewer system, which can result in significant fines.

    Grease Line Jetting in Lynwood

    Grease that escapes the trap often coats the line between the kitchen and the interceptor or the sewer. We use hydro jetting to scour the inside of the pipe and remove hardened grease. Jetting is far more effective than a standard drain snake for grease buildup. We can run a camera afterward to confirm the line is clear. This prevents repeat backups and keeps your trap working properly.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Cleaning in Lynwood

    FOG stands for Fats, Oils, and Grease. When poured down drains, FOG cools and solidifies, sticking to pipe walls and eventually leading to severe clogs. In grease traps, it separates from wastewater, but if not regularly removed, it overflows into the sewer system, causing blockages and environmental issues.

    Slow sink drains, foul odors, grease on the water surface near the outlet and backups from floor drains are common signs. If you notice any of these, schedule a cleaning right away.

    Generally, you do not need a permit to have your existing grease trap cleaned by a professional. However, commercial establishments in Lynwood are often required to maintain records of their grease trap cleaning for compliance audits. New grease trap installations may require plumbing permits.

    Indoor traps typically take 30 minutes to an hour. Outdoor interceptors can take one to two hours depending on size and how much buildup is present.
