    Identifying Signs of Sewer Line Clogs in Lynwood, CA

    Knowing the signs of a potential sewer line clog can help you act fast. Watch for multiple drains backing up at once, gurgling noises from toilets or drains, a sewage smell in your yard or basement, or water coming up through a drain when you flush a toilet in Lynwood, CA. If you notice any of these symptoms, it is time to call FKM Plumbing for a professional sewer line inspection and cleaning.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Lynwood

    A camera inspection shows the inside of your sewer line in real time. We look for bellies where water pools, offset joints, cracks, roots and corrosion. A locator in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the surface. You get a copy of the video and our notes. This is helpful for home buyers, sellers and anyone with recurring clogs.

    Root-Infested Sewer Line Cleaning in Lynwood, CA

    Roots are the top cause of sewer line blockages in established neighborhoods. They enter at clay joints and small cracks, then grow into a dense mat that catches waste. We cut them out with root saws and finish with jetting to remove the fine hairs. The camera then shows each entry point. You can choose ongoing cleaning or a lasting fix such as lining.

    Routine Sewer Line Cleaning in Lynwood

    Routine cleaning clears the slow buildup of grease, paper and scale that narrows sewer laterals over time. We enter through a cleanout and run a sectional cable with the correct blade for your pipe size. For heavier buildup we follow with hydro jetting to scrub the walls. The goal is a line that flows at full capacity. Most homeowners with older pipe benefit from cleaning every one to two years.

    Sewer Line Odor Remediation in Lynwood, CA

    Persistent foul odors coming from your drains or yard can be a strong sign of a sewer line problem in Lynwood, CA.
