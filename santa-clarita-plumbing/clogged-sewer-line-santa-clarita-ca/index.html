    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Sewer Line in Santa Clarita

    The cost to clear a clogged sewer line in Santa Clarita, CA can range from $250 to $700, depending on the severity of the clog, the method required (e.g., snaking vs. hydro-jetting), and whether camera inspection is needed. Complex issues like extensive root intrusion or pipe damage will be on the higher end, as they may need more intensive work.

    Homeowners are typically responsible for the lateral from the house to the property line or city tap. If the clog is in the city main, the municipality handles it. Our camera and locator help determine where the blockage is.

    Rental drum augers can be dangerous and may get stuck or damage old pipe. Main sewer clogs also involve sewage exposure. It is safer and often more effective to call a professional.

    Most main line clearings take one to two hours. Severe root intrusion or lines without a cleanout can take longer. Camera inspection adds roughly 30 to 60 minutes.

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful method where high-pressure water is blasted through your sewer lines to scour the pipe walls clean. It effectively removes grease, mineral buildup, scale, and even tree roots, leaving your pipes in a near-new condition. It's much more effective than traditional snaking for thorough cleaning and preventing recurring clogs.

    Cutting roots clears the line but roots will grow back if the joints remain open. Root treatments can slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement seals the joints for good. We can show you the extent of root intrusion on video.

    Minor sewer line cleanings usually do not require a permit. However, if the clog calls for extensive repair work, such as replacing a section of the sewer line, a permit from your local permitting department will likely be required for code compliance.
