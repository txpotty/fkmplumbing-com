    Sump Pump Pit Installation in Santa Clarita, CA

    A well-built sump pit is fundamental to an effective sump pump system. If your basement lacks a suitable pit or needs an upgrade, FKM Plumbing provides expert sump pit installation. We carefully excavate and prepare the pit to the correct dimensions and depth for proper drainage and pump function. This first step is critical for any new sump pump installation and limits the sediment buildup that can damage the pump.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Santa Clarita

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by finding the low point where water collects. We saw cut the slab, excavate a pit roughly 18 to 24 inches wide and 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a perforated or solid liner depending on whether we are collecting groundwater or surface seepage. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor stays clean and level.

    Sump Pump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Santa Clarita

    A complete sump pump system requires a properly installed basin and discharge line. We handle the excavation and placement of the sump basin, setting it at the lowest point of your basement floor so it collects water well. We also install the discharge line, making sure it is correctly sloped, sealed and routed away from your foundation to a suitable drainage area, so water does not find its way back into your Santa Clarita property.
