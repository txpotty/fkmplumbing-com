    Pilot Light Repair in Santa Clarita

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Santa Clarita

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures often involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the failure. Correct vent slope and termination are checked before we leave.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Santa Clarita, CA

    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Repair in Santa Clarita

    The cost of gas water heater repair in Santa Clarita, CA can range from $150 to $800, depending on the complexity of the issue and the parts required. Minor repairs like a thermocouple replacement might be on the lower end, while a gas control valve replacement could be higher. We provide a detailed estimate after finding the problem.

    Most common gas water heater repairs can be finished within 1 to 3 hours. More complex issues, or cases where special parts need to be ordered, could extend the timeline. We strive for efficiency without compromising safety or quality.

    Typical causes include a weak thermocouple, a dirty pilot tube, a clogged flame arrestor or poor combustion air. Drafts in the garage can also snuff out older pilots.

    A thermocouple is a safety device in a gas water heater that senses if the pilot light is lit. If it doesn't detect heat, it shuts off the gas supply to prevent gas leaks. A faulty thermocouple is a very common reason for a pilot light not staying lit and is a vital safety component.

    Because of the dangers that come with natural gas and possible carbon monoxide poisoning, gas water heater repairs should always be handled by a licensed professional. DIY attempts can be dangerous and may void warranties or violate local Santa Clarita, CA plumbing codes.

    Many Honeywell and White-Rodgers valves use flash codes to report problems like a weak thermopile or a tripped sensor. We read the code and test the matching part.
