    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in San Marcos

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is badly deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an excellent long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This eliminates future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in San Marcos with aging copper or galvanized pipes.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in San Marcos, CA

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel. Mineral scale and constant recirculation can wear the pipe wall faster. We repair the leak or reroute the hot line, and we can adjust recirculation pumps to reduce wear. Warm floors and higher gas or electric bills are common signs.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in San Marcos

    Cold water slab leaks are harder to feel but still waste water and wet the soil. They often show up as a high water bill or a meter that moves with all fixtures off. We isolate the cold line, find the leak and repair or reroute it. Fixing cold leaks promptly helps protect your foundation.
