    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in San Marcos

    Most standard gas or electric tank replacements cost $1,400 to $3,000 including the heater, labor and typical code upgrades. Attic installs, larger tanks or relocations can raise the cost to $3,000 to $4,500.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but often have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.

    Flushing a traditional tank water heater annually is recommended in most homes. Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California install softeners or scale filters. This helps remove sediment buildup, which can reduce efficiency and shorten the lifespan of the unit. Regular flushing can also prevent scale from impacting heating elements.

    Tankless offers endless hot water and a longer lifespan but costs more upfront. We can compare both options around your household and budget.

    Most replacements take two to four hours. Attic installs or relocations can take longer.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, a metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit or a water heater that is over 10 to 12 years old. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a professional.
