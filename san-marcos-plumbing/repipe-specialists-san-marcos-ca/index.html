    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in San Marcos

    Copper piping offers exceptional durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in San Marcos. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Our team carefully solders each joint, creating a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that can withstand the test of time, including changing temperatures and local water conditions.     PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in San Marcos

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to three days.

    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in San Marcos, CA

    Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California install softeners or scale filters. Water in many areas contains minerals that contribute to scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can speed up corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral accumulation, supporting a longer-lasting plumbing system.
