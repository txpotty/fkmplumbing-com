    Leaky Shower Faucet Repair in Corona

    A dripping shower faucet can waste hundreds of gallons of water a year and drive up your utility bill. Our plumbers quickly identify the source of the leak, which is often a worn-out O-ring, washer, or cartridge within the faucet assembly. We carry a wide range of replacement parts for common brands like Delta, Moen, Kohler, and Price Pfister, allowing for same-day repair. We will ensure the new components are correctly installed, stopping the drip and saving you water.

    Tub and Shower Combination Repair in Corona, CA

    Many homes in Corona, CA feature tub and shower combination units, which means their plumbing is connected. Issues like faulty diverter valves, shared drain clogs, or a leaky tub spout can affect the entire unit. Our plumbers are skilled at diagnosing and repairing problems specific to these combination fixtures, ensuring both your tub and shower function perfectly. We handle all components, from the faucet to the overflow drain.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Corona, CA

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This restores full flow to the showerhead.

    Showerhead and Arm Repair in Corona

    Dripping showerheads, leaking arm connections and broken handheld hoses are common problems. We clean or replace showerheads, reseal shower arms and install new handheld hoses and holders. We can also add a slide bar for a handheld shower. Hard water clogs nozzles, so we recommend heads with flexible rubber nozzles that are simple to clean.

    Leaking Shower Valve Repair in Corona

    A shower that drips after it is turned off typically has a worn cartridge or seals. Hard water scale scratches cartridge surfaces and breaks down O-rings faster than soft water does. We replace the cartridge with a genuine part and lubricate the valve with silicone grease. This stops the drip and makes the handle turn easily again.
