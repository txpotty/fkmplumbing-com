    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Corona, CA

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial protector against corrosion. Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California add softeners or scale filters. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod.     Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in Corona

    Sediment buildup is a frequent issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high. Our standard flushing service removes these mineral deposits, which can reduce efficiency and lead to premature tank failure.     Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Corona, CA

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. This tank takes up excess pressure created during the heating cycle, protecting your hot water tank and other plumbing fixtures. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition. Proper expansion tank function is essential to preventing premature wear on your hot water tank and protecting system integrity.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Corona

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. High street pressure is not unusual in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.
