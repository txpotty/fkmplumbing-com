    Submersible sump pumps sit directly inside the sump pit, operating quietly and taking up less space. They are usually more powerful and durable, making them a popular choice for many homeowners.     Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Corona, CA

    Many older homes in Corona, CA have crawl spaces that are vulnerable to moisture issues and standing water.     Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Corona, CA

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to reach for maintenance and repair. Our team is experienced in installing these pumps, ensuring proper pit depth and discharge line setup for reliable performance in Corona, CA.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Corona, CA

    Strong storms can knock out power right when your pump is needed most. Battery backup pumps take over automatically when the main pump fails or power goes out. We install backup systems with sealed batteries and charging units. Many include alarms that warn you about problems. Backups add an extra layer of protection for finished basements.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Corona

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, compacted clay or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a dependable place to collect and remove water.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Corona

    For maximum protection, consider a combination sump pump system which integrates both a primary and a battery backup pump into one unit. These systems are pre-assembled for simpler installation and offer a compact solution for flood prevention. FKM Plumbing can install these efficient units, ensuring your Corona home has two layers of defense against water damage.
