    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Maintenance in Corona

    The cost of water softener maintenance in Corona, CA typically ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the system's condition and the services required. Factors like cleaning a heavily fouled brine tank or replacing minor worn parts can influence the final price. We provide a detailed quote before starting any work.

    Resin often lasts 10 to 15 years, but chlorinated city water and iron can shorten that. Fine resin beads in your faucet aerators can point to resin breakdown.

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank, preventing salt from dissolving into the water. This means the system can't regenerate properly, leading to hard water. Our maintenance service includes breaking up and removing salt bridges, bringing back your softener's function.

    Look for spots on dishes, scale on faucets, stiff laundry or soap that will not lather. Salt that never goes down in the brine tank is another telling sign.

    While you can perform basic tasks like checking salt levels and refilling the brine tank, professional maintenance involves inspecting internal components, testing resin efficiency and diagnosing control valve issues. These tasks require professional tools and experience to ensure the system functions correctly and safely.

    You can add salt, break small bridges and run manual regenerations. Valve rebuilds, injector cleaning and resin work are best left to a plumber with the right parts.

    For the best performance and longevity, we recommend professional water softener maintenance annually. In areas with extremely hard water, or for older systems, a twice-yearly check-up might be worthwhile to prevent issues before they become serious.

    Maintenance usually includes inspecting the brine tank for salt bridges, cleaning the tank, checking and cleaning the resin bed, lubricating moving parts, sanitizing the system and running diagnostic checks on the control valve. We also test water hardness to confirm effectiveness.
