    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Corona, CA

    Municipal water across much of Southern California is hard, leaving chalky scale on faucets, showerheads, and inside water heater tanks. Water in many areas contains minerals that cause scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can accelerate corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral buildup, supporting a longer-lasting plumbing system.

    Copper Repiping in Corona, CA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes typically cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.
