    Drywall and flooring replacement usually do not need permits, but water heater replacement, electrical repairs and structural work often do. We handle plumbing permits when needed.

    We use modern equipment for efficient flood restoration, including high-capacity submersible pumps, industrial-grade wet vacuums, powerful air movers from brands like Dri-Eaz and Abatement Technologies, and commercial dehumidifiers.     Most standard homeowner's insurance policies do not cover flood damage, which typically requires a separate flood insurance policy.     While you can handle very small, clean water spills, professional flood restoration is highly recommended for serious water damage. DIY efforts often miss hidden moisture, leading to long-term problems.
