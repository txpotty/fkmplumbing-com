    Comprehensive Water Leak Detection Services in East Los Angeles, CA

    Toilet and Fixture Leak Detection in East Los Angeles, CA

    Toilets are one of the most common sources of wasted water. A worn flapper or a fill valve that never shuts off can leak quietly around the clock. We use dye tests to find leaking toilets and check faucets, showers and supply lines for drips. These fixes are usually quick and save water right away.

    Leak Detection for PEX and Copper Pipes in East Los Angeles

    Whether your plumbing system uses modern PEX tubing or traditional copper pipes, FKM Plumbing has the expertise to detect leaks in both materials. PEX, while flexible, can be susceptible to damage from rodents or improper installation, while copper can corrode over time, especially with hard water. Our tools are effective across various pipe materials, ensuring accurate detection regardless of your home's plumbing type.

    Slab Leak Detection in East Los Angeles

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often signaled by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. FKM Plumbing uses specialized acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is key to preventing major foundation damage.

    Pool Water Leak Detection in East Los Angeles, CA

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss. Then we pressure test pool plumbing and dye test fittings, lights and skimmers. Finding the leak safeguards the pool and the soil around it.

    Water Meter Leak Testing in East Los Angeles

    The water meter is the first and simplest leak test. With every fixture and appliance off, a moving leak indicator means water is escaping somewhere. We read the flow rate to estimate how many gallons are being lost. Then we shut off zones one at a time to see which part of the property is leaking.

    Smart Leak Detection Device Installation in East Los Angeles

    Smart shutoff valves like Moen Flo and Phyn monitor water flow and can shut off water automatically when they detect a leak. Small sensors placed near water heaters, sinks and washers send alerts to your phone. We install and set up these systems so you get warnings before small leaks become big ones. Some insurers offer premium discounts for these devices.

    Hidden Wall & Ceiling Leak Detection in East Los Angeles, CA

    Leaks behind walls or in ceilings can cause serious damage before they become visible, leading to stained drywall, peeling paint or a musty odor. These often stem from faulty pipe connections, old galvanized pipes or issues with shower pans and drains. Our technicians use moisture meters and infrared cameras to scan these areas, identifying elevated moisture levels and temperature differences that indicate a hidden leak, minimizing the need for extensive wall demolition.

    Preventative Leak Detection Audits in East Los Angeles, CA

    Beyond emergency services, FKM Plumbing also offers preventative leak detection audits for homeowners and businesses in East Los Angeles, CA. This proactive approach involves a thorough inspection of your plumbing system to identify potential weak points or very small, developing leaks before they become major problems. Regular audits can help extend the life of your plumbing, save on water bills and avoid unexpected expensive repairs, especially in older properties.
