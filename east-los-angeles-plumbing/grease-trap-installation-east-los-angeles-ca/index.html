    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in East Los Angeles, CA

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the involved excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, ensuring compliance with all local Southern California ordinances for waste management.

    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in East Los Angeles

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with tight space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid easily.

    Automatic Grease Removal Units in East Los Angeles

    Automatic grease removal devices skim grease from the water and collect it in a container your staff empties. Brands like Thermaco Big Dipper are popular in kitchens with limited space and frequent cleaning needs. We install these units with the correct power supply and drain connections. They can reduce the need for frequent pump-outs in smaller kitchens. We explain daily maintenance so your staff keeps the unit running well.

    New Restaurant Build-Out Grease Traps in East Los Angeles

    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in East Los Angeles

    Small indoor hydromechanical traps typically cost $1,500 to $5,000 installed. Large outdoor gravity interceptors often run $8,000 to $30,000 or more with excavation, concrete and site restoration. We provide a written estimate after a site visit.

    Determining the correct grease trap size involves calculating your kitchen's wastewater flow rate, fixture count, and peak operating hours. Our experienced plumbers will conduct a complete assessment of your East Los Angeles business to recommend the appropriate size and type of grease trap that meets local codes and your operational needs.

    We calculate flow from every connected fixture, including sinks, floor sinks and dish machines. Hydromechanical units are typically rated by gallons per minute, while gravity interceptors are sized by volume and retention time.

    The timeline for grease trap installation depends on the project's scope. A simple under-sink unit might take a few hours. Larger outdoor gravity interceptors, which involve excavation and more extensive plumbing, can take anywhere from one to several days. We always aim to limit disruption to your business operations.

    Most food service businesses that cook, wash dishes or prepare food with fats, oils or grease are required to have one. Local pretreatment programs set the specific rules, and we can help you find out what applies to your site.

    Indoor traps should be close to the fixtures they serve and easy to open for cleaning. Outdoor interceptors are typically placed where a pump truck can reach them without blocking customer traffic.

    We install all common types of grease traps, including gravity grease interceptors, hydro-mechanical grease traps (automatic grease removal units), and compact under-sink grease traps. Our team helps you determine the best system for your commercial kitchen's specific needs and local code requirements.
