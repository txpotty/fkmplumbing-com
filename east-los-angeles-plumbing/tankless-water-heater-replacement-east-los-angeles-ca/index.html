    Tank to Tankless Conversions in East Los Angeles, CA

    Converting from a 40 or 50 gallon tank to tankless frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss in a garage or closet. The catch is that tankless units often need a larger gas line, often three quarter inch or bigger, plus new venting. We evaluate your meter size and pipe run before quoting so there are no surprises mid-job. We also cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer used. Plan for a conversion to take a full day.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in East Los Angeles, CA

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can wear out plastic covers and wiring over time. On an occasional cold night, covering backflow preventers and outdoor valves is typically enough to avoid a break. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to guard the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the easiest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in East Los Angeles

    When your existing tankless has a cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board that is not worth repairing, a direct replacement is often the fastest fix. We also replace the old service valves if they are corroded or stuck. Most like-for-like swaps are done in four to six hours.
