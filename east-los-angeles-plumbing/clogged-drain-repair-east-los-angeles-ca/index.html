    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Drain Repair in East Los Angeles

    Clearing a single sink, tub or shower drain typically costs $125 to $300. Harder clogs that need a larger machine or access through a roof vent run $250 to $450. Hydro jetting a heavily scaled or greasy line usually costs $350 to $800.

    Most common household clogs, like those in sinks or showers, can be cleared within one to two hours. More severe blockages, such as main sewer line clogs requiring camera inspection and hydro-jetting, might take a few hours to a full day, depending on the difficulty.

    Most standard clogged drain repairs, such as those in a sink or bathtub, can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. More complex issues, like main sewer line clogs or those requiring camera inspection and hydro-jetting, might take 3 to 4 hours or longer, depending on the severity.

    Common signs of a clogged drain include slow drainage, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets, foul odors coming from your pipes, and water backing up into sinks, tubs, or showers. If multiple drains are affected, it often points to a main line issue.

    Drain snaking uses a flexible auger to physically break through or pull out blockages. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of pipes, thoroughly removing scale, grease, and roots, providing a much more complete cleaning. Hydro-jetting is often used for tough clogs or routine maintenance.

    A plunger or a hand-held hair snake can handle many simple tub and sink clogs. If the clog returns, affects more than one fixture or does not respond to a plunger, call a plumber. Forcing a cable can crack old pipes or scratch fixtures.

    You should call a professional if a plunger or simple home remedies don't work, if you experience multiple clogged drains, sewage smells, or water backing up. These symptoms often point to a deeper, more serious issue in your main sewer line that requires expert diagnosis and specialized tools.

    To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down kitchen drains, use drain screens to catch hair and food particles, and flush only toilet paper. Regular professional drain maintenance, especially in areas with hard water, can also help prevent mineral buildup.
