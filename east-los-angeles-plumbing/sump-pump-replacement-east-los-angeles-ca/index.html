    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in East Los Angeles, CA

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more durable. We provide expert pedestal sump pump replacement in East Los Angeles, CA, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a primary concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in East Los Angeles, CA

    Sometimes it is not just the pump that needs replacing, since the sump basin or pit itself may be cracked, damaged or too small. FKM Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in East Los Angeles, CA so your entire system functions as it should. A properly sized and intact pit is essential for the efficient operation and longevity of your new sump pump.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in East Los Angeles

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in East Los Angeles for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, appropriately sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and maximize efficiency. That is key for handling heavy rainfall.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in East Los Angeles

    Power outages during severe storms can leave a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump offers crucial protection during these times. We install and replace complete battery backup systems, including dedicated marine-grade batteries and smart charging units. Our team helps you select a system with enough run-time to protect your East Los Angeles home through extended power interruptions. That gives you peace of mind when the lights go out.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in East Los Angeles

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.
