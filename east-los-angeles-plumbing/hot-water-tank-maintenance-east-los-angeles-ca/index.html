    A properly working temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank.     Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in East Los Angeles, CA

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial defense against corrosion.     Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in East Los Angeles

    A standard flush and inspection usually runs $125 to $250 depending on tank size and access. Anode replacement typically adds $75 to $200 in parts and labor, and powered anodes cost more.

    A typical hot water tank maintenance service usually takes about 1 to 2 hours. This includes draining, flushing, inspection and any minor adjustments or part swaps like an anode rod.

    Once a year is the minimum for most homes, and more often where water is hard. Homes without a softener or with heavy use may benefit from a flush every six months.

    That smell typically comes from bacteria reacting with a magnesium anode. We can switch to an aluminum-zinc or powered anode and sanitize the tank to get rid of it.
