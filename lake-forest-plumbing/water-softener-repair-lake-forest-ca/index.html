    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Lake Forest, CA

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank and keeps salt from dissolving. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine.     Resin Bed Replacement in Lake Forest

    Over time, the resin beads in your water softener can become fouled or degrade, losing their ability to exchange ions and soften water.
