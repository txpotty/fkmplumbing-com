    Frequently Asked Questions About Faucet Repair in Lake Forest

    The cost of faucet repair in Lake Forest can range from $120 to $350, depending on the type of faucet, the specific parts needed, and the complexity of the repair. A simple washer replacement will be on the lower end, while a cartridge replacement in a high-end faucet might be more. We provide a clear estimate before any work begins.

    A worn cartridge, washer or valve seat is the most common culprit. Hard water scale makes these parts wear out faster, so drips are common in many homes.

    The most common signs include a persistent drip or leak, difficulty turning the handles, inconsistent water temperature, reduced water pressure from the faucet, or strange noises when operating it. Any of these could point to internal component wear or damage requiring attention.

    Simple cartridge swaps can be a DIY job if you identify the correct part. Stuck cartridges, corroded valve bodies and leaks under the sink are best handled by a plumber.

    Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California install softeners or scale filters. Hard water contains high levels of minerals like calcium and magnesium. Over time, these minerals can build up inside your faucets, corroding internal parts, clogging aerators, and stiffening moving components. This often leads to leaks, reduced water pressure, and a shorter faucet lifespan, making regular maintenance or timely repair important.

    Low pressure at one faucet is typically a clogged aerator or cartridge. Low pressure everywhere could mean a failing pressure regulator, a supply issue or old corroded pipes.

    Most repairs take 30 to 60 minutes. If a special part must be ordered, we might need a second short visit.
