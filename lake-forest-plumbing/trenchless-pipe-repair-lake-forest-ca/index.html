    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Lake Forest

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light. Once hardened, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Lake Forest

    We use proven trenchless technologies and brands like Perma-Liner for reliable pipe repair in Lake Forest. Perma-Liner systems are well known for their durability and effectiveness in creating long-lasting, structural pipe repairs. Their CIPP products are designed to hold up to harsh conditions, offering a robust solution for your underground plumbing.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Lake Forest, CA

    For severely damaged or collapsed pipes, pipe bursting offers a full replacement solution. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, breaking apart the old pipe at the same time. This provides a completely new, durable pipe, often HDPE (high-density polyethylene), which resists corrosion and leaks. It's an effective long-term fix for extensive issues in Lake Forest, CA.
