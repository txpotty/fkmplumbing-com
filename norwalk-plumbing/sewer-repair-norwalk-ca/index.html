    Sewer Line Leak Detection & Repair in Norwalk, CA

    Undetected sewer line leaks can lead to unpleasant odors, wet spots in your yard and even structural damage to your home's foundation. FKM Plumbing uses accurate leak detection methods to pinpoint the source of the leak, whether it's a minor crack or a major break.     Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Norwalk

    When trenchless repair isn't possible due to severe damage or specific pipe configurations, traditional dig-and-replace methods are necessary. This involves excavating a trench to access and replace the damaged section of the sewer line. While more intrusive, it is sometimes the only way to address severely collapsed pipes, significant offsets or certain pipe materials common in older Southern California properties. Our team makes sure of careful excavation and efficient replacement, minimizing the impact on your property in Norwalk.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Norwalk

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's far more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and stopping recurring clogs. This service is especially helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Norwalk dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with persistent drain issues.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Norwalk

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to repeat clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it will hold.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Norwalk, CA

    Tree roots are a leading cause of sewer line blockages and damage, especially with the mature trees found in many Norwalk neighborhoods. Roots can infiltrate tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog pipes. We use specialized augers and hydro-jetting to cut through and remove roots, followed by inspection to assess pipe condition. For ongoing issues, we may recommend trenchless repair or pipe replacement to prevent future root problems.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Norwalk, CA

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive solution. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from major disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an effective and popular choice for preserving property appearance in Norwalk.
