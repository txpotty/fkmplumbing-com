    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in Norwalk

    Polybutylene's inherent weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California install softeners or scale filters. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already breaking down. Understanding this failure mechanism is important for diagnosing issues and recommending the most effective and permanent replacement strategy for your home.
