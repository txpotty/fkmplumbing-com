    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Norwalk, CA

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, similar to a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to weigh cost and performance.

A warm, dry climate is the main factor for plumbing in Southern California, with long irrigation seasons and year-round demand on outdoor lines. While rigid pipes can burst when water freezes, PEX can expand and contract, greatly lowering the risk of burst pipes during cold snaps.     Properly installed PEX is expected to last many decades. Manufacturers often offer 25 year warranties when their pipe and fittings are used together.
