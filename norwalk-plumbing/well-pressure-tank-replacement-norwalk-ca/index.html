    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Norwalk

    When your well pressure tank shows signs of trouble, it is important to determine if a repair is practical or if full replacement is necessary. Small air leaks or issues with the air valve might be repairable. However, if the tank itself is corroded, has a ruptured diaphragm, or is waterlogged, replacement is almost always the more cost-effective and reliable option. Our technicians in Norwalk provide an straightforward assessment, explaining the pros and cons of repair versus a complete well pressure tank replacement, so you can make an informed decision.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Norwalk

    The tank tee is the manifold that connects the tank, switch, gauge, relief valve and drain. Galvanized tees corrode and restrict flow, especially with sandy or mineral-heavy water. We replace them with brass or stainless steel tank tees that hold up much longer. A new liquid-filled gauge gives accurate readings, and a fresh relief valve protects against a stuck switch. These small parts make a big difference in reliability.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Norwalk, CA

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop, typically at 30/50 or 40/60 psi. Pitted contacts, insect nests and a clogged sensing nipple are frequent causes of failure in well houses. We replace worn switches with Square D or similar models and clean or replace the nipple below it. A low-pressure cutoff switch can also be added to stop the pump if the well runs dry. We set the switch to suit your new tank's pre-charge.

    Constant Pressure System Conversion in Norwalk, CA

    A constant pressure system pairs a variable speed drive like the Franklin SubDrive or Grundfos SQE with a smaller pressure tank. The pump speeds up or slows down to hold a set pressure, so showers do not fade when a sprinkler kicks on. These systems cost more, often $2,500 to $5,000, but they end pressure swings. They also cut down on hard starts on the motor. We explain whether your well and pump are good candidates.

    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Norwalk

    Bladder tanks hold water inside a flexible butyl bag surrounded by pressurized air. When the bag ruptures, water fills the whole tank, there is no air cushion and the pump short cycles. A simple test is pressing the air valve on top, since water spitting out means the bladder has failed. We replace the tank, set the new pre-charge and verify the pump runs a full cycle. Most bladder tank swaps take about two to three hours.
