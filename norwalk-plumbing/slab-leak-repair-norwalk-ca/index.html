    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Norwalk, CA

    When a slab leak is localized and accessible, direct repair is often the most practical solution. This involves carefully jackhammering a small section of your foundation to expose the damaged pipe, then repairing or replacing the damaged section. Our technicians are skilled in careful concrete cutting and restoration, so the repair is effective and the area is properly restored. We use durable materials designed to hold up to soil movement under the slab.

    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Norwalk, CA

    Whether your home in Norwalk, CA has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our plumbers are skilled in repairing both. Pinhole leaks are common in older copper due to corrosive soil conditions and water chemistry, while PEX can sometimes suffer from installation issues or rodent damage. We carry a full range of parts and tools for both materials, making for a correct and durable repair no matter your pipe type.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Norwalk

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method skips the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Norwalk with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Norwalk, CA

    A major slab leak can push water up through flooring or into walls quickly. We help you shut off the water and send a technician to isolate the leaking line. Often we can restore water to the rest of the house while we plan the repair. Prompt action limits flooring damage and soil saturation.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Norwalk, CA

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Norwalk, CA
