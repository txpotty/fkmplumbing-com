    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Norwalk

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through inexpensive PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. A warm, dry climate is the main factor for plumbing in Southern California, with long irrigation seasons and year-round demand on outdoor lines. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth the price.

    When your existing tankless has a cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board that is not worth repairing, a direct replacement is often the quickest fix.     Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Norwalk, CA

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside.
