    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Norwalk

    Well pump replacement usually costs $1,800 to $4,500 depending on well depth and pump size. Deep wells or constant pressure systems can cost $4,000 to $8,000.

    Most new well pumps come with a manufacturer's warranty, usually ranging from 1 to 5 years, covering defects in materials and workmanship. FKM Plumbing also backs our installation work with our own labor warranty for complete peace of mind.

    In many areas, permits may be required for significant plumbing work like well pump replacement, especially if it involves changing the well system's electrical or structural components. FKM Plumbing will help you work through any permitting in Norwalk, CA so your installation is fully compliant with local regulations.

    It is often a good idea to consider replacing your pressure tank at the same time as your well pump, especially if both are of similar age. A new pressure tank can improve the efficiency and lifespan of your new pump by reducing cycling. It also saves on labor costs to do both jobs at once.

    If your pressure tank is old or waterlogged, replacing it at the same time can protect the new pump. A failed tank causes rapid short cycling, which is one of the most common reasons submersible motors burn out early.

    Well pump replacement is a complex job that involves high voltage electricity, heavy equipment and possibly deep well access. It calls for specialized tools and experience for proper installation, electrical safety and compliance with local plumbing codes. It is strongly recommended to hire a licensed professional to avoid injury, property damage or costly mistakes.

    Most well pump replacements are completed in one day. Deep wells or complex systems may take longer.
