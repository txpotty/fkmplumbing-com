    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in San Buenaventura (Ventura), CA

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Municipal water across much of Southern California is hard, leaving white scale on faucets, showerheads, and inside water heater tanks. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides extra protection.
