    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Pomona, CA

    The inlet and outlet baffles inside your septic tank are essential for separating solids from liquids and keeping them out of the drain field. Over time, these baffles can deteriorate, leading to system failure. FKM Plumbing provides professional repair and replacement of damaged septic tank baffles, using durable materials that hold up to the harsh conditions within the tank and protect your entire septic system in Pomona, CA.

    Septic Tank Clog Removal in Pomona

    Stubborn clogs in your septic tank or main line can quickly lead to backups and foul odors. Our technicians use hydro-jetting equipment to clear even the toughest blockages, restoring proper flow. We identify the cause, whether it is grease buildup, non-flushable items or root intrusion, and provide solutions to prevent future issues. This service is crucial for maintaining a healthy septic system in Pomona homes.

    Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Pomona

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field quickly. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where appropriate.

    Septic Repair Cost Comparison in Pomona, CA

    Baffle or tee replacement often costs $300 to $900, and effluent pump replacement typically runs $900 to $2,000. Inlet line repairs range from $800 to $3,000 depending on depth and length.
