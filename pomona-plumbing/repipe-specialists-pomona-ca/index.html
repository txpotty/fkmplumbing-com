    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Pomona

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Most homes in Southern California sit on concrete slabs, so slab leaks often show up as warm floors, damp carpet, or an unexplained jump in the water bill. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Pomona, CA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. FKM Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in Pomona, CA, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points. PEX also noticeably dampens water hammer noise, providing a quieter plumbing system for your home.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Pomona

    Copper piping offers exceptional durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in Pomona. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Our team meticulously solders each joint, creating a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that can withstand the test of time, including changing temperatures and local water conditions. Copper also inherently inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.

    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Pomona, CA

    Municipal water across much of Southern California is hard, leaving white scale on faucets, showerheads, and inside water heater tanks. Water in many areas contains minerals that contribute to scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can speed up corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral accumulation, ensuring a longer-lasting plumbing system.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Pomona

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to three days.

    Emergency Repipe and Leak Repair in Pomona

    While a full repipe is a planned project, sometimes a major pipe burst or multiple severe leaks can call for immediate action. Our repipe specialists in Pomona are equipped to handle emergency situations, providing temporary fixes and assessing the need for a more complete repiping solution. We can quickly isolate damaged sections and offer options for immediate repair or a phased repiping approach to restore your water service safely and efficiently, preventing extensive water damage.
