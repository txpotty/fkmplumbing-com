    Some local homes have water heaters in attics or interior closets. These installs require careful planning for access, drain pans and relief line routing.     Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Pomona, CA

    Electric tank water heaters are common in homes without natural gas service. We install models with the correct voltage and breaker size for your electrical panel. These units do not need venting, which allows more flexible placement. We also install a drain pan when the heater is in a closet or attic. Electric models are simple, dependable and quiet.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Pomona, CA

    Tank water heater installations usually cost less upfront than tankless systems. Gas tankless installs can cost more because of gas line upgrades and venting.     Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Pomona

    Gas tank water heaters provide strong recovery and are common in many local homes. We install 40, 50 and 75 gallon models with proper venting and combustion air.     Gas Water Heater Replacement in Pomona

    Many homes in Pomona use natural gas for their water heaters due to its efficiency and lower operating costs. When replacing a gas water heater, precise gas line connections, proper ventilation and carbon monoxide detection are critical. Our licensed plumbers are experienced with gas appliance installation, making sure every safety measure is met. We check for leaks, test the igniter and confirm proper exhaust, adhering to all local Southern California codes. You can trust us to safely and effectively install your new gas water heater, providing dependable hot water.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Pomona

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, particularly in closed-loop systems. Plumbing contractors here must hold a state contractor license, and permits are typically required for water heaters, repiping, and sewer work. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to support proper system operation and prevent premature wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Pomona. This small component offers significant long-term protection.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Pomona, CA

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and plumbing.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Pomona

    The cost of water heater installation in Pomona, CA typically ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of water heater (tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed plumbing or electrical modifications. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your specific needs.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 6 to 12 years on the tank and parts. We also warranty our installation labor.

    FKM Plumbing installs all major trusted brands of water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz. We work with both gas and electric models, as well as various sizes and efficiencies. Our team can help you choose the best brand and model to suit your hot water demands and budget.

    Common signs that you might need a new water heater include a lack of hot water, rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit and an age of 10 to 15 years or more. If you notice any of these issues, it is wise to have a professional inspection.

    Most standard water heater installations, particularly for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours. However, a conversion from a tank to a tankless system, or installations requiring major plumbing or electrical modifications, might take a full day or longer. We will provide an estimated timeline during our initial assessment.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. For tank units, a 40 to 50 gallon tank is common for 2 to 4 people, while larger families might need 60 to 80 gallons. For tankless units, it is about flow rate (GPM) and temperature rise. Our plumbers will assess your needs to recommend the ideal size.

    A standard tank replacement typically takes two to four hours. Tankless installs or relocations can take most of a day.

    A traditional tank water heater usually lasts between 8 and 12 years with proper maintenance. Tankless water heaters often have a longer lifespan, ranging from 15 to 20 years, thanks to their durable components and on-demand operation.
