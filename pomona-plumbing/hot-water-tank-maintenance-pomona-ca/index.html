    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Pomona, CA

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. This tank takes up excess pressure created during the heating cycle, protecting your hot water tank and other plumbing fixtures. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition.     Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Pomona

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. Plenty of families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Pomona, CA

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is vital for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it changes the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's badly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, sparing you a full tank replacement.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Pomona

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure jump every heating cycle. High street pressure is not unusual in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.
