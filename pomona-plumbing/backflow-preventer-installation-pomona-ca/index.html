    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer Installation in Pomona

    The cost for backflow preventer installation in Pomona, CA can range from about $300 to $1,500, depending on the type of device required, such as a DCVA or an RPZ, the complexity of the installation and any changes needed to existing plumbing. Commercial installations with larger devices may cost more. We provide a clear, upfront quote after an on-site assessment.

    Backflow preventers are usually installed as a preventive measure or because code requires them. Signs of possible cross-contamination include discolored or foul-smelling tap water, unexplained drops in water pressure or visible dirty water entering a clean water source, though these can also point to broader plumbing issues as well.

    Backflow preventers are installed at cross-connection points where non-potable water could enter the potable water supply. Common locations include irrigation system connections, fire sprinkler lines, boiler systems and connections to auxiliary water supplies, often just after the main water meter or at the point of hazard.

    An RPZ, or reduced pressure zone device, provides the highest level of protection and is used for high-hazard applications where contaminants could be toxic. A DCVA, or double check valve assembly, is used for low-hazard applications where contaminants are non-toxic. The choice comes down to the risk of contamination at the cross-connection point.

    You probably need a backflow preventer if you have an irrigation system, a fire sprinkler system, a swimming pool or any auxiliary water source that connects to the main water supply. Local regulations in Pomona often require these devices for certain applications to protect public health.

    In Pomona, most backflow preventers must be tested once a year by a certified backflow tester to confirm they are working correctly and providing adequate protection. This is a separate service from installation but is essential for ongoing compliance and safety.
