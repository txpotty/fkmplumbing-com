    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Pomona, CA

    PEX and HDPE are flexible, which means fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. HDPE is especially durable for long runs and resists cracking in expansive clay. We use approved compression or fusion fittings and add tracer wire so the line can be found with a locator later. PEX must stay out of sunlight before burial, so we store and lay it carefully on every job site.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Pomona

    PEX, or cross-linked polyethylene, is a popular choice for its flexibility, durability and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Intense sun breaks down exposed plastic pipe, sprinkler parts, and outdoor fittings, so they wear out faster than indoor plumbing. FKM Plumbing professionally installs PEX water lines for both new construction and replacements in Pomona. PEX offers excellent flow rates and is less prone to bursting than traditional rigid pipe, providing a long-lasting, cost-effective solution for your home's plumbing.

    New Construction Water Service Lines in Pomona

    New homes, guest houses and additions need a correctly sized service line from the meter to the building. We review the plans, confirm meter size with your water provider and choose a line diameter that matches the fixture load. Most homes need a 3/4 inch or 1 inch line, while larger homes with pools and big irrigation systems often benefit from 1 1/4 inch. We coordinate the rough-in timing with your builder so the line is ready when the inspector arrives.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Installation in Pomona

    The cost of water line installation in Pomona, CA varies a lot depending on pipe material, PEX or copper, the length of the line, excavation depth and site access. A basic main water line replacement can range from $2,500 to $8,000, while new construction installations might be part of a larger plumbing package. We provide a detailed estimate after a site assessment.

    In most communities the homeowner owns the line from the meter to the home, while the utility owns the meter and the line on the street side. We verify responsibility with your provider before work begins.

    Most main water line installations or replacements for a typical home in Pomona can be finished within one to three days. This timeline includes excavation, pipe laying, connections, testing and backfilling. More complex projects, such as long trenches or difficult access, may take a bit longer.

    Some homeowners dig their own trench, but connecting to the meter, sizing the pipe and passing inspection are best handled by a licensed plumber. A poor joint underground can leak for months before anyone notices.

    Pipe manufacturers offer long warranties on PEX and HDPE, and copper has a long track record. We also warranty our installation labor and will review the specific terms with you before work starts.

    PEX is flexible, less expensive, resistant to freezing and corrosion, and has fewer fittings, which means fewer potential leak points. Copper is rigid, more expensive, has a proven track record, is naturally antimicrobial and can withstand higher temperatures. Both are excellent choices, and the best option depends on your budget, property needs and local water conditions in Pomona.

    Most homes in Southern California sit on concrete slabs, so slab leaks often show up as warm floors, damp carpet, or an unexplained jump in the water bill. Soil that shifts and settles puts stress on underground pipes. This is why flexible materials like PEX are frequently recommended, and why proper bedding and backfill during installation are essential to prevent pipe damage from ground movement.

    Yes, installing or replacing a main water line in Pomona, CA usually requires a plumbing permit from the local building department. This ensures the work meets code and is inspected for safety and compliance. FKM Plumbing handles all permit applications for your project.
