    Ball Faucet Repair in Pomona, CA

    Ball faucets, recognizable by their single lever that moves over a rounded cap, are common in many kitchens. They use a rotating ball joint to control water flow and temperature. If your ball faucet is leaking from the spout or base, or if the handle feels loose, it's probably time for a repair. We can replace the spring-loaded rubber seats and O-rings, which are usually the culprits, restoring smooth operation and preventing drips.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Pomona

    Ceramic disc faucets are known for their durability and are a popular choice in many contemporary Pomona bathrooms. While generally reliable, they can develop leaks if the ceramic discs become scratched or misaligned, or if the inlet and outlet seals wear out. Our plumbers have the expertise to carefully disassemble these precise mechanisms, clean or replace the ceramic discs, and reseal the faucet to eliminate leaks and restore full performance.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Pomona

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. On an occasional cold night, covering backflow preventers and outdoor valves is typically enough to avoid a break. We repair leaking packing nuts, replace worn washers and install new frost-free hose bibs when necessary. We also add vacuum breakers to meet backflow prevention requirements. A leaking hose bib can waste a lot of water over a full season of yard use.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Pomona

    Compression faucets, often found in older Pomona homes, are known for their separate hot and cold handles. When these faucets leak, it's usually due to worn-out rubber washers or packing. Our team is skilled in disassembling these classic fixtures, replacing the necessary seals and washers, and reassembling them to stop leaks effectively. We respect the character of older homes and make sure these repairs are done carefully.

    Emergency Faucet Repair in Pomona

    Sometimes, a faucet issue can't wait. A badly leaking or broken faucet can quickly cause significant water damage, especially if it's spraying uncontrollably. We offer emergency faucet repair services in Pomona to address urgent situations promptly. Our team is equipped to respond quickly, limit damage, and provide immediate repairs to secure your property and prevent further water loss, even outside of regular business hours.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Pomona

    Weak or sputtering flow is usually caused by mineral buildup in the aerator or cartridge. We remove and clean or replace the aerator, flush the lines and check the cartridge for scale blockage. On pull-down faucets, the spray head and check valves can also clog. If flow is low at every fixture, we test whole-house water pressure.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Pomona, CA

    A cartridge repair often costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life. However, if the faucet body is cracked, parts are discontinued or it has needed several repairs, replacement may be better value. We give you both prices so you can choose. Many brands like Moen and Delta offer lifetime warranties on cartridges, which can reduce your repair cost.
