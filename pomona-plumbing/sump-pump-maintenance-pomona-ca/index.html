    Dependable Sump Pump Maintenance Services in Pomona, CA

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Pomona

    Our team is experienced in maintaining submersible sump pumps, a popular choice in Pomona due to their quiet operation and compact design. Maintenance involves pulling the pump to inspect the watertight seals, clearing the intake screen and checking the internal components for corrosion or wear. We also verify the electrical connections are secure and free from moisture, avoiding electrical hazards and supporting longevity.

    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Pomona

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacement every three to five years.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Pomona, CA

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    Sump Pump Cleaning and Debris Removal in Pomona

    Over time, sump pits can accumulate dirt, gravel and other debris, which can clog the pump's intake or jam the float switch. Our maintenance process includes carefully cleaning the sump pit and removing any obstructions. This lets the pump operate freely and efficiently, avoiding breakdowns and extending its lifespan for your Pomona property.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Pomona, CA

    For homes equipped with pedestal sump pumps, FKM Plumbing provides specialized maintenance services in Pomona, CA. We focus on inspecting the external motor, ensuring proper lubrication and checking the impeller for any obstructions. This type of pump, with its motor above the water, requires specific attention to its shaft and bearings to prevent premature wear and support reliable performance during heavy rains.

    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Pomona

    Regular annual maintenance is crucial for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump. Our technicians perform a thorough inspection, checking the motor, impeller, float switch and discharge lines for any signs of wear or blockage. We also test the system's operation by filling the pit with water to ensure it activates correctly and pumps water away efficiently, protecting your Pomona home from unexpected flooding.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Pomona

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to premature wear. We confirm these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Pomona residence.

    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Pomona

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last years longer.
