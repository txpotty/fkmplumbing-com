    Drainage Services and Hydrojet Plumbing in Pomona

    Preventative drain maintenance is key to avoiding clogs and sewer backups. Our hydrojet plumbing service can blast away stubborn blockages, keeping your drainage system clear and free-flowing, which is especially important for homes with mature trees near the sewer line.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Pomona

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get swapped out. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility first.

    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Pomona

    Postwar tract homes are common in Southern California, and many still have original galvanized or copper supply lines and cast iron drains under the slab. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Finding a leak early prevents structural damage and mold.

    Sewer Line and Drain Camera Inspection in Pomona, CA

    A camera inspection shows the condition of your sewer line and main drains. We look for roots, bellies, cracks and buildup before they cause a backup. Older homes with clay or cast iron lines benefit most from regular checks. We record the inspection and give you a copy. If cleaning or repair is needed, we explain your options.
