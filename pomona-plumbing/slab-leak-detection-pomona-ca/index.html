    Precise Slab Leak Detection Services in Pomona, CA

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Pomona, CA

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of repeat slab leaks. We can usually complete a full repipe in two to four days.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Pomona, CA

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to see temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a distinct thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in Pomona.

    Electronic Leak Detection in Pomona

    For hard-to-find leaks, our electronic leak detection equipment sends a signal through your pipes and measures changes that indicate a breach. This technology allows us to locate leaks in various pipe materials, including copper, PEX and PVC, commonly found in Pomona homes. It helps prevent exploratory digging, saving you time and money.

    Pressure Testing & Isolation in Pomona

    When a leak is suspected, we can perform pressure tests on individual water lines. By isolating sections of your plumbing system and monitoring pressure gauges, we can confirm the presence of a leak and often determine which particular line is compromised. This systematic approach is crucial for homes with complex plumbing layouts, giving you an accurate diagnosis before any excavation in Pomona, CA.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Pomona, CA

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are harder to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know exactly where to listen.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Pomona

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs nonstop and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before flagging the spot.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Pomona

    Our technicians use highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab. This method involves listening for the subtle sound of escaping water, even through concrete. It's particularly effective for pressurized water lines, allowing us to find leaks without unnecessary excavation and minimizing disruption to your Pomona home.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Pomona

    A slab leak that is flooding floors or causing water to bubble up through tile needs quick attention. We respond quickly, help you shut off the water and isolate the leaking side if possible. That often lets you keep some water service until the repair. Then we locate and fix the leak.
