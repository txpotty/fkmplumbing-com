    Pinhole Leak Repair in Pomona

    Pinhole leaks form when copper pipe wall thins from the inside, often due to water chemistry, high velocity or poor grounding. They start as tiny drips that stain drywall or ceilings. We cut out the affected section and replace it with new pipe or a PEX transition. If we find several pinholes in the same area, we will talk with you about repiping that zone.
