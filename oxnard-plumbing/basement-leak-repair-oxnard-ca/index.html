    Supply Line Leak Repair in Oxnard

    Water lines that run along basement ceilings and walls can leak at fittings, valves or pinholes. Municipal water across much of Southern California is hard, leaving white scale on faucets, showerheads, and inside water heater tanks. Water chemistry affects how fast copper corrodes, and older galvanized lines often rust from the inside. We locate the leak, replace the damaged section with copper or PEX and pressure test the repair. If the line shows widespread wear, we will tell you whether a partial repipe makes more sense.

    Hydrostatic Pressure Management in Oxnard, CA

    Heavy rain can lead to significant hydrostatic pressure against basement walls, forcing water through concrete. Tree roots invade sewer lines during dry months, so the first big rain of the season often exposes a cracked or blocked lateral. FKM Plumbing installs interior and exterior drainage systems, including French drains and weeping tile systems, to alleviate this pressure. We also install sump pump systems to efficiently remove collected water, keeping your basement dry.

    Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in Oxnard

    A basement floor drain that backs up during heavy use or a storm typically points to a blockage or a failed trap. We clear the drain, check the line to the main sewer and replace cracked traps and covers. Installing a backwater valve stops sewage from pushing back into the basement if the main line surcharges. We set the valve where it can be serviced without trouble.

    Interior Drainage Systems in Oxnard

    When exterior excavation isn't practical, interior drainage systems provide an effective solution for managing basement leaks. We install perimeter drains along the basement walls, collecting water before it can surface and directing it to a sump pump for removal. This system is less disruptive and can be a cost-effective way to dry out a wet basement.

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in Oxnard, CA

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes crack and scale with age, and plastic joints can separate if a line was poorly supported. These leaks often show up only when an upstairs fixture runs. We run water through each drain while watching the basement, then confirm the crack with a camera. Repairs range from a single section swap to replacing an entire stack with PVC or ABS.

    Sump Pump Repair and Installation in Oxnard, CA

    Many basements rely on a sump pit to handle storm runoff and groundwater. Tree roots invade sewer lines during dry months, so the first big rain of the season often reveals a cracked or blocked lateral. Pumps from brands like Zoeller, Liberty and Wayne fail most often from a stuck float or a burned-out motor after sitting idle for long stretches. We repair or replace the pump, check the check valve and route the discharge well away from the foundation. A battery backup is worth considering because big storms often knock out power.
