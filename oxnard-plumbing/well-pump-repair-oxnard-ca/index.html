    Jet Pump Repair in Oxnard, CA

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Oxnard

    Submersible pumps are common in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms usually include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use specialized equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, protecting the integrity of your well system.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Oxnard

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Well Pump Control Box & Electrical Repair in Oxnard, CA

    Many well pump failures trace back to electrical issues within the control box or wiring. This can include tripped breakers, faulty capacitors, or corroded connections. Our licensed technicians are trained to safely diagnose and repair these complex electrical components. We make sure all wiring meets Southern California electrical codes, preventing hazards and ensuring reliable pump operation in Oxnard homes.
