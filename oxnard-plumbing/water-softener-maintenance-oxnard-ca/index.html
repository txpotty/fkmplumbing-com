    Resin Bed Analysis and Regeneration in Oxnard

    The resin bed is the heart of your water softener, where ion exchange occurs.     Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repair in Oxnard, CA

    A leaking bypass valve can let hard water slip past the softener without you noticing. We test and rebuild bypass valves and replace cracked fittings. We also inspect the drain line for kinks and proper air gap and the brine overflow line for correct routing. These small fixes prevent leaks and backflow problems.

    Routine Water Softener Tune-Ups in Oxnard

    Our routine tune-ups involve a thorough inspection of your entire water softener system. We check for proper salt levels, inspect the brine tank for salt bridges and confirm the resin bed is working effectively. This preventive service is vital wherever hard water minerals can quickly affect system performance if not regularly addressed.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Oxnard

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, dictating regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages. If issues are found, we can often repair or replace worn parts to restore full function, avoiding the need for a costly full system replacement in Oxnard.
