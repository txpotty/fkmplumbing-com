    Pressure Testing & Isolation in Hemet

    When a leak is suspected, we can perform pressure tests on individual water lines. By isolating sections of your plumbing system and monitoring pressure gauges, we can confirm the presence of a leak and often determine which specific line is compromised. This step-by-step approach is crucial for homes with complex plumbing layouts, ensuring an accurate diagnosis before any excavation in Hemet, CA.

    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in Hemet

    Our team uses electronic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a slab leak. These highly sensitive microphones can detect the faint sound of water escaping from a pressurized pipe under your foundation. This non-invasive method helps us avoid unnecessary concrete breaking, saving you time and money on repairs. It's particularly effective for copper and PEX lines commonly found in Hemet homes.

    Leak Detection for Older Southern California Homes in Hemet, CA

    Many older homes in Hemet, CA have copper or galvanized steel pipes that are more susceptible to corrosion and leaks over time. Our technicians are highly experienced in detecting leaks in these older systems, understanding their common failure points. We provide careful analysis for the challenges presented by vintage plumbing under concrete slabs.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Hemet, CA

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of recurring slab leaks. We can typically complete a full repipe in two to four days.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Hemet, CA

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are more difficult to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know exactly where to listen.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Hemet

    Our technicians use highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab. This method involves listening for the subtle sound of escaping water, even through concrete. It's particularly effective for pressurized water lines, allowing us to find leaks without unnecessary excavation and minimizing disruption to your Hemet home.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Hemet

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak safely or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.
