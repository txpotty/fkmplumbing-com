    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Hemet, CA

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Hemet, CA, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a popular and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers expertly install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.

    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in Hemet, CA

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Hemet

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a common backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Hemet, DCVAs are frequently installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines.     Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights.     Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Hemet

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Hemet. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. FKM Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties.
