    Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in El Cajon

    Hard water can lead to mineral buildup, or scaling, in your tankless water heater's heat exchanger, cutting efficiency and eventually causing breakdowns. Municipal water across much of Southern California is hard, leaving chalky scale on faucets, showerheads, and inside water heater tanks. Our descaling service in El Cajon involves flushing the unit with a specialized cleaning solution to dissolve these mineral deposits.     Ignition Failure Repair in El Cajon, CA

    If your tankless unit will not fire, the cause may be a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. We test each component and measure gas pressure at the inlet.     Recirculation Pump Repair in El Cajon

    Many tankless systems use internal or external recirculation to deliver hot water more quickly. A properly tuned system saves both water and gas.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in El Cajon, CA
