    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in El Cajon

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints.     Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in El Cajon, CA

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is well suited for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a quick and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Southern California homeowners.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in El Cajon

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for long trenching. It is ideal for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in El Cajon that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in El Cajon, CA

    Sewer lines in El Cajon, CA can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Expansive clay and hillside soils can shift over time, putting stress on slab plumbing and cracking older sewer joints. Tree root intrusion is another major culprit, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also add to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.
