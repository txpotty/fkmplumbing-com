    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in El Cajon

    Most water softener installations cost $1,500 to $4,000 including equipment. Adding a new softener loop typically adds $500 to $1,500 depending on access.

    Softeners remove hardness minerals using salt, while conditioners change how minerals behave to reduce scale. Softeners are more reliable for very hard water.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is required for significant plumbing alterations like water softener installation to confirm compliance with local codes. FKM Plumbing will advise you on any required permits for your El Cajon installation and can assist with the application process.

    With proper maintenance, a quality water softener system can last between 10 and 15 years, and sometimes even longer. The lifespan depends on the quality of the unit, the hardness of your water and how well it is maintained. Regular servicing can help extend its life.

    Most residential water softener installations can be completed in half a day, usually between 3 and 6 hours. This timeline can vary based on the complexity of your home's existing plumbing and the type of system being installed. We aim for efficient service with little disruption.

    Maintenance primarily involves periodically adding salt to salt-based systems, usually every 4 to 8 weeks depending on water usage and hardness. It's also good practice to schedule annual check-ups with a professional plumber to ensure the system is operating efficiently and to address any potential issues.

    A properly sized and installed water softener should not significantly affect your water pressure. If you experience a noticeable drop in pressure after installation, it could indicate an issue with the system or installation, which our plumbers can diagnose and resolve.

    We install softeners with Fleck and Clack control valves and other dependable brands. We recommend systems based on performance and serviceability.
