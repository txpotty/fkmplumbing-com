    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in El Cajon

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Expansive clay and hillside soils can shift over time, putting stress on slab plumbing and cracking older sewer joints. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.
