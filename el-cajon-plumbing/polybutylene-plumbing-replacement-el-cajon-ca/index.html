    Full Home Repiping with PEX in El Cajon

    When a home with polybutylene pipes sits on a slab foundation, repiping becomes a more complex process. PEX is an excellent choice for Southern California homes, as it resists freezing better than rigid pipes and expands to accommodate temperature changes. We carefully plan the repipe to limit disruption, often running new lines through attics or existing wall cavities, delivering a complete and long-lasting upgrade for your entire plumbing system.
