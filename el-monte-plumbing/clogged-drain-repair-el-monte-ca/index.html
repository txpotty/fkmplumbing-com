    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Drain Repair in El Monte

    Clearing a single sink, tub or shower drain typically costs $125 to $300. Harder clogs that need a larger machine or access through a roof vent run $250 to $450. Hydro jetting a heavily scaled or greasy line usually costs $350 to $800.

    While chemical drain cleaners are readily available, we generally advise against them. They often contain harsh chemicals that can corrode pipes, especially older ones, and can be hazardous to your health and the environment. Professional plumbers use safer, more effective methods.

    A plunger or a hand-held hair snake can handle many simple tub and sink clogs. If the clog returns, affects more than one fixture or does not respond to a plunger, call a plumber. Forcing a cable can crack old pipes or scratch fixtures.

    Most common household clogs, like those in sinks or showers, can be cleared within one to two hours. More severe blockages, such as main sewer line clogs requiring camera inspection and hydro-jetting, might take a few hours to a full day, depending on the complexity.

    Caustic drain cleaners can damage ABS fittings, corrode older metal pipes and sit in the trap if they do not clear the clog. They also create a hazard for anyone working on the drain afterward. Mechanical clearing is safer and more effective.
