    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in El Monte

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is pulled in and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a smart option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you plainly if your line is a candidate.

    Clay and Orangeburg Line Replacement in El Monte, CA

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, and Orangeburg fiber pipe flattens and blisters with age. Both are near the end of their life in many older properties. When the camera shows multiple failures, replacement is typically the best value. We remove or burst the old line and install modern pipe with a long expected service life. You get a smooth, root-resistant line out to the main.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in El Monte, CA

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement replaces pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward. This is a great fit when protecting landscaping or driveways is a priority. The method works well for many homes in El Monte and can often be finished faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in El Monte

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows behind. This typically needs only an entry pit and an exit pit instead of a long trench. It works well under driveways, mature trees and landscaping. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no joints for roots to enter.
