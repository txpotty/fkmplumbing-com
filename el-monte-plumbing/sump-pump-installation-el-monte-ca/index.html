    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, hardpan or rock and set a durable basin.     Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in El Monte, CA

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to reach for maintenance and repair.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in El Monte, CA

    Severe storms can knock out power right when your pump is needed most. Battery backup pumps take over automatically when the main pump fails or power goes out.     Signs you might need a new sump pump include unusual noises during operation, frequent cycling, visible rust or corrosion, or a pump that runs continuously without much water.     Strange noises, constant running, failure to turn on and visible rust are common signs. Testing your pump before the rainy season helps catch problems early.

    A submersible sump pump sits inside the sump pit, making it quieter and more powerful, ideal for heavy use. A pedestal sump pump has its motor above the pit, making it easier to reach for maintenance and often more affordable, though it can be louder.

    To keep your new sump pump running well, test it monthly by pouring water into the pit to confirm it activates.
