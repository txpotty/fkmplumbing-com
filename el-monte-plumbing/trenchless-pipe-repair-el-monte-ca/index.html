    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in El Monte, CA

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing severely damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is well suited for completely replacing a pipe segment with a new, stronger material, such as HDPE. It's often chosen when the existing pipe is collapsed or too damaged for lining. FKM Plumbing uses specialized bursting heads and winches for a smooth and efficient replacement, reducing the overall project time and cost for homeowners in El Monte, CA.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in El Monte

    CIPP lining is a popular trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is excellent for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in El Monte.

    Sectional Point Repair in El Monte

    When damage is confined to a specific section of pipe, sectional point repair is an ideal trenchless solution. We target only the damaged area, installing a patch or liner to seal the leak or reinforce the pipe. This method is cost-effective and minimizes the scope of work, which suits isolated issues in El Monte without affecting the entire line.
