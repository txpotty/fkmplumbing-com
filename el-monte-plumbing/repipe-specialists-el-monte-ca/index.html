    Copper piping offers exceptional durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in El Monte. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Our team carefully solders each joint, creating a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that can withstand the test of time, including changing temperatures and local water conditions. Copper also naturally inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in El Monte, CA

    Polybutylene pipes, installed in many homes between the 1970s and 1990s, are known for premature failure due to chemical reactions with disinfectants in the water supply.     Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in El Monte

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Expansive clay and hillside soils can shift over time, putting stress on slab plumbing and cracking older sewer joints. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Copper Repiping in El Monte, CA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.
