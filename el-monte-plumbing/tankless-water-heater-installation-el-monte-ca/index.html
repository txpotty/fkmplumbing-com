    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in El Monte, CA

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year.
