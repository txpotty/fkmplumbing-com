    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in El Monte

    Hard water can cause scale buildup in tankless heat exchangers and heating elements. Scale reduces efficiency and can trigger error codes. Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California install softeners or scale filters. We recommend a water softener or scale inhibitor to protect your unit, and we install service valves for periodic descaling. Regular maintenance helps extend the life of your tankless heater.
