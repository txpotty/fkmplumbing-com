    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Repair in Menifee

    Most disposal repairs, like clearing jams, fixing flange leaks or replacing a switch, cost $125 to $300. If replacement is needed, a new installed unit typically runs $275 to $700.

    To prevent future problems, avoid putting grease, coffee grounds, eggshells, fibrous vegetables, or large bones down the disposal. Always run cold water before, during, and after use for at least 30 seconds. Regularly grind citrus peels to help clean and deodorize the unit.

    A humming disposal usually has a jammed flywheel. Turn off the power and do not put your hand inside. We can free the jam safely.

    Frequent jams are usually caused by putting fibrous foods like celery, potato peels, or corn husks down the drain, or by disposing of non-food items. Hard items like bones or fruit pits can also lead to jams. Using plenty of cold water while grinding helps prevent buildup.

    A leak from the bottom usually means the internal seal has failed. This usually cannot be repaired, and the unit needs replacing.
