    Root Intrusion Repair in Menifee, CA

    Tree and shrub roots go after moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause repeat backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every few months.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Menifee

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is highly resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Menifee homes. This method is ideal when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Menifee

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their shape.

    Sectional Point Repair in Menifee

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.

    Sewer Line Video Inspection in Menifee

    Before any trenchless repair, we conduct a thorough video inspection of your sewer line. This involves inserting a high-resolution camera into the pipe to accurately diagnose the problem's exact location and nature. This key step makes sure we choose the most appropriate and effective trenchless method for your Menifee property, avoiding guesswork.
