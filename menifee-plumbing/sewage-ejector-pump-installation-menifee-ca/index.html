    While both sump pumps and sewage ejector pumps remove water, their applications are distinct. FKM Plumbing provides expert guidance and installation for both in Menifee. A sewage ejector pump, however, is designed for wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in below-grade plumbing.
