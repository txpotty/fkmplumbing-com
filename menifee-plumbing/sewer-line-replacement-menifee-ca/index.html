    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Menifee, CA

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the proper grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we account for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we cover it.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Menifee, CA

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement swaps out pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward. This is ideal when protecting landscaping or driveways is a priority. The method works well for many homes in Menifee and can often be completed faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.

    Partial Sewer Line Repair in Menifee, CA

We only recommend it when the rest of the line is in good condition.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Line Replacement in Menifee

    Sewer line replacement in Menifee commonly ranges from about $4,000 to $15,000 for a typical residential lateral, and can run higher for long, deep or hard-to-reach lines. Trenchless work is often priced around $80 to $250 per foot depending on conditions.

    Most policies do not cover wear and tear or root damage to sewer lines. Some offer service line coverage as an add-on, so check your policy.
