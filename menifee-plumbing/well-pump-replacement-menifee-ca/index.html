    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Menifee

    Well pump replacement usually costs $1,800 to $4,500 depending on well depth and pump size. Deep wells or constant pressure systems can cost $4,000 to $8,000.

    The lifespan of a well pump varies widely depending on usage, water quality and pump type, but most last between 10 and 20 years. Regular maintenance and proper sizing can extend its life, but eventually wear and tear will require replacement.

    Most well pumps last 8 to 15 years. Sediment, hard water and frequent cycling can shorten lifespan.

    Low pressure, sputtering faucets, no water and high electric bills can signal pump trouble. Frequent cycling is another warning sign.

    Most well pump replacements are finished in one day. Deep wells or complex systems may take longer.

    It is often a good idea to consider replacing your pressure tank at the same time as your well pump, especially if both are of similar age. A new pressure tank can improve the efficiency and lifespan of your new pump by reducing cycling. It also saves on labor costs to do both jobs at once.

    In many areas, a plumbing or well permit is required for well pump replacement to confirm compliance with local building and health codes. FKM Plumbing handles all necessary permitting on your behalf, so your installation is fully compliant and safe.

    FKM Plumbing installs and services a wide range of reputable well pump brands known for their durability and performance. These commonly include Grundfos, Franklin Electric, Goulds Pumps and Sta-Rite. We can recommend the best brand and model to suit your well's requirements.
