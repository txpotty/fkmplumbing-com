    Dependable Trenchless Pipe Repair Services in Menifee, CA

    Pipe Bursting in Menifee, CA

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, fracturing it outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind. The new pipe is fused into one continuous length with no joints. Bursting needs an entry and exit pit but avoids digging the full length.

    Commercial Trenchless Pipe Repair in Menifee

    Commercial properties often have sewer and storm lines under parking lots, sidewalks and buildings. Trenchless repair avoids costly concrete demolition and disruption to tenants and customers. We line larger diameter pipes and multiple laterals. We schedule work to minimize impact on business operations.

    Lateral and Spot Lining in Menifee

    Not every pipe needs to be lined end to end. When damage is limited to a few joints or a short section, we can install a short sectional liner. This costs less than a full-length liner and is completed quickly. We verify the rest of the line is sound before recommending a spot liner.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Menifee

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light. Once hardened, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Repair Cost in Menifee, CA

    Trenchless repair can cost more per foot of pipe than open trenching. However, once you add the cost of restoring driveways, patios, landscaping and irrigation, trenchless is often the better value. It is also faster and less disruptive. We compare both methods with real numbers for your home.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Menifee, CA

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing severely damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time.
