    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Menifee

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading correctly and the burner breathing properly.

    Maintenance Cost Versus Repair Cost in Menifee, CA

    A tankless descaling visit typically costs far less than replacing a scaled heat exchanger, which can run $600 to $1,500 or more in parts and labor. Some manufacturers deny exchanger claims when scale damage is found. Regular maintenance helps protect a unit that can last 20 years when cared for. We help you decide whether a softener or scale inhibitor would reduce your long-term costs too.

    Navien Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Menifee

    Navien tankless water heaters often feature condensing technology, making them highly efficient but also requiring particular maintenance. Our tune-ups include checking the condensate trap for blockages, cleaning the secondary heat exchanger and verifying the operation of the circulator pump. We also confirm a proper air-fuel mixture and calibrate sensors for peak efficiency in Menifee homes.

    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Menifee

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is protected where it runs outdoors.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Tune-Ups in Menifee, CA

    Noritz tankless water heaters are sturdy systems, but they still require annual tune-ups to maintain their performance through every season. Our maintenance includes checking the unit's exhaust system, confirming proper combustion air intake and verifying the integrity of the heat exchanger. We also calibrate the temperature and flow settings for maximum efficiency and comfort, preventing common issues like cold water sandwiches.
