    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Tustin, CA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies usually form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Expansive clay and hillside soils can shift over time, putting stress on slab plumbing and cracking older sewer joints. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Tustin, CA

    A single spot repair is the most affordable fix when the rest of the line is sound. Pipe lining costs more per foot but avoids excavation and works well for long runs under driveways or landscaping. Full replacement is the right call when the pipe is Orangeburg, badly bellied or broken in several places.     Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Tustin
