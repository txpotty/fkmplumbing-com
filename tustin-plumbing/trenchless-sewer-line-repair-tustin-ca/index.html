    Advanced Trenchless Sewer Line Repair Services in Tustin, CA

    Sectional Point Repair in Tustin

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are a good fit on otherwise healthy lines.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Tustin, CA

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is particularly useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add many years of service.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Solutions in Tustin

    We use proven trenchless products like those from Perma-Liner, a leader in CIPP technology. Their materials and systems deliver a durable, long-lasting repair that stands up to the demands of local soil and climate conditions. Using trusted brands like Perma-Liner supports the integrity and longevity of our work.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Tustin

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for long trenching. It is ideal for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Tustin that require full replacement rather than just relining.
