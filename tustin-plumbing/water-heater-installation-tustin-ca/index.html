    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Tustin

    The cost of water heater installation in Tustin, CA can range from $1,200 to $4,000 or more, depending on the type of unit (traditional tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed plumbing or electrical upgrades.     In most areas, a plumbing permit is required for water heater replacement or installation to confirm the work meets local building and safety codes. Plumbing contractors here must hold a state contractor license, and permits are usually required for water heaters, repiping, and sewer work. FKM Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and inspections in Tustin as part of our service.

    Most standard water heater installations, especially for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours. We will provide an estimated timeline during our first assessment.

    An expansion tank absorbs excess water pressure created as water heats up inside the tank. Many homes in Tustin, CA, particularly those with closed-loop plumbing systems, are required by code to have an expansion tank to protect the water heater and plumbing system from damage.

    Common signs that you might need a new water heater include a lack of hot water, rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit and an age of 10 to 15 years or more.     The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand.
