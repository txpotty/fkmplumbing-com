    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Tustin

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections.
