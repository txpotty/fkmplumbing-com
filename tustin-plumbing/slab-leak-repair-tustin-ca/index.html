    Professional Slab Leak Repair Services in Tustin, CA

    Slab Leak Detection in Tustin

    We start by shutting off fixtures and watching the meter to confirm a leak. Then we isolate the hot and cold lines to find out which one is leaking. Using acoustic ground microphones, we listen for the sound of escaping water through the concrete. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor surface.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Tustin, CA

    When the leak is isolated and the rest of the pipe is in good shape, a spot repair through the slab is a sensible option. We cut a small opening, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The trench is compacted and the concrete is patched. Flooring is then restored by your preferred contractor.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Tustin

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method skips the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Tustin with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Slab Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Tustin, CA

    Spot repairs usually have a lower upfront cost but involve breaking concrete and repairing flooring. Reroutes cost somewhat more but avoid concrete work and future leaks in that line. Whole-house repipes are the best choice when you have had multiple slab leaks or old copper throughout. We give you written prices for each option.

    Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Tustin, CA

    A large slab leak can push water up through flooring or into walls quickly. We help you shut off the water and send a technician to isolate the leaking line. Often we can restore water to the rest of the house while we plan the repair. Prompt action limits flooring damage and soil saturation.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Tustin

    Cold water slab leaks are harder to feel but still waste water and wet the soil. They often show up as a high water bill or a meter that moves with all fixtures off. We isolate the cold line, locate the leak and repair or reroute it. Fixing cold leaks quickly helps protect your foundation.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Tustin

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is simplest. Reroutes eliminate future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Tustin, CA

    When a slab leak is localized and accessible, direct repair is often the most practical solution. This involves carefully jackhammering a small section of your foundation to expose the damaged pipe, then repairing or replacing the damaged section. Our technicians are skilled in precise concrete cutting and restoration, so the repair is effective and the area is properly restored. We use durable materials designed to hold up to soil movement under the slab.
