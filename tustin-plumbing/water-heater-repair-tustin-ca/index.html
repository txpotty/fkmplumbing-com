    Most homes still rely on traditional tank water heaters. We regularly repair issues like faulty thermostats, sediment buildup, anode rod corrosion, and pressure relief valve malfunctions.     Anode Rod Replacement in Tustin, CA

    The anode rod sacrifices itself so the steel tank does not rust. In hard water it can be eaten away within a few years. We pull and inspect the rod, then install a magnesium, aluminum or powered anode depending on your water and any odor problems. A rotten egg smell from hot water often goes away with the right anode choice.

    Sediment Flush and Noise Repair in Tustin

    Popping, rumbling or crackling sounds often mean a thick layer of sediment is trapping water under it and overheating the tank bottom. We drain and flush the tank, break up scale with repeated fills and check the drain valve for blockage. Flushing restores capacity, lowers energy use and helps extend tank life. We usually recommend yearly flushes for homes on hard water.
