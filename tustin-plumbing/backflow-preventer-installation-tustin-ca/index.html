    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Tustin

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device.     Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Tustin, CA

    For high-hazard applications in Tustin, CA where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is usually required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. FKM Plumbing specializes in RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes. Proper installation is essential for effective operation.

    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in Tustin, CA

    Irrigation systems in Tustin, CA are a frequent source of backflow, as stagnant water and lawn chemicals can be drawn back into the potable water supply. Installing a dedicated backflow preventer for your sprinkler system is often a local code requirement.     Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Tustin

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a widely used backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Tustin, DCVAs are often installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers carefully install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.
