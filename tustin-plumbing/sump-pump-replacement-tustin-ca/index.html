    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in Tustin, CA

    Sometimes it is not just the pump that needs replacing, since the sump basin or pit itself may be cracked, damaged or too small. FKM Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in Tustin, CA so your entire system functions as it should. A properly sized and intact pit is essential for the efficient operation and longevity of your new sump pump.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Tustin

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Tustin for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, properly sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and maximize efficiency. That is key for handling heavy rainfall.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Tustin

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation prevents sewer gas odors.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Tustin

    Power outages during severe storms can leave a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump offers crucial protection during these times. We install and replace complete battery backup systems, including dedicated marine-grade batteries and smart charging units. Our team helps you select a system with sufficient run-time to protect your Tustin home through extended power interruptions. You get peace of mind when the lights go out.

    Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Tustin

    Commercial buildings often have elevator pits, parking garage drains and mechanical room sumps. These require pumps rated for higher flow and sometimes oil-sensing controls. We replace commercial pumps and alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. Duplex systems with alternating pumps provide redundancy. We schedule work to avoid interrupting operations.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Tustin

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Tustin, CA

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more long-lasting. We provide professional pedestal sump pump replacement in Tustin, CA, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a major concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Tustin, CA

    For added protection, many homeowners in Tustin choose a combination sump pump system, which pairs a primary electric pump with a battery backup pump in one unit. This keeps the system running even if the power fails or the primary pump quits. FKM Plumbing can replace your existing combination system or install a new one, evaluating your needs for groundwater management. We make sure the components are properly matched for strong performance and longevity.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Tustin

    The cost of a sump pump replacement in Tustin, CA can range from $500 to $1,500, depending on the type of pump, its horsepower and the complexity of the installation. Factors like needing a new sump pit, major re-piping or adding a battery backup system can affect the final price. We provide transparent, upfront quotes after assessing your needs.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 3 to 5 years. We also offer a labor warranty.

    In many areas, minor like-for-like sump pump replacements usually do not require a permit. However, if the replacement involves major changes to the electrical system, discharge piping or the sump pit itself, a permit might be necessary. Our team will advise you on local code requirements in Tustin.

    It is recommended to test your sump pump at least every three months, and especially before and after rainy seasons. To test, simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit. The pump should activate, drain the water and then shut off automatically. If it does not, it is time for a professional inspection.

    To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by FKM Plumbing can also help extend its lifespan.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity. A backup sump pump, usually battery-powered or water-powered, is a secondary system that kicks on if the primary pump fails, loses power or cannot keep up with heavy water inflow. Many Tustin homes benefit from a backup system due to storm risks.

    Most standard sump pump replacements can be completed within 2 to 4 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new pit excavation or extensive discharge line work, might take longer, up to a full day.

    While it might seem like a simple task, sump pump replacement involves electrical connections, proper plumbing and making sure the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding. DIY replacement can lead to improper operation, electrical hazards or system failure. It is best to hire a professional plumber for a safe and effective installation.
