    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Jetting in Tustin

    The cost of drain jetting in Tustin, CA can vary a good deal, typically ranging from $250 to $800, depending on the severity and location of the clog, the pipe size, and the length of the line. Complex jobs, like extensive root removal or main sewer line blockages, may be on the higher end. We provide a detailed estimate after an on-site assessment.

    We do not recommend using a home pressure washer for drain jetting. These machines lack the power, specialized nozzles, and safety features of professional equipment. Improper use can damage your pipes, cause serious injury, or push clogs deeper. Always rely on trained professionals for drain jetting.

    Drain snaking typically involves pushing an auger through a clog to create a small opening for water to pass. Drain jetting, or hydro jetting, uses high-pressure water to scour the entire pipe wall, removing buildup and leaving the pipe much cleaner. Jetting is better for stubborn blockages and preventative maintenance.

    Yes, it is generally best that you or an authorized adult be present during the drain jetting service. This lets our technicians access the necessary areas, discuss findings, and answer any questions you may have about the process or next steps.

    A drain jetting service in Tustin, CA typically takes 1 to 3 hours, depending on the severity and accessibility of the blockage and the length of the pipe being cleaned. More complex situations, like main sewer line issues, might take longer.

    Yes, drain jetting is very effective at removing tree roots that have gotten into sewer lines. We use root-cutting nozzles that break up the roots with high-pressure water, flushing them out of the system. This provides a more complete and lasting solution than snaking for root intrusions.

    Generally, a permit is not required for routine drain jetting because it is maintenance within existing plumbing. However, if the jetting reveals serious pipe damage requiring excavation and repair or replacement, a plumbing permit from Tustin may be necessary. Our team will let you know if that happens.
