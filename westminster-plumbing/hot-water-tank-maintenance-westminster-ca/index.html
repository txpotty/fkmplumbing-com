    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Westminster, CA

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial protector against corrosion. Municipal water across much of Southern California is hard, leaving white scale on faucets, showerheads, and inside water heater tanks. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's significantly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Westminster, CA, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.

    Sediment Flushing & Cleaning in Westminster, CA

    Over time, minerals and sediment settle at the bottom of hot water tanks, reducing efficiency and possibly leading to premature failure. Our maintenance includes a thorough flushing of the tank to remove these deposits. This process helps your tank heat water more evenly and quietly, a vital step for any hot water tank in Westminster, CA, where mineral content in the water is a common concern.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Westminster, CA

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can block the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in Westminster

    Sediment buildup is a frequent issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high. Our standard flushing service removes these mineral deposits, which can reduce efficiency and lead to premature tank failure. We drain the tank completely, flush out all sediment and refill it, ensuring clear water flow and improved heating performance. This routine service is crucial for extending the life of your unit.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Costs in Westminster, CA

    A annual flush costs a small fraction of a new tank, and a new installed tank often runs $1,400 to $3,000 or more. We give you an straight read on whether maintenance is still worth it. If a tank is past 10 to 12 years old, shows rust at the base or leaks from the seams, we will tell you replacement is the smarter spend. When the tank still has life, maintenance keeps it running well for years.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Westminster

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure jump every heating cycle. High street pressure is common in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.
