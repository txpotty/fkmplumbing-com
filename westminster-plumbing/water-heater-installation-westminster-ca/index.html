    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Westminster

    Gas tank water heaters provide strong recovery and are common in many local homes. We install 40, 50 and 75 gallon models with proper venting and combustion air.     Water Heater Installation Cost in Westminster, CA

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Westminster

    Expansion Tank Installation in Westminster, CA

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and plumbing.
