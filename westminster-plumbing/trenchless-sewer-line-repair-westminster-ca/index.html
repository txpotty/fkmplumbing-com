    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Westminster

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Westminster homes. This method is ideal when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Trenchless Technologies in Westminster

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Westminster

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We identify the material on camera before recommending a method.
