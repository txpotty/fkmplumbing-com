    The cost of flood restoration in Westminster, CA can vary a great deal depending on the extent of the damage, the size of the affected area, and the type of water, whether clean, gray, or black water.     Signs of hidden moisture after a flood include musty odors, peeling paint or wallpaper, warped flooring, discolored drywall, and visible mold growth in unexpected places. Our technicians use moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras to detect moisture that isn't visible to the naked eye.

    Mold prevention is a key part of our process. We achieve this by rapid water extraction, thorough drying to reduce humidity and moisture levels, and applying EPA-approved antimicrobial treatments to all affected porous materials. Controlling moisture within the first 24 to 48 hours is crucial for inhibiting mold spores.

    Most standard homeowner's insurance policies do not cover flood damage, which typically requires a separate flood insurance policy.     Most homeowner's insurance policies cover water damage from sudden and accidental events, like a burst pipe or water heater leak. However, damage from natural floods typically requires a separate flood insurance policy. We can help you navigate the claims process and provide documentation for your insurer.
