    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Westminster, CA

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested. We carry proven brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Commercial Backflow Preventer Installation in Westminster

    Restaurants, salons, dental offices and light industrial buildings often need multiple backflow devices on soda machines, boilers, dishwashers and process water. We survey the building, identify each cross-connection and install containment and isolation assemblies as required. Work is scheduled after hours or on weekends when needed to avoid shutting down your business. You receive documentation for each device for your records and your water utility.

    Pool and Spa Fill Backflow Protection in Westminster, CA

    An auto-fill line or hose connection on a pool or spa creates a straight path for chlorinated water to reach the supply.     Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Westminster, CA

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Westminster, CA, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Westminster

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a common backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Westminster, DCVAs are often installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers precisely install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

Common locations include irrigation system connections, fire sprinkler lines, boiler systems and connections to auxiliary water supplies, usually just after the main water meter or at the point of hazard.

    In Westminster, most backflow preventers must be tested once a year by a certified backflow tester to confirm they are working correctly and providing adequate protection. This is a separate service from installation but is essential for ongoing compliance and safety.
