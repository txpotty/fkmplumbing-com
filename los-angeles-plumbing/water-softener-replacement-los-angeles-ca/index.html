    Traditional Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Los Angeles

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and effective solution for hard water in Los Angeles. These systems use ion exchange to remove calcium and magnesium minerals, which are well known for causing scale buildup. We replace old, inefficient salt-based units with modern, high-efficiency models that use less salt and water. Our plumbers confirm proper brine tank installation and regeneration settings for strong performance.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Los Angeles

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore consistent brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the best salt type for your system.

    Water Softener Failure Diagnosis in Los Angeles, CA

    Before replacing, we check whether your softener has a simple fix like a clogged injector, failed motor or wrong settings. If the valve is obsolete or parts are no longer available, replacement is usually the better value. We test hardness before and after the softener to confirm the problem. We explain the cost of repair versus replacement clearly. Call (213) 871-0701 to schedule a check.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Los Angeles

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly exhausted. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Los Angeles

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water all day and night. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but avoid hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Los Angeles

    The cost of water softener replacement in Los Angeles, CA can vary widely, typically ranging from $1,500 to $4,000. This depends on the type and size of the new system, labor costs and any necessary plumbing changes. We provide clear, upfront pricing after assessing your needs.

    With proper maintenance, most quality water softeners can last between 10 and 15 years, and sometimes even longer. Factors like water hardness, usage volume and the quality of the original installation can influence its lifespan. Regular servicing helps extend the life of your unit.

    Most softener replacements take two to four hours. New loops or drain lines can take a bit longer.

    Requirements vary by city. Simple replacements may not require one, but new plumbing connections may. We check local rules for you.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is usually not required for a direct like-for-like water softener replacement if no significant changes to the existing plumbing are made.
