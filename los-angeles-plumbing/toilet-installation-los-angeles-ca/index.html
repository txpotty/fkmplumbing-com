    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilet Installation in Los Angeles

    Comfort-height toilets put the seat at about 17 to 19 inches, which is gentler on knees and backs. For accessible bathrooms, we follow ADA guidelines for seat height, centerline distance from the side wall and grab bar clearance. We can install elongated bowls and add blocking for grab bars while the wall is open. These upgrades are common with homeowners planning to age in place.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Los Angeles, CA

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, with the tank hidden behind an access plate. They free up floor space and make cleaning easy, but they need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep or a furred-out chase. We install Geberit, Grohe and Kohler carrier systems and set the bowl height to your preference. This work is easiest during a remodel when the wall is open.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in Los Angeles, CA

    Installing a toilet in a new bathroom or during a complete renovation often involves 'rough-in' work, which includes setting the waste pipe and water supply lines. Our plumbers are experts in establishing the correct rough-in dimensions for various toilet types, ensuring future compatibility and proper function.
