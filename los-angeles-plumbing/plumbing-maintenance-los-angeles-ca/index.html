    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Los Angeles

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get replaced. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility first.

    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Los Angeles, CA

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits significantly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to premature tank failure.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Los Angeles

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers require annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Sewer Line and Drain Camera Inspection in Los Angeles, CA

    A camera inspection shows the condition of your sewer line and main drains. We look for roots, bellies, cracks and buildup before they cause a backup. Older homes with clay or cast iron lines benefit most from regular checks. We record the inspection and give you a copy. If cleaning or repair is needed, we explain your choices.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Los Angeles

    Regular water heater maintenance is key to preventing sediment buildup. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements.     Drain Line Inspections for Maintenance in Los Angeles

    As part of comprehensive plumbing maintenance, we offer drain line camera inspections. This lets us see the condition of your main sewer lines, identifying potential blockages from roots or grease buildup, or structural issues like cracks and offsets made worse by shifting soil. Earthquakes are a constant consideration in Southern California, and seismic straps and flexible connectors help keep water and gas lines intact during shaking.     Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Los Angeles

    Postwar tract homes are common in Southern California, and many still have original galvanized or copper supply lines and cast iron drains under the slab. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks.
