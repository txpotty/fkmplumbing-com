    Slab Leak Detection in Los Angeles

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often signaled by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. FKM Plumbing uses dedicated acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is essential to preventing major foundation damage.

    Advanced Leak Detection Technology in Los Angeles

    FKM Plumbing invests in modern leak detection technology to provide the most accurate and non-invasive service possible in Los Angeles. This includes high-resolution thermal cameras for detecting temperature changes caused by hidden moisture, sensitive acoustic sensors that listen for the subtle sounds of escaping water, and video pipe inspection cameras for internal pipe diagnostics. These tools allow us to find leaks with little disruption to your property.

    Pool Water Leak Detection in Los Angeles, CA

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss. Then we pressure test pool plumbing and dye test fittings, lights and skimmers. Finding the leak protects the pool and the soil around it.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Los Angeles

    Leaks in underground water lines, from your meter to your home, can go unnoticed for long periods, wasting water and causing yard erosion. Signs include oddly green patches in your lawn, persistent wet spots or a constantly running water meter. FKM Plumbing uses ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection methods to accurately map out and pinpoint leaks in buried pipes, allowing for targeted excavation and repair rather than digging up your entire yard.

    Toilet and Fixture Leak Detection in Los Angeles, CA

    Toilets are one of the most common sources of wasted water. A worn flapper or a fill valve that never shuts off can leak silently around the clock. We use dye tests to find leaking toilets and check faucets, showers and supply lines for drips. These fixes are typically quick and save water right away.

    Main Service Line Leak Detection in Los Angeles, CA

    The line from the meter to your house carries full pressure and can leak underground for a long time. Wet soil, soft spots or unusually green patches of yard may be the only clues. We trace the line, listen for the leak and mark it for a targeted repair. Replacing old galvanized or polybutylene service lines is usually recommended.
