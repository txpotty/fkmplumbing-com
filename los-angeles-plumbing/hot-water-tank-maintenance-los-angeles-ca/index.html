    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Los Angeles, CA

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial protector against corrosion. Around Los Angeles, flushing the water heater yearly and cleaning aerators keeps mineral buildup from cutting flow and efficiency. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's badly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Los Angeles, CA, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Los Angeles, CA

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a stable blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Emergency Hot Water Tank Inspections in Los Angeles

    If you notice sudden changes in your hot water tank's performance, like strange noises, discolored water or a sudden drop in hot water supply, FKM Plumbing offers emergency inspections in Los Angeles.     Sediment Flushing & Cleaning in Los Angeles, CA

    Over time, minerals and sediment settle at the bottom of hot water tanks, reducing efficiency and possibly leading to premature failure. Our maintenance includes a thorough flushing of the tank to remove these deposits. This process helps your tank heat water more evenly and quietly, a vital step for any hot water tank in Los Angeles, CA, where mineral content in the water is a frequent concern.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Los Angeles, CA

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing widely. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank. A leak in an attic tank without a working pan can ruin a ceiling, so we confirm that protection is in place.

    Electric Hot Water Tank Element Check in Los Angeles

    Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout. We also check the thermostat settings and wiring connections to confirm they are functioning correctly and safely. This preventive check can identify failing elements before they stop heating altogether, avoiding unexpected cold showers for your family in Los Angeles.
