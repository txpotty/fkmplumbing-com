    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Los Angeles, CA

    Many older homes in Los Angeles, CA still have galvanized steel water lines, which are prone to corrosion, rust, and reduced water flow over time. Our plumbers are comfortable working with these older systems, carefully assessing the pipe's condition and recommending the most effective repair strategy to extend its life or plan for eventual replacement.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Los Angeles

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a especially challenging issue. Repiping with PEX or copper is a popular upgrade across Southern California, often done to fix low pressure and recurring slab leaks. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to serious structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to pinpoint slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak quickly in Los Angeles is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Los Angeles, CA

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Around Los Angeles, flushing the water heater yearly and cleaning aerators keeps scale buildup from cutting flow and efficiency. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe rarely lasts because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and ends repeat leaks.

    Main Water Line Repair in Los Angeles

    The main water line brings water from the public supply to your home. When this line leaks or breaks, it can cause significant water loss, foundation damage, and low water pressure. Our team specializes in diagnosing and repairing main water line issues, from pinpoint leaks to full pipe ruptures, so your home's primary water source is secure. We use advanced detection methods to pinpoint the exact location of the problem, limiting excavation and disruption to your property in Los Angeles. We also check for common issues like root intrusion or shifting soil.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Los Angeles

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off quickly in an emergency.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Los Angeles, CA

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can work loose those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is responsible for the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve in the same visit.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Los Angeles

    For some water line repairs, traditional trenching can be avoided. Trenchless repair methods, like pipe bursting or pipe lining, allow us to fix or replace underground pipes with little disruption to your landscaping, driveway, or foundation. This method is often faster and less intrusive than conventional digging, making it a popular choice for homeowners in Los Angeles looking to protect their property. We'll check whether your water line issue is a good candidate for trenchless technology.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Repair in Los Angeles

    A spot repair on an accessible service line typically runs $350 to $1,500 depending on depth and access. Full replacement from meter to house usually costs $1,800 to $5,500, and trenchless replacement often falls between $3,000 and $7,000. We give you a written price before we start.

    Common signs include a wet or unusually green patch of yard, a hissing sound near the meter, low pressure at every fixture and a water bill that climbs without explanation. Dirty or rusty water can also signal a damaged line. If your meter moves with all fixtures off, you probably have a leak.

    Common signs of a broken water line include surprisingly high water bills, a sudden drop in water pressure, wet spots in your yard or on pavement that don't dry, discolored water, or the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use. For homes on slab foundations, watch for cracks in the foundation or warm spots on the floor.

    Trenchless water line repair is a method of repairing or replacing underground pipes without extensive digging. It involves creating small access points and using specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one (pipe bursting) or insert a new lining into the existing pipe (pipe lining). This method protects landscaping and reduces repair time.

    A homeowner can sometimes tighten a leaking fitting at an exposed hose bib. Buried service line repairs require locating, safe excavation, 811 utility marking and code-approved fittings. Mistakes can cause bigger leaks, so most homeowners hire a licensed plumber.

    We typically install Type K copper, PEX rated for direct burial or HDPE for trenchless pulls. Each holds up well in most soils when properly bedded. We will recommend the right material based on your soil, line length and budget.

    Most spot repairs are finished in two to four hours. A full replacement typically takes one day, and trenchless jobs are often done in a day as well. Water is typically back on the same day.

    In most communities the water provider owns the meter and the pipe on the street side of it. The homeowner is responsible for the service line from the meter to the house. We confirm which side the leak is on before quoting you.
