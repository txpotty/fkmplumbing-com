    Partial Sewer Line Repair in Los Angeles, CA

    Not every damaged sewer needs full replacement. If the camera shows a single break or one root-damaged joint, a spot repair may fix the problem.     Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Los Angeles

    Cast iron drains in older homes corrode from the inside, especially along the bottom of the pipe. Over time this leaves cracks, holes and a rough surface that catches waste. Replacing the cast iron sewer outside the home is often the first step. We can also evaluate under-slab cast iron and discuss lining or rerouting options. New PVC or HDPE will not rust or scale the same way.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair and Replacement in Los Angeles, CA

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive option to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining inserts a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe. Both methods significantly reduce the need for excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Los Angeles, CA while providing a long-lasting solution.

    Permitting and Code Compliance in Los Angeles, CA

    Sewer line replacement projects in Los Angeles, CA often require permits and must meet local plumbing codes. FKM Plumbing handles the permitting process and makes sure every part of the replacement meets or exceeds municipal standards. Our knowledge of local regulations means your new sewer line is installed correctly and safely. That helps you avoid potential fines or future issues.

    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in Los Angeles

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is pulled in and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a smart option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you honestly if your line is a candidate.
