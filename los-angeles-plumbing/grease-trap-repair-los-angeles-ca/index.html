    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Los Angeles, CA

    Tees direct flow into and out of the trap at the correct depth. Missing or broken tees allow floating grease to escape into the sewer line. We replace damaged tees with durable PVC or other approved materials. We also check that they are set at the right depth for your unit. This is one of the most common and necessary grease trap repairs.

    Grease Trap Odor Control & Repair in Los Angeles

    Persistent foul odors coming from your grease trap are a sign of inefficiency, excessive FOG accumulation, or bacterial growth. Beyond cleaning, our repair services address the root cause, whether it's poor ventilation, sludge buildup, or structural issues preventing proper flow. We make sure all components are working correctly to contain and process waste effectively, eliminating unpleasant smells that can deter customers in Los Angeles.

    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Los Angeles, CA

    Simple repairs like gasket or tee replacements usually cost far less than a new trap. Major damage such as a cracked tank body, failing concrete or a badly corroded steel unit may make replacement the better value. We compare repair cost, remaining life and compliance risk for you. Older steel traps often rust through and are good candidates for upgrade. We give you options and pricing in writing so you can decide.

    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Los Angeles

    Grease traps often clog due to excessive accumulation of fats, oils, and grease (FOGs) or food solids. Our technicians use high-pressure hydro-jetting to break down stubborn blockages and thoroughly clean the internal baffles and lines. We also inspect for underlying issues like damaged pipes or improper disposal practices that contribute to repeat clogs, offering solutions to prevent future blockages. Identifying the precise location and nature of the clog is key for effective repair and long-term performance in your Los Angeles establishment.

    Residential Grease Trap Repair in Los Angeles, CA

    Even residential properties in Los Angeles, CA can benefit from a grease trap, especially those with septic systems or older plumbing. If your home's under-sink grease trap is backing up or giving off foul odors, it indicates a need for repair.     Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Los Angeles, CA

    Damaged lids and failed gaskets let odors escape and create safety hazards. Outdoor risers can crack from vehicle traffic or ground movement. We replace lids, install new gaskets and repair or replace risers. Traffic-rated lids are installed where trucks or cars drive over the interceptor. These repairs keep odors in and keep people safe.

    Flow Control Device Repair in Los Angeles

    Hydromechanical traps rely on a flow control fitting to keep water moving at the rated speed. If this fitting is missing or clogged, the trap can be overwhelmed and grease escapes. We inspect, clean or replace flow control devices during repair visits. We also check the air intake that allows the device to work properly. Correct flow control is necessary for a trap to pass inspection.
