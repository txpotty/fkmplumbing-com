    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Los Angeles

    The cost for garbage disposal replacement in Los Angeles, CA usually ranges from $200 to $600. This includes the new unit itself, which can vary from $100 for a basic 1/3 HP model to over $300 for a high-end 1 HP unit, plus labor costs. Factors like the disposal's horsepower, brand, and any necessary plumbing modifications can affect the final price.

    Key signs you need a new garbage disposal include persistent leaks from the unit's housing, a humming noise without grinding, frequent clogs despite proper usage, foul odors that won't go away, or if the unit simply won't turn on. These symptoms usually indicate internal motor or component failure.

    New garbage disposals usually come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years, depending on the model and brand. FKM Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation services for your peace of mind.

    Most garbage disposal replacements can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. This timeframe includes disconnecting the old unit, installing the new one, and testing it carefully. More complex situations, such as significant plumbing modifications or electrical work, might extend the duration a bit.

    We install InSinkErator, Waste King, Moen and GE. We help you choose based on power, noise and warranty.

    If your current disposal jams often or struggles with food waste, a 3/4 or 1 HP model can make a real difference.

    If your disposal is old, frequently leaking, or making grinding noises without grinding food, replacement is usually more cost-effective and provides a longer-lasting solution. Minor issues like a simple jam can often be repaired.

    To prolong the life of your new disposal, avoid putting fibrous foods like celery, corn husks, or onion skins down it. Also, do not dispose of grease, coffee grounds, or non-food items. Always run cold water before, during, and after use, and grind small amounts of citrus peels or ice cubes now and then to clean the unit.
