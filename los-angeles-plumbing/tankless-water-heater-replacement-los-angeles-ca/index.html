    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Los Angeles, CA

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to guard the water and gas lines.     Condensing Tankless Replacement in Los Angeles

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by recovering heat from exhaust gases. They vent through low-cost PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Hot inland summers push water heaters and pool plumbing hard, while coastal salt air can corrode exposed metal fittings. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.

    A standard tankless water heater replacement, if you are upgrading from an existing tankless unit, usually takes 4 to 8 hours.     To protect the life and efficiency of your new tankless water heater, regular maintenance is crucial, especially in areas with hard water. This usually involves annual flushing to remove mineral buildup, checking the air filter, and inspecting gas and water connections. FKM Plumbing offers maintenance plans to help keep your unit in top condition.

    Most jurisdictions in Southern California require a permit for water heater replacement, especially when gas piping or venting changes. We take care of permits where they are required.
