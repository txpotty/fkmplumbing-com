    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Los Angeles

    When trenchless repair isn't feasible due to severe damage or specific pipe configurations, traditional dig-and-replace methods are necessary. This involves excavating a trench to access and replace the damaged section of the sewer line. While more intrusive, it is sometimes the only way to address badly collapsed pipes, significant offsets or certain pipe materials common in older Southern California properties. Our team ensures careful excavation and efficient replacement, minimizing the impact on your property in Los Angeles.

    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Los Angeles

    Sewer backups are often caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or extensive tree root intrusion. Our team uses advanced sewer camera inspections to identify the exact cause and location of the blockage. We then use hydro-jetting or mechanical snaking to clear the obstruction, restoring proper flow to your system and preventing further damage. This approach is highly effective for the stubborn clogs often found in older Southern California homes.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection and Repair in Los Angeles, CA

    Undetected sewer line leaks can lead to unpleasant odors, wet spots in your yard and even structural damage to your home's foundation. FKM Plumbing uses accurate leak detection methods to pinpoint the source of the leak, whether it's a minor crack or a major break. Once identified, we provide targeted repairs, from patching small leaks to replacing compromised sections of pipe, giving you a watertight system.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Los Angeles

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's much more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and preventing recurring clogs. This service is especially helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Los Angeles dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with recurring drain issues.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Los Angeles

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to repeat clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it will hold.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Los Angeles

    Every sewer repair starts with a look inside the pipe. We run a self-leveling camera from your cleanout or a pulled toilet and record the full length of the line. A sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact location and depth of every problem on the surface. You get a copy of the video and a plain-language summary of what we found. This step alone often saves homeowners from paying for repairs they do not need.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Los Angeles, CA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Expansive clay and hillside soils can shift over time, putting stress on slab plumbing and cracking older sewer joints. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Watch for multiple drains backing up at once, gurgling toilets, sewage smells in the yard and wet or unusually green patches over the line. Water backing up into a tub or shower when you run the washing machine is another common warning sign.

    A small rental auger can clear a soft clog, but it will not fix a crack, belly or root intrusion. Forcing a cable through a broken pipe can make the damage much worse. A camera inspection tells you whether you have a clog or a structural problem.
