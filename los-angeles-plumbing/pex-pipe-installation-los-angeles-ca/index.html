    Full Home PEX Repiping in Los Angeles

    If your home has old, corroded or leaking pipes, a full home PEX repipe can greatly improve your plumbing system. This prevents future leaks, improves water pressure and eliminates the rust-colored water often seen with older piping in Los Angeles.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Los Angeles

    FKM Plumbing frequently installs Uponor PEX systems, a leading brand known for high-quality, durable PEX-A piping. Uponor's ProPEX expansion fittings create strong, reliable and leak-resistant connections, ideal for the demands of a home's plumbing. We recommend Uponor for its long track record and longevity, giving Los Angeles homeowners peace of mind about their plumbing investment.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Los Angeles

    PEX is also a premier choice for hydronic heating systems, including radiant floor heating. Its ability to withstand high temperatures and resist corrosion makes it perfect for circulating hot water. If you are considering radiant heat for a specific area of your home or business in Los Angeles, PEX is the pipe to use.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Los Angeles

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a solid option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer reliable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to verify a proper seal.

    Commercial PEX Pipe Solutions in Los Angeles, CA

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Los Angeles

    Kitchen and bathroom remodels are a great time to replace old supply lines with PEX. Additions and guest suites get properly sized lines tied into your existing system. We coordinate with your contractor to keep the project schedule on track.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Los Angeles, CA

    FKM Plumbing is experienced with installing all types of PEX piping, including PEX-A, PEX-B and PEX-C, each with distinct properties for specific applications. We help you choose the best PEX type for your home's needs and budget in Los Angeles, CA. Our experience ensures the correct PEX material and fitting method, whether it is expansion for PEX-A or crimp and clamp for PEX-B and PEX-C, for a secure and lasting system.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Los Angeles, CA

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.
