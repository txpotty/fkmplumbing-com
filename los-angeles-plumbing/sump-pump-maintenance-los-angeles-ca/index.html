    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Maintenance in Los Angeles

    A standard sump pump inspection and cleaning typically runs $125 to $250. Replacing a float switch or check valve usually adds $75 to $250 depending on parts.

    Watch out for odd noises, such as grinding or rattling, that point to debris or a failing motor. If the pump runs constantly, fails to turn on or gives off a foul odor from the pit, it's clearly time for a professional check. Visible rust or corrosion are also red flags.

    Around Los Angeles, flushing the water heater yearly and cleaning aerators keeps scale buildup from cutting flow and efficiency. Where groundwater is hard, mineral deposits can build up on the pump's impeller and in the pit, reducing efficiency and potentially causing clogs or damage over time. Regular maintenance helps reduce these effects.

    The check valve is crucial because it prevents water that has been pumped out from flowing back into the sump pit after the pump shuts off. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short cycle, leading to premature wear and higher energy use. Our maintenance includes checking its function.

    If your primary sump pump fails during a power outage and you don't have a working battery backup or water-powered backup system, your basement is at risk of flooding. This shows the importance of maintaining backup systems.

    Most local codes do not allow sump discharge into the sanitary sewer. We route discharge to the yard, a dry well or another approved location.

    We recommend having your sump pump professionally maintained at least once a year. If your home is in an area prone to heavy rainfall or has a high water table, twice-yearly maintenance might be more beneficial. Regular checks confirm it's ready for peak seasons.

    Routine sump pump maintenance, which involves inspection, cleaning and testing, usually does not require a permit in Los Angeles, CA.
