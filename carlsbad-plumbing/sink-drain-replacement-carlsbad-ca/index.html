    P-Trap Replacement in Carlsbad

    Traps fail from cracks, corrosion and repeated clog clearing. We replace old chrome or thin plastic traps with heavy-wall PVC or polished brass that matches exposed plumbing. Each trap is sized correctly and aligned so slip joints seal without strain. A new trap is one of the most affordable fixes under a sink.

    Trap Arm and Wall Drain Repair in Carlsbad, CA

    When a leak comes from where the drain enters the wall, the trap arm or the stub may be cracked or corroded. Older homes sometimes have galvanized drain stubs that rust from the inside. We cut back to sound pipe and transition to new PVC or ABS with approved fittings. This keeps the trap properly vented and draining.

    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Carlsbad

    Vessel sinks and many glass basins use grid or umbrella drains without overflow holes. Using the wrong drain can cause slow draining or water spilling through the overflow port. We fit the correct drain type and height for your bowl. We also make sure the tailpiece length fits your vanity.

    Pop-Up Drain Replacement in Carlsbad

    The lift-rod pop-up is the most common bathroom drain and the one that wears out most often. We remove the old body, clean the flange seat, and install a new assembly with a fresh pivot ball and gasket. The lift rod is adjusted so the stopper seals and opens fully. This also fixes leaks from the pivot nut on the back of the drain.
