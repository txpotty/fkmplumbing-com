    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in Carlsbad, CA

    Irrigation systems in Carlsbad, CA are a common source of backflow, as stagnant water and lawn chemicals can be drawn back into the potable water supply. Installing a dedicated backflow preventer for your sprinkler system is often a local code requirement. We specialize in selecting and installing the correct device, such as a pressure vacuum breaker or atmospheric vacuum breaker, to protect your home's main water line from contaminants introduced by your landscape irrigation.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Carlsbad

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Carlsbad. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. FKM Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.

    Emergency Backflow Preventer Replacement in Carlsbad

    While installations are usually planned, sometimes an existing backflow preventer fails suddenly, creating an urgent need for replacement in Carlsbad.     Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Carlsbad

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Carlsbad

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the design of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.

    Pool and Spa Fill Backflow Protection in Carlsbad, CA

We install the correct device on pool fill lines and check that hose bibbs have vacuum breakers. This simple step protects your household water and keeps you in line with local code. We can add the work when a pool is being built or renovated.
