    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Carlsbad

    Residential hydro jetting in Carlsbad usually costs $350 to $800, depending on line length, access and how heavy the buildup is. Commercial jetting is often priced by the hour and may run higher.

    While hydro jetting is very powerful, it is generally safe for most sound plumbing systems, including PVC, cast iron, and concrete pipes. However, it is not recommended for severely damaged, corroded, or fragile pipes, which is why a pre-jetting camera inspection by FKM Plumbing is essential to assess pipe integrity and prevent further damage.

    FKM Plumbing uses professional-grade hydro jetting equipment from established manufacturers like Spartan Tool and MyTana. These brands are known for their reliability, powerful performance, and safety features, so we can deliver the most effective cleaning for your pipes.

    It is safe for most pipes in fair condition when pressure is set correctly. We camera the line first and avoid jetting pipe that is broken or collapsed.

    No permit is needed for jetting. If the inspection shows damage that needs repair, permits may be required and we handle them.

    While small, low-pressure drain jetters are available for rent, professional hydro jetting equipment is far more powerful and requires training to operate safely and effectively. Improper use can damage pipes, cause injury, or fail to clear the blockage. For severe clogs, it is always best to call experienced professionals like FKM Plumbing.

    Most residential jobs take 1 to 3 hours including inspection. Long commercial lines or heavy grease can add time.
