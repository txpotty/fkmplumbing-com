    Pipe Bursting in Carlsbad, CA

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, fracturing it outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind. The new pipe is fused into one continuous length with no joints. Bursting requires an entry and exit pit but avoids digging the full length.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Carlsbad, CA

    For severely damaged or collapsed pipes, pipe bursting offers a full replacement solution. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, breaking apart the old pipe at the same time. This provides a completely new, durable pipe, often HDPE (high-density polyethylene), which resists corrosion and leaks. It's an effective long-term fix for widespread issues in Carlsbad, CA.

    Lateral and Spot Lining in Carlsbad

    Not every pipe needs to be lined end to end. When damage is limited to a few joints or a short section, we can install a short sectional liner. This costs less than a full-length liner and is completed fast. We verify the rest of the line is sound before recommending a spot liner.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Trenchless Pipe Repair in Carlsbad

    The cost of trenchless pipe repair in Carlsbad varies significantly depending on the pipe's length, diameter, type of damage, and the specific trenchless method used, such as CIPP lining versus pipe bursting. In general, you can expect costs to range from $80 to $250 per linear foot for residential projects. A camera inspection is essential for an accurate estimate from FKM Plumbing.

    Most common pipe materials, including cast iron, clay, PVC, and concrete, can be repaired using trenchless methods. However, the condition of the existing pipe is a factor. If a pipe is completely collapsed or badly misaligned, pipe bursting might be a more suitable trenchless option than lining. Our inspection will determine the best approach.

    Trenchless pipe repairs, especially CIPP lining and pipe bursting using materials like epoxy resins and HDPE pipe, are designed to last for 50 years or more. These materials are highly resistant to corrosion, root intrusion, and chemical breakdown, offering a very durable solution.

    We use liners and resins from established manufacturers such as Perma-Liner and similar systems rated for sewer use. We choose materials based on pipe size and condition.

    Lining reduces the inside diameter slightly, but the smooth surface usually improves flow. Pipe bursting can maintain or increase the diameter.

    While trenchless methods are highly versatile, they are not suitable for all types of pipe damage. Severely collapsed pipes, pipes with significant offsets, or pipes that have suffered extreme structural failure may still require traditional excavation. A professional camera inspection from FKM Plumbing will determine what is possible.

    Most residential trenchless repairs are completed in one day. Longer or more complex lines may take two days.

    Signs you might need trenchless pipe repair include recurring drain clogs, sewage backups, foul odors from drains, wet spots in your yard or foundation without visible leaks, and oddly lush patches of grass. These often point to a damaged or leaking underground pipe.
