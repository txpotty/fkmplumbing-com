    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Whittier

    The cost of water heater installation in Whittier, CA usually ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of water heater (tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed plumbing or electrical modifications. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your particular needs.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, water heater installation involves working with gas lines, high voltage electricity and water connections, all of which carry significant risks if not handled correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, gas explosions, electrical fires and carbon monoxide poisoning. It can also void your warranty, and local codes typically require a licensed plumber for this work.

    Many tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years, though hard water can shorten that. Regular flushing and anode rod checks help lengthen life.

    New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. FKM Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, ensuring peace of mind. We will discuss exact warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    The decision to repair or replace often depends on the age of your unit, the cost of the repair and its energy efficiency. If your water heater is over 10 years old, often breaks down, or the repair cost is more than 50% of a new unit, replacement is often the more cost-effective choice. Newer models also offer much better energy efficiency.

    FKM Plumbing installs all major reputable brands of water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz. We work with both gas and electric models, as well as various sizes and efficiencies. Our team can help you choose the best brand and model to suit your hot water demands and budget.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for water heater replacement. We pull permits when required and confirm the install meets code.

    A standard tank replacement usually takes two to four hours. Tankless installs or relocations can take most of a day.
