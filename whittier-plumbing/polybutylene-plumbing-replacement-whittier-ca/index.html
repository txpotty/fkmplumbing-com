    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in Whittier

    Polybutylene's built-in weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Municipal water across much of Southern California is hard, leaving chalky scale on faucets, showerheads, and inside water heater tanks. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already weakened. Understanding this failure mechanism is important for diagnosing issues and recommending the most effective and permanent replacement strategy for your home.
