    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Whittier

    For significant damage or older, failing systems, traditional sewer line replacement often involves excavating a trench to access and replace the entire pipe.     Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Whittier, CA

    Where applicable, we offer trenchless sewer repair methods like pipe bursting. This technique involves pulling a new pipe through the old one, at the same time breaking the existing pipe apart. It's a less disruptive option that requires only two small access points, saving your landscaping and driveway from extensive excavation. Trenchless methods are well suited for certain types of pipe damage and property layouts in Whittier.

    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Whittier, CA

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, while Orangeburg pipe made from tar paper deforms over time. Both are likely to fail. We replace these lines with modern materials. Camera inspection shows the extent of damage. Replacement ends ongoing root problems.

    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in Whittier

    Deciding between repair and full replacement depends on the extent and nature of the damage. Minor cracks or isolated clogs might be resolved with targeted repairs, but widespread corrosion, multiple breaks, or a collapsed pipe often necessitates a full replacement. Our technicians in Whittier will conduct a thorough camera inspection to accurately diagnose the problem and recommend the most cost-effective and long-term solution. We provide clear explanations and transparent pricing for both options.

    Under-Slab Sewer Replacement in Whittier

    Many homes are built on slab foundations with drain lines underneath. When these lines fail, replacement requires tunneling or rerouting. We evaluate options to limit impact on your home.     Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Whittier, CA

    Many older homes in Whittier, CA still have original sewer lines made of clay, cast iron, or Orangeburg pipe, which are prone to degradation, cracking, and root intrusion over time.
