    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Carson

    When your main water line from the street to your home needs replacement, PEX is an smart option. Its flexibility allows for easier trenchless or minimal-dig installations, limiting disruption to your yard in Carson. PEX main lines handle ground movement and resist corrosion better than rigid pipe. Most homes in Southern California sit on concrete slabs, so slab leaks often show up as warm floors, damp carpet, or an unexplained jump in the water bill.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Carson

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a good option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer reliable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to verify a proper seal.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Carson, CA

    Expanding your home or remodeling a kitchen or bathroom often requires new plumbing lines. PEX is an excellent choice for these projects in Carson, CA due to its adaptability and ease of installation in existing wall cavities or new structures. We can seamlessly tie new PEX lines into your current system, ensuring proper water supply to new fixtures and appliances without compromising your existing plumbing.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Carson, CA

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is notorious for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Many insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to insure and sell.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Carson

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California install softeners or scale filters. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more often follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and lowers the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Carson

    FKM Plumbing frequently installs Uponor PEX systems, a leading brand known for high-quality, durable PEX-A piping. Uponor's ProPEX expansion fittings create strong, reliable and leak-resistant connections, ideal for the demands of a home's plumbing. We recommend Uponor for its proven track record and longevity, giving Carson homeowners peace of mind about their plumbing investment.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Carson, CA

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, similar to a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to balance cost and performance.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Carson

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, usually using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Carson can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.
