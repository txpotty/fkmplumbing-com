    Frequently Asked Questions About Leak Detection in Carson

    The cost of leak detection in Carson, CA can range from $250 to $800, depending on the complexity and location of the leak. Factors like slab leaks or hard-to-reach pipe leaks may be at the higher end. We provide a written estimate after our initial assessment.

    We use acoustic ground microphones, thermal imaging cameras from brands like FLIR, line tracers, moisture meters and pressure test gauges. Each tool helps confirm a different type of leak. Using several together gives accurate results.

    You can check your meter, test toilets with food coloring in the tank and look for damp spots. Finding leaks under slabs or inside walls requires professional equipment. That is where professional detection saves time and money.

    Older homes may have galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes, both known for higher leak rates due to corrosion or material breakdown. Copper pipes can develop pinhole leaks over time, while PVC and PEX are usually more reliable but can still leak at fittings or due to installation errors.

    Many homeowner's insurance policies cover the cost of repairing sudden and accidental leaks, and sometimes the cost of leak detection itself, especially if it leads to a covered repair. It's best to check your own policy or contact your insurance provider for details.

    To prevent future leaks, consider regular plumbing inspections, especially for older homes. Keep water pressure in a safe range, address any signs of corrosion and maintain your plumbing system. For homes with known issues, proactive repiping or pipe upgrades can be a long-term solution.

    Most residential leak detection appointments take one to three hours. Larger homes, commercial buildings or multiple leaks can take longer. We give you an estimate when we schedule.
