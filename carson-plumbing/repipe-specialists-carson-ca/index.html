    Drain Line Repiping in Carson

    While often overlooked, your home's drain lines are just as important as your supply lines.     Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Carson

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Earthquakes are a constant consideration in Southern California, and seismic straps and flexible connectors help keep water and gas lines secure during shaking. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Efficient PEX Repiping in Carson, CA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly popular choice for repiping projects in Carson, CA. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also highly resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a reliable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.
