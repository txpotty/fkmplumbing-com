    PEX resists the corrosion and scale that cause copper pinholes and is flexible and quick to install. If you have repeated copper leaks, repiping with PEX is often a cost-effective long-term solution.

    Most standard copper pipe leak repairs, such as fixing a visible pinhole leak or replacing a small section, can be completed within two to four hours.     Type K, L and M copper pipes differ mainly in their wall thickness and thus their pressure ratings. Type K has the thickest walls and is used for underground service and main water lines. Type L is common for interior water supply and radiant heating. Type M has the thinnest walls and is typically used for less demanding applications. We are skilled in repairing all types.

    A repair clamp or epoxy can buy time in an emergency, but they are not permanent fixes. A plumber should cut out the damaged section and install proper fittings, particularly inside walls.
