    Slow Drain and Clog Clearing in Irvine

    Bathroom sinks clog with hair, soap scum and toothpaste mixed with mineral scale. We pull the stopper, clean the overflow channel, and clear the trap and branch line with a hand auger instead of harsh chemicals. If the clog sits past the wall, we check whether a shared line with the tub or shower is the actual problem. You end up with a sink that drains at full speed.

    Leaking P-Trap Repair in Irvine

    Most puddles under a bathroom sink start at the P-trap. Slip-joint washers flatten over time, plastic traps crack, and old chrome traps rust through from the inside. We replace the trap with a properly sized 1-1/4 inch assembly, align the tailpiece and wall arm, and run water to confirm a dry seal. This is one of the quickest and least expensive fixes we do.

    Pop-Up Drain and Stopper Repair in Irvine, CA

    When the stopper will not close, will not open or lets water slowly escape, the pop-up linkage is typically to blame. The pivot rod nut can leak, the clevis strap can slip, and hair wraps around the stopper body. We clean or replace the assembly and adjust it so the lift rod works freely. If the drain body itself is corroded, we replace it with a matching finish.

    Sink Trap and P-Trap Repair in Irvine, CA

    The P-trap under your sink is essential for preventing sewer gases from entering your home and catching items that fall down the drain. If it's leaking, corroded, or improperly installed, it can cause serious problems. We provide expert repair and replacement of P-traps, making sure of a proper seal and function for your sink plumbing in Irvine, CA.

    Sink Repair vs Replacement Costs in Irvine, CA
