    Pipe Bursting Services in Irvine, CA

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless technique where a new pipe is pulled through the path of the old, broken pipe, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is very effective for complete pipe replacement, creating a new, durable pipe of the same or larger diameter. It's a solid solution for severely damaged or collapsed sewer and water lines, offering a long-term fix in Irvine, CA.
