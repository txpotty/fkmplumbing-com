    Gas and Electric Water Heater Checks in Irvine, CA

    Whether your home in Irvine, CA uses a gas or electric hot water tank, our maintenance covers the particular needs of each.     Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in Irvine

    Sediment buildup is a frequent issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high. Our standard flushing service removes these mineral deposits, which can reduce efficiency and lead to premature tank failure. We drain the tank completely, flush out all sediment and refill it, giving you clear water flow and improved heating performance. This routine service is key for extending the life of your unit.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Irvine, CA

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing widely. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank. A leak in an attic tank without a working pan can destroy a ceiling, so we confirm that protection is in place.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Irvine

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause loud popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Irvine

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure spike every heating cycle. High street pressure is not unusual in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Sediment Flushing & Cleaning in Irvine, CA

    Over time, minerals and sediment settle at the bottom of hot water tanks, reducing efficiency and potentially leading to premature failure. Our maintenance includes a thorough flushing of the tank to remove these deposits. This process helps your tank heat water more efficiently and quietly, a vital step for any hot water tank in Irvine, CA, where mineral content in the water is a common concern.

    Electric Hot Water Tank Element Check in Irvine

    Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout. We also check the thermostat settings and wiring connections to confirm they are functioning correctly and safely. This preventative check can identify failing elements before they stop heating altogether, preventing unexpected cold showers for your family in Irvine.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Irvine

    The cost of hot water tank maintenance in Irvine, CA typically ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the tank type, age and specific services required. This normally includes flushing, anode rod inspection and safety valve checks. We provide upfront pricing before any work begins.

    FKM Plumbing stands behind our work. While maintenance services generally don't have long-term warranties like new installations, we guarantee our workmanship for a set period. Any parts replaced during maintenance will carry their manufacturer's warranty.

    Most manufacturers and plumbing experts recommend yearly hot water tank maintenance. This is particularly true where water is hard, since minerals speed up sediment buildup and anode rod depletion.

    In mineral-heavy water a magnesium rod may last three to five years. We measure it at every visit so you replace it before the tank lining begins to rust.

    Most flushes and inspections take 45 minutes to 90 minutes. Heavily scaled tanks or anode replacements in tight closets can take a bit longer.

    That smell usually comes from bacteria reacting with a magnesium anode. We can switch to an aluminum-zinc or powered anode and sanitize the tank to get rid of it.

    Routine hot water tank maintenance, such as flushing and inspections, usually does not require a permit in Irvine. However, if major components are replaced or the entire unit is swapped out, a plumbing permit may be necessary. Our team handles all necessary permit applications.

    Watch for slower recovery, rusty or cloudy hot water, noises, moisture around the base or a relief valve that drips. Any of these means it is time for an inspection.
