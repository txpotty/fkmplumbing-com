    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Irvine

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get replaced. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility first.

    Drain Line Inspections for Maintenance in Irvine

    As part of complete plumbing maintenance, we offer drain line camera inspections. This lets us see the condition of your main sewer lines, spotting potential blockages from roots or grease buildup, or structural issues like cracks and offsets made worse by shifting soil. Expansive clay and hillside soils can shift over time, putting stress on slab plumbing and cracking older sewer joints. Early detection through inspection can prevent severe backups and expensive emergency repairs.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Irvine

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers call for annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Irvine, CA

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits greatly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to early tank failure.

    Drainage Services and Hydrojet Plumbing in Irvine

    Preventative drain maintenance is key to avoiding clogs and sewer backups. We offer routine drain camera inspections to find grease buildup, roots, or other obstructions before they cause a problem. Our hydrojet plumbing service can blast away stubborn blockages, keeping your drainage system clear and free-flowing, which is especially important for homes with mature trees near the sewer line.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Irvine, CA

    Tree roots invade sewer lines during dry months, so the first big rain of the season often reveals a cracked or blocked lateral. A working sump pump is essential for homes with basements or crawl spaces when heavy rain overwhelms drainage. Our maintenance includes testing the float switch, checking for proper discharge, cleaning the pit of debris, and inspecting the check valve. That way your sump pump is ready to protect your home from flooding when sudden storms hit Irvine.

    Sewer Line and Drain Camera Inspection in Irvine, CA
