    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Irvine

    A whole-home PEX repipe usually costs $4,500 to $12,000 depending on home size, bathroom count and access. Smaller jobs like a single bathroom remodel rough-in usually run $800 to $2,500.

    PEX can be used for outdoor applications like irrigation lines or outdoor spigots, but it needs to be protected from direct UV light, which can degrade the material over time. When used outdoors, PEX should always be buried or otherwise shielded.

    PEX offers several advantages over copper, including lower material and installation costs, greater flexibility and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Unlike copper, PEX does not develop pinhole leaks over time due to water chemistry, making it a more durable choice for many Irvine homes. It also reduces water hammer noise.

    Properly installed PEX is expected to last several decades. Manufacturers often offer 25 year warranties when their pipe and fittings are used together.

    We mainly use high-quality, reputable PEX brands such as Uponor (PEX-A) and Viega (PEX-B), which are known for their durability and reliability. These brands meet or exceed industry standards and are approved for use in Southern California plumbing applications, providing a long-lasting and dependable plumbing system for your home.

    While PEX is simpler to install than copper, professional installation is strongly recommended. Correct sizing, routing and secure connections are crucial for preventing leaks and ensuring system longevity. Improper DIY installation can lead to costly water damage and code violations.

    Signs you might need PEX repiping include persistently low water pressure, discolored or rusty water, frequent leaks, or old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes. These issues are typical in older Irvine homes and point to a failing plumbing system.

    We install Uponor PEX-A along with Viega, Zurn and SharkBite PEX-B systems. We recommend the system that suits your project and budget.
