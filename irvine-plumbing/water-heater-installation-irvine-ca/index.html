    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Irvine

    The cost of water heater installation in Irvine, CA can range from $1,200 to $4,000 or more, depending on the type of unit (traditional tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any necessary plumbing or electrical upgrades. Tankless units usually have higher installation costs due to more complex venting and utility requirements. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your specific needs.

    A traditional tank water heater usually lasts between 8 and 12 years with proper maintenance. Tankless water heaters often have a longer lifespan, ranging from 15 to 20 years, thanks to their durable components and on-demand operation.

    A standard tank replacement typically takes two to four hours. Tankless installs or relocations can take most of a day.

    Common signs that you might need a new water heater include a lack of hot water, rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit and an age of 10 to 15 years or more. If you notice any of these issues, it is best to have a professional inspection.

    Most households of two to four people do well with a 40 or 50 gallon tank. Larger families may need a 75 gallon tank or a tankless unit sized by flow rate.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 6 to 12 years on the tank and parts. We also warranty our installation labor.
