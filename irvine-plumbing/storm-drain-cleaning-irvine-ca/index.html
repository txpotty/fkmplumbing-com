    Dry Well Cleaning and Inspection in Irvine, CA

    Dry wells let storm water soak into the ground instead of flowing to a street. They are found on commercial sites, HOA common areas and some residential lots. Silt slowly seals the settling chamber and the drainage rock, slowing how fast water drains. We vacuum the chamber, inspect the inlet and outlet, and check drain-down time. If the dry well no longer drains properly, we explain options for repair or replacement.

    Storm Drain Camera Inspection in Irvine

    Before and after cleaning, or to diagnose the cause of persistent blockages, FKM Plumbing offers storm drain camera inspection in Irvine. Using waterproof cameras, we can visually inspect the interior of your storm drain lines to identify the exact nature and location of blockages, assess pipe condition, and check for structural damage like cracks or collapses. This non-invasive diagnostic tool gives accurate problem identification and lets us provide the most effective and targeted cleaning or repair solutions.

    Yard and Area Drain Cleaning in Irvine

    Yard drains and area inlets collect runoff from lawns, rock landscaping and patios. Over time the grates fill with leaves and the pipes fill with silt and small gravel. We pull the grates, vacuum the inlet box and jet the pipe toward its outlet. This restores the line so it can carry a full storm. We also check that grates are secure and seated.

    Catch Basin Cleaning Services in Irvine

    Catch basins are vital parts of many storm drainage systems in Irvine, designed to trap debris before it enters the main pipes. Over time, these basins can fill with leaves, trash, and sediment, reducing their effectiveness. Our service includes thoroughly cleaning out catch basins, removing built-up material so they work correctly and prevent blockages further down the storm drain line, especially important in areas with mature trees.

    Downspout and Roof Drain Lines in Irvine, CA

    Many homes connect downspouts to buried pipe that carries water away from the foundation. These lines clog with roof granules, leaves and nests, then back up and dump water at the wall. We jet them clear and test with a hose to confirm flow at the outlet. For flat roofs with scuppers, we check that the discharge path is open too. Keeping water away from the slab helps protect against soil movement and cracking.

    Emergency Storm Drain Clearing in Irvine

    If water is pooling against your home during a storm, a clogged drain is often the cause. We respond to urgent calls to clear the blockage and get water moving. In the moment, we focus on the inlet and pipe that are causing the flooding. Once the storm passes, we can clean the rest of the system.     Frequently Asked Questions About Storm Drain Cleaning in Irvine

    The cost of storm drain cleaning in Irvine usually ranges from $200 to $600, depending on the severity of the clog, the size of the drain, and the equipment required. More complex blockages or those requiring extensive hydro jetting may be at the higher end of this range. We provide upfront pricing before any work begins.

    Water pooling around grates, overflowing downspouts, standing water against walls and mud stains near inlets are typical signs.

    In Irvine, common causes of storm drain clogs include built-up leaves, dirt, and organic debris from trees, silt and sediment from soil erosion, trash, and sadly, sometimes even illegally dumped waste. Tree root intrusion into the pipes is also a major factor, especially in older systems.

    Hydro jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to wash out stubborn blockages and thoroughly clean the interior of drain pipes. For storm drains, it is very effective at removing compacted soil, heavy silt, grease, and roots, restoring the drain to its full flow capacity much better than snaking.

    Our technicians are trained to service all types and brands of storm drain systems typically found in residential and commercial properties. This includes standard PVC, corrugated HDPE, concrete, and cast iron drainpipes, as well as various types of catch basins and grates from manufacturers like NDS, ADS, and Zurn.

    Usually, routine storm drain cleaning does not require special permits in Irvine. However, if the cleaning uncovers a need for significant repair, excavation, or connection to public storm sewer lines, then permits from the local municipality or water authority may be necessary. We will advise you if any permits are required for your situation.

    It is generally recommended to have your storm drains inspected and cleaned at least once a year. Properties with many trees or those that see frequent heavy rains may need twice-yearly cleaning to prevent buildup and blockages.

    Just before your area's rainy or storm season starts is best. A second check after the season ends is smart for properties with lots of trees or gravel.
