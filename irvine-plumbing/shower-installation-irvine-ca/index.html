    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Irvine, CA

    Converting an old tub to a walk-in shower is one of the most requested bathroom upgrades. We remove the tub, relocate or resize the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height.     Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Irvine, CA

    Accessible showers need low or no thresholds, handheld showers on slide bars and properly placed controls. We install grab bar blocking while walls are open and set valves within easy reach.     Shower Pan Installation in Irvine

    A correctly installed shower pan is fundamental for preventing leaks and protecting your bathroom subfloor.     New Construction Shower Installation in Irvine, CA

    Curbless Shower Installation in Irvine
