    While you can handle very small, clean water spills, professional flood restoration is highly recommended for serious water damage. DIY efforts often miss hidden moisture, leading to lasting problems.

    Small amounts of clean water may be manageable, but contaminated flood water requires protective equipment and proper disinfection. Hidden moisture in walls and floors can cause mold if not dried thoroughly. Professional cleanup is safer and more thorough.
