    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Garden Grove, CA

    A cleanout provides easy access for future cleaning and inspection. Many older homes lack an exterior cleanout. We install one during sewer replacement. This saves time and money on later maintenance. It also helps in emergencies.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Garden Grove

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup catches debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and ends corrosion. Many homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.

    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Garden Grove, CA

    Many older homes in Garden Grove, CA still have original sewer lines made of clay, cast iron, or Orangeburg pipe, which are prone to degradation, cracking, and root intrusion over time. When we perform a sewer replacement, we often suggest upgrading to modern, durable materials like PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene). These materials are more flexible, resistant to corrosion and roots, and have a much longer lifespan, providing a significant upgrade to your home's plumbing infrastructure.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Garden Grove

    For severely damaged or collapsed sewer lines, traditional trenching is often the most reliable solution. This involves excavating a trench along the sewer line's path to allow for direct access and replacement of the entire pipe. While more invasive, it provides a complete overhaul and is often necessary for older homes with clay or cast iron pipes that have reached the end of their lifespan. We ensure thorough planning and execution to minimize disruption to your yard.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Garden Grove

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It needs only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are finished in one day.
