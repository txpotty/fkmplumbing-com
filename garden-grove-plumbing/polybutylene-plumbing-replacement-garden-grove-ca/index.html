    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Garden Grove, CA

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. We use Uponor PEX-A with expansion fittings that do not restrict flow. New lines run from the main shutoff to every fixture, water heater and appliance. Most homes are completed in two to four days.

    Copper Repiping Solutions in Garden Grove, CA

    For homeowners in Garden Grove, CA who prefer traditional materials, we also offer full polybutylene pipe replacement with copper. Copper piping is known for its longevity, resistance to bacterial growth, and proven track record. While more rigid than PEX, modern copper installations are very durable and provide excellent water pressure. Our plumbers are skilled in meticulous copper soldering and installation, creating a robust and reliable plumbing system that will last for decades, often outliving the home itself when properly maintained.

    Copper Replacement Option in Garden Grove

    Some homeowners choose Type L copper for its long history and rigid feel. We solder with lead-free solder or use pressed fittings for faster installation. Copper costs more than PEX and takes a little longer to install. We help you weigh the options for your budget and water conditions.

    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Garden Grove

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. You may see it at the water heater, under sinks or near the main shutoff. We inspect visible lines, check the stamps and trace runs through the attic. You get a clear answer and a written replacement estimate.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Garden Grove

    When a home with polybutylene pipes sits on a slab foundation, repiping becomes a more complex process. Our team specializes in full home repiping using PEX (cross-linked polyethylene), a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant material. PEX is an excellent choice for Southern California homes, as it resists freezing better than rigid pipes and expands to accommodate temperature changes. We carefully plan the repipe to minimize disruption, often running new lines through attics or existing wall cavities, delivering a complete and long-lasting upgrade for your entire plumbing system.
