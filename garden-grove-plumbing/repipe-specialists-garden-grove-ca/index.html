    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Garden Grove, CA

    Many older homes in Garden Grove, CA still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to corrosion, rust, and mineral buildup.     Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Garden Grove

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us what you have.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Garden Grove

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to four days.

    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Garden Grove, CA

    Municipal water across much of Southern California is hard, leaving white scale on faucets, showerheads, and inside water heater tanks. Water in many areas contains minerals that cause scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can accelerate corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral accumulation, ensuring a longer-lasting plumbing system.

    Efficient PEX Repiping in Garden Grove, CA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly popular choice for repiping projects in Garden Grove, CA. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also highly resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use high-quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a dependable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.
