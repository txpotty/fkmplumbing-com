    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Garden Grove

    The cost of water heater replacement in Garden Grove, CA usually ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 for a standard tank unit, including installation. Tankless units can range from $3,000 to $5,000 or more, depending on the model and needed plumbing modifications. Factors like unit type, capacity, brand and labor complexity influence the final price.

    Leaks at the base, rusty water, rumbling noises, inconsistent hot water and an age over 10 years are typical signs. A leaking tank should be replaced right away.

    In general, if your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down or has a major issue like a leaking tank, replacement is often more cost-effective in the long run. For newer units with minor, easily fixable issues, repair might be a sensible option. Our technicians can provide an honest assessment.

    FKM Plumbing installs and services all major brands, including Bradford White, Rheem, AO Smith, Rinnai, Navien and State Water Heaters. We can help you choose the best brand and model that suits your budget and hot water demands.

    Many households of two to three people do well with a 40 gallon tank, while larger families often need 50 gallons or more. We size according to your bathroom count and usage.

    Flushing a traditional tank water heater annually is recommended in most homes. Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California install softeners or scale filters. This helps remove sediment buildup, which can reduce efficiency and shorten the lifespan of the unit. Regular flushing can also prevent scale from impacting heating elements.

    Choosing the right size depends on the number of people in your household, their hot water usage habits and peak demand times. Our technicians will calculate the appropriate GPM (gallons per minute) for tankless or gallon capacity for tank models so you never run out of hot water, especially in multi-bathroom Garden Grove homes.
