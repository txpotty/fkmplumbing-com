    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Costa Mesa, CA

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are frequent culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry for good.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Costa Mesa, CA

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already passed through the foundation walls. This typically involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Costa Mesa

    Even minor cracks in foundation walls can allow significant water intrusion. Our services include crack repair using epoxy or polyurethane injection to seal these entry points for good. This method fills the entire depth of the crack, restoring strength and preventing water penetration. We address both vertical and horizontal cracks, knowing that horizontal cracks can signal structural issues that need a thorough assessment before sealing. Addressing these early prevents further water damage and costly future repairs.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Costa Mesa

    Many homes in Costa Mesa have crawl spaces, which are highly susceptible to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting prevents ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an effective way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Costa Mesa, CA

    Older homes, especially those built before modern waterproofing standards, are particularly prone to water intrusion. Copper pinhole leaks and corroded cast iron drains are frequent repair calls in homes that are now several decades old. Many older foundations may lack adequate exterior drainage or proper sealing. We specialize in assessing and upgrading waterproofing systems for these properties, often combining exterior excavation with interior drainage solutions. We take care to respect the existing structure while installing modern, effective waterproofing, with attention to the challenges of older brick and stone foundations.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Costa Mesa

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra reinforcement before new tile is set.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Costa Mesa

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most effective method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Expansive clay and hillside soils can shift over time, putting stress on slab plumbing and separating older sewer joints. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also integrated to divert water away.
