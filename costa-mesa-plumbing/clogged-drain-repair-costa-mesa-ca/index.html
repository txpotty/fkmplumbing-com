    While chemical drain cleaners are readily available, FKM Plumbing generally advises against them for homes in Costa Mesa. They often provide only temporary relief, can damage pipes over time, and pose environmental and health risks. Professional tools like augers or hydro-jetting are safer and more reliable.

    Caustic drain cleaners can damage ABS fittings, corrode older metal pipes and sit in the trap if they do not clear the clog. They also create a hazard for anyone working on the drain afterward. Mechanical clearing is safer and more effective.

    Enzyme or bacterial cleaners can help reduce organic buildup in kitchen and bathroom drains over time. They are gentler than caustic products and safe for most pipes. They will not clear a full clog but can help prevent one.

    You should call a professional plumber for clogged drain repair if DIY methods like plungers are ineffective, if you experience multiple clogged drains, or if you suspect a main sewer line blockage. Recurring clogs or slow drains also signal a need for professional diagnosis to prevent bigger issues.
