    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Costa Mesa

    Residential hydro jetting in Costa Mesa usually costs $350 to $800, depending on line length, access and how heavy the buildup is. Commercial jetting is often priced by the hour and may run higher.

    We use professional cart and trailer jetters along with rotating and root-cutting nozzles from well-known manufacturers. Color camera systems record the line before and after.

    Generally, a permit is not required for standard hydro jetting aimed at clearing blockages in existing lines. However, if the hydro jetting is part of a larger repair or replacement project that alters the plumbing system, a permit from the local building department may be necessary. We will tell you if a permit is needed for your situation.

    For most modern and well-maintained pipes, hydro jetting is very safe. However, for very old, corroded, or severely damaged pipes, the high pressure could cause further damage. That is why FKM Plumbing always performs a camera inspection first to check your pipe's condition and confirm hydro jetting is appropriate.

    No permit is needed for jetting. If the inspection shows damage that needs repair, permits may be required and we handle them.

    Hydro jetting is very effective against almost all types of clogs. It can break through solid obstructions like tree roots, flush out heavy grease and food buildup, clear mineral scale, and remove compacted sludge and debris that traditional snaking often leaves behind.

    Key signs you might need hydro jetting include regularly recurring clogs in the same drain, multiple slow drains throughout your home, persistent foul odors coming from drains, or gurgling sounds from toilets or drains. These often indicate a significant buildup or blockage that needs more than just a snake.

    Snaking uses a rotating cable to break through a clog. Jetting uses pressurized water to clean the entire pipe wall and flush debris away.
