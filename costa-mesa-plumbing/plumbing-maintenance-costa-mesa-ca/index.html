    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Costa Mesa

    Postwar tract homes are common in Southern California, and many still have original galvanized or copper supply lines and cast iron drains beneath the slab. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Finding a leak early prevents slab damage and mold.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Costa Mesa

    Tankless water heaters need specific maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is especially important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and potential breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.

    Sewer Line and Drain Camera Inspection in Costa Mesa, CA

    A camera inspection shows the condition of your sewer line and main drains. We look for roots, bellies, cracks and buildup before they cause a backup. Older homes with clay or cast iron lines benefit most from regular checks. We record the inspection and give you a copy. If cleaning or repair is needed, we explain your choices.

    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Costa Mesa, CA

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits significantly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to premature tank failure.

    Commercial Plumbing Maintenance in Costa Mesa, CA

    Water Softener Maintenance in Costa Mesa

    Water softeners fight hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance extends its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing expensive scale damage.

    Repiping and Polybutylene Replacement in Costa Mesa, CA

    Homes with polybutylene or aging galvanized pipe face a higher risk of leaks and low water pressure. During maintenance, we check pipe materials and look for signs of failure. If repiping makes sense, we explain PEX and copper options and give a detailed estimate. Repiping can be done in stages to fit your budget. New pipe brings back strong pressure and confidence.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Costa Mesa

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers call for annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.
