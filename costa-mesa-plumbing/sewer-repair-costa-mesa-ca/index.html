    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Costa Mesa, CA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Most homes in Southern California sit on concrete slabs, so slab leaks often show up as warm floors, damp carpet, or an unexplained jump in the water bill. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Costa Mesa, CA

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Costa Mesa.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Costa Mesa, CA

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Costa Mesa

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time.     Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Costa Mesa

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it holds.
