    Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Costa Mesa

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field quickly. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where it makes sense.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Septic Tank Repair in Costa Mesa

    The cost of septic tank repair in Costa Mesa, CA can vary widely, typically ranging from $300 for minor issues like a broken baffle to $5,000 or more for major problems such as drain field remediation or tank replacement. Factors include the nature of the problem, required excavation and parts needed. FKM Plumbing provides upfront, clear pricing after a thorough on-site diagnosis.

    Common causes of septic tank failure include infrequent pumping, which lets solids accumulate, tree root intrusion into pipes or the tank, excessive water usage overloading the system, driving heavy vehicles over the drain field and using harsh chemicals that kill beneficial bacteria. Dense, clay-rich soils can also contribute to drain field issues.

    To extend your septic system's life, keep up regular pumping every 3 to 5 years, avoid flushing non-biodegradable items, limit harsh chemical use, conserve water and keep vehicles off the drain field. Regular inspections by FKM Plumbing can also catch small issues before they become major problems.

    Septic tank repairs are complex and can be hazardous, involving exposure to raw sewage and potential soil contamination. It is highly recommended to hire a licensed professional like FKM Plumbing so the repair is done safely, effectively and in compliance with local health codes.

    FKM Plumbing stands behind the quality of our septic tank repair services with a solid warranty on parts and labor. We will provide specific warranty details for your particular repair during the initial consultation, giving you peace of mind.

    FKM Plumbing services and repairs all major septic tank brands and types, including concrete, fiberglass and plastic tanks and components from manufacturers like Infiltrator Systems, Presby Environmental and Advanced Drainage Systems. Our technicians are experienced with many septic system designs, from conventional gravity systems to aerobic treatment units.

    The length of a septic tank repair depends on the complexity of the issue. Simple clog removals might take a few hours, while more extensive repairs like drain field work could span several days. We will provide an estimated timeline during our initial assessment.
