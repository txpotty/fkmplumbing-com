    While it's possible for a homeowner to attempt a DIY toilet installation, it's often best left to professionals.     While DIY toilet installation is possible, hiring a professional makes sure the job is done correctly. Professionals have the tools and expertise to prevent leaks, ensure proper sealing, and comply with local plumbing codes, saving you potential headaches and costly repairs down the road.

    Common signs that you might need a new toilet include frequent clogs, constant running, visible cracks in the bowl or tank, excessive wobbling, or a toilet that is very old and inefficient. If you're always repairing it, replacement is often more cost-effective.
