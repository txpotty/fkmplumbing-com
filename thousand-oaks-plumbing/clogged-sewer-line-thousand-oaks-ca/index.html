    Sewer Camera Inspection in Thousand Oaks

    Before any work begins, a sewer camera inspection is key for accurately diagnosing the cause and location of a clog. Our high-resolution cameras provide a clear view inside your sewer lines, allowing us to identify issues like cracks, breaks, root intrusions, or excessive grease. This non-invasive method saves time and money by avoiding unnecessary digging, giving you a precise repair plan for your Thousand Oaks property. It is essential for older homes with cast iron or clay pipes.

    Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Thousand Oaks

    Roots from thirsty trees and shrubs seek out moisture and push into sewer pipe joints. Clay and older cast iron pipe are especially prone to root intrusion because of their many joints. We cut roots with a sewer machine fitted with root saws and then camera the line to see how much damage remains. For ongoing root problems, we can recommend a root treatment schedule or pipe lining.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Thousand Oaks

    Hydro jetting uses water at thousands of psi through a specialized nozzle to cut roots, grease and scale. It cleans the full diameter of the pipe rather than just boring a hole through the clog. We check the pipe condition with a camera first so jetting is only used on lines that can handle it. Many homeowners find that jetting buys them years before another backup.

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Thousand Oaks, CA

    If the camera shows a sound pipe with a grease or root clog, cleaning is usually all you need. Cracked, offset or collapsed pipe will keep clogging no matter how often it is cleared. Cleaning typically costs a few hundred dollars, while repairs range from a spot fix to trenchless lining or full replacement. We walk you through the footage and costs so you can decide.

    Emergency Sewer Backup Service in Thousand Oaks, CA

    Sewage coming up through a shower or floor drain needs immediate attention. Our emergency crews stop the backup, clear the line and help contain the mess.     Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Thousand Oaks, CA

    Cooking grease cools and hardens in the main line, and so-called flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. Together they form dense plugs that a cable may only punch a hole through. Hydro jetting at high pressure scours the pipe walls clean and flushes debris downstream. We also share simple habits that help prevent another grease or wipe clog.

    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Thousand Oaks, CA

    Most homes in Southern California sit on concrete slabs, so slab leaks often show up as warm floors, wet carpet, or an unexplained jump in the water bill. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. We can then quote a spot repair to restore proper slope.
