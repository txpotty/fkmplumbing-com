    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Rancho Cucamonga

    Well pump replacement typically costs $1,800 to $4,500 depending on well depth and pump size. Deep wells or constant pressure systems can cost $4,000 to $8,000.

    The lifespan of a well pump varies greatly depending on usage, water quality and pump type, but most last between 10 and 20 years. Regular maintenance and proper sizing can extend its life, but eventually wear and tear will require replacement.

    Low pressure, sputtering faucets, no water and high electric bills can signal pump trouble. Frequent cycling is another warning sign.

    Common signs of a failing well pump include no water, sputtering faucets, reduced water pressure, discolored water or surprisingly high electricity bills. If your pump runs constantly or cycles on and off too frequently, these are also signs that replacement might be necessary.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from 1 to 5 years. We also provide a labor warranty.

    In many areas, a plumbing or well permit is required for well pump replacement to ensure compliance with local building and health codes. FKM Plumbing handles all necessary permitting on your behalf, so your installation is fully compliant and safe.

    It is often a good idea to consider replacing your pressure tank at the same time as your well pump, especially if both are of similar age. A new pressure tank can improve the efficiency and lifespan of your new pump by reducing cycling. It also cuts labor costs to do both jobs at once.

    Well pump replacement is a complex job involving electrical wiring, heavy lifting and specialized tools, especially for submersible pumps. It is strongly recommended to hire a professional plumber for safety, proper installation and compliance with all codes, preventing costly mistakes.
