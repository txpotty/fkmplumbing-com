    Foundation Leak Detection in Rancho Cucamonga

    Accurate detection is the most important step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to confirm a leak exists. Then we use acoustic listening equipment and electronic line tracing to find the exact spot beneath the foundation. Thermal imaging helps confirm hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.

    Water Line Reroute in Rancho Cucamonga

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This avoids breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for simple patching.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Rancho Cucamonga

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs with steel cables under high tension. Cutting into these slabs requires care and scanning to avoid hitting a cable. We use ground-penetrating radar or other scanning methods before opening the floor. When the risk is too great, rerouting the plumbing is usually the safer option.

    Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Rancho Cucamonga

    Slab leaks are notorious for being difficult to detect, often showing subtle signs like warm spots on the floor or a sudden jump in your water bill. FKM Plumbing uses acoustic and infrared technology to accurately locate these hidden leaks under your concrete foundation. Once found, we offer fitting repair solutions, whether it's a spot repair, re-routing, or pipe bursting, to restore your plumbing system in Rancho Cucamonga without extensive damage to your property.

    Foundation Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Rancho Cucamonga, CA

    Direct repairs are often the lowest upfront cost but involve breaking concrete and may need flooring repairs. Reroutes cost a bit more but avoid concrete work and future leaks in the abandoned line. A full repipe is the most comprehensive option for homes with multiple leaks or aging pipe. We help you compare total costs, including flooring and drywall repairs.
