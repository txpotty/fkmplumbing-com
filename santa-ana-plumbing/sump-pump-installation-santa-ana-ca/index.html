    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Santa Ana

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit usually costs $600 to $1,500. Installing a new pit with slab cutting and excavation often runs $1,500 to $4,000. Battery backups usually add $500 to $1,500.

    FKM Plumbing installs a variety of trusted sump pump brands known for their durability and performance. Our common selections include Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty Pumps. We can recommend the best brand and model to suit your particular needs and budget for your Santa Ana home.

    Most standard sump pump installations can be completed within 3 to 6 hours. However, if a new sump pit needs to be excavated, or if extensive plumbing and electrical work is required, the process might take a full day. We always aim for efficient service with little disruption to your Santa Ana home.

    It's recommended to have your sump pump inspected and cleaned annually, especially before the rainy season. This maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning the pit and testing the discharge line to ensure it's free of obstructions. Regular checks prevent surprise failures.

    In many cities, a plumbing permit is typically required for new sump pump installations or major replacements. This ensures the work meets local building and plumbing codes. FKM Plumbing handles all necessary permitting in Santa Ana, CA to ensure your installation is fully compliant.

    Most manufacturers offer warranties from 3 to 5 years, and some premium models carry longer coverage. We also warranty our installation workmanship.

    A well-maintained sump pump typically lasts between 7 and 10 years. The lifespan can vary depending on the quality of the pump, how often it runs and how well it is maintained. Regular inspections and cleaning can help extend its life.

    A simple replacement usually does not need a permit. New pits, electrical work or discharge connections may require one, and we will let you know.
