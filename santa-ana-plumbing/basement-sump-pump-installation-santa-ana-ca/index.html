    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Santa Ana

    Pedestal sump pumps are an excellent choice for narrow or shallow sump pits, as their motor sits above the water level.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Santa Ana, CA

    Power outages can happen, especially during severe weather, and they leave your primary sump pump useless. We supply and install battery backup sump pump systems that automatically activate if the power goes out, providing continuous protection for your basement in Santa Ana, CA. Tree roots invade sewer lines during dry months, so the first big rain of the season often exposes a cracked or blocked lateral. These systems include a deep cycle battery and a secondary pump, so your basement stays dry even when the electricity fails.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Santa Ana

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by finding the low point where water collects. We saw cut the slab, excavate a pit roughly 18 to 24 inches wide and 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a perforated or solid liner depending on whether we are collecting groundwater or surface seepage. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor stays clean and level.

    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Santa Ana

    Office buildings, parking structures and multi-unit properties with below-grade levels often need duplex sump systems with two pumps and an alternating control panel. The panel switches lead and lag pumps on each cycle so wear stays even, and a high water alarm notifies building staff. We coordinate electrical with your electrician and follow manufacturer specs for basin size and float settings. Property managers get a clear maintenance schedule to keep both pumps exercised.

    Residential Basement Sump Pump Installation in Santa Ana

    Protecting your family home from water damage is our top priority. We install robust residential sump pump systems sized to your basement and the rainfall Santa Ana actually gets. Our plumbers precisely dig the sump pit, install the liner, and connect the pump to your home's drainage system and power supply for reliable operation for years to come. We also make sure the discharge line directs water far away from your foundation.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Santa Ana

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a good fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.
