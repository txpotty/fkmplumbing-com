    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Santa Ana

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Santa Ana

    If your current sump pump struggles to keep up with heavy water flow, especially during intense thunderstorms, it may be undersized. We offer high-capacity sump pump replacements and upgrades designed to handle significant water volumes. Our plumbers will assess your water intrusion patterns and recommend a pump with greater horsepower and a higher GPM (gallons per minute) rating. This prevents the pump from cycling too often or failing under stress, common issues in some Santa Ana properties.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Santa Ana, CA

    For added protection, many homeowners in Santa Ana choose a combination sump pump system, which pairs a primary electric pump with a battery backup pump in one unit. This keeps the system running even if the power fails or the primary pump quits. FKM Plumbing can replace your existing combination system or install a new one, evaluating your needs for groundwater management. We make sure the components are properly matched for strong performance and longevity.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Santa Ana

    Power outages during severe storms can leave a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump offers important protection during these times. We install and replace complete battery backup systems, including dedicated marine-grade batteries and smart charging units. Our team helps you select a system with enough run-time to protect your Santa Ana home through extended power interruptions. That gives you peace of mind when the lights go out.

    Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Santa Ana

    Commercial buildings often have elevator pits, parking garage drains and mechanical room sumps. These require pumps rated for higher flow and sometimes oil-sensing controls. We replace commercial pumps and alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. Duplex systems with alternating pumps provide redundancy. We schedule work to avoid interrupting operations.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Santa Ana

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation prevents sewer gas odors.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Santa Ana, CA

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more durable. We provide professional pedestal sump pump replacement in Santa Ana, CA, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a major concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Santa Ana

    The cost of sump pump replacement in Santa Ana, CA typically ranges from $500 to $1,500, depending on the type of pump, submersible or pedestal, its horsepower and any needed pit changes. Battery backup systems will add to this cost, usually between $300 and $800. We provide a written, upfront quote after assessing your needs.

    To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by FKM Plumbing can also help extend its lifespan.

    It is recommended to test your sump pump at least every three months, and especially before and after rainy seasons. To test, simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit. The pump should activate, drain the water and then shut off automatically. If it does not, it is time for a professional's inspection.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from 3 to 5 years. We also offer a labor warranty.

    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods can fail earlier due to stuck switches.

    Key signs include the pump not turning on, unusual noises such as grinding or rattling, running nonstop, visible rust or corrosion, or an age over 7 to 10 years. Any of these could indicate coming failure and a need for replacement.

    Constant running, loud grinding, frequent cycling or failure to start are typical signs. Rust, age and a burning smell also indicate trouble.

    While it might seem like a simple task, sump pump replacement involves electrical connections, proper plumbing and making sure the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding. DIY replacement can lead to improper operation, electrical hazards or system failure. It is best to hire a professional plumber for a safe and effective installation.
