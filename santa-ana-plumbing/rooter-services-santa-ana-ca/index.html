    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Santa Ana

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet typically costs $125 to $300. Main sewer line rooting through a cleanout generally runs $200 to $500. Adding hydro jetting brings the total to about $450 to $1,000.

    When performed by trained professionals like those at FKM Plumbing, rooter services are safe for your pipes. Our technicians are skilled in operating the equipment correctly to clear blockages without causing damage. However, if pipes are already compromised, such as corroded or cracked, some methods may worsen existing issues, which is why a camera inspection is often recommended first.

    We use industry-leading professional-grade equipment, including Spartan Tool and Ridgid rooter machines and hydro-jetters. These brands are known for their power and reliability, allowing us to tackle the toughest blockages without damaging your plumbing system.

    Rooting cuts roots back but does not seal the pipe joints, so roots will eventually regrow. Root treatments slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement provides a lasting fix.

    Slow drains in several fixtures, gurgling toilets and sewage odors are common signs. Water backing up into a tub when you flush is another clue. Frequent clogs in the same line also suggest a deeper blockage.

    Generally, simple rooter services to clear clogs do not require a permit in Santa Ana. However, if the service reveals a need for pipe repair or replacement, especially for main sewer lines, a plumbing permit may be needed. We will handle all necessary permitting if that situation arises.

    Tree roots are naturally attracted to water and nutrients, which they find in sewer lines through tiny cracks or loose joints. As roots grow, they can expand and completely block the pipe, a frequent issue in Santa Ana neighborhoods with mature trees and older pipes.

    Homes with tree roots or older clay pipe often need rooting every 12 to 24 months. Homes with newer PVC lines may go years without it. A camera inspection can help set the best schedule.
