    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Santa Ana

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, controlling regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages.     Control Valve and Injector Service in Santa Ana, CA

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a complete regeneration every time.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Maintenance in Santa Ana

    Template assisted crystallization and other salt-free conditioners do not remove hardness but change how it behaves. They still need media replacement every few years and sediment prefilter changes much more often. We inspect the media tank, replace prefilters and check bypass valves. We also explain honestly how these systems differ from a true softener.

    Resin Bed Analysis and Regeneration in Santa Ana

    The resin bed is the heart of your water softener, where ion exchange occurs. During maintenance, we analyze the condition of the resin beads and confirm the regeneration cycles are programmed correctly for your household's water usage. Proper resin bed function is key to consistent soft water throughout your home in Santa Ana.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Santa Ana, CA

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their capacity. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for full softening capacity. We make sure your system is regenerating efficiently for your water quality, providing truly soft water.
