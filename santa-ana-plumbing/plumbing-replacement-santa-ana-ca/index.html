    The timeline for plumbing replacement depends on the complexity of the job. A water heater replacement usually takes 2 to 4 hours. A main water line or sewer line replacement can take 1 to 3 days. Whole-home repiping projects typically span 3 to 7 days, depending on the size of the house and accessibility.

    Frequent leaks, rusty or discolored water, low pressure, recurring backups and equipment over 10 years old are typical signs. We can inspect and give you an honest assessment.

    We mainly use PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) and copper piping for water supply lines. For sewer and drain lines, we use durable PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene) pipes, based on the application and local codes.

    Replacing old pipes with PEX or copper offers numerous benefits. PEX is flexible, corrosion-resistant and less prone to bursting in freezes, while copper is durable and long-lasting. Both improve water quality, eliminate leaks from old pipes and can significantly boost water pressure and flow throughout your Santa Ana home.

    Climate, freezes and soil type all affect how long plumbing lasts. Intense sun breaks down exposed plastic pipe, sprinkler parts, and exterior fittings, so they wear out faster than indoor plumbing. Freeze-thaw cycles can damage pipes, and corrosive or shifting soils can accelerate pipe degradation. Most homes in Southern California sit on concrete slabs, so slab leaks often show up as warm floors, wet carpet, or an unexplained jump in the water bill. Our replacement solutions are chosen to withstand these local conditions for lasting durability.

    While small fixes might be possible, major plumbing replacements like water heaters, sewer lines or full repipes are complex and best left to licensed professionals.
