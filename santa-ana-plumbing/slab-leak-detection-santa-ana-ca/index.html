    Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Santa Ana, CA

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most economical repair. We cut a small opening in the floor at the marked spot, repair the pipe and pressure test it. Then we patch the concrete and leave the area ready for flooring. Accurate detection keeps that opening as small as possible.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Santa Ana

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is popular when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Santa Ana

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs nonstop and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before flagging the spot.

    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in Santa Ana

    Our team uses electronic listening devices to pinpoint the precise location of a slab leak. These highly sensitive microphones can detect the faint sound of water escaping from a pressurized pipe under your foundation. This non-invasive method helps us avoid unnecessary concrete breaking, saving you time and money on repairs. It's especially effective for copper and PEX lines commonly found in Santa Ana homes.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Santa Ana, CA

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to see temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a clear thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in Santa Ana.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Santa Ana

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak without risk or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.

    Non-Invasive Leak Detection in Santa Ana, CA

    Our primary goal is to locate your slab leak with the least amount of disruption possible. We favor non-invasive techniques like acoustic and thermal imaging before considering any intrusive methods. This approach saves your home in Santa Ana, CA from unnecessary damage, preserves your landscaping and reduces the overall cost and time of the repair process.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Santa Ana, CA

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of repeat slab leaks. We can usually complete a full repipe in two to four days.

    Common signs of a slab leak in Southern California homes include unexplained increases in water bills, warm spots on your floor, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, damp or mildewy smells and visible cracks in your foundation or walls.     A water line slab leak involves pressurized fresh water escaping from supply pipes, often resulting in higher water bills, warm spots or the sound of running water.
