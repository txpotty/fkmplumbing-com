    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Santa Ana

    Well pump replacement typically costs $1,800 to $4,500 depending on well depth and pump size. Deep wells or constant pressure systems can cost $4,000 to $8,000.

    We install Grundfos, Goulds and Franklin Electric pumps. These brands are dependable and widely supported.

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up from shallower wells, typically less than 25 feet. Submersible pumps are placed directly inside the well casing, submerged in water, and are used for deeper wells, pushing water up to the surface. Each has its own applications and benefits.

    In many areas, permits may be required for major plumbing work like well pump replacement, especially if it involves changing the well system's electrical or structural components. FKM Plumbing will help you work through any permitting in Santa Ana, CA so your installation is fully compliant with local regulations.

    Most new well pumps come with a manufacturer's warranty, typically ranging from 1 to 5 years, covering defects in materials and workmanship. FKM Plumbing also stands behind our installation work with our own labor warranty for complete peace of mind.

    Regular maintenance, such as checking your well's water level, keeping the pressure tank air charge correct and fixing any leaks promptly, can extend your pump's life. Avoiding heavy water use and installing a good quality sediment filter can also reduce wear and tear on the pump.

    Most standard well pump replacements can be finished within a single day, typically 4 to 8 hours. However, complex installations involving very deep wells, difficult access or additional system repairs may take longer. Our team works efficiently to restore your water as soon as possible.

    Most well pumps last 8 to 15 years. Sediment, hard water and frequent cycling can shorten lifespan.
