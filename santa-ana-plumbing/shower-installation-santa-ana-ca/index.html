    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Santa Ana, CA

    Converting an old tub to a walk-in shower is one of the most requested bathroom upgrades. We remove the tub, relocate or resize the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height. On slab foundations, the drain may need to be moved, which involves cutting concrete. The result is a safer, more accessible shower.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Santa Ana, CA

    Accessible showers need low or no thresholds, handheld showers on slide bars and properly placed controls. We install grab bar blocking while walls are open and set valves within easy reach. These features help seniors and people with mobility needs bathe more safely. We follow ADA guidelines when requested.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Santa Ana

    Steam showers need a steam generator, a dedicated water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator, plumb the supply and drain, and connect the steam head. A licensed electrician handles the circuit. Steam showers also need sealed enclosures and sloped ceilings.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Santa Ana, CA

    Fiberglass and acrylic shower units are affordable and quick to install. We set the base, connect the drain and install the valve and trim. Prefab units are ideal for guest baths, rentals and guest houses. We make sure the base is fully supported so it does not flex or split.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Santa Ana

    Rain showerheads, body sprays and dual heads need careful planning for valve capacity and water pressure. We install thermostatic valves with volume controls and diverters to run multiple outlets. We also confirm your water heater can handle higher flow. The result is a spa-like shower that works as intended.
