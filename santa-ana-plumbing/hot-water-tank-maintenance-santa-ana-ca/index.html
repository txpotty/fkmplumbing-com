    Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Santa Ana

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. Many families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Santa Ana

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure jump every heating cycle. High street pressure is not unusual in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Santa Ana, CA

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition. Proper expansion tank function is essential to preventing premature wear on your hot water tank and protecting system integrity.
