    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very popular choice for repiping projects in Santa Ana, CA. We use high-quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a reliable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.
