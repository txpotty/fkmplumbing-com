    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Buena Park, CA

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to reach for maintenance and repair. They are often a good choice for shallow sump pits or areas where space is a concern. Our team is skilled in installing these pumps, making sure of proper pit depth and discharge line setup for optimal performance in Buena Park, CA.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Buena Park

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, compacted clay or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a dependable place to collect and remove water.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Buena Park, CA

    Many older homes in Buena Park, CA have crawl spaces that are vulnerable to moisture issues and standing water.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Buena Park, CA

    Severe storms can knock out power right when your pump is needed most. Battery backup pumps take over automatically when the main pump fails or power goes out. We install backup systems with sealed batteries and charging units. Many include alarms that alert you to problems. Backups add an extra layer of protection for finished basements.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Buena Park, CA

    For complete peace of mind, consider a combination sump pump system. This setup includes a primary electric sump pump and an integrated battery backup pump in a single unit, providing full protection against both power failures and primary pump malfunctions. FKM Plumbing can install these advanced systems in your Buena Park, CA home, offering stronger flood prevention. This solution is ideal for homes in flood-prone areas or those with valuable basement finishes.
