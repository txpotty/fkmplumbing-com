    Why Choose FKM Plumbing for PEX Pipe Installation in Buena Park, CA?

    PEX piping offers superior freeze resistance than copper or CPVC. Freezing pipes are rare in most of Southern California, and many homes have exposed outdoor plumbing that would never pass in a colder climate. We make sure your new PEX system is installed correctly to maximize that benefit.

    • Durability, PEX is highly resistant to corrosion, scale buildup and chlorine, common problems that degrade older metal pipes.
    • Freeze Resistant, PEX can expand and contract, making it more resistant to bursting in freezing temperatures than rigid pipes.
    • Flexibility, Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings and connections, reducing potential leak points in your plumbing system.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Buena Park, CA

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Buena Park, CA

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, much like a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when several fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to balance cost and performance.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Buena Park, CA

    Expanding your home or remodeling a kitchen or bathroom often requires new plumbing lines. PEX is an excellent choice for these projects in Buena Park, CA due to its adaptability and ease of installation in existing wall cavities or new structures. We can seamlessly tie new PEX lines into your current system, ensuring adequate water supply to new fixtures and appliances without compromising your existing plumbing.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Buena Park, CA

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.
