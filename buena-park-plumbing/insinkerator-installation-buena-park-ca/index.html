    Hard Water and Mineral Considerations in Buena Park

    Municipal water across much of Southern California is hard, leaving white scale on faucets, showerheads, and inside water heater tanks. Hard water leaves scale on disposal grind rings, sink flanges and drain tailpieces. Over time that buildup can make a disposal noisy and cause slow drainage. We inspect the drain line for scale and debris before connecting a new unit. If heavy buildup is present, we recommend cleaning or replacing the trap and tailpiece at the same time. Stainless steel grind components on Evolution models resist corrosion better in these conditions.
