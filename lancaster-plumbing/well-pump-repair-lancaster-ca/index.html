    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps prematurely.     Jet Pump System Repair in Lancaster, CA

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. FKM Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Lancaster operates at peak efficiency.
