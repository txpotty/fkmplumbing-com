    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Lancaster, CA

    Submersible sump pumps are designed to sit directly in the sump pit, submerged in water, which makes them quieter and less intrusive. They are powerful and effective for basements that take on a lot of water. Our team in Lancaster, CA will install your submersible pump with precision, making sure of proper sealing, discharge piping and electrical connections for quiet, reliable operation, which matters most in finished basements.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Lancaster

    Pedestal sump pumps are an good choice for narrow or shallow sump pits, as their motor sits above the water level. This design makes them easier to maintain and can often offer a longer lifespan because the motor stays out of the water. FKM Plumbing can properly install pedestal sump pumps in your Lancaster home, with the right sizing and setup for effective water removal without crowding your pit.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Lancaster

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod set to your pit depth.

These systems include a deep cycle battery and a secondary pump, so your basement stays dry even when the electricity fails.
