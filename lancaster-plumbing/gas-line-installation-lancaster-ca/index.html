    We mainly install black iron pipe and corrugated stainless steel tubing, or CSST. Black iron is a traditional, rigid option, while CSST offers flexibility and faster installation.     Common materials include black iron pipe, which is very durable, and corrugated stainless steel tubing, which offers flexibility and can be easier to install.
