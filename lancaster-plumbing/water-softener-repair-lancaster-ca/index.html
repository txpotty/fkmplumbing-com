    Control Valve Repair in Lancaster

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, directing the flow of water and initiating regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water or waste salt. FKM Plumbing technicians are experts at diagnosing and repairing common control valve issues, whether mechanical or electronic, ensuring precise timing and efficient operation. We stock common replacement parts to speed up your repair in Lancaster.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Lancaster

    A frequent issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to inefficient regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and making sure the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering precise repairs or replacements.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Lancaster, CA

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank and keeps salt from dissolving. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. We also suggest salt types that bridge less in warm or humid spaces.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Lancaster, CA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates suction to draw brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. When it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying. We clean or replace the injector and screen and check the brine line for leaks.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Lancaster, CA

    Leaks in a water softener can range from minor drips around fittings to significant puddles from a cracked tank or faulty valve. These leaks can cause water damage and waste water. Our plumbers promptly pinpoint the source of leaks, whether from seals, O-rings, hoses, or internal components, and perform durable repairs. We use appropriate sealing compounds and replacement parts for a lasting fix in Lancaster, CA.
