    Expansive clay and hillside soils can shift over time, putting stress on slab plumbing and separating older sewer joints. Soil that shifts and settles puts stress on underground pipes.     Common signs you might need a new water line include a sudden drop in water pressure, discolored or rusty water from your faucets, constantly wet spots in your yard, unusually high water bills without a change in usage, or frequent leaks and repairs on your existing main line.     Type K copper, PEX and HDPE are all approved choices. HDPE and PEX flex with soil movement and have fewer joints, while copper offers rigidity and puncture resistance.
