    High-Pressure Water Jetting for Drain Cleaning in Mission Viejo

    High-pressure water jetting, or hydro jetting, is a non-invasive method that uses highly pressurized water to clear and clean pipes. Our equipment in Mission Viejo can generate water pressures up to 4,000 PSI, powerful enough to break apart stubborn blockages and scour pipe walls clean without chemicals. This method is effective for many pipe materials, from PVC to cast iron, and is an eco-friendly choice for deep drain cleaning.

    Root Cutting With Hydro Jetting in Mission Viejo

    Specialty root-cutting nozzles spin or use focused streams to slice through fine roots inside a sewer. This is useful on root hairs that a cable saw can leave behind. Roots from yard trees and shrubs often enter at clay pipe joints or cracks. Jetting clears them out and gives a clean view of each entry point on camera. That helps you decide whether lining or a spot repair is the next step.

    Scale Removal in Cast Iron Pipes in Mission Viejo

    Cast iron drains in older homes build up rust tubercles and mineral scale, and hard water speeds that process. Jetting with the right nozzle can strip much of this buildup and restore flow. We keep pressure in a safe range for aged iron and watch the camera for thin spots. This cleaning is also the standard prep step before pipe lining. Clean pipe walls help a liner bond properly.

    Hydro Jetting Cost Compared to Snaking in Mission Viejo, CA

    Jetting costs more than a basic cable cleaning on the first visit. However, it often lasts much longer because it removes the coating that causes repeat clogs. If you are paying for snaking several times a year, jetting often costs less over time. We give you both prices and our honest view on which fits your line. For simple soft clogs, a cable may be all you need.
