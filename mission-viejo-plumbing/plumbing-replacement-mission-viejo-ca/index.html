    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Replacement in Mission Viejo

    Costs vary by job. A tank water heater replacement usually runs $1,400 to $3,000, a water softener $1,500 to $3,500, a water line $1,800 to $5,500 and a sewer line $4,000 to $15,000 or more.

    If you have isolated leaks on newer pipes, a repair might be enough. However, if you have older galvanized, polybutylene or lead pipes, or if leaks are widespread and frequent, replacement is generally recommended. Our plumbers can conduct an inspection, often with camera technology for sewer lines, to provide an accurate assessment and recommendation.

    Key signs include frequent, recurring leaks in the same area, discolored water, very low water pressure throughout the house, extensive corrosion visible on pipes or a major component like a water heater being over 10 to 15 years old and failing. If repair costs approach the cost of replacement, replacement is often the better long-term investment.

    While some minor plumbing repairs can be DIY, major replacements like water heaters, sewer lines or repiping should always be handled by licensed professionals. These jobs involve complex systems, potential safety hazards (gas, high water pressure) and strict code compliance. Improper installation can lead to leaks, damage and even severe safety risks, costing more in the long run.

    Most tank water heater replacements take two to four hours. Code upgrades or relocations can take longer.

    For water heater replacements, we primarily recommend and install reliable brands such as Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien. These manufacturers are known for their durability, energy efficiency and strong warranty coverage, providing long-term value for your Mission Viejo home.

    Consider the age of the equipment, the cost of repair compared with replacement and how often it has needed service. We give you both options with pricing so you can choose.

    Replacing old pipes with PEX or copper offers many benefits. PEX is flexible, corrosion-resistant and less prone to bursting in freezes, while copper is durable and long-lasting. Both improve water quality, eliminate leaks from old pipes and can noticeably boost water pressure and flow throughout your Mission Viejo home.
