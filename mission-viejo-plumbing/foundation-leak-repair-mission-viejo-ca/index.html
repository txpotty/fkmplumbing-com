    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Mission Viejo

    Slab leaks are common in homes built on concrete foundations, often caused by shifting soil or corroded pipes underneath. Our technicians use low-impact methods like electronic leak detection and hydrostatic pressure testing to find these hidden leaks. Once located, we can perform targeted repairs, either by slab tunneling or direct access, minimizing disruption to your home. We work with many pipe materials, including copper, PEX, and PVC, providing a durable repair for your Mission Viejo property.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Mission Viejo, CA

    Many older homes in Mission Viejo, CA have copper water lines beneath their foundations, which can corrode over time due to soil conditions or water chemistry. Municipal water across much of Southern California is hard, leaving chalky scale on faucets, showerheads, and inside water heater tanks.     Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Mission Viejo, CA

    Leaks in drain lines under the foundation can be harder to detect because they are not under constant pressure.     Water Line Reroute in Mission Viejo

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This skips breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for easy patching.
