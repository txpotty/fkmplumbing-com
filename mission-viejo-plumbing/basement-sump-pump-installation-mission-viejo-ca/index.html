    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Mission Viejo

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which saves wear on the motor. On an occasional cold night, covering backflow preventers and outdoor valves is typically enough to avoid a break. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the line.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Mission Viejo

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Mission Viejo

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by finding the low point where water collects. We saw cut the slab, excavate a pit roughly 18 to 24 inches wide and 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a perforated or solid liner depending on whether we are collecting groundwater or surface seepage. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor stays clean and level.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Mission Viejo

    The cost of basement sump pump installation in Mission Viejo typically ranges from $800 to $2,000, depending on the type of pump, the complexity of the installation and whether a new sump pit needs to be dug. Battery backup systems or long discharge line runs can affect the final price. We provide a detailed, upfront estimate after an on-site assessment.

    Most residential basements need a 1/3 or 1/2 horsepower pump, but the right answer depends on how fast water enters the pit and how high it has to be lifted. We measure both before recommending a model.

    We recommend having your sump pump inspected and maintained once a year, ideally before the rainy season. Regular maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning debris out of the pit, testing the check valve and making sure the discharge line is clear. This helps prevent unexpected failures and lengthens the life of your pump.

    Watch for a pump that runs constantly, cycles on and off rapidly, makes grinding noises or never seems to turn on. Rust stains, a stuck float and water sitting above the switch level are also red flags.

    FKM Plumbing offers a full warranty on our basement sump pump installations, covering both parts and labor. The specific warranty period will be spelled out in your service agreement. We stand behind our work and the quality of the products we install in your Mission Viejo home.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, built to handle everyday water removal. A secondary sump pump, often a battery-powered backup, serves as an emergency system that kicks on if the primary pump fails, loses power or is overwhelmed by excess water. Both work together for the best protection.

    FKM Plumbing offers a warranty on our labor for basement sump pump installations, typically for one year. The sump pump itself comes with a manufacturer's warranty, which can range from 1 to 5 years depending on the brand and model. We will give you all warranty details at the time of installation.

    A standard basement sump pump installation typically takes 4 to 8 hours. If a new sump pit needs to be excavated, or there are unexpected challenges with plumbing or electrical access, the job might stretch to a full day. Our team works efficiently to minimize disruption to your home.
