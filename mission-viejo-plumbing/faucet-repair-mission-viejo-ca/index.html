    Compression Faucet Repair in Mission Viejo

    Compression faucets, often found in older Mission Viejo homes, are known for their separate hot and cold handles. When these faucets leak, it's typically due to worn-out rubber washers or packing. Our team is skilled in disassembling these classic fixtures, replacing the necessary seals and washers, and reassembling them to stop leaks effectively. We respect the character of older homes and make sure these repairs are done carefully.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Mission Viejo

    A dripping spout typically means a worn cartridge, washer or valve seat. Hard water speeds up this wear by leaving scale that scratches seals and keeps them from closing tight. We disassemble the faucet, replace the worn parts and clean the valve body. Fixing a drip right away keeps a small leak from building up on your water bill.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Mission Viejo

    Weak or sputtering flow is often caused by mineral buildup in the aerator or cartridge. We remove and clean or replace the aerator, flush the lines and check the cartridge for scale blockage. On pull-down faucets, the spray head and check valves can also clog. If flow is low at every fixture, we test whole-home water pressure.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Mission Viejo

    Ceramic disc faucets are known for their durability and are a common choice in many contemporary Mission Viejo bathrooms. While generally reliable, they can develop leaks if the ceramic discs become scratched or misaligned, or if the inlet and outlet seals wear out. Our plumbers have the expertise to carefully disassemble these precise mechanisms, clean or replace the ceramic discs, and reseal the faucet to eliminate leaks and restore full performance.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Mission Viejo, CA

    Many modern single-handle faucets, often found in newer homes, use a cartridge system. When these fail, you might experience inconsistent water temperature, reduced flow, or difficulty turning the faucet on or off. Our technicians are experts at diagnosing cartridge issues and replacing them with the correct manufacturer-specific part, so your faucet works like new. We work with all major brands to provide precise repairs.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Mission Viejo, CA

    Ball faucets, recognizable by their single lever that moves over a rounded cap, are common in many kitchens. They use a rotating ball joint to control water flow and temperature. If your ball faucet is leaking from the spout or base, or if the handle feels loose, it's likely time for a repair. We can replace the spring-loaded rubber seats and O-rings, which are usually the culprits, restoring smooth operation and preventing drips.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Mission Viejo, CA

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base points to failed O-rings or a loose mounting nut. If left alone, the leak can soak into the cabinet below and damage the sink base. We replace O-rings, tighten mounting hardware and reseal the base. We also check the deck for water damage.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Mission Viejo, CA

    A cartridge repair often costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life. However, if the faucet body is cracked, parts are discontinued or it has needed several repairs, replacement might be better value. We give you both prices so you can choose.
