    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Mission Viejo

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin typically costs $900 to $2,000. A new ejector system with basin, venting and floor work usually ranges from $2,500 to $6,000 or more.

    Installing a sewage ejector pump is a complex job that involves plumbing, electrical and sometimes excavation work. It requires specialized knowledge to get sizing, venting and code compliance right. We firmly recommend hiring a licensed professional like FKM Plumbing to avoid costly errors, safety hazards and potential code violations.

    We recommend having your sewage ejector pump inspected annually, especially if it is in a high-use area. Regular inspections can identify potential issues early, extend the life of your pump and prevent unexpected failures and costly backups. This is particularly important for older units.

    A sump pump removes groundwater or seepage, while an ejector pump handles sewage and wastewater from fixtures. Ejector pumps are designed to pass solids and require a sealed, vented basin.

    Most ejector pumps last 7 to 10 years with normal use. Flushing wipes or other non-flushable items can shorten that life considerably.

    With proper installation and regular maintenance, a high-quality sewage ejector pump can last 7 to 15 years. Factors such as usage frequency, the type of waste it handles and water quality can influence its lifespan. Annual inspections can help maximize its longevity.

    A sewage ejector pump is designed to handle solid waste and wastewater from plumbing fixtures below the main sewer line, like toilets and sinks, discharging it into the sewer system. A sump pump, in contrast, removes groundwater from a basement or crawl space, only handling clear water to prevent flooding. They serve distinct purposes for different types of water.

    New ejector installations usually require a plumbing permit and inspection. Straight pump replacements may not, and we confirm local requirements for each job.
