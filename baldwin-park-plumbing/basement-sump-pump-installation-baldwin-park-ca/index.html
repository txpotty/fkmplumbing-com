    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Baldwin Park, CA

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Baldwin Park

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.
