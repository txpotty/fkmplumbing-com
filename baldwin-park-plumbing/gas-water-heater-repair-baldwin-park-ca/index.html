    Gas Water Heater Repair Cost in Baldwin Park, CA

    Thermocouple and pilot repairs usually cost $150 to $350. Gas control valve replacement often runs $350 to $700, and power vent blower repairs can range from $400 to $800. A new gas tank installed typically costs $1,600 to $3,000 depending on size and venting. We help you choose based on tank age and condition.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Baldwin Park

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it senses vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and shut down the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as required.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Baldwin Park

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures usually involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the failure. Correct vent slope and termination are checked before we leave.

    Water Heater Tank Leaks and Pressure Issues in Baldwin Park, CA

    While gas water heaters are robust, tank leaks or pressure problems can occur. We can often fix small fitting leaks or replace relief valves if pressure is too high. If the tank itself is failing due to rust or old age, a replacement might be the more economical long-term solution. We will provide an honest assessment of the repair options for your Baldwin Park, CA home.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Baldwin Park

    Rural properties often run water heaters on propane rather than natural gas. Propane units use different orifices and regulator pressures, and improper conversions cause sooting and poor performance. We confirm the correct parts are installed and repair pilots, valves and burners. We also check tank regulators for proper pressure.

    Pilot Light Repair in Baldwin Park

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most frequent gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and clean or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are completed in about an hour.
