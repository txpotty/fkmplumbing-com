    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Baldwin Park, CA

    Many older homes in Baldwin Park, CA still have original sewer lines made of clay, cast iron, or Orangeburg pipe, which are prone to degradation, cracking, and root intrusion over time. When we perform a sewer replacement, we often suggest upgrading to modern, durable materials like PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene).     Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Baldwin Park, CA

    Tree roots are a leading cause of sewer line damage, especially with older, more porous pipe materials like clay or cast iron. Our sewer replacement services specifically address root-damaged pipes, often recommending more resistant materials like PVC or ABS to stop future intrusions.     Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Baldwin Park

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Baldwin Park, CA

    Where applicable, we offer trenchless sewer repair methods like pipe bursting. This technique involves pulling a new pipe through the old one, simultaneously breaking the existing pipe apart.
