    Pressure Reducing Valve and Expansion Tank Pairing in Baldwin Park

    Many homes see high incoming pressure, so pressure reducing valves are widespread. The PRV protects your fixtures but also turns your plumbing into a closed system, which makes an expansion tank necessary. We test the PRV output and replace it if it is creeping or failing. Pairing a healthy PRV with a correctly charged expansion tank gives you stable pressure throughout the house.

    Expansion Tank Replacement in Baldwin Park, CA

    Expansion tanks wear out over time as the internal bladder or diaphragm fails and the tank fills with water. A waterlogged tank feels heavy, and tapping it produces a dull sound all the way up instead of a hollow top. Pressing the air valve and getting water instead of air is a sure sign it has failed. We replace failed tanks with a correctly sized new unit and check the precharge before installing.

    Replacing Old Expansion Tanks in Baldwin Park

    Expansion tanks do not last forever, and an old, waterlogged or corroded tank offers no protection. If your existing tank in Baldwin Park is showing signs of failure, such as a dripping relief valve or fluctuating water pressure, it is time for a new one. We expertly remove the old unit, dispose of it responsibly and install a new, correctly sized expansion tank. This timely replacement prevents costly damage to your water heater and plumbing system and restores proper pressure control.

    New Expansion Tank Installation in Baldwin Park

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve and no expansion tank, the water heater is probably seeing a pressure spike every heating cycle. We install a new tank on the cold water supply near the heater using a tee and short nipple. The air side is precharged to match your static pressure, usually between 50 and 70 psi. We then run the heater and watch the gauge to confirm pressure stays steady.
