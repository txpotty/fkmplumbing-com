    Water Heater Maintenance in Baldwin Park

    Regular water heater maintenance is crucial to preventing sediment buildup. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements.
