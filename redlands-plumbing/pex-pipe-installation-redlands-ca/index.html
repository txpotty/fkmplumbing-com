    Copper to PEX Conversions in Redlands

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Municipal water across much of Southern California is hard, leaving chalky scale on faucets, showerheads, and inside water heater tanks. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more usually follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and reduces the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Redlands

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the distinct advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, usually using crimp or compression fittings. Our plumbers in Redlands can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.
