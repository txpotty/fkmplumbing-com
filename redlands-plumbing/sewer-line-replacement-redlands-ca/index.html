    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Redlands

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows close behind. This usually needs only an entry pit and an exit pit instead of a long trench. It works well under driveways, mature trees and landscaping. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no seams for roots to enter.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Redlands

    Many older homes in Redlands still have sewer lines made from outdated materials like clay or cast iron, which are prone to cracking, corrosion, and root invasion. We offer upgrades to newer, more durable materials such as PVC or HDPE (high-density polyethylene). These materials stand up to corrosion, roots, and shifting soil, giving your sewer system a much longer lifespan.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Line Replacement in Redlands

    For significant damage or certain pipe layouts, traditional trenching may be the most effective method. This involves digging a trench to access and replace the damaged sewer line directly. Our team carefully plans the excavation to minimize disruption to your landscape, using the right equipment for efficient, precise digging.     Trenchless Sewer Line Repair & Replacement in Redlands, CA

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive alternative to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining installs a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe. Both methods greatly reduce the need for excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Redlands, CA while providing a long-lasting solution.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Redlands, CA

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the correct grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we account for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we close it up.

    Partial Sewer Line Repair in Redlands, CA

    Not every damaged sewer needs full replacement. If the camera shows a single break or one root-damaged joint, a spot repair may solve the problem. We locate the exact spot and depth, excavate a small area and replace that section. This costs much less than replacing the whole lateral. We only recommend it when the rest of the line is in sound condition.
