    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Redlands

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Redlands

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device.     Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Redlands, CA

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Redlands, CA, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers expertly install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.
