    Toilet and Fixture Leak Detection in Redlands, CA

    Toilets are one of the most common sources of wasted water. A worn flapper or a fill valve that never shuts off can leak quietly around the clock. We use dye tests to find leaking toilets and check faucets, showers and supply lines for drips. These fixes are typically quick and save water right away.

    Slab Leak Detection in Redlands

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often signaled by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. FKM Plumbing uses specialized acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is essential to preventing major foundation damage.

    Yard and Outdoor Leak Detection in Redlands

    Leaks in outdoor plumbing, irrigation systems or main water lines buried in your yard can lead to wasted water and landscaping damage. We use ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection to locate buried pipes and identify leaks without major digging. Our approach minimizes disruption to your lawn and garden, protecting your property's appearance. Detecting these outdoor leaks early can also prevent erosion and potential foundation issues caused by saturated soil, a serious concern for homes in Redlands, CA.
