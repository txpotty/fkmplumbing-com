    Electric tank water heaters are common in homes without natural gas service. We install models with the correct voltage and breaker size for your electrical panel.     Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Redlands

    Most homes still rely on traditional tank water heaters for their hot water needs. These units store and heat a large volume of water, making them a dependable choice for families.     Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Redlands

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, particularly in closed-loop systems. Code here requires seismic strapping on water heaters and low-flow fixtures, and inspectors check both closely on remodels. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to ensure proper system operation and prevent premature wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Redlands. This small component offers real long-term protection.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Redlands

    Gas tank water heaters provide strong recovery and are common in many local homes. We install 40, 50 and 75 gallon models with proper venting and combustion air. Each install includes a new gas connector, sediment trap and shutoff valve. We check draft and test for gas leaks. Gas units are a solid choice for families with high hot water demand.

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves.     Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Redlands

    The cost of water heater installation in Redlands, CA typically ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of water heater (tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any necessary plumbing or electrical modifications. We provide a written, upfront quote after assessing your particular needs.

    The average lifespan of a traditional tank water heater is about 10 to 15 years, while tankless water heaters can last 20 years or more with proper maintenance. Factors like local water quality, maintenance frequency and usage habits can all influence the actual lifespan of your unit. Regular flushing can help extend its life.

    Most standard water heater installations, particularly for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours. However, a conversion from a tank to a tankless system, or installations requiring major plumbing or electrical modifications, might take a full day or longer. We will provide an estimated timeline during our initial assessment.

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve or check valve, an expansion tank is typically required. It protects your water heater and pipes from pressure spikes.

    Many tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years, though hard water can shorten that. Regular flushing and anode rod checks help extend life.

    To improve efficiency, make sure your new water heater is properly sized for your home, consider a tankless model for on-demand heating, insulate hot water pipes and set the thermostat to 120°F (49°C) to prevent scalding and cut energy consumption.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. For tank units, a 40 to 50 gallon tank is common for 2 to 4 people, while larger families might need 60 to 80 gallons. For tankless units, it is about flow rate (GPM) and temperature rise. Our plumbers will assess your needs to recommend the ideal size.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from 6 to 12 years on the tank and parts. We also warranty our installation labor.
