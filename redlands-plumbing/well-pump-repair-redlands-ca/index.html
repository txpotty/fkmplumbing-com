    Jet Pump System Repair in Redlands, CA

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Typical problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. FKM Plumbing can reliably troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Redlands operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is crucial for jet pump function.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Redlands, CA

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Tree roots invade sewer lines during dry months, so the first big rain of the season often reveals a cracked or blocked lateral. Lightning and power surges frequently damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help prevent repeat failures.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Redlands

    Submersible pumps are common in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms usually include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use specialized equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, protecting the integrity of your well system.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Redlands, CA

    Many well pump issues come from electrical problems, including tripped circuit breakers, faulty wiring, or a malfunctioning control box. These can prevent the pump from starting or cause it to run erratically. Our licensed plumbers are also adept at electrical diagnostics, safely testing circuits, relays, and capacitors to identify and repair electrical faults. We ensure all wiring and connections meet Southern California electrical codes, providing a safe and reliable power supply to your well pump.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Redlands

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.
