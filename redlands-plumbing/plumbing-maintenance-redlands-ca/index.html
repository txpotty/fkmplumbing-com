    Water Heater Maintenance in Redlands

    Regular water heater maintenance is key to preventing sediment buildup. Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California install softeners or scale filters. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, improves efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.

    Drain Line Inspections for Maintenance in Redlands

    As part of comprehensive plumbing maintenance, we offer drain line camera inspections. Expansive clay and hillside soils can shift over time, putting stress on slab plumbing and cracking older sewer joints.     Backflow Preventer Testing in Redlands

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers call for annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where necessary. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Redlands

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get replaced. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility right away.

    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Redlands, CA

We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to early tank failure.
