    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Redlands

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs much less than a new system.

    Resin Bed Analysis and Regeneration in Redlands

    The resin bed is the heart of your water softener, where ion exchange occurs. During maintenance, we check the condition of the resin beads and confirm the regeneration cycles are programmed correctly for your household's water usage. Proper resin bed function is key to steady soft water throughout your home in Redlands.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Inspection in Redlands, CA

    Over time, sludge and insoluble materials can accumulate in your brine tank, hindering the softening process. Our maintenance includes thoroughly cleaning the brine tank, inspecting for any cracks or leaks and ensuring the float valve operates correctly. This step is essential for the efficient regeneration of your water softener in Redlands, CA.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Redlands, CA

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a complete regeneration every time.
