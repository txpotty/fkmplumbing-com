    Dependable Drainage Services in Santa Barbara, CA

    Main Sewer Line Clearing in Santa Barbara, CA

    A blocked main sewer line can cause widespread backups throughout your home, usually due to tree root intrusion or sediment buildup. Our team uses heavy-duty equipment, including hydro-jetting, to blast through tough obstructions and thoroughly clean your main sewer line. We can also inspect the line with a camera to identify the exact cause and location of the problem.

    French Drain Installation in Santa Barbara

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects water and carries it away from problem areas. It works well along foundations, behind retaining walls and in low spots that hold water after heavy rain. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so fine soil does not clog the system. The drain is sloped to an outlet, dry well or permitted discharge point.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Santa Barbara

    For the toughest clogs and stubborn buildup, our hydro-jetting service in Santa Barbara is a highly effective solution. This method uses high-pressure water streams to scour the interior of your pipes, removing years of accumulated grease, scale, and debris. It's an excellent preventive maintenance tool, especially for commercial kitchens or homes with persistent scale problems, leaving your pipes clean and free-flowing.

    Dry Well Installation in Santa Barbara, CA

    Where there is no place to discharge water on the surface, a dry well lets it soak into deeper soil. Dense clay or hardpan layers can block absorption, so we test the soil and dig through hardpan when needed. Some jurisdictions have rules on dry well registration and placement, and we follow them. A properly sized dry well with a sediment chamber can handle back-to-back heavy storms.

    Downspout and Roof Drain Solutions in Santa Barbara

    Gutters and scuppers concentrate a lot of roof water into a few points, usually right next to the foundation. We connect downspouts to buried solid pipe and run it to pop-up emitters or a dry well well away from the house. Most homes in Southern California sit on concrete slabs, so slab leaks often show up as warm floors, damp carpet, or an unexplained jump in the water bill. Moving that water away also stops erosion that eats away at walkways and landscaping.

    Drainage System Maintenance in Santa Barbara

    Proactive maintenance is key to avoiding costly drainage problems. Our drainage system maintenance services in Santa Barbara include routine inspections, preventive drain cleaning, and recommendations for addressing potential issues before they escalate. Regular maintenance helps extend the lifespan of your plumbing system, reduce the risk of unexpected repairs, and support optimal performance year-round.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Santa Barbara, CA

    When you have recurring drain problems, a sewer camera inspection is the best next step. We insert a flexible, waterproof camera into your pipes to visually diagnose issues like cracks, breaks, root intrusion, or significant blockages. This no-dig method allows for precise problem identification, saving you time and money on unnecessary digging.
