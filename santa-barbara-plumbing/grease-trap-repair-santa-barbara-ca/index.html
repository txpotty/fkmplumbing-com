    Flow Control Device Repair in Santa Barbara

    Hydromechanical traps rely on a flow control fitting to keep water moving at the rated speed. If this fitting is missing or clogged, the trap can be overwhelmed and grease escapes. We inspect, clean or replace flow control devices during repair visits. We also check the air intake that allows the device to work properly.     Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Santa Barbara

    Grease traps often clog due to excessive accumulation of fats, oils, and grease (FOGs) or food solids.     Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Santa Barbara

    When traditional snaking isn't enough, hydro-jetting is our go-to method for severe grease trap blockages. This powerful technique uses high-pressure water streams to thoroughly scour the interior of pipes and the grease trap itself, removing years of FOG buildup, scale, and debris. It's a highly effective, non-invasive method that not only clears clogs but also cleans the lines, significantly reducing the likelihood of future blockages in your Santa Barbara business.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Santa Barbara

    The baffles inside a grease trap are critical for separating FOGs from wastewater. If these baffles are corroded, broken, or dislodged, the trap's efficiency drops sharply, allowing grease into the sewer system. Our service includes inspecting the condition of all baffles and repairing or replacing them as needed, often using durable, corrosion-resistant materials. Proper baffle function is key to compliance with wastewater regulations in Santa Barbara.

    Damaged lids and failed gaskets let odors escape and create safety hazards. Outdoor risers can crack from vehicle traffic or ground movement.
