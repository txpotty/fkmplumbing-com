    Metered Softener Replacement in Santa Barbara

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly exhausted. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first month.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Santa Barbara

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore consistent brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the best salt type for your system.

    Water Softener Failure Diagnosis in Santa Barbara, CA

    Before replacing, we check whether your softener has a simple fix like a clogged injector, failed motor or wrong settings. If the valve is obsolete or parts are no longer available, replacement is usually the better value. We test hardness before and after the softener to confirm the problem. We explain the cost of repair versus replacement clearly. Call (213) 871-0701 to schedule a check.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Alternatives in Santa Barbara, CA

    Some homeowners prefer a salt-free scale reduction system that uses template-assisted crystallization media. These systems do not remove hardness minerals but change them so they are less likely to stick. They need no drain and no salt. We explain the differences honestly, since they work differently than true softeners. They can be a good fit when drain access is limited or sodium is a concern.
