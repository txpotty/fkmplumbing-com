    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Santa Barbara

    The cost of hydro jetting in Santa Barbara, CA can range from $300 to $800, depending on the severity of the clog, the length and diameter of the pipe, and whether camera inspection is required. More complex jobs, like removing extensive tree roots or cleaning long commercial lines, may be on the higher end. We provide a clear estimate after assessing your situation.

    Small consumer jetters exist, but they lack the pressure and flow of professional units. High-pressure water can also injure untrained users and harm fragile pipe.

    Drain snaking uses a cable with an auger that punches a hole through a blockage to restore some flow. Hydro jetting, however, uses high-pressure water to completely scour the inside of the pipe, removing all buildup and restoring the pipe to a near-new condition. Hydro jetting offers a more complete and longer-lasting clean.

    For most modern and well-maintained pipes, hydro jetting is very safe. However, for very old, corroded, or severely damaged pipes, the high pressure could cause further damage. That is why FKM Plumbing always performs a camera inspection first to assess your pipe's condition and confirm hydro jetting is appropriate.

    The duration of a hydro jetting service in Santa Barbara varies depending on the severity and location of the clog. A typical residential drain might take 1 to 2 hours, while a major sewer line or commercial job could take 3 to 4 hours or more. Our technicians work efficiently to minimize disruption.

    We use professional cart and trailer jetters along with rotating and root-cutting nozzles from well-known manufacturers. Color camera systems record the line before and after.

    Usually, a permit is not required for standard hydro jetting aimed at clearing blockages in existing lines. However, if the hydro jetting is part of a larger repair or replacement project that alters the plumbing system, a permit from the local building department may be necessary.
