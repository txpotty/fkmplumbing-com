    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Santa Barbara

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a solid option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer reliable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to verify a proper seal.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Santa Barbara

    Kitchen and bathroom remodels are a good time to replace old supply lines with PEX. We rough in new lines to the correct heights for vanities, showers, tubs and appliances, then pressure test before the walls are closed. Additions and guest suites get correctly sized lines tied into your existing system. We coordinate with your contractor to keep the project schedule on track.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Santa Barbara, CA

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, much like a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to balance cost and performance.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Santa Barbara

    When your main water line from the street to your home needs replacement, PEX is an smart option. Its flexibility allows for easier trenchless or minimal-dig installations, reducing disruption to your yard in Santa Barbara. PEX main lines handle ground movement and resist corrosion better than rigid pipe. Expansive clay and hillside soils can shift over time, putting stress on slab plumbing and separating older sewer joints.
