    Water Line Reroute in Santa Barbara

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This avoids breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for simple patching.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Santa Barbara, CA

    Leaks in drain lines under the foundation can be harder to detect because they are not under constant pressure. We use camera inspection and hydrostatic testing to locate cracks and separated joints. Repairs may involve tunneling, cutting the slab or pipe lining. We walk you through the trade-offs of each method.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Santa Barbara

    Newer homes often feature PEX or PVC plumbing, which, while durable, can still develop foundation leaks due to ground movement or improper installation. FKM Plumbing has the tools and expertise to repair leaks in these plastic piping systems. We use correct fusion and solvent welding techniques to make strong, leak-proof connections, restoring the integrity of your plumbing system beneath your Santa Barbara foundation.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Santa Barbara, CA

    Many older homes in Santa Barbara, CA have copper water lines beneath their foundations, which can corrode over time due to soil conditions or water chemistry. Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California add softeners or scale filters. When these copper pipes develop pinhole leaks or cracks, they need specialized repair. Our technicians fix or replace sections of damaged copper piping, providing a durable seal that withstands pressure and ground movement and helps prevent future leaks.

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Santa Barbara, CA

    When the leak is in an accessible spot and the rest of the pipe is in good condition, direct repair is often the most affordable choice. We cut a small opening in the floor, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The soil is compacted and the concrete is patched to match. Flooring can then be restored by your flooring contractor.

    Basement Wall Sealing and Waterproofing in Santa Barbara, CA

    For homes with basements in Santa Barbara, CA, wall leaks can be a real concern. We offer complete basement waterproofing services, including interior and exterior sealing. This may involve applying waterproof coatings, sealing cracks with polyurethane or epoxy, or setting up interior drainage systems. These measures stop water from penetrating porous concrete and masonry, keeping your basement dry and healthy.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Santa Barbara

    Slab leaks are common in homes built on concrete foundations, often caused by shifting soil or corroded pipes underneath. Our technicians use non-invasive methods like electronic leak detection and hydrostatic pressure testing to find these elusive leaks. Once located, we can perform targeted repairs, either by slab tunneling or direct access, limiting disruption to your home. We work with many pipe materials, including copper, PEX, and PVC, ensuring a durable repair for your Santa Barbara property.

    Foundation Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Santa Barbara, CA

Reroutes cost a bit more but avoid concrete work and future leaks in the abandoned line. A full repipe is the most complete option for homes with multiple leaks or aging pipe.
