    FKM Plumbing installs high-quality expansion tanks from reputable brands like Watts, Amtrol and Zurn. We select brands known for durability and performance so your system gets the best protection. Our plumbers can recommend the best option for your water heater and plumbing setup.

    When water heats up, it expands. Without an expansion tank, this expanded water has nowhere to go, increasing pressure within your closed plumbing system. This constant pressure can prematurely wear out water heater components and relief valves and even cause leaks or ruptures. The expansion tank absorbs this excess volume, protecting your entire system.

    While some homeowners attempt DIY installation, it is strongly recommended to have a professional plumber install your expansion tank.
