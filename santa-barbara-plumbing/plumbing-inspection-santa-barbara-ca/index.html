    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Santa Barbara, CA

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test angle stops and the main shutoff valve, which often seize in hard water. We also check for proper venting and gurgling drains. Small fixes like these are easy to plan for once you know they exist.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Santa Barbara

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a strong sign of a hidden leak. We test the meter, check pressure loss and inspect for warm spots, damp baseboards and mildew. On slab homes we can follow up with acoustic and thermal equipment to pinpoint leaks. Catching a slab leak early reduces damage to floors and foundations.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Santa Barbara, CA

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. We run a high-resolution camera from a cleanout to the city connection or septic tank. The video shows offsets, bellies, roots and breaks along with their depth and location. You keep a copy of the recording for your records.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Santa Barbara
