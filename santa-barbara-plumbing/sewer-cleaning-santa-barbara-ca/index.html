    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Cleaning in Santa Barbara

    Main sewer cleaning in Santa Barbara typically costs $200 to $500 with a cable machine from an accessible cleanout. Hydro jetting a sewer line typically runs $400 to $900, and a camera inspection adds about $150 to $350.

    Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to fully clean the inside of your sewer pipes, removing grease, sludge, and roots. It is used for stubborn blockages, preventative maintenance, or when traditional snaking is not sufficient to restore full pipe diameter.

    In most cases the homeowner owns the lateral from the house to the city main or property line. Your city utility can tell you where its responsibility begins.

    Usually, standard home insurance policies do not cover routine sewer cleaning or damage from gradual wear and tear. However, if a sudden, unexpected event causes sewer damage, it might be covered. It is best to check with your insurance provider directly.

    Homes with no issues may never need it. Lines with known roots or grease buildup often do well with cleaning every 12 to 24 months.

    Common signs include multiple slow drains throughout your home, gurgling noises from toilets or drains, foul odors coming from drains, and sewage backing up into sinks, tubs, or toilets. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a professional.

    While minor drain clogs can sometimes be handled with DIY solutions, main sewer line blockages require professional tools and expertise. Trying to clear a sewer line without proper equipment can aggravate the problem or damage your pipes.
