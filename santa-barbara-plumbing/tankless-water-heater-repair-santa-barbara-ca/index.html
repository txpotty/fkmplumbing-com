    Ignition Failure Repair in Santa Barbara, CA

    If your tankless unit will not fire, the cause may be a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. We test each component and measure gas pressure at the inlet. Undersized gas lines are a frequent hidden cause when a tankless unit replaced a tank. We correct the issue so the unit lights reliably.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Santa Barbara

    Tankless heaters rely on flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace failed parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are correct.

    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Santa Barbara

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Santa Barbara
