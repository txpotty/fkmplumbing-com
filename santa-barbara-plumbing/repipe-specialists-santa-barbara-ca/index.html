    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Santa Barbara, CA

    Polybutylene pipes, installed in many homes between the 1970s and 1990s, are notorious for premature failure due to chemical reactions with disinfectants in the water supply. If your home in Santa Barbara, CA has polybutylene, a full replacement is essential to prevent major water damage. FKM Plumbing offers specialized polybutylene plumbing replacement, carefully removing the old, brittle pipes and installing a reliable new system.

    Copper Repiping in Santa Barbara, CA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Santa Barbara, CA

    Many older homes in Santa Barbara, CA still have galvanized steel pipes, which are vulnerable to corrosion, rust, and mineral buildup. This leads to low water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. Our team specializes in the complete removal and replacement of these outdated systems with modern PEX or copper piping. This upgrade not only resolves existing issues but also greatly improves water quality and flow, boosting your home's value and plumbing reliability for decades to come.

    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Santa Barbara, CA

    Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California install softeners or scale filters. Water in many areas contains minerals that cause scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can accelerate corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral buildup, supporting a longer-lasting plumbing system.

    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in Santa Barbara

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more complex. We coordinate with HOAs, schedule water shutoffs and protect neighboring units. For property managers we offer building-wide repipes done in phases to keep residents in place. Each unit gets clear communication and daily cleanup.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Santa Barbara, CA

    Repiping requires small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures. Our crews cut clean openings, protect surrounding areas and patch every hole when the plumbing is done. We match common textures like knockdown and orange peel. Your walls are ready for paint when we leave.
