    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Santa Barbara

    Pedestal sump pumps are an excellent choice for narrow or shallow sump pits, as their motor sits above the water level. This design makes them easier to maintain and can often offer a longer lifespan because the motor stays out of the water. FKM Plumbing can properly install pedestal sump pumps in your Santa Barbara home, with the right sizing and setup for effective water removal without crowding your pit.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Santa Barbara

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by finding the low point where water collects. We saw cut the slab, excavate a pit roughly 18 to 24 inches wide and 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a perforated or solid liner depending on whether we are collecting groundwater or surface seepage. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor remains clean and level.

    Residential Basement Sump Pump Installation in Santa Barbara

    Protecting your family home from water damage is our top priority. We install sturdy residential sump pump systems sized to your basement and the rainfall Santa Barbara actually gets. We also ensure the discharge line directs water far away from your foundation.
