    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Vista, CA

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are more difficult to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know where to listen.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Vista, CA

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of recurring slab leaks. We can typically complete a full repipe in two to four days.

    Electronic Leak Detection in Vista

    For elusive leaks, our electronic leak detection equipment sends a signal through your pipes and measures changes that indicate a breach. This technology allows us to locate leaks in many pipe materials, including copper, PEX and PVC, commonly found in Vista homes. It helps prevent exploratory digging, saving you time and money.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Vista

    A slab leak that is flooding floors or causing water to bubble up through tile needs fast attention. We respond quickly, help you shut off the water and isolate the leaking side if possible. That often lets you keep partial water service until the repair. Then we locate and fix the leak.

    Non-Invasive Leak Detection in Vista, CA

    Our primary goal is to locate your slab leak with the least amount of disruption possible. This approach saves your home in Vista, CA from unnecessary damage, preserves your landscaping and reduces the overall cost and time of the repair process.

    Leak Detection for Older Southern California Homes in Vista, CA

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Vista

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak safely or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.
