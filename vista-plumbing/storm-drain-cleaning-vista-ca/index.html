    Downspout and Roof Drain Lines in Vista, CA

    Many homes connect downspouts to buried pipe that carries water away from the foundation. These lines clog with roof granules, leaves and nests, then back up and dump water at the wall. We jet them clear and test with a hose to verify flow at the outlet. For flat roofs with scuppers, we check that the discharge path is open too. Keeping water away from the slab helps protect against soil movement and settling.

    Emergency Storm Drain Unclogging in Vista

    Storm Drain Camera Inspection in Vista

    Before and after cleaning, or to diagnose the cause of persistent blockages, FKM Plumbing offers storm drain camera inspection in Vista. Using waterproof cameras, we can see the interior of your storm drain lines to identify the exact nature and location of blockages, assess pipe condition, and check for structural damage like cracks or collapses. This non-invasive diagnostic tool gives precise problem identification and lets us provide the most effective and targeted cleaning or repair solutions.

    Yard and Area Drain Cleaning in Vista

    Yard drains and area inlets collect runoff from lawns, rock landscaping and patios. Over time the grates fill with leaves and the pipes fill with silt and small gravel. We pull the grates, vacuum the inlet box and jet the pipe toward its outlet. This restores the line so it can handle a full storm. We also check that grates are secure and level.

    Storm Drain Cleaning Cost Versus Water Damage in Vista, CA

    A routine storm drain cleaning costs a small fraction of what it takes to dry out a flooded garage or repair a damaged slab. Many homeowners only call after water has already gotten in. Cleaning once a year, ideally before the rainy season, prevents nearly all problems. If a pipe is crushed or disconnected, we can locate it with a camera and repair just that section. We give you clear pricing for both cleaning and repairs.

    Root Removal from Storm Drains in Vista

    Tree roots are a common cause of severe storm drain blockages in Vista, especially in older neighborhoods with mature trees. Roots seek out moisture and can work into drain pipes through tiny cracks or loose joints, growing and eventually completely blocking water flow. FKM Plumbing uses specialized root-cutting attachments on our drain snakes and hydro jetting equipment to cut through and remove root intrusions. After removal, we can recommend further steps to prevent future root problems.

    Dry Well Cleaning and Inspection in Vista, CA

    Dry wells let storm water soak into the ground instead of flowing to a street. They are found on commercial sites, HOA common areas and some residential lots. Silt slowly seals the settling chamber and the drainage rock, slowing how fast water drains. We vacuum the chamber, inspect the inlet and outlet, and check drain-down time. If the dry well no longer drains properly, we explain options for repair or replacement.
