    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Vista

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Vista

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are often required for high-hazard applications in Vista. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. FKM Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties.
