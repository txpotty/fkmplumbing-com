    Brine Tank Replacement in Vista

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore consistent brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the right salt type for your system.

    Emergency Water Softener Replacement in Vista

    While not always an emergency, a sudden water softener failure can cause immediate issues with hard water. If your system leaks significantly or stops working entirely, FKM Plumbing can provide prompt emergency water softener replacement in Vista. We move quickly on urgent situations to minimize disruption and protect your home from hard water damage.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Vista, CA

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more cost effective than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes more sense.

    Traditional Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Vista

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and proven solution for hard water in Vista. These systems use ion exchange to remove calcium and magnesium minerals, which are well known for causing scale buildup. We replace old, inefficient salt-based units with modern, high-efficiency models that use less salt and water. Our plumbers confirm proper brine tank installation and regeneration settings for strong performance.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Vista

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water around the clock. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but prevent hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Vista

    Restaurants, salons and small businesses rely on soft water to protect dishwashers, espresso machines and water heaters. We replace commercial softeners sized to flow rate and daily usage. Larger valves and multiple tanks may be needed to meet demand. We schedule installs to minimize downtime. We can also add filtration ahead of the softener if needed.
