    Copper Repair vs Repipe Cost in Vista, CA

    A single copper leak repair is affordable and fast. If you have had two or more pinhole leaks in a year, the copper is likely thinning throughout the system. At that point a PEX or Type L copper repipe can cost less over time than repeated repairs and water damage. We help you compare both options with real figures.

    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Vista, CA

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original workmanship, thermal expansion or vibration. A leaking joint often shows a white or green crust around the fitting. We clean, reheat and resolder the joint or replace the fitting entirely if it is corroded. The result is a clean, fully sweated connection that holds pressure.

    Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Vista, CA

    Hot water lines see more corrosion than cold lines because heat speeds up chemical reactions. Recirculation pumps that run constantly can also wear copper from the inside. We repair leaking hot lines and can adjust or add timers to your recirculation system. That reduces wear and lowers energy use.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Vista

    Pinhole leaks are tiny holes that form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher. We cut out the affected section and replace it with new Type L copper or a PEX transition. While the wall is open, we inspect nearby pipe for discoloration or thinning.

    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Vista, CA

    A burst copper pipe is a plumbing emergency that requires immediate attention, especially during hard freezes. Mountain and high desert communities in Southern California do get hard freezes, so pipes there need insulation and proper winter care. When temperatures drop sharply, water inside pipes can freeze and expand, causing them to rupture. FKM Plumbing offers fast response for burst copper pipe repair in Vista, CA, limiting water damage to your home. We quickly isolate the leak, remove the damaged section and replace it with new copper piping, restoring your system to full function and protecting it against future freezes.
