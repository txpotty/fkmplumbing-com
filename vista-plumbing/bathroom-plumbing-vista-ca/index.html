    Common signs include a running toilet, weak flush, water pooling around the base, or a gurgling sound from other drains when flushed. These often indicate issues with the fill valve, flapper, or a partial clog in the drain line.

    Common signs include dripping faucets, running toilets, slow or clogged drains, reduced water pressure in showers, strange noises from pipes, and visible leaks around fixtures or under sinks. Any of these symptoms call for a professional inspection to prevent further damage.

    Low water pressure can come from several issues, including mineral buildup in pipes or showerheads, a failing pressure regulator, leaks in your plumbing system, or even issues with your water heater.
