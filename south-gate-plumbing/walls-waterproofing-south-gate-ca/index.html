    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in South Gate

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most reliable method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Expansive clay and hillside soils can shift over time, putting stress on slab plumbing and cracking older sewer joints. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also integrated to divert water away.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in South Gate

    Crawl space encapsulation involves sealing the crawl space entirely with heavy-duty vapor barriers and often includes a dehumidifier. This not only waterproofs the walls but also controls humidity, preventing mold growth and wood rot in the crawl space. Intense sun breaks down exposed plastic pipe, sprinkler parts, and exterior fittings, so they wear out faster than indoor plumbing. An encapsulated crawl space can significantly improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency by keeping moisture from moving up into the home. We use tough, reinforced poly sheeting for walls and floors, sealed at all seams and penetrations.
