    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Laguna Niguel

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit typically costs $600 to $1,500. Installing a new pit with slab cutting and excavation often runs $1,500 to $4,000. Battery backups typically add $500 to $1,500.

    A simple replacement usually does not need a permit. New pits, electrical work or discharge connections may require one, and we will let you know.

    FKM Plumbing installs a variety of reputable sump pump brands known for their durability and performance. Our common selections include Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty Pumps. We can recommend the best brand and model to suit your particular needs and budget for your Laguna Niguel home.

    It's recommended to have your sump pump inspected and cleaned annually, ideally before the rainy season. This maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning the pit and testing the discharge line to ensure it's free of obstructions. Regular checks prevent surprise failures.

    Most manufacturers offer warranties from 3 to 5 years, and some premium models carry longer coverage. We also stand behind our installation workmanship.

    A submersible sump pump sits inside the sump pit, making it quieter and more powerful, well suited for heavy use. A pedestal sump pump has its motor above the pit, making it easier to access for maintenance and often more affordable, though it can be louder.

    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods may fail sooner, so regular testing is important.

    While DIY sump pump installation is possible, it is not recommended.
