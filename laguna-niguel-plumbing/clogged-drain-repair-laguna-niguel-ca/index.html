    Common signs of a clogged drain include slow drainage, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets, foul odors coming from your pipes, and water backing up into sinks, tubs, or showers. If multiple drains are affected, it often indicates a main line issue.

    A plunger or a hand-held hair snake can handle many simple tub and sink clogs. If the clog returns, affects more than one fixture or does not respond to a plunger, call a plumber. Forcing a cable can crack old pipes or scratch finishes.

    While chemical drain cleaners are readily available, FKM Plumbing generally advises against them for homes in Laguna Niguel. They often provide only temporary relief, can damage pipes over time, and pose environmental and health risks. Professional tools like augers or hydro-jetting are safer and more reliable.
