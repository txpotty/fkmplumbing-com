    Pressure Tank Sizing and Upsizing in Laguna Niguel

    Many older systems have a tank that is too small for the pump, so the motor starts every time someone washes their hands. Tank size is based on drawdown, which is the usable water stored between cut-out and cut-in pressure. For example, a 10 gallon per minute pump usually needs at least 10 gallons of drawdown for a one minute run time. That often means an 80 to 119 gallon tank rather than the 20 gallon unit that came with the home. Upsizing is one of the most effective ways to extend pump life.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Laguna Niguel

    Flex-Lite composite pressure tanks are an good option for those seeking a lightweight, corrosion-resistant alternative to traditional steel tanks. They are especially well-suited for homes with aggressive water conditions that might accelerate corrosion in metal tanks. FKM Plumbing expertly replaces old or failing tanks with Flex-Lite models, ensuring a precise fit and proper connection to your existing well system.     Constant Pressure System Conversion in Laguna Niguel, CA

    A constant pressure system pairs a variable speed drive like the Franklin SubDrive or Grundfos SQE with a smaller pressure tank. The pump speeds up or slows down to hold a set pressure, so showers do not fade when a sprinkler kicks on. These systems cost more, often $2,500 to $5,000, but they eliminate pressure swings. They also reduce hard starts on the motor. We explain whether your well and pump are good candidates.
