    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Laguna Niguel, CA

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can loosen those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is in charge of the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve in the same visit.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Laguna Niguel, CA

    A full break can flood a yard, wash out soil under walkways and leave your home without water. Our emergency crews shut off the supply, find the break and set up a temporary bypass when the repair will take more than a few hours. Most emergency breaks are repaired the same day. We also document the damage with photos for your insurance or HOA.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Laguna Niguel

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off quickly in an emergency.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Laguna Niguel, CA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option more and more used in homes across Laguna Niguel, CA. While PEX is less prone to leaks than older materials, damage can still occur from improper installation, rodent chewing, or impact. Our team is fully experienced in PEX repair, using specialized crimping tools and fittings to make strong, leak-proof connections. We ensure your PEX system continues to perform optimally.

    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Laguna Niguel, CA

    Many older homes in Laguna Niguel, CA still have galvanized steel water lines, which are prone to corrosion, rust, and reduced water flow over time. While replacement is often the best long-term solution, we can provide targeted repairs for individual leaks in galvanized pipes where practical. Our plumbers are comfortable working with these older systems, carefully assessing the pipe's condition and recommending the most effective repair strategy to extend its life or plan for future replacement.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Laguna Niguel

    Copper pipes are known for their durability and long life, but they can still develop leaks due to corrosion, excessive water pressure, or physical damage. In Laguna Niguel, we often see pinhole leaks in older copper lines. Our experienced plumbers are adept at repairing copper water lines, whether it involves soldering a small patch or replacing a damaged section. We ensure all repairs meet current plumbing standards for a reliable, leak-free connection.

    Main Water Line Repair in Laguna Niguel

    The main water line brings water from the municipal supply to your home. When this line leaks or breaks, it can cause significant water loss, foundation damage, and low water pressure. Our team focuses on diagnosing and repairing main water line issues, from pinpoint leaks to full pipe ruptures, so your home's primary water source is secure. We use advanced detection methods to pinpoint the exact location of the problem, limiting excavation and disruption to your property in Laguna Niguel. We also check for common issues like root intrusion or shifting soil.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Laguna Niguel, CA

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a quick spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades.
