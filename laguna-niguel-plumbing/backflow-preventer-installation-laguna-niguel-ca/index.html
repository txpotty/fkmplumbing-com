    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Laguna Niguel

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.

    Pool and Spa Fill Backflow Protection in Laguna Niguel, CA

    An auto-fill line or hose connection on a pool or spa creates a direct path for chlorinated water to reach the supply.     Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Laguna Niguel

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Laguna Niguel. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. FKM Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with trusted brands like Watts and Febco.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer Installation in Laguna Niguel

    The cost for backflow preventer installation in Laguna Niguel, CA can range from about $300 to $1,500, depending on the type of device required, such as a DCVA or an RPZ, the complexity of the installation and any changes needed to existing plumbing. Commercial installations with larger devices may cost more. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after an on-site assessment.

    Most residential installs take two to four hours including testing. Large commercial assemblies can take a full day, especially if piping must be reconfigured.

    In Laguna Niguel, most backflow preventers must be tested annually by a certified backflow tester to confirm they are working correctly and providing adequate protection. This is a separate service from installation but is essential for ongoing compliance and safety.

    We install Watts, Febco, Wilkins by Zurn and Apollo assemblies. Each has readily available repair kits, which keeps future service affordable.

    Backflow is when water flows the wrong way through a pipe and carries contaminants into the clean water supply. It can happen from back siphonage during a pressure drop or back pressure from pumps and other equipment.

    An RPZ, or reduced pressure zone device, provides the highest level of protection and is used for high-hazard applications where contaminants could be toxic. A DCVA, or double check valve assembly, is used for low-hazard applications where contaminants are non-toxic. The choice comes down to the risk of contamination at the cross-connection point.

    Most residential backflow preventer installations can be completed within 2 to 4 hours. More complex commercial systems or installations requiring significant pipe changes might take longer. We always aim to complete the work efficiently while keeping our quality standards high.
