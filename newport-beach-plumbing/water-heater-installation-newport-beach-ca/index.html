    Expansion Tank Installation in Newport Beach, CA

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and pipes.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Newport Beach, CA

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from surrounding air, which can make them very efficient in warm garages and utility rooms. We install models from Rheem and A.O. Smith with proper condensate drainage. These units need adequate air space and clearance. They may qualify for utility rebates or tax credits. We help you decide if a hybrid is right for your home.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Newport Beach, CA

    Tank water heater installations usually cost less upfront than tankless systems. Gas tankless installs can cost more because of gas line upgrades and venting. Attic installs, relocations and code upgrades may add to the price. We provide a written quote that includes the unit, labor, permits and haul-away. We also compare long-term energy costs so you can make an educated choice.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Newport Beach, CA

    Electric tank water heaters are common in homes without natural gas service. We install models with the correct voltage and breaker size for your electrical panel. These units do not need venting, which allows more flexible placement. We also install a drain pan when the heater is in a closet or attic. Electric models are simple, dependable and quiet.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Newport Beach

    The cost of water heater installation in Newport Beach, CA usually ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of water heater (tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any necessary plumbing or electrical modifications. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your particular needs.

    New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. FKM Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, giving you peace of mind. We will discuss exact warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    In most areas, a plumbing permit is required for water heater replacement or installation to ensure the work meets local building and safety codes. Plumbing contractors here must hold a state contractor license, and permits are typically required for water heaters, repiping, and sewer work. FKM Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and inspections in Newport Beach as part of our service.

    An expansion tank absorbs excess water pressure created as water heats up inside the tank. Many homes in Newport Beach, CA, especially those with closed-loop plumbing systems, are required by code to have an expansion tank to protect the water heater and plumbing system from damage.

    A traditional tank water heater typically lasts between 8 and 12 years with proper maintenance. Tankless water heaters often have a longer lifespan, ranging from 15 to 20 years, thanks to their durable components and on-demand operation.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. For tank units, a 40 to 50 gallon tank is common for 2 to 4 people, while larger families might need 60 to 80 gallons. For tankless units, it is about flow rate (GPM) and temperature rise. Our plumbers will assess your needs to recommend the right size.

    The average lifespan of a traditional tank water heater is about 10 to 15 years, while tankless water heaters can last 20 years or more with proper maintenance. Factors like local water quality, maintenance frequency and usage habits can all affect the actual lifespan of your unit. Regular flushing can help extend its life.

    To improve efficiency, ensure your new water heater is properly sized for your home, consider a tankless model for on-demand heating, insulate hot water pipes and set the thermostat to 120°F (49°C) to prevent scalding and reduce energy consumption.
