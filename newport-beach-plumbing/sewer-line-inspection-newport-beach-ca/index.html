    Belly and Offset Detection in Newport Beach, CA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds water and waste. Offsets happen when pipe sections shift out of alignment, often because of soil movement. Expansive clay and hillside soils can shift over time, putting stress on slab plumbing and separating older sewer joints. The camera shows these defects clearly, and we mark their location for targeted repair.
