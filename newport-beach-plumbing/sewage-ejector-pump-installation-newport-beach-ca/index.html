    Pump replacements usually take two to four hours. New systems with floor excavation and venting typically take one to two days.

    Regular maintenance for a sewage ejector pump involves periodically checking the basin for debris, confirming the check valve works correctly and testing the float switch.     Avoid flushing wipes, paper towels, feminine products, grease and cat litter. These items clog pumps and cause early failure.
