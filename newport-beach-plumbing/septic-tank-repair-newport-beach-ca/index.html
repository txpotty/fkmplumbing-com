    Septic Tank Alarm Troubleshooting in Newport Beach

    Many modern septic systems, especially those with pumps or advanced treatment units, include alarms to warn of high water levels or system malfunctions. If your septic alarm is sounding in Newport Beach, it is a clear sign of trouble. FKM Plumbing technicians can accurately troubleshoot the cause of the alarm, whether it is a float switch issue, pump failure or a clogged line, and perform the necessary repairs to silence the alarm and restore system balance.

    Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Newport Beach

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field fast. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where appropriate.

    Drain Field Clog Remediation in Newport Beach

    A clogged drain field is a frequent cause of septic system backups and can be a significant issue for homeowners in Newport Beach. This can be caused by excessive solids, tree root intrusion or soil compaction. We offer professional drain field remediation, which may include hydro-jetting the distribution lines, addressing root infiltration or assessing the soil's percolation capacity to restore proper effluent absorption and prevent future flooding.

    Septic Inlet Line Repair in Newport Beach

    The line from your house to the tank can crack, sag or separate from soil movement and roots. Symptoms include gurgling drains and backups even after pumping. We camera the line to find the problem and repair or replace the damaged section. A cleanout near the tank makes future maintenance easier.

    Alternative Septic System Repair in Newport Beach, CA

    Some properties use aerobic treatment units or other alternative systems because of soil or lot size limits. These systems have air pumps, timers and alarms that need routine service. We repair aerators, replace diffusers and troubleshoot control panels. We also help keep maintenance records called for by your permit.
