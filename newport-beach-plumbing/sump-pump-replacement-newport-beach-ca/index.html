    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Newport Beach

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump System Installation in Newport Beach

    Tree roots invade sewer lines during dry months, so the first big rain of the season often exposes a cracked or blocked lateral. Power outages during severe storms can leave standard sump pumps useless. Our battery backup sump pump system installation in Newport Beach provides an important layer of protection. We add a secondary, battery-powered pump that automatically activates if the primary power fails, keeping flood protection running even without electricity. This is a valuable upgrade for many homes.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Newport Beach

    If your current sump pump struggles to keep up with heavy water flow, especially during intense thunderstorms, it may be undersized.
