    The duration of a gas leak repair varies greatly. Simple repairs might take an hour or two, while complex issues involving excavation or extensive pipe replacement could take a full day or more. We aim for efficient service while putting safety and thoroughness first.

    Utilities lock meters when they find a leak or unsafe condition. A licensed contractor must repair the problem and pass a pressure test before service can be turned back on.

    The most common signs of a gas leak include a distinct smell of rotten eggs or sulfur, a hissing or whistling sound near a gas line, visible damage to a gas pipe or dead vegetation over an underground gas line. Indoor symptoms might also include dizziness, nausea or fatigue. If you notice any of these, evacuate and call for help right away.

    CSST is flexible stainless steel gas tubing used in many homes. It is safe when installed and bonded correctly, and we check bonding as part of our repair work.

    Hissing near gas lines, dead patches of landscaping over a buried line, bubbles in standing water and higher gas bills can all indicate a leak. Some people also notice headaches or dizziness.

    A carbon monoxide detector does not detect natural gas leaks. Natural gas has no smell of its own, so an additive gives it a rotten egg odor, while carbon monoxide is an odorless, colorless gas that results from incomplete combustion. While a carbon monoxide detector is essential for safety, it won't alert you to a gas leak. You need to rely on your sense of smell or a dedicated gas leak detector.
