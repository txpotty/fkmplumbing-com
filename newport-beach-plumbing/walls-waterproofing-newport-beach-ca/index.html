    Foundation Crack Repair in Newport Beach

    Even small cracks in your foundation walls can allow water to seep into your basement or crawl space.     Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Newport Beach, CA

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to confirm the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test confirms the pan is watertight before tile.

It's an affordable way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Newport Beach

    The cost of walls waterproofing in Newport Beach, CA can vary widely, typically ranging from $3,000 for interior crack sealing to $15,000 or more for extensive exterior foundation waterproofing with drainage systems. Factors like the size of the area, severity of water damage, chosen methods, and accessibility all influence the final price. We provide detailed, upfront quotes after a thorough inspection.

    Walls waterproofing is generally not recommended as a DIY project due to its complexity and the high importance of a sound foundation. It often involves heavy excavation, specialized equipment, precise material application, and knowledge of drainage principles and local building codes. Improper waterproofing can lead to more severe and costly damage down the line, so professional help is highly advisable.

    Interior waterproofing addresses water after it has entered the foundation, typically managing it with sealants and drainage systems inside the basement or crawl space. Exterior waterproofing prevents water from reaching the foundation walls in the first place, usually by excavating and applying membranes or coatings to the outside of the foundation.

    For extensive walls waterproofing projects involving excavation, structural changes, or major changes to drainage systems, a permit from the city of Newport Beach may be required. Simple crack repairs usually do not need a permit. FKM Plumbing will help you determine if a permit is necessary for your project and assist with the application process if needed.

    Walls waterproofing is a key step in preventing future mold growth by eliminating the moisture source. However, it does not remove existing mold. If mold is already present, it will need to be professionally remediated before or along with the waterproofing process. Once the area is dry and mold-free, waterproofing will help make sure it stays that way.

    Damp proofing is a less durable solution, typically a spray-on asphalt coating that resists soil moisture but isn't designed to withstand hydrostatic pressure. Waterproofing uses stronger membranes, coatings, or drainage systems built to prevent liquid water from entering, even under pressure. For basements or crawl spaces in areas with high water tables or heavy rainfall, waterproofing is essential.

    When professionally installed using high-quality materials, walls waterproofing can last for decades, often 20 to 50 years or even longer. The lifespan depends on the materials used, the quality of installation, and environmental factors like soil movement and water exposure. Modern membranes and sealants are designed for lasting durability.

    A shower wall waterproofing project usually takes two to four days, including drying time between coats. Exterior block sealing can often be completed in a day. Larger foundation jobs may take several days.
