    Push-Button and Click-Clack Drain Installation in Newport Beach, CA

    Many homeowners switch to push-button drains to get rid of the lift rod entirely. These drains open and close with a press on the stopper. We confirm your sink has an overflow or choose a non-overflow version, then seal and test it. They are easy to clean and work well with modern faucets.

    Hard-Water Drain Damage Repair in Newport Beach, CA

    Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California add softeners or scale filters. Hard water builds scale inside pop-up bodies and on stopper rods, and over time this freezes the mechanism or snaps the pivot rod. We remove scaled parts and install new drains with smoother materials that resist buildup. We can also suggest softening options to protect the new parts.

    Corroded Drain Pipe Replacement in Newport Beach, CA

    Older homes in Newport Beach often have galvanized steel or cast iron drain pipes that are prone to internal corrosion and rust, leading to leaks and reduced flow.
