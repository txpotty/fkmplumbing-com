    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Newport Beach

    Our technicians rely on highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab. This method involves listening for the subtle sound of escaping water, even through concrete.     Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Newport Beach, CA

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most affordable repair. We cut a small opening in the floor at the marked spot, repair the pipe and pressure test it. Then we patch the concrete and leave the area ready for flooring.     Slab Leak Reroutes in Newport Beach

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is popular when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Newport Beach, CA

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to see temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a distinct thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area quickly without any physical disruption to your property in Newport Beach.
