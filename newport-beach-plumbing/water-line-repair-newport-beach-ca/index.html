    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Newport Beach, CA

    Many older homes in Newport Beach, CA still have galvanized steel water lines, which are prone to corrosion, rust, and reduced water flow over time. While replacement is often the best long-term solution, we can provide targeted repairs for individual leaks in galvanized pipes where feasible. Our plumbers are comfortable working with these older systems, carefully assessing the pipe's condition and recommending the most effective repair strategy to extend its life or plan for future replacement.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Newport Beach

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process usually takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a long manufacturer warranty.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Newport Beach

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off fast in an emergency.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Newport Beach

    For some water line repairs, traditional trenching can be avoided. Trenchless repair methods, like pipe bursting or pipe lining, allow us to fix or replace underground pipes with minimal disruption to your landscaping, driveway, or foundation. This method is often faster and less intrusive than traditional digging, making it a popular choice for homeowners in Newport Beach looking to preserve their property. We'll assess whether your water line issue is a good candidate for trenchless technology.

    Underground Leak Detection in Newport Beach, CA

    Many water line leaks occur underground, making them difficult to detect without specialized equipment. Our technicians use advanced acoustic and thermal imaging tools to accurately locate hidden leaks without unnecessary digging. Early detection saves you money on water bills and prevents expensive damage to your foundation. Most homes in Southern California sit on concrete slabs, so slab leaks often show up as warm floors, wet carpet, or an unexplained jump in the water bill. Once located, we provide a clear repair plan, so the problem is resolved efficiently for your property in Newport Beach.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Newport Beach

    Copper pipes are known for their durability and long life, but they can still develop leaks due to corrosion, excessive water pressure, or physical damage. In Newport Beach, we often see pinhole leaks in older copper lines. Our experienced plumbers are adept at repairing copper water lines, whether it involves soldering a small patch or replacing a damaged section. We ensure all repairs meet current plumbing standards for a reliable, leak-free connection.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Newport Beach, CA

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a simple spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with actual information.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Newport Beach, CA

    A full break can flood a yard, wash out soil under walkways and leave your home without water. Our emergency crews shut off the supply, find the break and set up a temporary bypass when the repair will take more than a few hours. Most emergency breaks are repaired on the same day. We also document the damage with photos for your insurance or HOA.
