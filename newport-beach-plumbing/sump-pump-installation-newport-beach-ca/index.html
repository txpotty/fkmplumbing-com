    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Newport Beach

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit usually costs $600 to $1,500. Installing a new pit with slab cutting and excavation often costs $1,500 to $4,000. Battery backups typically add $500 to $1,500.

    While DIY sump pump installation is possible, it is not recommended. Professional installation by FKM Plumbing ensures your system works effectively, preventing costly water damage in your Newport Beach home.

    To keep your new sump pump running efficiently, test it monthly by pouring water into the pit to ensure it activates. Once a year, inspect the discharge pipe for obstructions, clean the pit and check the float switch. Regular professional maintenance from FKM Plumbing can extend its lifespan in your Newport Beach home.

    A well-maintained sump pump usually lasts between 7 and 10 years. The lifespan can vary depending on the quality of the pump, how frequently it runs and how well it is maintained. Regular inspections and cleaning can help extend its life.

    Strange noises, constant running, failure to turn on and visible rust are common signs. Testing your pump before storm season helps catch problems early.

    A submersible sump pump sits inside the sump pit, making it quieter and more powerful, ideal for heavy use. A pedestal sump pump has its motor above the pit, making it easier to reach for maintenance and often more affordable, though it can be louder.

    In many cities, a plumbing permit is usually required for new sump pump installations or major replacements. This confirms the work meets local building and plumbing codes. FKM Plumbing handles all necessary permitting in Newport Beach, CA to make sure your installation is fully compliant.
