    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Newport Beach

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Newport Beach can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Newport Beach

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Newport Beach, CA

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.
