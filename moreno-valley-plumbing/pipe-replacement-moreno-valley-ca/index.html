    Copper Pipe Replacement in Moreno Valley, CA

    Copper piping is a long-standing, reliable choice known for its durability and resistance to corrosion. While it's a premium material, its longevity often makes it a cost-effective solution over time. FKM Plumbing offers expert copper pipe replacement for sections or entire homes in Moreno Valley, CA. Our plumbers are skilled in the precise soldering and installation techniques required for copper, ensuring a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that holds up season after season.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Moreno Valley, CA

    Many older homes in Moreno Valley, CA still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to internal corrosion over time. This corrosion chokes water flow, reduces water pressure, and can introduce rust particles into your drinking water. Our pipe replacement service targets these corroded sections, replacing them with new, non-corrosive materials like PEX or copper. This restores proper water flow and ensures clean, clear water throughout your home.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Moreno Valley, CA

    Cast iron drain pipe in older homes can rust through at the bottom, while some ABS from the 1980s was made with defective resin that cracks. Signs include sewer odors, slow drains and stains on ceilings below upstairs bathrooms. We camera the line first, then replace reachable sections with PVC or ABS. Under-slab drains can be tunneled, rerouted or lined depending on location. We explain the options with the camera footage in hand.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Moreno Valley, CA

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep coming back. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you compare the cost of both paths.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Moreno Valley

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack without warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when listing the home.
