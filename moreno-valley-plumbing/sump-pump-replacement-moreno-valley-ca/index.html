    We install a variety of trusted sump pump brands known for their reliability and performance, including Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty Pumps and Little Giant. Our plumbers can help you choose the best brand and model for your home's requirements and budget.

    A battery backup sump pump system is a secondary pump powered by a battery, designed to activate automatically if your primary sump pump loses power during an outage or fails. This is important for maintaining flood protection during power outages caused by severe weather.

    To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by FKM Plumbing can also help extend its lifespan.

    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods can fail earlier due to stuck switches.

    It is a good idea to test your sump pump at least every three months, and especially before and after rainy seasons. To test, simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit.
