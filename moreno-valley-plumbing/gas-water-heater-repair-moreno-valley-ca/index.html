    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Repair in Moreno Valley

    The cost of gas water heater repair in Moreno Valley, CA can vary a lot depending on the issue. Minor repairs like a thermocouple replacement might range from $150 to $350, while more complex issues such as a gas valve replacement could be $400 to $800 or more. We always provide a detailed, upfront estimate once we diagnose the problem.

    Look out for a lack of hot water, inconsistent water temperature, a pilot light that won't stay lit, unusual noises like rumbling or popping, a rotten egg smell, which can point to a gas leak, or visible leaks around the tank. Any of these call for a professional inspection.

    A thermocouple is a safety device in a gas water heater that senses if the pilot light is lit. If it doesn't detect heat, it shuts off the gas supply to prevent gas leaks. A faulty thermocouple is a leading reason for a pilot light not staying lit and is a critical safety component.

    Replacing a part like a thermocouple or gas valve typically does not. Replacing the water heater or altering gas piping or venting often requires a permit, which we take care of.

    Our technicians are experienced in repairing all major gas water heater brands, including but not limited to Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith, State and Noritz. We have access to parts for a wide variety of models and manufacturers.

    In most cases, minor repairs like replacing a thermocouple or a thermostat do not require a permit.
