    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Moreno Valley, CA

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing badly damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is excellent for completely replacing a pipe segment with a new, stronger material, such as HDPE. It's often chosen when the existing pipe is collapsed or too damaged for lining. FKM Plumbing uses specialized bursting heads and winches for a smooth and efficient replacement, reducing the overall project time and cost for homeowners in Moreno Valley, CA.

    Sectional Point Repair in Moreno Valley

    When damage is confined to a specific section of pipe, sectional point repair is an effective trenchless solution. We target only the damaged area, installing a patch or liner to seal the leak or reinforce the pipe. This method is cost-effective and minimizes the scope of work, which suits isolated issues in Moreno Valley without affecting the entire line.

    Pipe Bursting in Moreno Valley, CA

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, fracturing it outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind. The new pipe is fused into one continuous length with no joints. Bursting requires an entry and exit pit but avoids digging the full length.

    We use liners and resins from established manufacturers such as Perma-Liner and similar systems rated for sewer use. We choose materials based on pipe size and condition.

    Most cracked, rooted or separated pipes can be lined. Pipes that are fully collapsed or badly bellied may need bursting or traditional repair. A camera inspection tells us which method fits.

    Trenchless pipe repairs, especially CIPP lining, are designed to be extremely durable and long-lasting. The new pipe created inside the old one can have a lifespan of 50 years or more, often exceeding the expected life of the original piping. These repairs are resistant to corrosion, root intrusion, and chemical damage.

    CIPP (cured-in-place pipe) lining involves inserting a new liner inside the existing pipe to create a pipe within a pipe. Pipe bursting, on the other hand, involves breaking apart the old pipe while pulling a new pipe into its place, which leaves you with a completely new pipe.

    A correctly installed epoxy liner is designed to last 50 years or more. HDPE pipe used in bursting has a similar long service life.
