    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Replacement in Moreno Valley

    Sewer replacement usually costs $4,500 to $12,000 depending on length, depth and method. Trenchless pipe bursting often runs $6,000 to $15,000, while under-slab work can cost more.

    Trenchless replacements often take one day. Open-trench jobs usually take two to four days.

    Modern PVC and HDPE pipes have tight joints that resist roots. Proper installation significantly reduces risk.

    Frequent backups, slow drains throughout the house, sewage odors and soggy yard areas are typical signs. Camera inspection verifies the problem.

    We provide a written labor warranty on every sewer replacement. HDPE and PVC pipe also carry long-term manufacturer warranties, and a correctly installed line can last 50 years or more.

    After replacement, avoid planting large trees or shrubs near the sewer line's path. Be mindful of what goes down your drains, and avoid flushing grease, wipes, and harsh chemicals. Regular professional inspections, especially if you have older pipes, can also help catch potential issues early.

    Trenchless sewer replacement, such as pipe bursting, is a great option for many, but not all, situations in Moreno Valley. If the pipe is completely collapsed, severely misaligned, or made of certain materials like Orangeburg, traditional trenching might be the only viable solution.

    Sewer line replacement is a complex and dangerous job that should only be performed by licensed and experienced professionals.
