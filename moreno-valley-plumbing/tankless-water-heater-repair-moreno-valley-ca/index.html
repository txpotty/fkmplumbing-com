    Ignition Failure Repair in Moreno Valley, CA

    If your tankless unit will not fire, the cause may be a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. We test each component and measure gas pressure at the inlet. Undersized gas lines are a common hidden cause when a tankless unit replaced a tank. We correct the issue so the unit lights every time.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in Moreno Valley

    Many tankless systems use internal or external recirculation to deliver hot water faster. Pump failures, check valve problems or wrong schedule settings can cause long waits or lukewarm water. We test the pump, check valves and controls and set the schedule to match your routine. A properly tuned system saves both water and gas.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Moreno Valley

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside the heat exchanger, limiting flow and causing overheating. Many units warn of this with scale codes, such as the LC code on Rinnai models. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves and run a descaling solution through the exchanger. Annual descaling is wise for most homes on hard water.

    Tankless Repair vs Replacement Cost in Moreno Valley, CA

    Descaling service typically costs $175 to $350, and sensor or igniter repairs run $250 to $600. Heat exchanger replacement can cost $800 to $1,500 or more unless covered by warranty.
