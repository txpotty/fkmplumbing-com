    Leak Detection and Repair in San Bernardino, CA

    Leaks from your garbage disposal can lead to serious water damage under your sink or even affect your cabinets.     Leaking Disposal Repair in San Bernardino, CA

    Leaks can come from the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube or the disposal body itself. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty, and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed. In that case, replacement is the better option.

    Noisy or Grinding Disposal Repair in San Bernardino

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove debris. If the bearings are failing, the noise will continue. In that case, we can quote a less noisy replacement unit.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in San Bernardino

    A humming disposal that will not spin almost always has a jam. Bones, fruit pits, glass or utensils can wedge the flywheel. We cut power, use a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit to free the flywheel and remove the object. Then we test the motor and press the reset button when needed.

    Electrical Issues & Motor Repair in San Bernardino

    If your garbage disposal hums but doesn't grind, or doesn't turn on at all, it could be an electrical issue or a motor problem. We check circuit breakers, reset buttons, and internal wiring. For motor failures, we determine if a repair is feasible or if replacement is the more cost-effective solution, always keeping your safety first in San Bernardino.
