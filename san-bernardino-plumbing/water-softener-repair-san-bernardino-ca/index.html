    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in San Bernardino, CA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates suction to draw brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. When it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying. We clean or replace the injector and screen and check the brine line for leaks.

    Water Softener Leak Detection & Repair in San Bernardino, CA

    Leaks around your water softener can be caused by cracked tanks, loose fittings, faulty O-rings, or pressure issues. Even a small leak can lead to water damage and wasted water. Our team uses specialized tools to quickly locate the source of any leak, whether it's a slow drip or a significant pool. We then perform careful repairs, from tightening connections and replacing seals to patching cracks or replacing damaged components, protecting your home from water intrusion.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in San Bernardino

    A softener using far too much salt or water often has settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units may regenerate on a schedule regardless of use. We retest hardness, reprogram the valve or upgrade the head to a metered control. Proper settings can significantly cut salt and water use.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in San Bernardino, CA

    Resin Bed Replacement & Regeneration Issues in San Bernardino

    Over time, the resin beads inside your water softener can become fouled, damaged, or depleted, reducing their ability to remove hardness minerals. If your system is regenerating but still delivering hard water, the resin bed may be the cause. We can test the resin, flush the system to remove iron or sediment buildup, or perform a complete resin replacement. This process restores your softener's core function, ensuring effective hard water treatment for years to come.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in San Bernardino

    A common issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to poor regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and ensuring the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering precise repairs or replacements.
