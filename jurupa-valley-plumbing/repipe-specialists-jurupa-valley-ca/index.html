    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Jurupa Valley, CA

    Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California add softeners or scale filters. Water in many areas contains minerals that cause scale buildup inside pipes over time. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral buildup, supporting a longer-lasting plumbing system.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Jurupa Valley

    Most PEX repipes for Southern California homes cost $5,000 to $12,000, depending on size, bathrooms and access. Copper repipes typically run 20 to 40 percent more.

    Repiping is a complex plumbing project that requires specialized tools, knowledge of plumbing codes, and significant experience. It's highly recommended to hire professional repipe specialists like FKM Plumbing to make sure the job is done correctly, safely, and to code.

    A documented repipe can make your home easier to sell and insure, especially when replacing polybutylene or Kitec. Buyers and inspectors notice it.

    While some minor wall and ceiling access is usually necessary for a repipe, our repipe specialists use techniques that limit damage.     We provide a written workmanship warranty, and PEX manufacturers such as Uponor offer long-term warranties when certified installers use their system. We explain the details before we start.

    Both PEX and copper are strong choices for repiping in Southern California, each with distinct advantages.     Common signs you need repiping include persistent low water pressure throughout your home, discolored or rusty-looking water, frequent pipe leaks, visible corrosion on exposed pipes, and a metallic taste or odor in your water.
