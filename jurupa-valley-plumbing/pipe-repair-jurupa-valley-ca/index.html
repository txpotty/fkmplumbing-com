    Pinhole Leak Repair in Jurupa Valley

    Pinhole leaks form when copper pipe wall thins from the inside, often due to water chemistry, high velocity or poor grounding. They start as tiny drips that stain drywall or ceilings. We cut out the affected section and replace it with new pipe or a PEX transition. If we find several pinholes in the same area, we will talk with you about repiping that zone.

    Leaky Pipe Repair in Jurupa Valley, CA

    Even a small leak can lead to mold growth, wood rot and higher water bills over time. FKM Plumbing provides precise leaky pipe repair services for all types of plumbing systems in Jurupa Valley. We use advanced leak detection equipment, including acoustic sensors and thermal imaging, to pinpoint the exact source of the leak without unnecessary demolition. Once located, we offer targeted repairs, from patching minor pinhole leaks to replacing corroded sections of pipe, for a watertight seal and lasting reliability.

    Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Jurupa Valley

    Drain pipes made of ABS, PVC or cast iron can crack, separate or leak at joints. A leaking drain often shows up as a stain below a bathroom or a musty smell in a cabinet. We locate the leak, replace the damaged section and test the drain under full flow. We also check for proper slope and venting while the line is exposed.

    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Jurupa Valley

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in older homes and rusts from the inside until flow drops and leaks start at threaded joints. Repairs are possible, but every disturbed fitting can start a new leak. We replace failing sections with PEX or copper using dielectric unions to stop galvanic corrosion. For homes with widespread galvanized piping, we will quote a full repipe so you can weigh both options.
