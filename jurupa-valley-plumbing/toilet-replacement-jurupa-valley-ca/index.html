    Municipal water across much of Southern California is hard, leaving chalky scale on faucets, showerheads, and inside water heater tanks. Hard water can lead to mineral buildup in toilet tanks and bowls, affecting flush mechanisms and causing stains. When replacing a toilet, we can recommend models with surfaces designed to fight mineral buildup and discuss water softening options.

    We frequently recommend and install reliable brands like American Standard, Kohler, Toto, and Delta. These brands offer a range of durable, water-efficient models that perform well across a wide range of water conditions and are known for their longevity.

    Key signs include persistent leaks that are hard to pinpoint or fix, a cracked toilet bowl or tank, frequent clogs despite proper use, excessive rocking or instability, or a toilet that is very old and inefficient, using too much water per flush.
