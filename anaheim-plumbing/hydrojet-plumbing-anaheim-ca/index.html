    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydrojet Plumbing in Anaheim

    The cost of hydrojet plumbing in Anaheim, CA can vary, typically ranging from $300 to $800, depending on the severity and location of the clog, as well as the length and diameter of the pipe.     Jetting removes the roots inside the pipe, but roots will grow back if the joint or crack remains open. Regular jetting can control them for years. Pipe lining or repair is the lasting fix.

    Snaking involves using an auger to punch a hole through a clog, often leaving behind some debris. Hydrojetting, on the other hand, uses high-pressure water to thoroughly scour the entire inner surface of the pipe, removing buildup and leaving it clean. Hydrojetting provides a much more comprehensive and longer-lasting solution.

    FKM Plumbing uses professional hydrojetting equipment from reputable manufacturers known for their power and reliability, such as Spartan Tool and MyTana. These professional-grade machines ensure we can deliver high quality, effective drain cleaning services for our clients in Anaheim.

    When performed by a trained professional, hydrojetting is generally safe for most types of pipes, including older cast iron or PVC. However, severely damaged, corroded, or fragile pipes might not withstand the high pressure. This is why FKM Plumbing always recommends a pre-service camera inspection to assess pipe integrity before proceeding with hydrojet plumbing.

    The duration of hydrojet plumbing depends on the complexity of the clog and the length of the pipe being cleaned. Most residential hydrojetting jobs in Anaheim can be completed within 1 to 3 hours, but more severe or extensive blockages, especially those involving main sewer lines or heavy root intrusion, may take longer.
