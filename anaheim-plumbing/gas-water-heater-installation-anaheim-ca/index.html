    Gas Line Upgrades for Water Heaters in Anaheim, CA

    Upgrading to a larger or tankless gas water heater may require more gas flow. We calculate total BTU demand and pipe length to size the line correctly. We install black iron or CSST piping and bond it where required. A properly sized line prevents low flame and weak performance. We pressure test new lines before service.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Anaheim

    Atmospheric vent water heaters rely on natural draft to move exhaust through a metal flue. They are common in older homes and are often the most affordable gas option. We inspect the existing vent for damage, proper slope and clearance. If the vent is undersized or corroded, we replace it. Proper draft is essential for safety.

    Standard Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Anaheim

    Many homes in Anaheim use traditional tank-style gas water heaters due to their reliability and cost-effectiveness. Our service includes professional removal of your old unit, precise gas line connection, proper venting setup and thorough testing to ensure optimal function.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Anaheim, CA

    Power vent water heaters use a fan to push exhaust through PVC or other approved vent pipe. This allows installation in places without a chimney or vertical flue. Power vent units need an electrical outlet nearby. We install the vent run to manufacturer specs and check for proper termination. These units offer versatile placement options.

    Propane Water Heater Installation in Anaheim

    Homes in rural areas often use propane instead of natural gas. Propane water heaters require dedicated burner orifices and gas pressure settings. We install propane models or convert units with manufacturer kits where allowed. We coordinate with your propane supplier when needed. Every connection is leak tested before use.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Anaheim

    Direct vent water heaters draw combustion air from outside and vent exhaust through a sealed system. They are a good choice for tight utility rooms or interior closets. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall with proper clearances. Many direct vent models do not need electricity. They offer safe, efficient operation.
