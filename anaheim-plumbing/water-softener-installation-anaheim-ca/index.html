    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Anaheim

    Accurate testing is essential for proper softener settings. We test your water for hardness, iron and chlorine before installation. Then we program regeneration frequency and salt dosage. This helps reduce salt use while keeping water soft. We review settings with you so you understand how the system works.

    Water Softener Replacement in Anaheim, CA

    Older softeners can lose efficiency due to worn resin, failing valves or clogged injectors. We remove the old unit and install a new, more efficient model. Newer metered valves regenerate only when needed, saving salt and water. We check the loop and drain line during replacement. A new softener can restore like-new performance.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Anaheim, CA

    Pelican Water Systems offer a variety of water treatment solutions, including both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners. If you've chosen a Pelican system for your Anaheim, CA home, FKM Plumbing can provide expert installation.     Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Anaheim

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. FKM Plumbing carefully installs various models of salt-based softeners, making sure of proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Anaheim home.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Anaheim

    Reverse osmosis systems remove dissolved solids, which can be high in some water supplies. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet. Some systems also connect to refrigerators or ice makers. RO pairs well with a softener for complete water treatment. We change filters on a schedule so the system keeps working well.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Anaheim, CA
