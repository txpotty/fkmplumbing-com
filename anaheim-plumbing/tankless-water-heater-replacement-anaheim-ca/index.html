    Tankless vs. Tank Water Heater Conversion in Anaheim, CA

    Thinking about converting from a traditional tank water heater to a tankless system in your Anaheim, CA home? This is a common and worthwhile upgrade, but it requires specialized expertise. We handle the entire conversion, which can involve rerouting gas lines, upgrading electrical service, modifying water lines and installing new venting. Our team ensures a smooth transition, so you can enjoy the long-term benefits of a modern, efficient tankless system.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Anaheim

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by recovering heat from exhaust gases. They vent through low-cost PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Intense sun breaks down exposed plastic pipe, sprinkler parts, and outdoor fittings, so they wear out faster than indoor plumbing. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Anaheim, CA

    Converting from a 40 or 50 gallon tank to tankless frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss in a garage or closet. The catch is that tankless units usually need a larger gas line, often three quarter inch or bigger, plus new venting. We check your meter size and pipe run before quoting so there are no surprises mid-job. We also cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer used. Expect a conversion to take a full day.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Anaheim

    The cost of tankless water heater replacement in Anaheim, CA usually ranges from $3,000 to $6,000, depending on the unit's capacity, brand, complexity of installation, such as converting from a tank model, and any needed electrical or gas line upgrades. This price usually includes the unit itself and labor. We provide a written, upfront quote after a thorough assessment of your home.

    A direct swap usually takes four to six hours. A tank to tankless conversion with new gas and venting typically takes a full day.

    We install Navien, Rinnai, Noritz, Takagi and Rheem tankless heaters. Each has its own strengths, and we recommend based on your flow needs, venting options and budget.

    Well-maintained tankless units can last 15 to 20 years. In hard water areas without regular descaling, heat exchangers can fail much sooner. Annual flushing makes the biggest difference.

    With proper installation and regular maintenance, a new tankless water heater usually lasts 20 to 25 years. This is much longer than traditional tank water heaters, which usually last 10 to 15 years. Regular flushing and descaling can help extend the life of your tankless unit, especially in areas with hard water.

    New tankless water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty, which usually covers the heat exchanger for 10 to 15 years and parts for 3 to 5 years. FKM Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work for your peace of mind. We will discuss warranty details for your chosen unit during your consultation.

    DIY tankless water heater replacement is generally not recommended due to the complexity of gas lines, high-voltage electricity, water connections and venting requirements. Improper installation can lead to serious gas leaks, carbon monoxide poisoning, water damage or electrical hazards. It is best to hire a licensed and experienced plumber like FKM Plumbing.
