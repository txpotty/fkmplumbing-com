    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Anaheim

    The cost of water heater installation in Anaheim, CA usually ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of water heater (tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed plumbing or electrical modifications. We provide a written, upfront quote after assessing your specific needs.

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve or check valve, an expansion tank is usually required. It protects your water heater and pipes from pressure spikes.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 6 to 12 years on the tank and parts. We also warranty our installation labor.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. For tank units, a 40 to 50 gallon tank is common for 2 to 4 people, while larger families might need 60 to 80 gallons. For tankless units, it is about flow rate (GPM) and temperature rise. Our plumbers will assess your needs to recommend the right size.

    To improve efficiency, ensure your new water heater is properly sized for your home, consider a tankless model for on-demand heating, insulate hot water pipes and set the thermostat to 120°F (49°C) to prevent scalding and cut energy consumption.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for water heater replacement. We pull permits when required and confirm the install meets code.

    The decision to repair or replace often depends on the age of your unit, the cost of the repair and its energy efficiency. If your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down, or the repair cost is more than 50% of a new unit, replacement is often the more cost-effective choice. Newer models also offer much better energy efficiency.

    Common signs that you might need a new water heater include a lack of hot water, rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit and an age of 10 to 15 years or more. If you notice any of these issues, it is best to have a professional inspection.
