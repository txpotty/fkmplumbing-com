    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s.     PEX Pipe Replacement in Anaheim

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a common choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Mountain and high desert communities in Southern California do get hard freezes, so pipes there need insulation and careful winter care. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during sudden cold snaps. FKM Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers strong performance and water quality for your home.
