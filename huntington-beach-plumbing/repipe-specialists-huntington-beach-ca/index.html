    Both PEX and copper are strong choices for repiping in Southern California, each with distinct advantages. Copper is highly durable, has a longer lifespan, and naturally inhibits bacterial growth, but costs more. We can discuss which material best suits your budget and needs.

    While fixing individual leaks might seem cheaper in the short term, it's often a temporary solution for an aging plumbing system.     Common signs include discolored or rusty water, low water pressure at multiple fixtures, frequent leaks or pinhole leaks, and strange noises coming from your pipes.
