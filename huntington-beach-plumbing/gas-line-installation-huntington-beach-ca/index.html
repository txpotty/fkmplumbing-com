    Gas Water Heater and Tankless Line Upgrades in Huntington Beach, CA

    Tankless water heaters often need up to 199,000 BTU, far more than the 40,000 BTU a typical tank heater uses. This upgrade is one of the most important steps in a successful tankless conversion.
