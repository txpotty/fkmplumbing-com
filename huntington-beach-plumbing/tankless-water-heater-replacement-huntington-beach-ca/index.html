    The cost of tankless water heater replacement in Huntington Beach, CA usually ranges from $3,000 to $6,000, depending on the unit's capacity, brand, complexity of installation, such as converting from a tank model, and any needed electrical or gas line upgrades.     A standard tankless water heater replacement, if you are upgrading from an existing tankless unit, typically takes 4 to 8 hours. If you are converting from a traditional tank water heater, the process can take longer, possibly a full day, due to additional plumbing, gas or electrical changes required for proper installation and venting.

    With proper installation and regular maintenance, a new tankless water heater typically lasts 20 to 25 years. This is significantly longer than traditional tank water heaters, which usually last 10 to 15 years. Regular flushing and descaling can help extend the life of your tankless unit, especially in hard water areas.

    We install Navien, Rinnai, Noritz, Takagi and Rheem tankless heaters. Each has strengths, and we recommend based on your flow needs, venting options and budget.

    Tankless water heater replacement is a complex job that involves gas lines, electrical wiring, water connections and venting. Professionals also ensure code compliance and warranty validity.

    DIY tankless water heater replacement is usually not recommended due to the complexity of gas lines, high-voltage electricity, water connections and venting requirements.
