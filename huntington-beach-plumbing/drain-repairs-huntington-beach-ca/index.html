    The length of drain repairs depends entirely on the issue. A simple clog can often be cleared in an hour or two. More involved repairs, like replacing a section of a main sewer line, might take a full day or even several days, especially if permits or extensive digging are involved.

    We work with all standard drain pipe materials found in Southern California homes, including PVC, ABS, cast iron and copper. Our repairs and replacements use high-quality, code-compliant materials from trusted manufacturers for durability and longevity.

    Minor fixture drain repairs usually do not require a permit. Replacing drain stacks, relocating drains or adding new lines often does, depending on the city. We handle permits when they apply.

    It can. Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California install softeners or scale filters.     Hydro-jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of pipes, thoroughly removing stubborn clogs, grease buildup and even tree roots. It's usually used when traditional snaking isn't enough, or for preventive maintenance on main sewer lines, especially in older homes with buildup issues.
