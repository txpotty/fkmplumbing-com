    Pipe Bursting Services in Huntington Beach, CA

    For badly damaged or collapsed pipes, pipe bursting offers a full replacement solution. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, breaking apart the old pipe at the same time. This provides a completely new, durable pipe, often HDPE (high-density polyethylene), which resists corrosion and leaks. It's an effective long-term fix for extensive issues in Huntington Beach, CA.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Huntington Beach

    CIPP lining is a popular trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is excellent for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Huntington Beach.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Huntington Beach, CA

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing badly damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is excellent for completely replacing a pipe segment with a new, stronger material, such as HDPE. It's often chosen when the existing pipe is collapsed or too damaged for lining. FKM Plumbing uses specialized bursting heads and winches for a smooth and efficient replacement, reducing the overall project time and cost for homeowners in Huntington Beach, CA.

    Lateral and Spot Lining in Huntington Beach

    Not every pipe needs to be lined end to end. When damage is limited to a few joints or a short section, we can install a short sectional liner. This costs less than a full-length liner and is completed quickly. We verify the rest of the line is sound before recommending a spot liner.
