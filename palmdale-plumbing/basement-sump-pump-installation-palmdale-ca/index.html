    New Sump Pit Excavation in Palmdale

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by locating the low point where water collects.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Palmdale, CA

    Power outages can happen, especially during severe weather, and they leave your primary sump pump useless. Tree roots invade sewer lines during dry months, so the first big rain of the season often reveals a cracked or blocked lateral.     Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Palmdale

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which saves wear on the motor. On an occasional cold night, covering backflow preventers and outdoor valves is usually all it takes to avoid a break. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the discharge.
