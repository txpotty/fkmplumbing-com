    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Palmdale, CA

We specialize in assessing and upgrading waterproofing systems for these properties, often combining exterior excavation with interior drainage solutions.     Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Palmdale

    Even minor cracks in foundation walls can allow serious water intrusion. Our services include crack repair using epoxy or polyurethane injection to seal these entry points for good. Addressing these early prevents further water damage and costly future repairs.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Palmdale

    Many homes in Palmdale have crawl spaces, which are very prone to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting stops ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an affordable way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Palmdale, CA

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already penetrated the foundation walls. This usually involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Palmdale

    Crawl space encapsulation involves sealing the crawl space entirely with heavy-duty vapor barriers and often includes a dehumidifier. This not only waterproofs the walls but also controls humidity, stopping mold growth and wood rot in the crawl space. Hot inland summers push water heaters and outdoor plumbing hard, while coastal salt air can corrode exposed metal fittings. An encapsulated crawl space can noticeably improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency by keeping moisture from moving up into the home. We use tough, reinforced poly sheeting for walls and floors, sealed at all seams and penetrations.
