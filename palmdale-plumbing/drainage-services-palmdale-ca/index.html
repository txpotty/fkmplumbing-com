    French Drain Installation in Palmdale

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects water and carries it away from problem areas. It works well along foundations, behind retaining walls and in low spots that hold water after storms. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so fine soil does not clog the system.
