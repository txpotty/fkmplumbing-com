    Water Leak Detection in Walls & Ceilings in Indio, CA

    Water leaks behind walls or in ceilings can go unnoticed for long periods, leading to extensive drywall damage, wood rot and mold. We use non-invasive methods like moisture meters, thermal cameras and borescopes to precisely pinpoint the source of these leaks.     Pool and Spa Leak Detection in Indio

    Summer heat and wind can evaporate a lot of pool water, so it can be hard to tell normal loss from a leak. A bucket test compares evaporation to actual water loss. We then pressure test plumbing lines and use dye testing around lights, skimmers and fittings. Finding the leak protects the pool shell and the soil around it.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection in Indio, CA

    Sewer line leaks can cause foul odors, slow drains and potential health hazards. Our team uses camera inspections to visually identify breaks, cracks or blockages within your sewer lines. We address sewer leaks in all types of piping materials common in Indio homes, from PVC to cast iron.

    Irrigation and Yard Leak Detection in Indio, CA

    Drip and sprinkler systems are a common source of hidden water loss in many yards. A cracked lateral or a stuck valve can waste thousands of gallons before anyone notices. We test the irrigation system separately from the house and trace leaks along buried lines. Many water providers recommend checking irrigation first when bills spike in summer.

    To prevent future leaks, consider regular plumbing inspections, especially for older homes. Keep water pressure in a safe range, address any signs of corrosion and maintain your plumbing system. For homes with known issues, proactive repiping or pipe upgrades can be a lasting solution.

    Turn off all water in the house and watch the meter. If the leak indicator or dial moves, water is escaping somewhere. A sudden jump in your water bill is another clear sign.

    A slab leak is a leak in the water pipes embedded within or beneath your home's concrete slab foundation. Most homes in Southern California sit on concrete slabs, so slab leaks often show up as warm floors, wet carpet, or an unexplained jump in the water bill. Soil that shifts and settles strains plumbing lines, and temperature swings cause pipes to expand and contract over the years.

    Common signs include a sudden spike in your water bill, damp spots on walls or floors, mildew odors, visible mold growth, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, or a constantly running water meter even when all water is off.
