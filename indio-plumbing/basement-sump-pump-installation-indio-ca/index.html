    Sump Pump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Indio

    A complete sump pump system requires a properly installed basin and discharge line. We also install the discharge line, making sure it is correctly sloped, sealed and routed away from your foundation to a suitable drainage area, so water does not find its way back into your Indio property.

    Sump Pump Pit Installation in Indio, CA

    A well-built sump pit is fundamental to an effective sump pump system. If your basement lacks a suitable pit or needs an upgrade, FKM Plumbing provides expert sump pit installation.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Indio

    Power outages during heavy storms can render a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump provides vital protection when the power goes out.     Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Indio

    Pedestal sump pumps are an good choice for narrow or shallow sump pits, as their motor sits above the water level. This design makes them easier to maintain and can sometimes offer a longer lifespan because the motor stays out of the water. FKM Plumbing can properly install pedestal sump pumps in your Indio home, with the right sizing and setup for effective water removal without crowding your pit.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Indio

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by finding the low point where water collects. We saw cut the slab, excavate a pit roughly 18 to 24 inches wide and 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a perforated or solid liner depending on whether we are collecting groundwater or surface seepage. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor stays clean and level.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Indio, CA

    Submersible sump pumps are designed to sit directly in the sump pit, submerged in water, which makes them quieter and less intrusive. They are strong and effective for basements that take on a lot of water. Our team in Indio, CA will install your submersible pump with precision, ensuring proper sealing, discharge piping and electrical connections for quiet, reliable operation, which matters most in finished basements.
