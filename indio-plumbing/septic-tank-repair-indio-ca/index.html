    Alternative Septic System Repair in Indio, CA

    Some properties use aerobic treatment units or other alternative systems because of soil or lot size limits. These systems have air pumps, timers and alarms that need routine service. We repair aerators, replace diffusers and troubleshoot control panels. We also help keep maintenance records called for by your permit.

    Septic Tank Lid and Riser Repair in Indio

    Cracked or deteriorated concrete lids are a real safety hazard. We replace failing lids and install risers that bring access up to grade. Risers make pumping and inspection quicker and cheaper over time. Secure, child-resistant lids are standard on our installations.

    Septic Inlet Line Repair in Indio

    The line from your house to the tank can crack, sag or separate from soil movement and roots. Symptoms include gurgling drains and backups even after pumping. We camera the line to locate the problem and repair or replace the damaged section. A cleanout near the tank makes future maintenance easier.

    Drain Field Clog Remediation in Indio

    A clogged drain field is a common cause of septic system backups and can be a serious issue for homeowners in Indio. This can be caused by excessive solids, tree root intrusion or soil compaction. We offer professional drain field remediation, which may include hydro-jetting the distribution lines, addressing root infiltration or assessing the soil's percolation capacity to restore proper effluent absorption and prevent future saturation.

    Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Indio

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field quickly. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where appropriate.

    Effluent Pump Repair in Indio, CA

    Homes with uphill or distant fields often use an effluent pump in a pump tank. Failed pumps, stuck floats or tripped breakers trigger high water alarms. We test the pump, float switches and control panel and replace failed parts. A working alarm gives you warning before sewage comes back into the house.

    Septic Pipe Repair and Replacement in Indio, CA

    Damaged pipes leading to or from your septic tank can cause backups, leaks and foul odors. These pipes, often made of PVC or cast iron, can crack due to shifting soil, root invasion or age. Earthquakes are a constant consideration in Southern California, and seismic straps and flexible connectors help keep water and gas lines intact during shaking. FKM Plumbing offers complete pipe repair and replacement services, including excavation, precise pipe fitting and backfilling, protecting the integrity of your entire septic system in Indio, CA.

    Septic Pump and Alarm System Repair in Indio

    Many modern septic systems, especially those on sloped properties or with advanced treatment units, depend on a working septic pump and alarm. When your pump fails or the alarm sounds, it signals a serious issue. Our team can repair or replace faulty pumps, floats and electrical components so your system reliably moves wastewater to the drain field. We service all major pump brands for homes in Indio.
