    Routine Water Softener Inspections in Indio

    Regular inspections are the foundation of effective water softener maintenance.     Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Indio

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, dictating regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages. If issues are found, we can often repair or replace worn parts to restore full function, preventing the need for a costly full system replacement in Indio.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Indio

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs far less than a new system.

    Hardness Test and Water Quality Check in Indio

    As part of our maintenance, we conduct a water hardness test to verify your water softener is effectively reducing mineral content to proper levels.     Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Indio, CA

    The brine tank is crucial for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can accumulate, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a common issue we address in Indio homes.
