    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Apple Valley

    The cost of water heater replacement in Apple Valley, CA usually ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 for a standard tank unit, including installation. Tankless units can range from $3,000 to $5,000 or more, depending on the model and necessary plumbing modifications. Factors like unit type, capacity, brand and labor complexity influence the final price.

    Yes, in many areas of Apple Valley and Southern California, a plumbing permit is required for water heater replacement to make sure the installation meets local safety and building codes. FKM Plumbing handles all necessary permitting on your behalf, so you do not have to worry about compliance.

    Many households of two to three people do well with a 40 gallon tank, while larger families often need 50 gallons or more. We size based on your bathroom count and usage.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, a metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit or a water heater that is over 10 to 12 years old. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a professional.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but often have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.

    Flushing a traditional tank water heater annually is recommended in most homes. Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California install softeners or scale filters. This helps remove sediment buildup, which can reduce efficiency and shorten the lifespan of the unit. Regular flushing can also prevent scale from damaging heating elements.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line adjustments.

    New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and parts, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. FKM Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, giving you added peace of mind. Specific warranty details vary by brand and model.
