    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Apple Valley

    The cost of water softener replacement in Apple Valley, CA can vary widely, usually ranging from $1,500 to $4,000. This depends on the type and size of the new system, labor costs and any necessary plumbing changes. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after assessing your needs.

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had significant mineral buildup restricting flow. However, a water softener's main job is to remove hardness minerals, not to boost pressure. If low water pressure is your main concern, we can investigate other causes.

    FKM Plumbing installs a wide range of reputable water softener brands, including Fleck, Autotrol, Culligan, Pelican and Watts. We select brands known for their reliability, efficiency and long-term performance, so you get a high-quality system that meets your needs.

    We install systems with Fleck, Clack and Kinetico valves, among others. These brands have good parts availability and proven performance.

    We test your hardness in grains per gallon and estimate daily water usage. That determines the resin capacity needed.

    Municipal water across much of Southern California is hard, leaving chalky scale on faucets, showerheads, and inside water heater tanks. Hard water contains dissolved minerals like calcium and magnesium. These minerals can lead to scale buildup in pipes, lower efficiency of water heaters and appliances, spots on dishes, and dry skin and hair. A working water softener is the best defense for combating these issues in Apple Valley homes.

    Many systems include 5 to 10 years on the valve and longer coverage on tanks. We also provide a warranty on our labor.

    Common signs include a return of hard water symptoms like scale buildup on fixtures, dry skin and hair, dingy laundry or heavy soap use. Other signs are a noticeable drop in water pressure, leaks from the unit, or a softener over 10 to 15 years old that needs frequent repairs.
