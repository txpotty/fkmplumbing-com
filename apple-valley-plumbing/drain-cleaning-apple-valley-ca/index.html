    Water that drains slowly, gurgling sounds, sewer odors and water backing up into a tub or shower are the most common signs. If more than one fixture is affected, the problem is likely in the main line.

    Municipal water across much of Southern California is hard, leaving chalky scale on faucets, showerheads, and inside water heater tanks. Mineral scale inside pipes leaves a rough surface that traps grease, soap and hair. Roots also push into sewer lines looking for water, particularly during dry stretches.

    In Apple Valley, common causes include hair and soap scum in bathroom drains, grease and food particles in kitchen drains, and tree root intrusion into main sewer lines where mature trees grow close to the pipe.
