    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Camarillo

    A slow drip from a shower valve, supply stub or drain fitting can soak a wall for months before you notice. We locate the leak with moisture meters, thermal imaging and pressure tests. The damaged section of pipe or valve is replaced, and wet drywall is cut out and dried. Once the cavity is dry, we restore the wall and seal any wet-area surfaces.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Camarillo, CA

    Basement wall sealing is a key part of walls waterproofing. Intense sun breaks down exposed plastic pipe, sprinkler parts, and exterior fittings, so they wear out faster than indoor plumbing. We apply high-quality sealants to porous concrete and masonry walls to prevent moisture from wicking through and causing dampness, mold, or musty odors. This service can be performed alone or as part of a larger waterproofing system, offering a drier and healthier environment in your Camarillo, CA basement.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Camarillo

    Many homes in Camarillo have crawl spaces, which are highly susceptible to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting prevents ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an affordable way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Camarillo, CA

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to confirm the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test proves the pan is watertight before tile.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Camarillo, CA

    Sump pumps are a critical part of many interior waterproofing systems, particularly in homes that see heavy rainfall. We install high-capacity sump pumps designed to handle large volumes of water, keeping your basement dry even during heavy storms. Our services include primary sump pump installation, battery backup sump pump systems for power outages, and routine maintenance or repair of existing units. We make sure the discharge line is routed correctly so water does not circulate back toward the foundation.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Camarillo, CA

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already passed through the foundation walls. This usually involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and works well where exterior access is limited.
