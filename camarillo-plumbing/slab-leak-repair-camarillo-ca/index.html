    Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Camarillo, CA

    A large slab leak can push water up through flooring or into walls quickly. We help you shut off the water and send a technician to isolate the leaking line. Often we can restore water to the rest of the house while we plan the repair. Quick action limits flooring damage and soil saturation.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Camarillo, CA

    When the leak is isolated and the rest of the pipe is in good shape, a spot repair through the slab is a sensible option. We cut a small opening, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The trench is compacted and the concrete is patched. Flooring is then restored by your preferred contractor.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Camarillo, CA

    If multiple slab leaks indicate a systemic issue with your home's original plumbing, a full PEX repipe might be the most cost-effective and durable solution. PEX tubing is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and less prone to leaks caused by foundation shifts, making it a good choice for many homes. We can replace all the water supply lines in your Camarillo home, providing a brand-new, leak-free plumbing system that will last for decades.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Camarillo

    Locating a hidden slab leak takes specialized tools. Our plumbers use advanced electronic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to pinpoint the exact location of the leak under your concrete foundation. This precise detection reduces the need for extensive concrete removal, saving you time and money. We can detect leaks in both hot and cold water lines, as well as sewer lines, for a thorough diagnosis of your Camarillo home.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Camarillo, CA

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a load-bearing wall or in a difficult-to-access area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method. This lets our plumbers reach the damaged pipe from outside your home, avoiding disruption to your interior floors and finishes. Tunneling spares the interior but calls for skilled excavation and shoring to protect structural integrity throughout the repair process in Camarillo, CA.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Camarillo

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method skips the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Camarillo with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Slab Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Camarillo, CA

    Spot repairs usually have a lower upfront cost but involve breaking concrete and repairing flooring. Reroutes cost a bit more but avoid concrete work and future leaks in that line. Whole-house repipes are the best choice when you have had multiple slab leaks or old copper throughout. We give you written prices for each option.
