    Water Line Replacement vs. Repair in Camarillo, CA

    Deciding between pipe repair and full replacement depends on several factors, including the age of your pipes, the extent of the damage, and the frequency of past issues.     Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Camarillo, CA

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep coming back. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you weigh the cost of both paths.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Camarillo

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a popular choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Mountain and high desert communities in Southern California do get hard freezes, so pipes there need insulation and proper winter care. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during sudden cold snaps. FKM Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers strong performance and water quality for your home.
