    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Camarillo

    Most standard gas or electric tank replacements cost $1,400 to $3,000 including the heater, labor and typical code upgrades. Attic installs, larger tanks or relocations can raise the cost to $3,000 to $4,500.

    Most new water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components, depending on the model and brand. FKM Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, so your investment is covered.

    Tankless offers endless hot water and a longer lifespan but costs more upfront. We can compare both options based on your household and budget.

    While it might seem tempting, water heater replacement involves gas lines, high voltage electricity, water pressure and heavy equipment. It is a complex job best handled by licensed professionals to ensure safety, proper installation and compliance with local plumbing codes, preventing potential leaks or hazards.

    Most replacements take two to four hours. Attic installs or relocations can take more time.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, a metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit or a water heater that is over 10 to 12 years old. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a professional.

    Many households of two to three people do well with a 40 gallon tank, while larger families often need 50 gallons or more. We size according to your bathroom count and usage.

    Flushing a traditional tank water heater once a year is recommended in most homes. Around Camarillo, flushing the water heater yearly and cleaning aerators keeps scale buildup from cutting flow and efficiency. This helps remove sediment buildup, which can reduce efficiency and shorten the lifespan of the unit. Regular flushing can also prevent scale from impacting heating elements.
