    Understanding Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Camarillo, CA

    While snaking can punch a hole through a clog, hydrojetting in Camarillo, CA provides a complete pipe cleaning. Snaking is effective for simple, localized clogs, but it often leaves behind residual buildup that can lead to recurring problems. Hydrojetting, conversely, scours the entire interior of the pipe, removing debris, grease, and scale, offering a more long-term solution to your drainage issues.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Camarillo

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under floors and slabs. Snaking clears a path, but the grease soon builds back. Jetting with a rotating nozzle peels that layer off and flushes it downstream. Homeowners who cook often and restaurants benefit the most from regular kitchen line jetting.
