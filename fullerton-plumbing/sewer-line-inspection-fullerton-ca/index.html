    Many older homes have cast iron drain lines under the floor and clay sewer laterals outside. Cast iron can corrode and develop scale, cracks and channeling along the bottom of the pipe.     Belly and Offset Detection in Fullerton, CA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds water and waste. Offsets happen when pipe sections shift out of alignment, often because of soil movement. Earthquakes are a constant consideration in Southern California, and seismic straps and flexible connectors help keep water and gas lines secure during shaking. The camera shows these defects clearly, and we mark their location for targeted repair.
