    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in Fullerton

    The cost of underground pipe repair in Fullerton, CA can vary widely, typically ranging from $500 for minor spot repairs to $3,000 to $10,000 or more for trenchless full line replacements, or even more for extensive excavation and re-piping. Factors include the pipe material, depth, length of the damaged section, the repair method chosen and the extent of property restoration needed. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after a thorough diagnosis.

    For water lines we typically use PEX or Type K copper, both approved for direct burial. For sewer lines we use Schedule 40 PVC. The right choice depends on the existing pipe and local code.

    In most cities, the homeowner owns and maintains the water line from the meter to the house. The utility is usually responsible for the meter and the line on the street side. We can help confirm which side the leak is on.

    FKM Plumbing is equipped to repair a wide range of underground pipe materials, including PVC, ABS, cast iron, copper, PEX and even older materials like clay or polybutylene. Our technicians are trained on the specific repair techniques for each material.

    Many water utilities offer a one-time leak adjustment if you repair the leak quickly and provide proof. We give you an invoice and repair documentation to support your request.

    Common signs of a broken underground pipe include unexplained wet spots or lush patches in your yard, a sudden drop in water pressure, unusual odors such as sulfur or sewage, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets or an unexpected increase in your water bill. If you notice any of these, it's smart to call for a professional inspection promptly.

    Look for unusually wet spots in your yard, unexplained increases in your water bill, foul odors, decreased water pressure or gurgling sounds from drains. These can all point to a hidden leak or blockage in your underground plumbing system.

    The length of an underground pipe repair depends heavily on the severity and location of the damage, as well as the repair method chosen.
