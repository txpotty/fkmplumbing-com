    A properly sized and installed water softener should not noticeably affect your water pressure. If you experience a noticeable drop in pressure after installation, it could point to an issue with the system or installation, which our plumbers can diagnose and resolve.

    While some homeowners attempt DIY water softener installation, it's strongly recommended to hire a professional plumber.
