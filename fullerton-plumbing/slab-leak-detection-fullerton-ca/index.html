    Slab Leak Reroutes in Fullerton

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is popular when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Fullerton, CA

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to see temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a clear thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area quickly without any physical disruption to your property in Fullerton.

    Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Fullerton, CA

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most affordable repair.
