    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Fullerton

    Many older homes in Fullerton, CA still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to corrosion, rust and reduced water pressure over time. FKM Plumbing specializes in replacing these outdated pipes with modern, more durable materials like copper or PEX. This upgrade eliminates recurring leaks and rust-colored water and significantly improves water flow and quality throughout your home. We handle the entire process, from initial assessment to complete repiping, with minimal disruption.

    PEX Pipe Repair and Installation in Fullerton

    PEX piping is a popular, flexible and freeze-resistant alternative to traditional copper or PVC, commonly used in newer construction and remodels in Fullerton. Our team is proficient in PEX pipe repair, addressing crimp failures or accidental punctures. We also specialize in PEX pipe installation, a reliable, corrosion-resistant and cost-effective solution for re-piping projects that delivers a leak-free plumbing system for years to come.

    Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Fullerton

    Drain pipes made of ABS, PVC or cast iron can crack, separate or leak at joints. A leaking drain often shows up as a stain below a bathroom or a musty smell in a cabinet. We locate the leak, replace the damaged section and test the drain under full flow. We also check for proper slope and venting while the line is open.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Fullerton

    Older homes in Fullerton frequently have galvanized steel or aging copper pipes that are prone to corrosion, leading to discolored water, low water pressure and frequent leaks. FKM Plumbing provides full corroded pipe replacement services.     Galvanized Pipe Repair in Fullerton

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in older homes and rusts from the inside until flow drops and leaks start at threaded joints. Repairs are possible, but every disturbed fitting can start a new leak. We replace failing sections with PEX or copper using dielectric unions to stop galvanic corrosion. For homes with widespread galvanized piping, we will quote a full repipe so you can weigh both options.

    Pipe Leak Detection in Fullerton, CA

    Before any repair can happen, the leak needs to be found. FKM Plumbing offers modern pipe leak detection services in Fullerton, CA using non-invasive techniques. We use specialized equipment such as electronic listening devices, thermal cameras and inert gas tracers to accurately locate hidden leaks behind walls, under concrete slabs or underground. Our precise detection methods save you time and money by avoiding unnecessary demolition.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Fullerton

    Pinhole leaks form when copper pipe wall thins from the inside, often due to water chemistry, high velocity or poor grounding. They start as tiny drips that stain drywall or ceilings. We cut out the affected section and replace it with new pipe or a PEX transition. If we find several pinholes in the same area, we will talk with you about repiping that zone.

    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Fullerton, CA

    PEX and CPVC are common in homes built over the last few decades. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic. CPVC becomes brittle with age and heat and can crack when bumped. We replace failed fittings and brittle sections with modern components rated for your plumbing.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Pipe Repair in Fullerton

    The cost of pipe repair in Fullerton, CA varies a lot depending on the type of pipe, the location of the damage and the extent of the repair. Minor leak repairs can range from $150 to $500, while more complex issues like burst pipes or slab leaks might cost between $800 and $5,000 or more, especially if excavation is required. We provide a detailed estimate after a full assessment.

    Minor pipe repairs, such as fixing a small leak or replacing a short section of pipe, typically do not require a permit in Fullerton. However, larger projects like extensive re-piping, relocating a main water line or repairing a sewer line usually do require a permit to meet local building codes. We will advise you on any necessary permits and handle the application process.

    Homeowner policies frequently cover sudden water damage from a burst pipe, but not the cost of fixing worn or corroded pipe. Check your policy, and we will provide documentation to support any claim.

    The duration of a pipe repair depends on the complexity of the issue. A simple visible leak might be fixed within an hour or two. More involved repairs like burst pipes behind walls or slab leaks can take several hours to a full day, sometimes requiring multiple visits if specialized parts are needed. We work to complete repairs as efficiently as possible.

    A repair clamp or push-fit coupling can stop a leak temporarily in an emergency. For a permanent fix, especially inside walls, a licensed plumber should replace the damaged section with approved fittings.

    If the leak is isolated and the rest of the plumbing is in good condition, a repair makes sense. If you have repeated leaks, galvanized pipe or polybutylene, repiping is often the better long-term value.

    Watch for unexplained water bill increases, damp spots on walls or ceilings, warped flooring, musty smells and the sound of running water when fixtures are off. Your water meter can confirm a leak if it moves with everything turned off.

    Freezing temperatures are a common cause of burst pipes, especially when uninsulated pipes are exposed to sustained cold. Freezing pipes are rare in most of Southern California, and many homes have exposed outdoor plumbing that would never pass in a colder climate. Other causes include extreme water pressure, corrosion from aging pipes, shifting foundations and physical damage from construction or tree roots.
