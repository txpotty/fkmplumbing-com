    Frequently Asked Questions About Faucet Repair in Fullerton

    The cost of faucet repair in Fullerton can range from $120 to $350, depending on the type of faucet, the specific parts needed, and the difficulty of the repair. A simple washer replacement will be on the lower end, while a cartridge replacement in a high-end faucet might be more. We provide a written estimate before any work begins.

    Simple cartridge swaps can be a DIY job if you identify the correct part. Stuck cartridges, corroded valve bodies and leaks under the sink are better handled by a plumber.

    Low water pressure can stem from several issues, including mineral deposits clogging the aerator or internal components, a worn-out cartridge, issues with your home's main water supply, or even problems with municipal water pressure. Our technicians perform a thorough diagnosis to accurately pinpoint and resolve the cause.

    Low pressure at one faucet is typically a clogged aerator or cartridge. Low pressure everywhere could mean a failing pressure regulator, a supply issue or old corroded pipes.

    While a minor drip isn't typically an immediate emergency, it's a problem that should be addressed quickly to prevent water waste and potential damage. However, a rapidly gushing leak or a faucet that won't shut off completely is an emergency, requiring immediate professional attention to avoid significant water damage.

    A worn cartridge, washer or valve seat is the usual culprit. Hard water scale makes these parts wear out faster, so drips are common in many homes.

    If the faucet body is solid and parts are available, a repair is generally worth it. If it is corroded, cracked or discontinued, a new faucet is often the better choice.

    Generally, simple faucet repairs like replacing internal components, handles, or the entire faucet (if it's a like-for-like replacement) do not require a permit in Fullerton. However, if the repair involves altering the existing plumbing lines or substantially changing the fixture type, a permit might be necessary. Our plumbers know local codes and will let you know if a permit is required.
