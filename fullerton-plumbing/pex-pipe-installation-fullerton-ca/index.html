    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Fullerton

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a solid option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer dependable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to verify a proper seal.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Fullerton, CA

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, similar to a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to weigh cost and performance.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Fullerton, CA

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is known for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Plenty of insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.

    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Fullerton

    The cost of PEX pipe installation in Fullerton can range from $2,500 to $15,000 or more, depending on the size of your home, accessibility and whether it is a full repipe or a partial replacement. Minor repairs might be a few hundred dollars. We provide a detailed quote after an on-site assessment.

    PEX piping is designed to be highly durable and has an estimated lifespan of 50 years or more. Its resistance to corrosion, scaling and freeze damage contributes to its long-term reliability, making it a sound investment for your Fullerton home.

    We make small, targeted openings to run new pipe and patch them when done. Most homeowners are surprised by how little drywall work is needed.

    PEX can be used for outdoor applications like irrigation lines or outdoor spigots, but it needs to be protected from direct UV light, which can degrade the material over time. When used outdoors, PEX should always be buried or otherwise shielded.

    FKM Plumbing stands behind our PEX pipe installations with a full warranty on both labor and materials. Specific warranty details will be provided with your estimate, but you can be confident your new PEX system is installed correctly and will perform reliably for years to come in your Fullerton home.

    PEX offers several advantages over copper, including lower material and installation costs, greater flexibility and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Unlike copper, PEX does not develop pinhole leaks over time due to water chemistry, making it a more durable choice for many Fullerton homes. It also cuts down on water hammer noise.

    PEX must be protected from sunlight because UV light breaks down it. Outdoors it needs to be buried, sleeved or completely covered.

    PEX is well suited for a wide range of climates because of its flexibility and resistance to freezing. Intense sun breaks down exposed plastic pipe, sprinkler parts, and outdoor fittings, so they wear out faster than indoor plumbing.
