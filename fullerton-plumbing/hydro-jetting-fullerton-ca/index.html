    Usually, a permit is not required for standard hydro jetting aimed at clearing blockages in existing lines.     A standard residential hydro jetting service in Fullerton usually takes between 1 and 3 hours. This includes the initial camera inspection, the jetting process itself, and a final inspection. More extensive commercial jobs or severe main line blockages might require more time.

    Small consumer jetters exist, but they lack the pressure and flow of professional units. High-pressure water can also injure untrained users and harm fragile pipe.
