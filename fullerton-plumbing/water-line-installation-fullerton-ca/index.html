    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Fullerton, CA

    PEX and HDPE are flexible, which means fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. HDPE is especially durable for long runs and resists cracking in expansive clay. We use approved compression or fusion fittings and add tracer wire so the line can be found with a locator later. PEX must stay out of sunlight before burial, so we store and lay it carefully on every job site.

    Water Line Installation Cost Comparison in Fullerton, CA

    Cost depends mostly on run length, soil conditions and pipe material. A short PEX run through soft soil is the most affordable option, while long copper runs through rocky ground cost more in both labor and materials. Trenchless methods cost more per foot but can save thousands in concrete and landscape repair. We provide a written quote that itemizes trenching, pipe, fittings, permits and restoration.
