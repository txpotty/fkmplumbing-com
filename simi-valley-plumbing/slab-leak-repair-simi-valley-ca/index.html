    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Simi Valley

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is severely deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an effective long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This removes future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Simi Valley with aging copper or galvanized pipes.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Simi Valley

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is easiest. Reroutes end future leaks in that section of pipe.
