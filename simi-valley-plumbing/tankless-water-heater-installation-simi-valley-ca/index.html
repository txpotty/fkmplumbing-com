    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Installation in Simi Valley

    Most gas tankless installations cost $3,000 to $6,500 including the unit, labor, venting and typical gas line upgrades. Electric units can be less, though panel upgrades can add $1,000 to $3,000.

    Size depends on how many fixtures run at once and your incoming water temperature. Many homes need a unit rated around 7 to 10 gallons per minute.

    To maintain efficiency and extend the lifespan of your tankless unit, annual flushing to remove mineral buildup is recommended, especially in areas with hard water. Checking and cleaning the air filter (for gas models) and inspecting connections are also part of regular maintenance. FKM Plumbing offers complete maintenance services.

    Tankless water heaters offer several key benefits: endless hot water on demand, real energy savings from heating water only when needed, a longer lifespan (often 20+ years) and a compact, space-saving design. They also reduce the risk of tank leaks and the water damage that comes with them.

    We install leading tankless water heater brands known for their reliability and efficiency, including Rinnai, Navien, Noritz and Rheem. Our plumbers are trained and experienced with the particular requirements of each manufacturer to ensure strong performance.

    Sizing a tankless water heater depends on your household's peak hot water demand, measured in gallons per minute (GPM), and the temperature rise needed. Our plumbers will assess the hot water fixtures in your home and review your usage patterns to recommend the right GPM and BTU rating for your needs in Simi Valley.

    With annual descaling, tankless units often last 15 to 20 years. Skipping maintenance in hard water areas can shorten that significantly.
