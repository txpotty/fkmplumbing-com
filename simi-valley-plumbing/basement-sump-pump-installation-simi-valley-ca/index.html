    High-Capacity Sump Pump Solutions in Simi Valley, CA

    For homes with significant groundwater issues or large basements, a standard sump pump may not be enough. FKM Plumbing installs high-capacity sump pump systems designed to move larger volumes of water quickly and efficiently. These systems often feature more powerful motors and larger discharge lines. We conduct a thorough assessment of your property to recommend and install a system that can handle even the heaviest rainfall.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Simi Valley

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by locating the low point where water collects. We saw cut the slab, excavate a pit roughly 18 to 24 inches wide and 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a perforated or solid liner depending on whether we are collecting groundwater or surface seepage. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor remains clean and level.

    Primary Sump Pump Replacement in Simi Valley

    If your existing sump pump is old, failing or no longer adequate for your home, FKM Plumbing offers expert primary sump pump replacement. We can promptly remove your old unit and install a new, more efficient model, lowering the risk of future flooding. Our plumbers assess your current setup, recommend fitting replacements and handle a smooth changeover. This service is essential for keeping effective flood protection in your Simi Valley home.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Simi Valley, CA

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit is the least expensive option, typically a few hundred dollars plus the pump. Digging a new pit through the slab adds labor for saw cutting, excavation and concrete patching. Adding a battery backup increases the total but often costs less than one cleanup after a flooded basement. We give you an itemized quote so you can compare a basic primary pump against a full primary and backup system before we start.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Simi Valley

    Power outages during heavy storms can render a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump provides crucial protection when the power goes out. We install dependable backup systems that activate automatically, keeping water moving even without electricity. This is an essential safeguard for homes in Simi Valley that lose power during storms, offering peace of mind in severe weather. We integrate these systems cleanly with your existing plumbing.

    Wayne Sump Pump Installation in Simi Valley, CA

    Wayne sump pumps offer a range of options, from basic utility pumps to advanced systems with built-in alarms and smart features. Our technicians are familiar with installing Wayne pumps, providing efficient water removal for your Simi Valley, CA basement. We help you choose the right Wayne model for your needs and complete a professional installation that gets the most from its performance and lifespan.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Simi Valley

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which saves wear on the motor. On an occasional cold night, covering backflow preventers and outdoor valves is usually all it takes to avoid a break. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the discharge.

    Sump Pump Pit Installation in Simi Valley, CA

    A well-built sump pit is fundamental to an effective sump pump system. If your basement lacks a suitable pit or needs an upgrade, FKM Plumbing provides expert sump pit installation. We precisely excavate and prepare the pit to the correct dimensions and depth for proper drainage and pump function. This foundational step is critical for any new sump pump installation and prevents the sediment buildup that can damage the pump.
