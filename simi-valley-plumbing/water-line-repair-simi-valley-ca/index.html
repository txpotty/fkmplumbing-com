    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Simi Valley

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Simi Valley, CA

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California add softeners or scale filters. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.
