    PEX Repiping in Simi Valley

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for better flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with far fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most popular repipe material.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Simi Valley

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a popular choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Freezing pipes are rare in most of Southern California, and many homes have exposed outdoor plumbing that would never pass in a harsher climate. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during sudden cold snaps. FKM Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials.
