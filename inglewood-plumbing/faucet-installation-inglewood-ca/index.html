    Frequently Asked Questions About Faucet Installation in Inglewood

    Labor for a standard kitchen or bathroom faucet installation usually costs $150 to $350. Touchless faucets, wall-mount faucets and pot fillers range from $300 to $800 or more depending on the work involved. We give you a flat price before we start.

    Replacing a faucet does not typically require a permit. Adding new supply lines, like for a pot filler during a remodel, may be part of a larger permitted project.

    Typically, a permit is not required for a simple like-for-like faucet replacement in Inglewood. However, if the installation involves altering the main water lines, relocating a sink, or other major plumbing changes, a permit might be necessary. We can walk you through specific permit requirements.

    Touchless faucets are convenient and more hygienic in busy kitchens. They cost more and need batteries or power, but many homeowners find them worth the upgrade.

    FKM Plumbing stands behind our workmanship with a satisfaction guarantee on all faucet installations. Exact warranty details will be provided at the time of service, covering the labor for a set period.

    Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California install softeners or scale filters. Where water carries a lot of minerals, deposits can build up inside faucet components over time, causing reduced water flow, stubborn stains, and faster wear on internal seals and cartridges. Regular cleaning and professional installation can help reduce these effects, and new faucets are often more resilient.

    If your current faucet has internal blockages or a faulty aerator, a new faucet can improve water pressure at that specific fixture. However, if the low pressure is a whole-house issue, a new faucet alone will not fix the problem.

    While some homeowners attempt DIY faucet installation, it can be harder than it looks. Improper installation can lead to leaks, water damage, and issues with water pressure or drainage.
