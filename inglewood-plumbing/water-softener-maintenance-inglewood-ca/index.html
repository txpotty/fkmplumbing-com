    Control Valve and Injector Service in Inglewood, CA

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a full regeneration every time.

    Water Softener Brand Servicing in Inglewood

    FKM Plumbing has broad experience maintaining all major water softener brands, including Culligan, Kenmore, Whirlpool, Fleck, Kinetico and more. Our technicians are familiar with the specific components and maintenance requirements of each manufacturer. Whether you have an older model or a brand-new high-efficiency unit, you can trust us to provide careful maintenance that keeps your system operating smoothly in Inglewood.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Inglewood, CA

    The brine tank is essential for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can build up, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a frequent issue we address in Inglewood homes.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repair in Inglewood, CA

    A leaking bypass valve can let hard water slip past the softener without you noticing. We test and rebuild bypass valves and replace cracked fittings. We also inspect the drain line for kinks and proper air gap and the brine overflow line for correct routing. These small fixes prevent leaks and backflow problems.

    Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Inglewood, CA

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use. Metered systems regenerate based on gallons used, which saves salt compared to time clock models. Correct settings keep water soft without wasting salt or water.

    Commercial Softener Maintenance in Inglewood

    Restaurants, laundromats, salons and car washes rely on soft water to protect equipment like dishwashers, ice machines and boilers. Commercial twin-tank systems regenerate often and need closer attention. We set up scheduled visits to test hardness, clean brine tanks and service valves. Consistent maintenance keeps equipment warranties valid and lowers repair costs.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Inglewood, CA

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their capacity. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for maximum softening capacity. We confirm your system is regenerating efficiently for your water quality, providing truly soft water.

    Valve and Controller Diagnostics in Inglewood, CA

    The control valve and electronic controller regulate the entire softening process. Our technicians perform diagnostic checks to ensure these components are operating accurately, adjusting programming as needed. We check for wear and tear on moving parts and lubricate them to prevent future issues, keeping your water softener reliable in Inglewood, CA.
