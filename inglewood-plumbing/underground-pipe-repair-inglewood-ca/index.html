    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in Inglewood

    The cost of underground pipe repair in Inglewood, CA can vary widely, typically ranging from $500 for minor spot repairs to $3,000 to $10,000 or more for trenchless full line replacements, or even more for extensive excavation and re-piping. Factors include the pipe material, depth, length of the damaged section, the repair method chosen and the extent of property restoration needed. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after a thorough diagnosis.

    For water lines we typically use PEX or Type K copper, both approved for direct burial. For sewer lines we use Schedule 40 PVC. The right choice depends on the existing pipe and local code.

    Shallow irrigation leaks are sometimes a manageable DIY repair. Main water lines and sewer laterals require locating, utility marking and code-compliant fittings, so a licensed plumber is the safer choice.

    FKM Plumbing stands behind the quality of our underground pipe repair work. We offer a solid warranty on parts and labor for all our underground pipe services, giving you peace of mind. Warranty details will be provided with your repair estimate, so you understand the protection you get.

    FKM Plumbing uses high-quality, industry-approved materials from reputable brands known for durability and longevity. This includes PEX piping from manufacturers like Uponor or Viega, and various PVC and ABS pipe products that meet ASTM standards.

    In most cities, the homeowner owns and maintains the water line from the meter to the house. The utility is usually responsible for the meter and the line on the street side. We can help confirm which side the leak is on.

    We use acoustic listening devices that pick up the sound of escaping water, tracer gas that rises through soil at the leak point and electronic locators that trace the pipe path. Together these narrow the leak to a small area.
