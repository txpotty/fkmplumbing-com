    Water hardness varies from place to place. Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California install softeners or scale filters. Like any water heater, electric tankless units benefit from regular flushing to prevent mineral buildup, especially if your Inglewood area has harder water.     We install Stiebel Eltron, Rheem, EcoSmart and Bosch. We recommend models based on flow needs and panel capacity.
