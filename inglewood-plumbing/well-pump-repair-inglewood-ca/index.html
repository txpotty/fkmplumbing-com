    Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Inglewood

    Low water pressure from a well system can be a frustrating problem, often indicating issues beyond just the pump itself. This could be due to a failing pressure switch, a partially clogged well screen, a leak in the supply line, or even a pump that is no longer meeting demand. Our team conducts careful diagnostics to pinpoint the exact cause of low pressure in your Inglewood home, delivering a targeted and effective repair solution.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Inglewood

    Submersible pumps are widely used in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms usually include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use specialized equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, ensuring the integrity of your well system.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Inglewood, CA

    Jet pumps, typically found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. FKM Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Inglewood operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is essential for jet pump function.
