    Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Inglewood, CA

    Damaged lids and failed gaskets let odors escape and create safety hazards. Outdoor risers can crack from vehicle traffic or ground movement. We replace lids, install new gaskets and repair or replace risers. Traffic-rated lids are installed where trucks or cars drive over the interceptor. These repairs keep odors in and keep people safe.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Inglewood, CA

    The internal baffles of a grease trap are crucial for separating FOG from wastewater. If these are damaged or dislodged, the trap becomes inefficient, allowing grease to escape into the sewer system. We repair or replace broken baffles and cracked lids in Inglewood, CA, ensuring your trap functions as designed. A secure lid also prevents odors and keeps pests out, maintaining hygiene around the unit.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Inglewood, CA

    Tees direct flow into and out of the trap at the correct depth. Missing or broken tees allow floating grease to escape into the sewer line. We replace damaged tees with durable PVC or other approved materials. We also check that they are set at the right depth for your unit. This is one of the most common and important grease trap repairs.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Inglewood, CA

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Inglewood

We remove damaged baffles and install new ones made for your trap model. In concrete interceptors, we can repair or rebuild baffle walls. Restoring baffles brings back proper separation and helps you stay up to code.
