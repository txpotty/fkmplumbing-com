    In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in Inglewood, CA

    Larger restaurants and high-volume kitchens often need an outdoor gravity interceptor. These concrete or polyethylene tanks typically range from several hundred to over 1,500 gallons. We excavate, set the tank on a stable base, connect inlet and outlet piping and install manhole risers.     Gravity Grease Trap Installation in Inglewood

    Gravity grease traps are a common and effective solution for many commercial kitchens in Inglewood. These passive systems use the natural separation of FOG from water due to density differences. Our plumbers are experts in installing gravity grease traps, ensuring proper sizing, correct inlet and outlet piping, and convenient access for future cleaning and maintenance to maximize their efficiency.

    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Inglewood

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with tight space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid without trouble.
