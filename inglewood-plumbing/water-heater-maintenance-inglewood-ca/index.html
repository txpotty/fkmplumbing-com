    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Maintenance in Inglewood

    A standard tank water heater flush and inspection in Inglewood usually costs $100 to $250. Tankless descaling typically runs $150 to $300, and anode rod replacement adds about $75 to $200 including the part.

    The anode rod is a metal rod inside your water heater that sacrifices itself to keep the steel tank from corroding. It attracts corrosive elements in the water. Regular inspection and replacement of a depleted anode rod are key for extending the lifespan of your water heater, especially where water quality varies.

    Most tank flushes and inspections take about 45 to 90 minutes. Tankless descaling usually takes 1 to 2 hours.

    During a water heater flush, we drain the tank completely and then refill it, letting water flow out through the drain valve until it runs clear. This process removes mineral deposits and sediment that accumulate at the bottom of the tank, which can hurt efficiency and cause damage.

    With hard water, flushing once a year is recommended. Homes with very hard water or no softener may benefit from flushing every six months or so.
