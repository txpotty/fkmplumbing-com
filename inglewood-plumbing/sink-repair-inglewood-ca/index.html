    Leaking P-Trap Repair in Inglewood

    Most puddles under a bathroom sink start at the P-trap. Slip-joint washers flatten over time, plastic traps crack, and old chrome traps rust through from the inside. We replace the trap with a correctly sized 1-1/4 inch assembly, align the tailpiece and wall arm, and run water to confirm a dry seal. This is one of the quickest and least expensive fixes we do.

    Slow Drain and Clog Clearing in Inglewood

    Bathroom sinks clog with hair, soap scum and toothpaste mixed with mineral scale.
