    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Yorba Linda

    Well pump replacement typically costs $1,800 to $4,500 depending on well depth and pump size. Deep wells or constant pressure systems can cost $4,000 to $8,000.

    Regular maintenance, such as checking your well's water level, keeping the pressure tank air charge correct and fixing any leaks promptly, can extend your pump's life. Avoiding heavy water use and installing a good quality sediment filter can also reduce wear and tear on the pump.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 1 to 5 years. We also provide a labor warranty.

    Shallow well pumps, or jet pumps, are designed for wells where the water level is typically 25 feet or less from the pump, using suction to draw water. Deep well pumps, or submersible pumps, are placed directly in the water near the bottom of the well and push water up, suited for depths over 25 feet. Choosing the correct type is key for efficiency and performance.

    Well pump replacement is a complex job involving electrical wiring, heavy lifting and specialized tools, especially for submersible pumps. It is highly recommended to hire a professional plumber for safety, proper installation and compliance with all codes, preventing costly mistakes.

    If your pressure tank is old or waterlogged, replacing it at the same time can protect the new pump. A failed tank causes rapid short cycling, which is one of the most common reasons submersible motors burn out early.

    Permit requirements vary by county. We follow local requirements for all well work.

    Common signs of a failing well pump include no water, sputtering faucets, reduced water pressure, discolored water or surprisingly high electricity bills. If your pump runs constantly or cycles on and off too frequently, these are also signs that replacement might be necessary.
