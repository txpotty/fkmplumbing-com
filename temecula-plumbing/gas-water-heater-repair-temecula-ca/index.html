    Propane Water Heater Repair in Temecula

    Rural properties often run water heaters on propane rather than natural gas. Propane units use different orifices and regulator pressures, and improper conversions cause sooting and poor performance. We confirm the correct parts are installed and repair pilots, valves and burners. We also check tank regulators for correct pressure.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Temecula

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures often involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the failure. Correct vent slope and termination are verified before we leave.

    Pilot Light and Burner Issues in Temecula

    A frequent problem with gas water heaters is a pilot light that won't stay lit or a burner that fails to ignite. This can be caused by a faulty thermocouple, a dirty pilot tube or issues with the gas control valve. Our technicians in Temecula are skilled at diagnosing these components and performing the necessary repairs, whether it is cleaning, adjusting or replacing parts. We confirm proper gas flow and ignition for consistent hot water.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Temecula, CA

    The gas control valve controls temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something goes wrong.     Venting and Draft Repair in Temecula, CA

    Atmospheric gas water heaters depend on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and verify draft with a test. Proper venting is critical for your family's safety.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Temecula, CA

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame signals a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats faster and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes dropping from the flue.
