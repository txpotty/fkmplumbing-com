    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Temecula

    The cost of sump pump replacement in Temecula, CA usually ranges from $500 to $1,500, depending on the type of pump, submersible or pedestal, its horsepower and any needed pit changes. Battery backup systems will add to this cost, usually between $300 and $800. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your needs.

    To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by FKM Plumbing can also help lengthen its lifespan.

    We install a variety of trusted sump pump brands known for their reliability and performance, including Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty Pumps and Little Giant. Our plumbers can help you pick the best brand and model for your home's requirements and budget.

    If storms knock out power in your area or you have finished living space below grade, a backup is a wise investment. A battery unit with a high-water alarm usually runs six to ten hours of intermittent pumping on a full charge.

    It is a good idea to test your sump pump at least every three months, and especially before and after rainy seasons. To test, simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit. The pump should activate, drain the water and then shut off automatically. If it does not, it is time for a professional inspection.

    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods can fail earlier due to stuck switches.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity. A backup sump pump, usually battery-powered or water-powered, is a secondary system that activates if the primary pump fails, loses power or cannot keep up with heavy water inflow. Many Temecula homes benefit from a backup system due to storm risks.
