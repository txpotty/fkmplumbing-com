    Grease Trap Leak Repair in Temecula

    Leaks can come from cracked trap bodies, failed gaskets or loose pipe connections. Indoor leaks can damage floors and create slip hazards in the kitchen. We locate the source and replace gaskets, seal fittings or repair cracks when possible. Outdoor leaks can contaminate soil and lead to odors around the building. If the body is badly damaged, we suggest replacement.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Temecula

    The baffles inside a grease trap are critical for separating FOGs from wastewater. If these baffles are corroded, broken, or dislodged, the trap's efficiency drops sharply, allowing grease into the sewer system. Our service includes inspecting the condition of all baffles and repairing or replacing them as needed, often using durable, corrosion-resistant materials. Proper baffle function is key to compliance with wastewater regulations in Temecula.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Temecula, CA

    Tees direct flow into and out of the trap at the correct depth. Missing or broken tees allow floating grease to escape into the sewer line. We replace damaged tees with durable PVC or other approved materials. We also check that they are set at the right depth for your unit. This is one of the most common and important grease trap repairs.

    While our focus is on repair, we also emphasize preventive maintenance to reduce the need for emergency services.     Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Temecula

    Beyond the trap itself, the drain lines leading to and from the grease trap can also suffer damage or blockages. These lines are particularly susceptible to grease build-up.     Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Temecula

    When traditional snaking isn't enough, hydro-jetting is our go-to method for severe grease trap blockages. This powerful technique uses high-pressure water streams to thoroughly scour the interior of pipes and the grease trap itself, removing years of FOG buildup, scale, and debris. It's a highly effective, non-invasive method that not only clears clogs but also cleans the lines, significantly reducing the likelihood of future blockages in your Temecula business.

    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Temecula, CA

    A leaking grease trap can cause significant property damage, foul odors, and potential health code violations. Leaks often stem from corroded tanks, cracked seams, or failing pipe connections. We use leak detection techniques to pinpoint the source and provide targeted repairs, which may involve patching the tank, sealing connections, or replacing damaged sections of the trap or associated plumbing.     Flow Control Device Repair in Temecula

    Hydromechanical traps rely on a flow control fitting to keep water moving at the rated speed. If this fitting is missing or clogged, the trap can be overwhelmed and grease escapes. We inspect, clean or replace flow control devices during repair visits. We also check the air intake that allows the device to work properly. Correct flow control is essential for a trap to pass inspection.
