    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Pasadena

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit typically costs $600 to $1,500. Installing a new pit with slab cutting and excavation often runs $1,500 to $4,000. Battery backups usually add $500 to $1,500.

    It's recommended to have your sump pump inspected and cleaned annually, ideally before the rainy season. This maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning the pit and testing the discharge line to confirm it's free of obstructions. Regular checks prevent unexpected failures.

    Not every home does, but homes with basements, crawl spaces, sunken patios or lots that collect storm runoff can benefit a lot. A sump pump protects against fast-rising storm flooding.

    To keep your new sump pump running efficiently, test it monthly by pouring water into the pit to confirm it activates. Once a year, inspect the discharge pipe for obstructions, clean the pit and check the float switch. Regular professional maintenance from FKM Plumbing can extend its lifespan in your Pasadena home.

    In many cities, a plumbing permit is typically required for new sump pump installations or major replacements. This confirms the work meets local building and plumbing codes. FKM Plumbing handles all necessary permitting in Pasadena, CA to ensure your installation is fully compliant.

    A simple replacement usually does not need a permit. New pits, electrical work or discharge connections may require one, and we will let you know.

    A submersible sump pump sits entirely inside the sump pit, submerged in water, making it quieter and taking up less space. A pedestal sump pump has its motor mounted on a shaft above the pit, making it easier to access for maintenance and usually more affordable. Both types are effective, and our plumbers can help you choose the best fit for your Pasadena basement.
