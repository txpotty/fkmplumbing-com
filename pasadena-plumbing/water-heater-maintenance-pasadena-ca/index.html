    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Pasadena

    Tankless units are particularly sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures consistent.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Pasadena

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than expected. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is crucial for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Maintenance in Pasadena

    The cost of water heater maintenance in Pasadena, CA can range from $120 to $250, based on the type of water heater (tank or tankless) and the services required. This typically includes a full flush, anode rod inspection, and safety checks. More complex issues or parts replacement would be an additional cost.

    While some basic tasks like flushing a tank might seem DIY-friendly, professional maintenance is highly recommended. Our technicians have the right tools, knowledge, and experience to perform thorough safety checks, spot potential hazards, and meet local codes, which a DIY approach might miss.

    Many homeowners can do a basic flush, but older drain valves can break or leak. A pro also tests safety valves, venting and gas lines.

    FKM Plumbing services all major brands of water heaters, including Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, State, Kenmore, Noritz, Rinnai, and more. Our technicians are trained on a broad range of models and systems.

    Many manufacturers require regular maintenance to keep warranties valid, especially for tankless units. We provide service records for your records.

    Most tank flushes and inspections take about 45 to 90 minutes. Tankless descaling typically takes 1 to 2 hours.

    Sediment buildup consists of mineral deposits, mainly calcium and magnesium, that settle at the bottom of your water heater tank. Municipal water across much of Southern California is hard, leaving white scale on faucets, showerheads, and inside water heater tanks. Sediment cuts efficiency, causes strange noises, and can lead to premature tank failure if not flushed regularly.

    Popping or rumbling noises, lukewarm water, rusty or smelly hot water, and slow recovery times are typical signs.
