    Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Pasadena

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. We test duplex systems, alternating controls and high-water alarms on a scheduled basis. Maintenance logs help property managers stay on top of inspections.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Pasadena, CA

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in Pasadena, CA.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Pasadena, CA

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Pasadena

    Our team is experienced in maintaining submersible sump pumps, a popular choice in Pasadena due to their quiet operation and compact design. Maintenance involves pulling the pump to inspect the watertight seals, clearing the intake screen and checking the internal components for corrosion or wear. We also verify the electrical connections are secure and free from moisture, preventing electrical hazards and supporting longevity.
