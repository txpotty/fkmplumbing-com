    Sewer Camera Inspection in Pasadena

    Every sewer repair starts with a look inside the pipe. We run a self-leveling camera from your cleanout or a pulled toilet and record the full length of the line. A sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact location and depth of every problem on the surface. You get a copy of the video and a plain-language summary of what we found. This step alone frequently saves homeowners from paying for repairs they do not need.

    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Pasadena, CA

    When repairs are no longer enough due to extensive damage, age or material failure, sewer pipe replacement becomes necessary. We offer various replacement options, including traditional trenching and modern trenchless technologies like pipe bursting or pipe lining. Our experts will assess your situation and recommend the most cost-effective and least disruptive solution for your Southern California property, ensuring a long-lasting new sewer line.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Pasadena

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can usually use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Pasadena, CA

    Tree and shrub roots go straight for the moisture and nutrients leaking from old sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense mats that catch paper and waste. We cut the roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro-jetter, then repair or seal the joint so they cannot come right back. For lines with many rooted joints, we will show you when lining or replacement makes more sense than ongoing cleaning.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Pasadena, CA

    A single spot repair is the most affordable fix when the rest of the line is sound. Pipe lining costs more per foot but avoids excavation and works well for long runs under driveways or landscaping. Full replacement is the right call when the pipe is Orangeburg, badly bellied or broken in several places. We lay out all three options with real numbers so you can compare cost against how long the repair will last.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Pasadena, CA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Most homes in Southern California sit on concrete slabs, so slab leaks often show up as warm floors, damp carpet, or an unexplained jump in the water bill. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Pasadena, CA

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive option. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from major disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an effective and popular choice for protecting property appearance in Pasadena.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Pasadena, CA

    While often overlooked, the sewer vent pipe is crucial for proper drainage and preventing sewer gas buildup. Issues with vent pipes, such as blockages from animal nests or damage from storms, can lead to slow drains, gurgling noises and foul odors. FKM Plumbing diagnoses and repairs vent pipe problems, ensuring your entire drainage system works correctly and safely and protecting good indoor air quality.
