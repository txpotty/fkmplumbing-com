    New Sump Pit Excavation in Pasadena

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by finding the low point where water collects. We saw cut the slab, excavate a pit roughly 18 to 24 inches wide and 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a perforated or solid liner depending on whether we are collecting groundwater or surface seepage. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor remains clean and level.

    Sump Pump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Pasadena

    A complete sump pump system requires a properly installed basin and discharge line. We handle the excavation and placement of the sump basin, setting it at the lowest point of your basement floor so it collects water well. We also install the discharge line, ensuring it is correctly sloped, sealed and routed away from your foundation to a suitable drainage area, so water does not re-enter your Pasadena property.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Pasadena

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit usually runs $500 to $1,200 including the pump and check valve. Installing a new pit through the slab generally falls between $1,500 and $3,500, and adding a battery backup adds about $600 to $1,500.

    We routinely install Zoeller, Liberty Pumps and Wayne because they offer cast iron housings, reliable switches and good parts support. We will fit the brand and model to your pit size and budget.

    FKM Plumbing offers a warranty on our labor for basement sump pump installations, typically for one year. The sump pump itself comes with a manufacturer's warranty, which can range from 1 to 5 years depending on the brand and model. We will provide you all warranty details at the time of installation.

    Watch for a pump that runs constantly, cycles on and off rapidly, makes grinding noises or never seems to turn on. Rust stains, a stuck float and water sitting above the switch level are also warning signs.

    It is recommended to test your sump pump every 2 to 3 months, especially before and after the rainy season. Simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit to make sure the pump activates, drains the water and shuts off properly. Regular testing helps catch issues early.

    Discharge should go to your yard, a dry well or an approved drainage area at least 10 feet from the foundation. Connecting a sump pump to the sanitary sewer is generally not allowed by local codes.

    FKM Plumbing offers a comprehensive warranty on our basement sump pump installations, covering both parts and labor. The specific warranty period will be detailed in your service agreement. We stand behind our work and the quality of the products we install in your Pasadena home.

    Most residential basements need a 1/3 or 1/2 horsepower pump, but the right answer depends on how fast water enters the pit and how high it must be lifted. We measure both before recommending a model.
