    Signs you might need PEX repiping include persistently low water pressure, discolored or rusty water, frequent leaks, or old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes. These issues are typical in older Pasadena homes and point to a failing plumbing system.

    Multiple pinhole leaks, discolored water, low pressure throughout the house and gray polybutylene pipe are typical signs. Recurring slab leaks are also a strong reason to repipe overhead.

    Yes, PEX piping is certified safe for drinking water. It does not leach chemicals into water and is approved by all major plumbing codes for potable water systems. It is a cleaner alternative to older pipes that can corrode and affect water quality.

    PEX must be protected from sunlight because UV light degrades it. Outdoors it needs to be buried, sleeved or completely covered.

    We install quality PEX piping from reputable manufacturers such as Uponor (PEX-A), Viega (PEX-B) and Zurn (PEX-B). We also work with other trusted brands like SharkBite for fittings, so the best materials are used for your installation.

    PEX is well suited for a wide range of climates because of its flexibility and resistance to freezing.
