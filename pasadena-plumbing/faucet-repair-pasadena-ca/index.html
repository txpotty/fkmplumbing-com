    Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California add softeners or scale filters. Hard water contains high levels of minerals like calcium and magnesium. Over time, these minerals can build up inside your faucets, corroding internal parts, clogging aerators, and stiffening moving components. This often leads to leaks, reduced water pressure, and a shorter faucet lifespan, making regular maintenance or timely repair important.
