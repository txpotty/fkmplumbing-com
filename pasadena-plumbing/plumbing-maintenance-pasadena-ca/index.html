    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Pasadena

    Copper pinhole leaks and corroded cast iron drains are common repair calls in homes that are now several decades old. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Finding a leak early prevents slab damage and mold.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Pasadena, CA

    Tree roots invade sewer lines during dry months, so the first big rain of the season often reveals a cracked or blocked lateral. A working sump pump is essential for homes with basements or crawl spaces when heavy rain overwhelms drainage. Our maintenance includes testing the float switch, checking for proper discharge, cleaning the pit of debris, and inspecting the check valve. That way your sump pump is ready to protect your home from flooding when unexpected storms hit Pasadena.

    Drain Line Inspections for Maintenance in Pasadena

    As part of complete plumbing maintenance, we offer drain line camera inspections. This lets us see the condition of your main sewer lines, spotting potential blockages from roots or grease buildup, or structural issues like cracks and offsets made worse by shifting soil. Most homes in Southern California sit on concrete slabs, so slab leaks often show up as warm floors, wet carpet, or an unexplained jump in the water bill. Early detection through inspection can prevent severe backups and expensive emergency repairs.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Pasadena
