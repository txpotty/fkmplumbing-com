    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Pasadena, CA

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are more difficult to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards.     Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Pasadena, CA

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most economical repair.     Electronic Leak Detection in Pasadena

    For hard-to-find leaks, our electronic leak detection equipment sends a signal through your pipes and measures changes that indicate a breach.
