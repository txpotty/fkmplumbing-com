    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilet Installation in Pasadena

    Comfort-height toilets put the seat at about 17 to 19 inches, which is gentler on knees and backs. For accessible bathrooms, we follow ADA guidelines for seat height, centerline distance from the side wall and grab bar clearance.     Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Pasadena, CA

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, with the tank hidden behind an access plate. They free up floor space and make cleaning simple, but they need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep or a furred-out chase. We install Geberit, Grohe and Kohler carrier systems and set the bowl height to your preference. This work is easiest during a remodel when the wall is open.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Pasadena, CA

    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Pasadena, CA
