    When traditional snaking isn't enough, hydro-jetting is our go-to method for severe grease trap blockages.     Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Compton

    Grease traps often clog due to excessive accumulation of fats, oils, and grease (FOGs) or food solids.     Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Compton, CA

    The internal baffles of a grease trap are essential for separating FOG from wastewater. If these are damaged or dislodged, the trap becomes inefficient, allowing grease to escape into the sewer system. We repair or replace broken baffles and cracked lids in Compton, CA, making sure your trap functions as designed. A secure lid also prevents odors and keeps pests out, maintaining hygiene around the unit.

    Grease Trap Leak Repair in Compton

    Leaks can come from cracked trap bodies, failed gaskets or loose pipe connections. Indoor leaks can damage floors and create slip hazards in the kitchen. We locate the source and replace gaskets, seal fittings or repair cracks when possible. Outdoor leaks can contaminate soil and lead to odors around the building. If the body is badly damaged, we suggest replacement.

    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Compton, CA

    Simple repairs like gasket or tee replacements usually cost far less than a new trap.     Flow Control Device Repair in Compton

    Hydromechanical traps rely on a flow control fitting to keep water moving at the rated speed. If this fitting is missing or clogged, the trap can be overwhelmed and grease escapes. We inspect, clean or replace flow control devices during repair visits. We also check the air intake that allows the device to work properly. Correct flow control is necessary for a trap to pass inspection.
