    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Compton

    Common signs include frequent clogs, loud grinding noises without processing food, persistent leaks from the unit's housing, or if the disposal simply stops turning on. If you notice these issues, it's probably time for a replacement.

    We install and replace all major brands, including InSinkErator, Moen, Waste King, KitchenAid, and Badger. Our plumbers are familiar with the installation requirements for each brand, for a perfect fit.

    Key signs you need a new garbage disposal include persistent leaks from the unit's housing, a humming noise without grinding, frequent clogs despite proper usage, foul odors that won't go away, or if the unit simply won't turn on. These symptoms often indicate internal motor or component failure.

    New garbage disposals usually come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years, depending on the model and brand. FKM Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation services for your peace of mind.

    If your current disposal jams often or struggles with food waste, a 3/4 or 1 HP model can make a real difference.

    A continuous feed disposal operates when you flip a wall switch, allowing you to add food waste while it's running. A batch feed disposal only operates when a special stopper is inserted into the drain, making it generally safer as it prevents accidental activation while hands are near the opening.
