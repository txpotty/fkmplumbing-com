    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Compton, CA

    Understanding the difference between a main sewer line clog and a branch line clog is vital for proper diagnosis and repair in Compton, CA. A main line clog affects all drains in your home, often causing backups in lower-level fixtures. Branch line clogs are usually isolated to a single fixture or area. Our technicians are skilled at finding the exact location of the blockage, whether it is in the main line or a specific branch, for a targeted and efficient repair.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Compton, CA

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful method for clearing severe sewer line clogs, especially those caused by grease, scale, or heavy sludge buildup. This process uses highly pressurized water jets to scour the inside of your pipes, not only clearing the blockage but also cleaning the pipe walls. It is an excellent preventative maintenance tool and is particularly effective for older pipes in Compton that may have collected years of debris. This technique is safe for most pipe materials.

    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Compton

    FKM Plumbing uses powerful hydro-jetting technology to clear even the most stubborn sewer line blockages. This method uses high-pressure water streams to blast away grease, scale, mineral buildup, and tree roots, restoring your pipes to their original diameter. It's a very effective, non-invasive way to clean your sewer lines thoroughly and prevent future clogs. Unlike snaking, hydro-jetting cleans the whole pipe wall.
