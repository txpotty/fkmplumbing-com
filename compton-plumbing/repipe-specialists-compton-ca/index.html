    Drain Line Repiping in Compton

    While often overlooked, your home's drain lines are just as important as your supply lines.     PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Compton

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics.     Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Compton

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Earthquakes are a constant consideration in Southern California, and seismic straps and flexible connectors help keep water and gas lines intact during shaking. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.
