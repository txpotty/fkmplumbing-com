    Yard and Outdoor Leak Detection in Compton

    Toilet and Fixture Leak Detection in Compton, CA

    Toilets are one of the most common sources of wasted water. A worn flapper or a fill valve that never shuts off can leak quietly around the clock. We use dye tests to find leaking toilets and check faucets, showers and supply lines for drips. These fixes are usually quick and save water right away.

    Water Heater and Softener Leak Detection in Compton

    Water heaters and softeners can leak at fittings, valves and drain lines. A softener stuck in regeneration can send water down the drain continuously, and a dripping relief valve on a water heater wastes water too. We check these units carefully and repair or replace failed parts. That often explains a mystery bill in homes that have water softeners.

    Slab Leak Detection in Compton

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often signaled by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. FKM Plumbing uses dedicated acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is essential to preventing major foundation damage.
