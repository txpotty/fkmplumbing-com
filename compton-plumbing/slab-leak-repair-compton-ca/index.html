    Reliable Slab Leak Repair Services in Compton, CA

    Slab Leak Detection in Compton

    We start by shutting off fixtures and watching the meter to verify a leak. Then we isolate the hot and cold lines to find out which one is leaking. Using acoustic ground microphones, we listen for the sound of escaping water through the concrete. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor surface.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Compton, CA

    When a slab leak is localized and accessible, direct repair is often the most practical solution. This involves carefully jackhammering a small section of your foundation to expose the damaged pipe, then repairing or replacing the damaged section. Our technicians are skilled in precise concrete cutting and restoration, so the repair is effective and the area is properly restored. We use durable materials designed to hold up to soil movement under the slab.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Compton

    Locating a hidden slab leak requires specialized tools. Our plumbers use advanced electronic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to pinpoint the exact location of the leak under your concrete foundation. This precise detection reduces the need for extensive concrete removal, saving you time and money. We can detect leaks in both hot and cold water lines, as well as sewer lines, for a thorough diagnosis of your Compton home.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Compton

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is easiest. Reroutes eliminate future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Compton, CA

    A large slab leak can push water up through flooring or into walls quickly. We help you shut off the water and send a technician to isolate the leaking line. Often we can restore water to the rest of the house while we plan the repair. Quick action limits flooring damage and soil saturation.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Compton, CA

    If multiple slab leaks indicate a systemic issue with your home's original plumbing, a full PEX repipe might be the most cost-effective and durable solution. PEX tubing is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and less prone to leaks caused by foundation shifts, making it a good choice for many homes. We can replace all the water supply lines in your Compton home, providing a brand-new, leak-free plumbing system that will last for decades.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Compton

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is badly deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an effective long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This eliminates future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Compton with aging copper or galvanized pipes.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Compton, CA

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel. Mineral scale and constant recirculation can wear the pipe wall faster. We repair the leak or reroute the hot line, and we can adjust recirculation pumps to reduce wear. Warm floors and higher gas or electric bills are common signs.
