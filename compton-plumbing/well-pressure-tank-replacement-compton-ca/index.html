    Bladder-Style Pressure Tank Replacement in Compton, CA

    Most modern well pressure tanks are bladder-style, separating air and water for optimal efficiency. When the bladder fails, you'll experience symptoms like short-cycling of the well pump. FKM Plumbing carefully replaces these bladder-style tanks across Compton, CA, ensuring proper pre-charge pressure and system integration for long-lasting performance.

    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Compton

    Bladder tanks hold water inside a flexible butyl bag surrounded by pressurized air. When the bag ruptures, water fills the whole tank, there is no air cushion and the pump short cycles. A quick test is pressing the air valve on top, since water spitting out means the bladder has failed. We replace the tank, set the new pre-charge and verify the pump runs a full cycle. Most bladder tank swaps take about two to three hours.

    Large Capacity Tanks for Ranch and Commercial Wells in Compton

    Properties with livestock, irrigation or more than one home on a well need more storage than a single residential tank. We install multiple tanks manifolded together or large capacity models over 100 gallons. Proper sizing keeps big three-phase pumps from cycling too often. We also check the check valves and pressure ratings on the distribution lines. Work is scheduled to keep water downtime short for animals and occupants.

    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Compton, CA

    Diaphragm tanks like the Amtrol Well-X-Trol use a fixed membrane that separates air above from water below. They are tough, but the steel shell can rust through at the base after years of condensation in a hot, damp equipment space. We replace corroded diaphragm tanks and check the stand and fittings under them. The new tank is leveled and supported so it does not strain the piping. This restores steady pressure to every faucet.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Compton

    Flex-Lite composite pressure tanks are an excellent option for those seeking a lightweight, corrosion-resistant alternative to traditional steel tanks. They are especially well-suited for homes with aggressive water conditions that might accelerate corrosion in metal tanks. FKM Plumbing expertly replaces old or failing tanks with Flex-Lite models, ensuring a precise fit and proper connection to your existing well system. Their durable, fiberglass-wound construction provides long-lasting, trouble-free operation.

    Pressure Switch & Gauge Integration in Compton, CA

    A well pressure tank replacement often involves inspecting and, if necessary, replacing the associated pressure switch and pressure gauge. These components are essential for regulating your well pump's operation and monitoring system pressure. Our plumbers in Compton, CA ensure all these elements work together smoothly for a fully functional and efficient well water system.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Compton

    The tank tee is the manifold that connects the tank, switch, gauge, relief valve and drain. Galvanized tees corrode and restrict flow, especially with sandy or mineral-heavy water. We replace them with brass or stainless steel tank tees that hold up much longer. A new liquid-filled gauge gives reliable readings, and a fresh relief valve protects against a stuck switch. These small parts make a big difference in how well the system holds up.
