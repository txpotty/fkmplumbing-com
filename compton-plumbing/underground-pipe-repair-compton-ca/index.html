    Pipe bursting is a trenchless technique where a new pipe is pulled through the path of the old, broken pipe, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time.     The cost of underground pipe repair in Compton, CA varies greatly depending on the pipe's location, the extent of the damage and the repair method used.     Whether an underground pipe leak is covered by insurance depends on your homeowner's policy and the cause of the damage. Typically, sudden and accidental damage may be covered, but issues from normal wear and tear or neglect are generally not. It's best to review your policy or contact your insurance provider directly for details.

    Trenchless pipe repair offers several key advantages, including minimal disruption to your yard, landscaping and concrete, which can save on restoration costs. It's often faster than traditional excavation and less invasive, preserving the look and value of your property. This method is also durable, frequently extending the lifespan of your pipes.

    FKM Plumbing is equipped to repair a wide range of underground pipe materials, including PVC, ABS, cast iron, copper, PEX and even older materials like clay or polybutylene. Our technicians are trained on the specific repair techniques for each material.

    Common signs of a broken underground pipe include unexplained wet spots or lush patches in your yard, a sudden drop in water pressure, unusual odors such as sulfur or sewage, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets or an unexpected increase in your water bill.
