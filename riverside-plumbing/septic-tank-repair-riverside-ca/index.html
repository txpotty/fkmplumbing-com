    Septic Tank Alarm Troubleshooting in Riverside

    Many modern septic systems, especially those with pumps or advanced treatment units, include alarms to warn of high water levels or system malfunctions. If your septic alarm is sounding in Riverside, it is a clear sign of trouble. FKM Plumbing technicians can quickly troubleshoot the cause of the alarm, whether it is a float switch issue, pump failure or a clogged line, and perform the necessary repairs to silence the alarm and restore system balance.

    Effluent Pump Repair in Riverside, CA

    Homes with uphill or distant fields often use an effluent pump in a pump tank. Failed pumps, stuck floats or tripped breakers trigger high water alarms. We test the pump, float switches and control panel and replace failed parts. A working alarm gives you warning before sewage backs up.

    Leach Field Repair in Riverside, CA

    Soggy ground, strong odors or lush grass over the field can point to a struggling disposal area. Causes include clogged distribution pipes, a crushed distribution box or soil sealed by solids. We jet clean lines, replace distribution boxes and determine whether the field can be restored. If replacement is needed, we explain the design and permit steps.

    Septic Tank Lid and Riser Repair in Riverside

    Cracked or deteriorated concrete lids are a serious safety hazard. We replace failing lids and install risers that bring access up to grade. Risers make pumping and inspection faster and cheaper over time. Secure, child-resistant lids are standard on our installations.

    Emergency Septic Repair in Riverside

    Sewage backing up into tubs or floor drains is a health hazard that needs fast attention. We respond to emergency septic calls, find the blockage and restore flow.     Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Riverside

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field quickly. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where it makes sense.
