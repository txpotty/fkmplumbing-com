    New Construction Toilet Installation in Riverside

    In new homes and additions, we set toilets after the finished floor is in so the flange sits at the right height. We verify the drain is correctly vented and the rough-in matches the fixture you selected. Each toilet gets a new wax or wax-free seal, a stainless supply line and a quarter-turn angle stop. We flush-test and check for leaks before the final inspection.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Riverside, CA

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, with the tank hidden behind an access plate. They free up floor space and make cleaning easy, but they need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep or a furred-out chase. We install Geberit, Grohe and Kohler carrier systems and set the bowl height to your preference. This work is easiest during a remodel when the wall is open.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Riverside, CA

    Remodels often raise the floor with new tile, which can leave the old flange too low. We use flange extenders or replace the flange so the seal compresses properly. If you are moving the toilet, we can relocate the drain, which on a slab foundation requires cutting and patching concrete. We plan timing so the toilet goes in after tile and grout have cured.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Riverside, CA

    Upgrade to a high-efficiency toilet (HET) to save water and reduce your utility bills, a smart investment for any home. We install various HET models, including dual-flush and low-flow options, making sure they meet EPA WaterSense criteria. Our plumbers ensure strong flushing performance while maximizing water savings for your Riverside residence.

    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Riverside, CA

    Modern WaterSense toilets use 1.28 gallons per flush or less, and dual-flush models offer an even lighter option for liquid waste. Swapping an old 3.5 gallon toilet makes a noticeable difference on your water bill. Some local water providers have offered rebates for high-efficiency toilets, so it is worth checking with yours. We help you choose a model with a high MaP flush rating.
