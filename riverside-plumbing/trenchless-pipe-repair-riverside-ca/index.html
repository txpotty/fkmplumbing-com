    Pipe Bursting Services in Riverside, CA

    For severely damaged or collapsed pipes, pipe bursting offers a full replacement solution. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, breaking apart the old pipe at the same time. This provides a completely new, durable pipe, often HDPE (high-density polyethylene), which resists corrosion and leaks. It's an effective long-term fix for widespread issues in Riverside, CA.

    Cost-Effective Underground Pipe Repair in Riverside, CA

    Many homeowners in Riverside, CA are surprised to learn that trenchless pipe repair can be more cost-effective than traditional excavation. While the equipment might seem advanced, avoiding the expense of extensive digging, landscaping restoration, and potential concrete work often leads to significant overall savings on the project.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Riverside

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light. Once hardened, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Riverside, CA

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing badly damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is well suited for completely replacing a pipe segment with a new, stronger material, such as HDPE. It's often chosen when the existing pipe is collapsed or too damaged for lining. FKM Plumbing uses specialized bursting heads and winches for a smooth and efficient replacement, reducing the overall project time and cost for homeowners in Riverside, CA.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Riverside, CA

    Main water lines can also be replaced with trenchless methods. We can pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through the old path using bursting or a directional boring tool. This avoids digging across lawns, driveways and walkways. The new line is pressure tested before service is restored.

    We use trusted brands like Perma-Liner and Picote for our trenchless pipe repair projects.     Sectional Point Repair in Riverside

    When damage is confined to a specific section of pipe, sectional point repair is an ideal trenchless solution. We target only the damaged area, installing a patch or liner to seal the leak or reinforce the pipe. This method is cost-effective and minimizes the scope of work, which suits isolated issues in Riverside without affecting the entire line.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Riverside

    CIPP lining is a proven trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one.
