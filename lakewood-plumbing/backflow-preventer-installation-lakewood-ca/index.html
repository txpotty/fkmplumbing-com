    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Lakewood, CA

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Lakewood, CA, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Lakewood

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are often required for high-hazard applications in Lakewood. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. FKM Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties.     Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Lakewood

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.
