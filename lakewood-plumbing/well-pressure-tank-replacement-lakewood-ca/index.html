    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Lakewood

    Bladder tanks hold water inside a flexible butyl bag surrounded by pressurized air. When the bag ruptures, water fills the whole tank, there is no air cushion and the pump short cycles. A quick test is pressing the air valve on top, since water spitting out means the bladder has failed. We replace the tank, set the new pre-charge and verify the pump runs a full cycle. Most bladder tank swaps take about two to three hours.

    Bladder-Style Pressure Tank Replacement in Lakewood, CA

    Most modern well pressure tanks are bladder-style, separating air and water for optimal efficiency. When the bladder fails, you'll experience symptoms like short-cycling of the well pump. FKM Plumbing carefully replaces these bladder-style tanks across Lakewood, CA, ensuring proper pre-charge pressure and system integration for long-lasting performance.

    Emergency Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Lakewood

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Lakewood, CA

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop, typically at 30/50 or 40/60 psi. Pitted contacts, insect nests and a clogged sensing nipple are frequent causes of failure in well houses. We replace worn switches with Square D or similar models and clean or replace the nipple below it. A low-pressure cutoff switch can also be added to stop the pump if the well runs dry. We set the switch to match your new tank's pre-charge.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Lakewood

    Flex-Lite composite pressure tanks are an good option for those seeking a lightweight, corrosion-resistant alternative to traditional steel tanks. They are particularly well-suited for homes with aggressive water conditions that might accelerate corrosion in metal tanks. FKM Plumbing expertly replaces old or failing tanks with Flex-Lite models, ensuring a precise fit and proper connection to your existing well system. Their durable, fiberglass-wound construction provides long-lasting, trouble-free operation.

    Well-Rite Pressure Tank Installation in Lakewood, CA

    Well-Rite pressure tanks are a common choice known for their reliability and durable construction, featuring heavy-duty steel shells and high-quality butyl diaphragms. At FKM Plumbing, we regularly install Well-Rite tanks as part of our well pressure tank replacement services in Lakewood. These tanks are designed to provide consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling, extending the life of your well pump. We ensure proper sizing and installation of Well-Rite tanks, adhering to manufacturer specifications for full performance and warranty validity.

    Galvanized Steel Pressure Tank Upgrades in Lakewood

    Many older homes in Lakewood still have traditional galvanized steel pressure tanks, which can corrode over time and lose their air charge. We offer professional upgrades from these older tanks to more efficient and durable bladder-style tanks. This not only improves water pressure stability but also lengthens the life of your well pump by reducing short-cycling.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Lakewood

    The tank tee is the manifold that connects the tank, switch, gauge, relief valve and drain. Galvanized tees corrode and restrict flow, especially with sandy or mineral-heavy water. We replace them with brass or stainless steel tank tees that hold up much longer. A new liquid-filled gauge gives reliable readings, and a fresh relief valve protects against a stuck switch. These small parts make a big difference in reliability.
