    New Construction PEX Plumbing in Lakewood, CA

    FKM Plumbing works with builders and homeowners on PEX pipe installation for new construction across Lakewood, CA.     PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Lakewood

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a good option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer dependable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to confirm a proper seal.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Lakewood, CA

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is notorious for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Plenty of insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Lakewood, CA

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, much like a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to weigh cost and performance.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Lakewood

    Kitchen and bathroom remodels are a great time to replace old supply lines with PEX. We rough in new lines to the correct heights for vanities, showers, tubs and appliances, then pressure test before the walls are closed. Additions and guest suites get properly sized lines tied into your existing system. We coordinate with your contractor to keep the project schedule on track.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Lakewood

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California install softeners or scale filters. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more often follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and lowers the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

Whether it is a small office building or a multi-unit complex, our team can design and install a sturdy PEX plumbing system that meets commercial demands and codes.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Lakewood, CA

    PEX repipes typically cost less than copper repipes because the material is less expensive and installs faster. The total depends on the number of bathrooms, the home's size and how accessible the attic and walls are. Drywall patching and texture matching are often included or quoted separately. We give you a detailed estimate so you can compare PEX-A, PEX-B and copper options together.
