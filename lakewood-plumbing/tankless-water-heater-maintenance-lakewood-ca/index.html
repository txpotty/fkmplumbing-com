    Error Code Diagnostics in Lakewood, CA

    Codes like Rinnai LC, Navien E003 or Noritz 90 tell us a lot about what is going wrong. We read the code history, test flame rods, flow sensors and igniters and check gas pressure at the inlet. Many codes trace back to scale or a dirty filter and clear after maintenance. When a part has failed we explain the repair and the cost before doing any work.

    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Lakewood

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading correctly and the burner breathing properly.

    Annual Tankless Water Heater Inspection in Lakewood

    An annual inspection of your tankless water heater in Lakewood is a smart measure against costly repairs. Our thorough check includes examining the gas line connections for leaks, testing the pressure relief valve, inspecting the igniter and cleaning the air intake filter. We look for any signs of wear or potential failure, providing recommendations to keep your system running smoothly all year long, through hot spells and cold snaps alike.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Lakewood

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system completely. Restored flow means more stable temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Recirculation and Combi System Service in Lakewood, CA

    Many tankless units now include built-in recirculation pumps or buffer tanks to deliver faster hot water. These systems move more water through the exchanger and scale up sooner. We flush the recirculation loop, check pump operation and verify crossover or dedicated return line settings. Correct schedules can cut wasted energy while still giving you quick hot water.

    Rinnai Tankless Heater Servicing in Lakewood

    Rinnai units are popular for their efficiency and compact design. We also inspect the combustion chamber and ensure proper gas combustion. Our technicians are familiar with Rinnai's diagnostic codes and can address any issues proactively, improving performance for your Lakewood home.
