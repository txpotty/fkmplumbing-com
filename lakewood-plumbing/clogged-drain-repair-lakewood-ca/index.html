    Kitchen Sink Drain Clogs in Lakewood

    Kitchen drains clog from cooking grease, coffee grounds and starchy food that stick to the pipe walls and harden. Garbage disposals can push food further down the line where it collects in horizontal runs. We remove the trap, run a cable through the trap arm and branch line and clear the buildup back to the main stack. For heavy grease, we may follow with a small jetter to wash the walls clean.

    Recurring Drain Clogs and Camera Diagnosis in Lakewood, CA

    If the same drain clogs every few weeks, the problem is often a sagging pipe, a broken fitting or heavy scale. We run a small inspection camera through the line to see the inside of the pipe. You see the footage on screen with us. That lets you decide whether a deeper cleaning, a spot repair or a section replacement makes the most sense for you.

    Bathroom Sink and Vanity Drain Clogs in Lakewood, CA

    Bathroom sinks clog from hair, toothpaste and soap that bind together on the pop-up stopper and in the P-trap. Hard water can add a mineral crust that makes the pipe narrower over time. We pull and clean the stopper, clear the trap and run a small cable through the wall line. We can also replace worn pop-up assemblies and corroded tailpieces while we are there.

    Bathroom Sink & Shower Clog Repair in Lakewood, CA

    Tub and Shower Drain Clogs in Lakewood

    Hair and soap scum are the most common causes of slow tubs and showers. We remove the strainer or overflow plate and pull the clog out with a drum auger designed for small diameter drains.
