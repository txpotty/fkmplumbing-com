    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is installed and cured in place.     Clay and Orangeburg Line Replacement in Downey, CA

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, and Orangeburg fiber pipe flattens and blisters with age. Both are near the end of their service life in many older properties. When the camera shows multiple failures, replacement is usually the best value. We remove or burst the old line and install modern pipe with a long expected service life.     Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Downey, CA

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement replaces pipes without extensive digging.     Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Downey

    Cast iron drains in older homes corrode from the inside, particularly along the bottom of the pipe. Over time this leaves cracks, holes and a rough surface that catches waste.     Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Downey

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows behind. This usually needs only an entry pit and an exit pit instead of a long trench. It works well under driveways, mature trees and landscaping. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no joints for roots to enter.
