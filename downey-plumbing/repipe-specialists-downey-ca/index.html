    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes.     Efficient PEX Repiping in Downey, CA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly popular choice for repiping projects in Downey, CA. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also very resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use high-quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a reliable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Downey, CA

    Polybutylene pipes, installed in many homes between the 1970s and 1990s, are known for premature failure due to chemical reactions with disinfectants in the water supply. If your home in Downey, CA has polybutylene, a full replacement is essential to prevent major water damage. FKM Plumbing offers specialized polybutylene plumbing replacement, carefully removing the old, brittle pipes and installing a reliable new system.
