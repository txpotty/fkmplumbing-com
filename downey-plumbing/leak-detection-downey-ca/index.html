    Frequently Asked Questions About Leak Detection in Downey

    Leak detection in Downey usually costs $150 to $450 for a typical home, depending on the size of the system and the number of areas tested. Pool leak detection runs about $250 to $500. We tell you the price before we begin.

    A slab leak is a leak in the water pipes embedded within or beneath your home's concrete slab foundation. Expansive clay and hillside soils can shift over time, putting stress on slab plumbing and separating older sewer joints. Soil that shifts and settles strains plumbing lines, and temperature swings cause pipes to expand and contract over the years.

    Older galvanized steel pipes are prone to corrosion, and some older polybutylene pipes are known for premature failure. However, even modern materials like copper or PEX can leak due to improper installation, high water pressure or foundation movement.

    You can check your meter, test toilets with food coloring in the tank and look for damp spots. Finding leaks under slabs or inside walls requires specialized equipment. That is where professional detection saves time and money.

    To prevent future leaks, consider regular plumbing inspections, especially for older homes. Keep water pressure in a safe range, address any signs of corrosion and maintain your plumbing system. For homes with known issues, proactive repiping or pipe upgrades can be a long-term solution.

    Leak detection itself typically does not require a permit. However, if the leak requires significant repairs, such as replacing a section of a main water line, gas line or extensive slab repair, then a plumbing permit from Downey may be necessary. We will advise you on any permit requirements.

    Common signs include a sudden spike in your water bill, damp spots on walls or floors, mildew odors, visible mold growth, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, or a continuously running water meter even when all water is off.

    Most residential leak detection appointments take one to three hours. Larger homes, commercial buildings or multiple leaks can take longer. We give you an time estimate when we schedule.
