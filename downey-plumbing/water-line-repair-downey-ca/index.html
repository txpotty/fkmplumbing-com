    The main water line brings water from the municipal supply to your home. When this line leaks or breaks, it can cause serious water loss, foundation damage, and low water pressure.     Low water pressure can be a frustrating symptom of an underlying water line problem, such as blockages, leaks, or a failing pressure regulator.     Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Downey

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process typically takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a lengthy manufacturer warranty.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Downey, CA
