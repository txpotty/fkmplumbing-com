    Pilot Light Repair in Murrieta

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are completed in about an hour.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Murrieta

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it senses vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and trip the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as required.
