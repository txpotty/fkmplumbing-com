    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Maintenance in Murrieta

    The cost of water heater maintenance in Murrieta, CA can range from $120 to $250, based on the type of water heater (tank or tankless) and the services required. This typically includes a full flush, anode rod inspection, and safety checks. More complex issues or parts replacement would be an additional cost.

    FKM Plumbing services all major brands of water heaters, including Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, State, Kenmore, Noritz, Rinnai, and more. Our technicians are trained on a wide range of models and systems.

    Many homeowners can do a basic flush, but older drain valves can break or leak. A pro also tests safety valves, venting and gas lines.

    Many manufacturers require regular maintenance to keep warranties valid, especially for tankless units. We provide service records for your records.

    While some basic tasks like flushing the tank might seem DIY-friendly, professional maintenance makes sure all critical components, such as the anode rod, pressure relief valve, and heating elements, are properly inspected. Incorrect DIY methods can damage your unit or void warranties.

    Most manufacturers and plumbing experts recommend annual maintenance for your water heater. In homes with harder water, flushing every 6 to 12 months can be beneficial to prevent sediment buildup.

    During a water heater flush, we drain the tank completely and then refill it, letting water flow out through the drain valve until it runs clear. This process removes mineral deposits and sediment that collect at the bottom of the tank, which can hurt efficiency and cause damage.
