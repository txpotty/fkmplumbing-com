    Curbless showers have no step, which makes them easier to use and gives a modern look. They require a recessed floor and careful drain placement, often with a linear drain. On a slab, we may need to lower the floor in the shower area. We work closely with your contractor to plan the slope and waterproof layers.

    Walk-in Shower Installation in Murrieta

    Walk-in showers offer accessibility and a modern look, and FKM Plumbing is experienced with their installation. We handle the necessary drain relocation, waterproofing, and tile work to create a beautiful and functional walk-in shower.     Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Murrieta, CA
