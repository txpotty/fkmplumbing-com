    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, especially in closed-loop systems. Plumbing work in Southern California follows the statewide plumbing code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code with state amendments. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to support proper system operation and prevent premature wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Murrieta.     Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Murrieta

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon tank water heater typically costs $1,600 to $3,500 installed. Tankless installations often cost $3,000 to $6,500 depending on gas line and venting needs.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, water heater installation involves working with gas lines, high voltage electricity and water connections, all of which carry significant risks if not handled correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, gas explosions, electrical fires and carbon monoxide poisoning. It can also void your warranty, and local codes typically require a licensed plumber for this work.

    FKM Plumbing installs all major trusted brands of water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz. We work with both gas and electric models, as well as various sizes and efficiencies. Our team can help you choose the best brand and model to suit your hot water demands and budget.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. FKM Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, giving you peace of mind. We will discuss specific warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. For tank units, a 40 to 50 gallon tank is common for 2 to 4 people, while larger families might need 60 to 80 gallons. For tankless units, it is about flow rate (GPM) and temperature rise. Our plumbers will assess your needs to recommend the right size.

    Common signs that you might need a new water heater include a lack of hot water, rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit and an age of 10 to 15 years or more. If you notice any of these issues, it is best to have a professional inspection.

    Most households of two to four people do well with a 40 or 50 gallon tank. Larger families may need a 75 gallon tank or a tankless unit sized by gallons per minute.

    The decision to repair or replace often depends on the age of your unit, the cost of the repair and its energy efficiency. If your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down, or the repair cost is more than 50% of a new unit, replacement is often the more cost-effective choice. Newer models also offer significantly better energy efficiency.
