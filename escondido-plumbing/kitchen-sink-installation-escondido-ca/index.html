    Workstation Sink Installation in Escondido, CA

    Workstation sinks include ledges for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks. They are often deep, single-bowl designs with offset drains. We position the drain to fit your cabinet and disposal and confirm the faucet reach. These sinks are a common pick in modern kitchen remodels.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Escondido, CA

    Farmhouse or apron-front sinks add a distinctive look to any kitchen, but their installation requires specific expertise due to their weight and unique mounting requirements.     Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Escondido

    Undermount sinks need strong clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone, and support it while the adhesive sets. Then we install strainers, traps and supply lines. A proper undermount install helps prevent gaps and leaks along the counter edge.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Escondido

    Top-mount, or drop-in, sinks are a common and straightforward option for many Southern California homes. While simpler to install, proper sealing and alignment are still important to prevent water damage and ensure stability. Our plumbers in Escondido install top-mount sinks, applying high-quality sealant around the rim to create a watertight barrier. We make sure the sink is securely fastened and all plumbing connections, including the faucet and drain, are properly tightened and tested for leaks.

    Kitchen Sink Installation Cost Factors in Escondido, CA

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Escondido

    Drop-in sinks rest on the countertop with a rim, making them a good choice for laminate and tile. We check the cutout size, seal the rim, and tighten mounting clips evenly. This is often the fastest and most affordable kitchen sink upgrade. It also works well in rentals and older homes.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Escondido
