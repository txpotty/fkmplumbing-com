    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Escondido, CA

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial protector against corrosion. Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California add softeners or scale filters. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's badly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Escondido, CA, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Escondido, CA

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing widely. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank. A leak in an attic tank without a working pan can destroy a ceiling, so we confirm that protection is in place.

    Electric Hot Water Tank Element Check in Escondido

    Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout. We also check the thermostat settings and wiring connections to confirm they are functioning correctly and safely.     Sediment Flush and Descaling in Escondido

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause loud popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Escondido, CA

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can block the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.
