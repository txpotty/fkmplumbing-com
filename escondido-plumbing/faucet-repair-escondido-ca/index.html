    Compression Faucet Repair in Escondido

    Compression faucets, often found in older Escondido homes, are known for their separate hot and cold handles. When these faucets leak, it's typically due to worn-out rubber washers or packing. Our team is skilled in disassembling these classic fixtures, replacing the necessary seals and washers, and reassembling them to stop leaks for good. We respect the character of older homes and make sure these repairs are done carefully.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Escondido, CA

    Many modern single-handle faucets, commonly found in newer homes, use a cartridge system.     Ball Faucet Repair in Escondido, CA

    Ball faucets, recognizable by their single lever that moves over a rounded cap, are common in many kitchens. They use a rotating ball joint to control water flow and temperature. If your ball faucet is leaking from the spout or base, or if the handle feels loose, it's probably time for a repair. We can replace the spring-loaded rubber seats and O-rings, which are typically the culprits, restoring smooth operation and preventing drips.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Escondido, CA

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base points to failed O-rings or a loose mounting nut. If left alone, the leak can soak into the cabinet below and damage the sink base. We replace O-rings, tighten mounting hardware and reseal the base. We also check the deck for water stains.
