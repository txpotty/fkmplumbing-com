    Pinhole Leak Detection in Escondido, CA

    Pinhole leaks, often found in copper or PEX piping, are small but can cause significant damage over time, especially behind walls or under floors. We employ non-invasive moisture meters to detect elevated humidity levels and thermal cameras to identify cold spots that indicate water. Our plumbers are adept at tracing these elusive leaks without tearing apart your entire home. Swift action on pinhole leaks prevents extensive drywall damage, mold growth and compromised structural integrity.     Main Service Line Leak Detection in Escondido, CA

    The line from the meter to your house carries full pressure and can leak underground for a long time. Wet soil, soft spots or unusually green patches of yard may be the only clues.     Water Heater and Softener Leak Detection in Escondido

    Water heaters and softeners can leak at fittings, valves and drain lines.     Toilet and Fixture Leak Detection in Escondido, CA
