    For most homes in Escondido, annual or twice-yearly sewer line cleaning is sufficient, especially if you have mature trees nearby or an older plumbing system. If you have frequent clogs or a high-usage commercial property, more frequent cleaning might be wise to prevent major backups.

    Lines with roots or past clogs often need cleaning every 12 to 24 months. Newer PVC lines without issues may go years without service.

    Common causes include tree root intrusion, buildup of grease and food particles, flushing non-biodegradable items like wet wipes or feminine hygiene products, and structural issues like cracked or collapsed pipes. Older homes frequently have clay pipes that are prone to root invasion.

    Drain cleaning usually addresses blockages within individual drain pipes connected to sinks, showers, or toilets.
