    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Ontario

    Many water utilities install pressure regulators or backflow devices that create a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure spikes can strain your tank and fixtures. Code commonly requires a thermal expansion tank on these systems. We install a properly sized expansion tank and pre-charge it to match your water pressure. This simple add-on can help your new heater last longer.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Ontario, CA

    Big families, soaking tubs and multiple showers running at once often need a 75 gallon tank or a high-recovery 50 gallon model. These heaters use larger burners, sometimes 75,000 BTU, and may need a bigger gas line and flue. We check your meter and pipe size to make sure the larger tank gets the gas it needs. A high-recovery unit can be a good middle ground when space is tight. We walk you through recovery rates so you choose the right fit.

    Standard Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Ontario

    Many homes in Ontario rely on standard atmospheric vent gas water heaters. When these units reach the end of their lifespan, typically 10 to 12 years, we provide professional replacement.     Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Ontario

    Power vent gas water heaters use an electric blower to push exhaust through PVC pipe, which lets them sit far from a chimney or roof flue. When the blower motor or pressure switch fails repeatedly, replacing the whole unit can be the better choice. We install new power vent models and confirm a dedicated outlet is available. Venting length and number of elbows must stay within the manufacturer's limits. Power vent tanks cost more but solve difficult placement problems.
