    Copper pipes can leak due to several factors, including corrosion from water chemistry when the pH is too acidic or alkaline, aggressive soil conditions, high water pressure, poor installation or physical damage from foundation shifts. Around Ontario, flushing the water heater yearly and cleaning aerators keeps mineral buildup from cutting flow and efficiency. Pinhole leaks often result from long-term internal corrosion.

    We typically use Type L copper, which has thicker walls than Type M and lasts longer. In some situations we use ProPress fittings or a PEX transition.

    Type K, L and M copper pipes differ mainly in their wall thickness and thus their pressure ratings. Type K has the thickest walls and is used for underground service and main water lines. Type L is common for interior water supply and radiant heating. Type M has the thinnest walls and is usually used for less demanding applications. We are skilled in repairing all types.

    PEX resists the corrosion and scale that cause copper pinholes and is flexible and quick to install. If you have repeated copper leaks, repiping with PEX is often a cost-effective long-term solution.
