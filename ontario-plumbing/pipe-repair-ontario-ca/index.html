    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Ontario, CA

    PEX and CPVC are common in homes built over the last few decades. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic.     Leaking Pipe Repair in Ontario, CA

    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Ontario

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in older homes and rusts from the inside until flow drops and leaks start at threaded joints. Repairs are possible, but every disturbed fitting can start a new leak. We replace failing sections with PEX or copper using dielectric unions to stop galvanic corrosion. For homes with widespread galvanized piping, we will quote a full repipe so you can weigh both options.

    PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Ontario

    PEX piping is a popular, flexible and freeze-resistant alternative to traditional copper or PVC, often used in newer construction and remodels in Ontario. Our team is skilled in PEX pipe repair, addressing crimp failures or accidental punctures. We also offer PEX pipe installation, a reliable, corrosion-resistant and cost-effective solution for re-piping projects that delivers a leak-free plumbing system for years to come.
