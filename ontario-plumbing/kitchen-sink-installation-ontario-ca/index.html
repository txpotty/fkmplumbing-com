    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Ontario

    Drop-in sinks rest on the countertop with a rim, making them a good choice for laminate and tile. We check the cutout size, seal the rim, and tighten mounting clips evenly. This is often the fastest and most affordable kitchen sink upgrade. It also works well in rentals and older homes.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Ontario, CA

    Workstation sinks include ledges for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks. They are usually deep, single-bowl designs with offset drains. We position the drain to fit your cabinet and disposal and confirm the faucet reach. These sinks are a common pick in modern kitchen remodels.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Ontario

    Double bowl sinks need a continuous waste or end outlet layout to drain both sides through one trap. We plan the layout around your disposal and dishwasher connections. Proper slope keeps both sides draining freely. We use heavy-wall fittings that resist leaks.

    Under-mount Sink Installation in Ontario, CA

    Under-mount sinks provide a sleek, seamless look, making countertop cleaning easier. Their installation is more complex than top-mount sinks, requiring precise measurements and strong adhesive or clips for secure attachment to the underside of the counter. FKM Plumbing excels in under-mount sink installation, ensuring a robust bond and a perfectly sealed edge to prevent water infiltration. We confirm the cutout is smooth and the sink is level for proper drainage.

    Composite Sink Installation in Ontario

    Composite sinks, made from materials like granite or quartz composites, offer excellent durability and a wide range of colors to match your kitchen decor. Installing these sinks requires careful handling to prevent damage and get a perfect fit. Our plumbers in Ontario are experienced with composite sink installation, using appropriate tools and techniques for secure mounting and sealing. We ensure all drain and faucet connections are tight and leak-free, providing you with a long-lasting and attractive kitchen fixture.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Ontario

    Top-mount, or drop-in, sinks are a common and straightforward option for many Southern California homes. While simpler to install, proper sealing and alignment are still important to prevent water damage and ensure stability. Our plumbers in Ontario install top-mount sinks, applying durable sealant around the rim to create a watertight barrier. We make sure the sink is securely fastened and all plumbing connections, including the faucet and drain, are properly tightened and tested for leaks.
