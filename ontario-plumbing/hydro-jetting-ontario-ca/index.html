    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Ontario

    Residential hydro jetting in Ontario typically costs $350 to $800, depending on line length, access and how heavy the buildup is. Commercial jetting is often priced by the hour and may run higher.

    Small consumer jetters exist, but they lack the pressure and flow of professional units. High-pressure water can also injure untrained users and harm fragile pipe.

    Snaking uses a rotating cable to break through a clog. Jetting uses pressurized water to clean the entire pipe wall and flush debris away.

    Hydro jetting is exceptionally effective against almost all types of clogs. It can break through solid obstructions like tree roots, flush out heavy grease and food buildup, clear mineral scale, and remove compacted sludge and debris that traditional snaking often leaves behind.

    For most modern and well-maintained pipes, hydro jetting is very safe. However, for very old, corroded, or severely damaged pipes, the high pressure could cause further damage. That is why FKM Plumbing always performs a camera inspection first to check your pipe's condition and confirm hydro jetting is appropriate.

    For most homes in Ontario, hydro jetting every 1 to 3 years can be a good preventative measure, especially if you have recurring drain issues or older pipes. Businesses, particularly restaurants, may benefit from quarterly or twice-yearly hydro jetting to manage grease buildup. We can suggest a schedule based on your needs.

    While hydro jetting effectively removes existing tree roots from your sewer line, it does not prevent new roots from growing back. For a long-term solution to recurring root intrusion, we might recommend trenchless sewer repair or pipe replacement, which we can discuss during our assessment.

    The length of a hydro jetting service in Ontario varies depending on the severity and location of the clog. A typical residential drain might take 1 to 2 hours, while a major sewer line or commercial job could take 3 to 4 hours or more. Our technicians work efficiently to minimize disruption.
