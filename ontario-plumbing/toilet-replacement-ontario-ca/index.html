    Signs of failing internal toilet parts include a toilet that runs constantly, a weak or incomplete flush, a handle that needs jiggling, or visible wear and tear on components like the flapper, fill valve, or flush valve. While some parts can be replaced one at a time, an older toilet with multiple failing components might benefit more from a full replacement.

    Older toilets can use 3.5 to 7 gallons per flush, while WaterSense toilets use 1.28 gallons or less. For a typical household, that can mean thousands of gallons saved each year.

    We frequently recommend and install reliable brands like American Standard, Kohler, Toto, and Delta. These brands offer a range of durable, water-efficient models that perform well across a wide range of water conditions and are known for their long service life.

    Key signs include persistent leaks that cannot be sealed, a cracked bowl or tank, frequent clogging despite repairs, or an old toilet that uses too much water. If your toilet is constantly running or has visible damage, replacement is often the more cost-effective long-term solution.
