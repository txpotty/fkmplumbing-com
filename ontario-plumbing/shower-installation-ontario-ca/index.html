    Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Installation in Ontario

    The cost of shower installation in Ontario varies widely, typically ranging from $1,500 to $6,000 for standard units, and $5,000 to $15,000 or more for custom or luxury systems. Factors like shower type, materials, plumbing changes, and labor complexity affect the final price. We provide upfront, detailed quotes after assessing your specific project.

    Plumbing rough-in usually takes 1 to 2 days, and trim installation takes a few hours. The full remodel timeline depends on tile and glass work.

    Pressure-balance valves keep the hot and cold ratio steady when pressure changes. Thermostatic valves control the actual temperature and allow separate volume control, which suits multi-head showers.

    DIY shower installation is generally not recommended due to the complexity of plumbing connections, waterproofing, and local building codes. Improper installation can lead to costly leaks, water damage, mold growth, and unsafe conditions. Hiring a professional like FKM Plumbing ensures the job is done correctly, safely, and to code, saving you potential headaches and expenses down the line.

    Shower plumbing is hidden in walls and floors, so mistakes lead to expensive leaks. We recommend a licensed plumber, especially when permits and inspections are involved.

    A standard shower stall installation can often be completed in one to two days. More complex projects, such as custom walk-in showers with extensive tile work or plumbing rerouting, might take three to five days, or even longer. We provide a clear timeline during your first consultation.

    New showers, tub-to-shower conversions and drain relocations often require a permit. Requirements vary by city, and we check and handle permits when needed.
