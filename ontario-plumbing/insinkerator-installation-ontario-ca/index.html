    Hard Water and Mineral Considerations in Ontario

    Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California add softeners or scale filters. Hard water leaves scale on disposal grind rings, sink flanges and drain tailpieces. Over time that buildup can make a disposal noisy and cause slow drainage. We inspect the drain line for scale and debris before connecting a new unit. If heavy buildup is present, we recommend cleaning or replacing the trap and tailpiece at the same time. Stainless steel grind components on Evolution models resist corrosion better in hard water.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Ontario

    Most InSinkErator units have a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. We knock that plug out, clear the debris and connect the dishwasher drain hose with a proper clamp.
