    Slab Leak Detection in Ontario

    We start by shutting off fixtures and watching the meter to confirm a leak. Then we isolate the hot and cold lines to find out which one is leaking. Using acoustic ground microphones, we listen for the sound of escaping water through the concrete. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor surface.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Ontario

    Cold water slab leaks are harder to feel but still waste water and wet the soil. They often show up as a high water bill or a meter that moves with all fixtures off. We isolate the cold line, locate the leak and repair or reroute it. Fixing cold leaks promptly helps protect your foundation.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Ontario

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method bypasses the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Ontario with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Ontario, CA

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel. Mineral scale and constant recirculation can wear the pipe wall faster. We repair the leak or reroute the hot line, and we can adjust recirculation pumps to limit wear. Warm floors and higher gas or electric bills are common signs.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Ontario, CA

    When the leak is isolated and the rest of the pipe is in good shape, a spot repair through the slab is a practical option. We cut a small opening, dig down to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The trench is compacted and the concrete is patched. Flooring is then restored by your preferred contractor.
