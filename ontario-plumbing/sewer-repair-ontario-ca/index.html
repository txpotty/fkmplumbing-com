    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Ontario, CA

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive solution. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from major disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an efficient and popular choice for protecting property appearance in Ontario.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Ontario

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can usually use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Ontario

    Before any repair, we use high-resolution video camera inspection to precisely locate and assess sewer line damage. This involves inserting a flexible rod with a camera into your sewer line, allowing us to see blockages, cracks, root intrusion or pipe collapses in real time. This non-invasive method prevents unnecessary digging and helps us provide a clear diagnosis and repair plan. It's crucial for understanding issues in older homes in Ontario and identifying problems like bellied pipes or collapsed sections.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Ontario

    Every sewer repair starts with a look inside the pipe. We run a self-leveling camera from your cleanout or a pulled toilet and record the full length of the line. A sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact location and depth of every problem on the surface. You get a copy of the video and a plain-English summary of what we found. This step alone frequently saves homeowners from paying for repairs they do not need.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Ontario

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's much more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and preventing recurring clogs. This service is particularly helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Ontario dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with recurring drain issues.

    When repairs are no longer sufficient due to extensive damage, age or material failure, sewer pipe replacement becomes necessary.
