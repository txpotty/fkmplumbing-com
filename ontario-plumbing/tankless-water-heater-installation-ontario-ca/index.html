    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Ontario, CA

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Municipal water across much of Southern California is hard, leaving white scale on faucets, showerheads, and inside water heater tanks. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Ontario

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to deliver hot water to distant fixtures. A recirculation pump or dedicated return line keeps hot water close to faucets, reducing wait time and water waste. Some units have built-in pumps with smart scheduling. We design the system to suit your plumbing layout and usage patterns.
