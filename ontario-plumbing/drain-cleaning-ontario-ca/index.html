    Hydro jetting is best for tough, persistent clogs, especially those caused by heavy grease buildup, mineral deposits, or significant tree root intrusion. It provides a more complete cleaning than traditional snaking, scouring the entire pipe interior.

    If a line clogs repeatedly or you are buying an older home, yes. A camera shows breaks, sags and root entry that cleaning alone cannot fix.

    To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens to trap hair and food particles, flush only toilet paper, and consider periodic professional drain maintenance, especially if you have older pipes or large trees nearby.

    Usually, you do not need a permit for routine drain cleaning or unclogging a drain. Permits are generally required for more extensive plumbing work, such as replacing a sewer line or making major changes to your plumbing system. We will advise you if any permit is needed for your situation.
