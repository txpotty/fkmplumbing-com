    Battery Backup Sump Pump System Installation in Ontario

    When heavy winter storms do arrive, runoff can overwhelm yard drains and storm systems, and burn areas can send debris into low-lying homes.     Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Ontario

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation stops sewer gas odors.

    Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Ontario

    Businesses in Ontario also rely on effective sump pump systems to protect their commercial properties. We provide specialized commercial sump pump replacement services, handling larger, more complex systems designed for industrial or multi-unit buildings.     Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Ontario

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Ontario

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Ontario for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, appropriately sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and improve efficiency. That is crucial for handling heavy rainfall.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Ontario

    If your current sump pump struggles to keep up with heavy water flow, especially during intense thunderstorms, it may be undersized. We offer high-capacity sump pump replacements and upgrades designed to handle large water volumes. Our plumbers will assess your water intrusion patterns and recommend a pump with greater horsepower and a higher GPM (gallons per minute) rating. This prevents the pump from cycling too often or failing under stress, common issues in some Ontario properties.

    Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Ontario

    Commercial buildings often have elevator pits, parking garage drains and mechanical room sumps. These require pumps rated for higher flow and sometimes oil-sensing controls. We replace commercial pumps and alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. Duplex systems with alternating pumps provide a backup. We schedule work to avoid interrupting operations.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Ontario

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Ontario

    A standard sump pump replacement typically costs $500 to $1,200 installed. Battery backup systems usually add $800 to $1,800. Sewage ejector pumps usually cost $1,200 to $2,500.

    Sump pumps generally have a lifespan of 7 to 10 years. Even if they appear to be working, their efficiency can decrease over time. Regular inspection and testing are crucial, and replacement is advisable as they approach the end of their expected service life.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 3 to 5 years. We also offer a labor warranty.

    While it might seem straightforward, professional sump pump replacement is highly recommended. It involves electrical connections, proper plumbing for the discharge line, and making sure the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding and comply with local Ontario codes.

    Most standard sump pump replacements can be completed within 2 to 4 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new pit excavation or major discharge line work, might take longer, up to a full day.

    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods can fail earlier due to stuck switches.

    Simple replacements typically do not require permits. New discharge lines or electrical work may, depending on your city.

    To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by FKM Plumbing can also help lengthen its lifespan.
