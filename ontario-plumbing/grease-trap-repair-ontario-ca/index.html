    A leaking grease trap can cause significant property damage, foul odors, and potential health code violations. Leaks often stem from corroded tanks, cracked seams, or failing pipe connections.     Grease Trap Odor Control & Repair in Ontario

    Persistent foul odors coming from your grease trap are a sign of inefficiency, excessive FOG accumulation, or bacterial growth.     Baffle Repair and Replacement in Ontario

    Baffles slow incoming water so grease can rise and solids can settle. When baffles crack, corrode or break loose, grease passes straight through the trap. We remove damaged baffles and install new ones made for your trap model. In concrete interceptors, we can repair or rebuild baffle walls. Restoring baffles brings back proper separation and helps you stay compliant.

    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Ontario, CA

    Simple repairs like gasket or tee replacements usually cost far less than a new trap. Major damage such as a cracked tank body, failing concrete or a badly corroded steel unit may make replacement the better value. We compare repair cost, remaining life and compliance risk for you. Older steel traps often rust through and are good candidates for upgrade. We give you options and pricing in writing so you can decide.

    Beyond the trap itself, the drain lines leading to and from the grease trap can also suffer damage or blockages. These lines are particularly susceptible to grease build-up. In Ontario, we diagnose and repair issues within these connecting pipes, including clearing FOG accumulation, fixing collapsed sections, or repairing root intrusions.
