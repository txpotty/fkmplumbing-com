    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Line Installation in Upland

    The cost of gas line installation in Upland, CA varies widely depending on the length of the line, the piping material, routing complexity and the number of appliances. A simple appliance hookup might range from $400 to $800, while a whole-home installation could be several thousand dollars. We provide a detailed estimate after an on-site assessment.

    Common materials include black iron pipe, which is very durable, and corrugated stainless steel tubing, which offers flexibility and can be faster to install. Copper tubing is sometimes used for smaller diameter lines but is less common for main lines. Our team chooses the best material for your application and local codes.

    A rotten egg or sulfur smell, a hissing sound near a line or appliance, and dead vegetation over a buried line are warning signs. If you smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility immediately.

    We primarily install black iron pipe and corrugated stainless steel tubing, or CSST. Black iron is a traditional, rigid option, while CSST offers flexibility and faster installation. Both are safe and code-compliant when installed by licensed professionals, and the choice usually depends on the application and property layout.

    FKM Plumbing stands behind our work with a comprehensive warranty on our gas line installations. This typically covers workmanship for a set period and may include manufacturer warranties on materials. Specific warranty details will be provided with your project estimate.

    We use schedule 40 black iron, CSST flexible tubing and yellow polyethylene for underground runs. The choice is based on location, code requirements and the appliance.

    We use proven, industry-standard brands for gas line materials and fittings for durability and safety. This includes manufacturers like Gastite and OmegaFlex for CSST, and high-quality black iron pipe and fittings from reputable suppliers. All materials are chosen to meet or exceed local plumbing codes.

    CSST bonding connects the gas piping to the home's electrical grounding system to reduce damage from lightning strikes. It is a code and manufacturer requirement we follow on every install.
