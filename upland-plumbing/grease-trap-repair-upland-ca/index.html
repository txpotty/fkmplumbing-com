    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Upland

    Baffles slow incoming water so grease can rise and solids can settle. When baffles crack, corrode or break loose, grease passes straight through the trap. We remove damaged baffles and install new ones made for your trap model. In concrete interceptors, we can repair or rebuild baffle walls. Restoring baffles brings back proper separation and helps you stay compliant.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Upland

    When traditional snaking isn't enough, hydro-jetting is our go-to method for severe grease trap blockages. This powerful technique uses high-pressure water streams to fully scour the interior of pipes and the grease trap itself, removing years of FOG buildup, scale, and debris. It's a very effective, non-invasive method that not only clears clogs but also cleans the lines, significantly reducing the likelihood of future blockages in your Upland business.

    Flow Control Device Repair in Upland

    Hydromechanical traps rely on a flow control fitting to keep water moving at the rated speed. If this fitting is missing or clogged, the trap can be overwhelmed and grease escapes.
