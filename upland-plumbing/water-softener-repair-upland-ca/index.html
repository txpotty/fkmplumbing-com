    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Upland, CA

    A salt bridge, a crust of salt that forms in the brine tank, can prevent your water softener from regenerating properly. This leads to hard water throughout your home. Our team can effectively break up and remove salt bridges, clean the brine tank, and advise on best practices to prevent future occurrences. We also repair leaks or damage to the brine tank itself, frequent in older systems.

    Hard Water Symptoms & Diagnostics in Upland

    If you're noticing scale buildup on faucets, cloudy dishes, dry skin and hair, or clothes that feel stiff after washing, your water softener likely needs attention. FKM Plumbing offers complete diagnostic services to identify the root cause of these hard water symptoms.     Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Upland, CA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates suction to draw brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. When it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying. We clean or replace the injector and screen and check the brine line for leaks.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Upland, CA

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, regulating the flow of water and initiating regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water, regenerate too often, or leak. We diagnose issues like stuck pistons, worn out seals, motor failures, or programming errors. Our plumbers carry a range of replacement parts for popular brands like Fleck, Clack, and Autotrol, ensuring a quick and effective repair to restore proper operation.
