    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Upland

    For severely damaged or collapsed sewer lines, traditional trenching is often the most reliable solution. This involves excavating a trench along the sewer line's path to allow for direct access and replacement of the entire pipe. While more invasive, it provides a complete overhaul and is often necessary for older homes with clay or cast iron pipes that have reached the end of their lifespan. We ensure careful planning and execution to minimize disruption to your yard.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Upland, CA

    When appropriate, trenchless sewer replacement offers a less invasive alternative to traditional digging. Methods like pipe bursting or pipe lining allow us to replace or repair your sewer line with minimal excavation, often only requiring two access points. This can save your lawn, driveway, and landscaping from major damage, making it a common choice for homeowners in Upland who want to preserve their property's appearance. We'll determine if your sewer line is a good candidate for this advanced technique.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Upland, CA

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the right option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench once it passes inspection. This method allows full correction of slope issues.

    Under-Slab Sewer Replacement in Upland

    Many homes are built on slab foundations with drain lines underneath. When these lines fail, replacement requires tunneling or rerouting. We evaluate options to limit impact on your home. Rerouting through walls or attic space is sometimes possible for branch lines. We explain the cost and disruption of each approach.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Upland

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It needs only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are completed in one day.
