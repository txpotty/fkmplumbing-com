    Residential Tankless Water Heater Flushing in Upland

    Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California install softeners or scale filters. Hard water can lead to heavy mineral buildup in tankless water heaters. Our residential flushing service in Upland involves circulating a descaling solution through your unit's heat exchanger to remove calcium and magnesium deposits. This process brings back efficiency, prevents overheating and extends the life of your appliance, so your family gets continuous hot water without interruption.

    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Upland

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is protected where it runs outdoors.

    Commercial Tankless System Checks in Upland, CA

    Commercial tankless water heaters face higher demand and require more frequent, detailed maintenance. Our service for businesses in Upland includes detailed inspections of multiple units, checking for proper gas pressure, water flow rates and exhaust venting.     Isolation Valve Kit Installation in Upland, CA

    Some tankless heaters were installed without service valves, which makes proper flushing very difficult. We install a set of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines so future maintenance takes minutes. The kit also lets you shut off water to the heater without closing the main. It is a one-time upgrade that pays for itself in easier service.
