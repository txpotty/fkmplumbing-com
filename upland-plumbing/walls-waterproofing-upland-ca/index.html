    Lasting Walls Waterproofing Solutions in Upland, CA

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Upland

    A slow drip from a shower valve, supply stub or drain fitting can soak a wall for months before you notice. We locate the leak with moisture meters, thermal imaging and pressure tests. The damaged section of pipe or valve is replaced, and wet drywall is cut out and dried. Once the cavity is dry, we restore the wall and seal any wet-area walls.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Upland

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra attention before new tile is set.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Upland

    Restaurants, gyms, salons and offices often have restrooms, locker rooms and kitchens with constant water exposure. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with durable membranes. Work can be scheduled after hours to reduce downtime. We also repair the plumbing behind those walls when leaks are discovered.

    Drainage System Integration in Upland, CA

    Lasting walls waterproofing often requires a solid drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Upland, CA properties, so water is efficiently managed and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Upland

    Copper pinhole leaks and corroded cast iron drains are common repair calls in homes that are now several decades old. Wherever a home has a basement or below-grade walls, heavy rain and snowmelt can push water through foundation walls and cracks. We seal cracks with injection products, apply interior or exterior waterproofing and install drainage where needed. A sump pump may be added if groundwater builds up.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Upland

    Many homes in Upland have crawl spaces, which are highly susceptible to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting prevents ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an affordable way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Upland, CA

    Basement wall sealing is a key part of walls waterproofing. Hot inland summers push water heaters and outdoor plumbing hard, while coastal salt air can corrode exposed metal fittings. We apply high-quality sealants to porous concrete and masonry walls to prevent moisture from wicking through and causing dampness, mold, or musty odors. This service can be performed alone or as part of a larger waterproofing system, offering a drier and healthier environment in your Upland, CA basement.
