    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Long Beach, CA

    Sewer lines in Long Beach, CA can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or misalign. Most homes in Southern California sit on concrete slabs, so slab leaks often show up as warm floors, damp carpet, or an unexplained jump in the water bill. Tree root intrusion is another major cause, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also contribute to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Long Beach

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We determine the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Long Beach, CA

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is particularly useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add many years of service.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Long Beach

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for extensive trenching. It is best for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Long Beach that require full replacement rather than just relining.
