    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Long Beach

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Long Beach. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. FKM Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with trusted brands like Watts and Febco.

    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in Long Beach, CA

    Irrigation systems in Long Beach, CA are a common source of backflow, as stagnant water and lawn chemicals can be drawn back into the potable water supply. Installing a dedicated backflow preventer for your sprinkler system is often a local code requirement. We are experienced in selecting and installing the correct device, such as a pressure vacuum breaker or atmospheric vacuum breaker, to protect your home's main water line from contaminants introduced by your landscape irrigation.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Long Beach, CA

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested. We carry trusted brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Long Beach, CA

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. On an occasional cold night, covering backflow preventers and outdoor valves is usually all it takes to avoid a break. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your location. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and repairs.
