    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Long Beach, CA

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor. Postwar tract homes are common in Southern California, and many still have original galvanized or copper supply lines and cast iron drains under the slab. Over time the inside of cast iron corrodes, scales and develops a channel along the bottom. Aggressive cutters can break thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron. This restores flow and gives a clear view on camera of the remaining wall. It also helps you decide if lining is a good option.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Long Beach

    Older properties may still have vitrified clay or even Orangeburg fiber pipe running to the street. Clay joints are open invitations for roots, and Orangeburg can flatten under soil weight. We clean these lines gently and use the camera to map joints and soft spots. If the pipe is failing, we will tell you plainly and show the footage. Cleaning keeps you running while you plan a replacement.

    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Long Beach, CA

    Grease from kitchens cools and coats the inside of sewer pipes, and minerals in the water bond with it to form a tough crust. Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California add softeners or scale filters. Over years the pipe opening shrinks until flow slows to a trickle. A cable can punch a hole through, but jetting cleans the walls back to full size. We choose the method based on pipe material and condition. Cleaning before the line fully closes saves money and stress.
