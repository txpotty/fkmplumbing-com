    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Long Beach

    The cost of water heater replacement in Long Beach, CA usually ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 for a standard tank unit, including installation. Tankless units can range from $3,000 to $5,000 or more, depending on the model and needed plumbing modifications. Factors like unit type, capacity, brand and labor complexity influence the final price.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, a metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit or a water heater that is over 10 to 12 years old. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a licensed plumber.

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and A.O. Smith water heaters. Each offers reliable performance and a range of warranties.

    Tank water heaters often last about 8 to 12 years, and hard water and sediment buildup can shorten that. Municipal water across much of Southern California is hard, leaving white scale on faucets, showerheads, and inside water heater tanks. Annual flushing and anode rod checks can help extend that life.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but often have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.

    Choosing the right size depends on the number of people in your household, their hot water usage habits and peak demand times. Our technicians will calculate the right GPM (gallons per minute) for tankless or gallon capacity for tank models so you never run out of hot water, especially in multi-bathroom Long Beach homes.

    Yes, in many areas of Long Beach and Southern California, a plumbing permit is required for water heater replacement to make sure the installation meets local safety and building codes. FKM Plumbing handles all required permitting on your behalf, so you do not have to worry about compliance.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line changes.
