    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Long Beach

    Pedestal sump pumps are an good choice for narrow or shallow sump pits, as their motor sits above the water level. This design makes them easier to maintain and can sometimes offer a longer lifespan because the motor stays out of the water. FKM Plumbing can expertly install pedestal sump pumps in your Long Beach home, with the right sizing and setup for effective water removal without crowding your pit.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Long Beach

    Zoeller pumps are known for their durability and reliability, making them a popular choice among homeowners. FKM Plumbing is experienced with installing many Zoeller sump pump models, including the M53 Mighty-Mate and other high-performance series. We make sure your Zoeller pump is correctly tied into your Long Beach home's plumbing to deliver steady, flood-preventing service, backed by the manufacturer's warranty.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Long Beach

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which saves wear on the motor. Freezing pipes are rare in most of Southern California, and many homes have exposed outdoor plumbing that would never pass in a harsher climate. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the line.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Long Beach

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They typically last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we generally recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.

    Sump Pump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Long Beach

    A complete sump pump system requires a properly installed basin and discharge line. We handle the excavation and placement of the sump basin, setting it at the lowest point of your basement floor so it collects water well. We also install the discharge line, making sure it is correctly sloped, sealed and routed away from your foundation to a suitable drainage area, so water does not find its way back into your Long Beach property.
