    Efficient PEX Repiping in Long Beach, CA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very popular choice for repiping projects in Long Beach, CA. PEX is also very resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use high-quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a dependable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.
