    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Repair in Long Beach

    Most disposal repairs, like clearing jams, fixing flange leaks or replacing a switch, cost $125 to $300. If replacement is needed, a new installed unit usually runs $275 to $700.

    Frequent jams, constant resets, leaks from the body, loud grinding and age over ten years are signs replacement is the better choice.

    Common signs include unusual noises like grinding or humming without grinding, a complete lack of power, persistent leaks from under the sink, or foul odors that won't go away. If you notice any of these, it's a good idea to call a licensed plumber.

    If your garbage disposal is found to be beyond economical repair, our plumber will provide options for a new garbage disposal installation. We can help you choose a good replacement unit that meets your needs and budget, and then professionally install it.

    To prevent future problems, avoid putting grease, coffee grounds, eggshells, fibrous vegetables, or large bones down the disposal. Always run cold water before, during, and after use for at least 30 seconds. Regularly grind citrus peels to help clean and deodorize the unit.

    A leak from the bottom usually means the internal seal has failed. This generally cannot be repaired, and the unit needs replacing.

    A humming disposal usually has a jammed flywheel. Turn off the power and do not put your hand inside. We can free the jam safely and quickly.

    If your garbage disposal is more than 10 years old, frequently leaks, or requires costly motor repairs, replacement might be more cost-effective in the long run. We will provide an honest assessment of your unit's condition and help you decide if repair or replacement is the best option for your Long Beach home.
