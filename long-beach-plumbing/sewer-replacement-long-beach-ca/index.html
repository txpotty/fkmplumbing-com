    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in Long Beach

    Deciding between repair and full replacement depends on the extent and nature of the damage. Minor cracks or isolated clogs might be resolved with spot repairs, but widespread corrosion, multiple breaks, or a collapsed pipe often necessitates a full replacement. Our technicians in Long Beach will conduct a complete camera inspection to accurately diagnose the problem and recommend the most cost-effective and long-term solution. We provide clear explanations and upfront pricing for both options.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Long Beach

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It requires only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are finished in one day.

    Commercial Sewer Replacement in Long Beach

    Commercial buildings often have larger sewer lines and grease issues. We replace commercial laterals and work with businesses to reduce downtime. We may install grease interceptors as needed. We handle permits and city coordination. Our crews work quickly to restore service.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Long Beach, CA

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the best option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench after inspection. This method allows full correction of slope problems.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Long Beach, CA

    A cleanout provides simple access for future cleaning and inspection. Many older homes lack an exterior cleanout. We install one during sewer replacement. This saves time and money on future maintenance. It also helps in emergencies.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Long Beach

    Many older homes in Long Beach still have original clay or cast iron sewer lines, which are prone to root intrusion, corrosion, and cracking. FKM Plumbing can upgrade your system to modern PVC (polyvinyl chloride) piping. PVC is durable, resistant to root penetration, and has a smooth interior that reduces the likelihood of blockages, providing a long-lasting solution for your home's drainage.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Long Beach

    For significant damage or older, failing systems, traditional sewer line replacement often involves excavating a trench to access and replace the entire pipe. This method is effective for replacing severely collapsed or extensively rooted lines, especially common in older Long Beach homes with cast iron or clay pipes. We thoughtfully plan the excavation to minimize disruption to your yard and landscaping, using heavy-duty equipment for efficient digging and backfilling.
