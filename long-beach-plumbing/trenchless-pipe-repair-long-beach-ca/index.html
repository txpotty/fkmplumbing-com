    Frequently Asked Questions About Trenchless Pipe Repair in Long Beach

    The cost of trenchless pipe repair in Long Beach varies widely depending on the pipe's length, diameter, type of damage, and the specific trenchless method used, such as CIPP lining versus pipe bursting.     Signs you might need trenchless pipe repair include recurring drain clogs, sewage backups, foul odors from drains, wet spots in your yard or foundation without visible leaks, and oddly lush patches of grass. These often indicate a damaged or leaking underground pipe.

    A properly installed epoxy liner is designed to last 50 years or more. HDPE pipe used in bursting has a similar long service life.

    Yes, in most cases, trenchless pipe repair that involves repairing or replacing underground sewer or water lines needs a plumbing permit from the city. FKM Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and makes sure all work complies with local codes and regulations.

    CIPP (cured-in-place pipe) lining involves inserting a new liner inside the existing pipe to create a pipe within a pipe. Pipe bursting, on the other hand, involves breaking apart the old pipe while pulling a new pipe into its place, which leaves you with a completely new pipe.

    FKM Plumbing uses trusted, industry-leading brands for our trenchless pipe repair equipment and materials, such as Perma-Liner and Picote. We choose brands known for their durability, effectiveness, and proven track record in providing long-lasting pipe repair solutions.

    Most common pipe materials, including cast iron, clay, PVC, and concrete, can be repaired using trenchless methods. However, the condition of the existing pipe is a factor.
