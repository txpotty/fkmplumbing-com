    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Bellflower

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system completely. Restored flow means steadier temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Tankless Water Heater Venting System Check in Bellflower

    Proper ventilation is critical for the safe operation of gas-fired tankless water heaters. Our maintenance service in Bellflower includes a thorough inspection of the venting system for blockages, proper slope and secure connections. We make sure that exhaust gases are safely directed outside and that no carbon monoxide leaks are present, which is vital for the health and safety of your household.

    Error Code Diagnostics in Bellflower, CA

    Codes like Rinnai LC, Navien E003 or Noritz 90 tell us a lot about what is going wrong. We read the code history, test flame rods, flow sensors and igniters and check gas pressure at the inlet.
