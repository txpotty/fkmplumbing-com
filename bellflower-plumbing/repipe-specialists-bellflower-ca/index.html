    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Bellflower

    Most PEX repipes for Southern California homes cost $5,000 to $12,000, depending on size, bathrooms and access. Copper repipes usually run 20 to 40 percent more.

    PEX resists scale and pinhole corrosion and handles expansion well, which makes it a solid choice for hard water. Copper is durable too but costs more and can pinhole in corrosive water.

    Most whole-home repipes can be completed within 3 to 5 days, depending on the size and complexity of your home and the chosen piping material. We strive to limit water shut-off periods, often restoring water service by the end of each workday. Our team works efficiently to get your plumbing fully operational as soon as possible.

    Both PEX and copper are excellent choices for repiping in Southern California, each with distinct advantages. PEX is more flexible, resistant to freezing, and generally less expensive, making it a great option for many homes. Copper is very durable, has a longer lifespan, and naturally inhibits bacterial growth, but costs more. We can discuss which material best suits your budget and needs.

    Repeated slab leaks, pinhole leaks, low pressure, discolored water or polybutylene, Kitec or galvanized pipe are all signs. Frequent leak repairs are the clearest signal.

    Common signs include discolored or rusty water, low water pressure at multiple fixtures, frequent leaks or pinhole leaks, and strange noises coming from your pipes. If your home has galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes, a repipe is often a proactive and wise investment to prevent future problems.

    Repiping is a complex plumbing project that requires specialized tools, knowledge of plumbing codes, and real experience. Attempting a DIY repipe can lead to costly mistakes, leaks, and potential code violations. It's strongly recommended to hire professional repipe specialists like FKM Plumbing to make sure the job is done correctly, safely, and to code.
