    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside.     Condensing Tankless Replacement in Bellflower

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by recovering heat from exhaust gases.
