    To prevent future leaks, consider regular plumbing inspections, especially for older homes. Keep water pressure in a safe range, address any signs of corrosion and maintain your plumbing system. For homes with known issues, proactive repiping or pipe upgrades can be a lasting solution.

    Older galvanized steel pipes are prone to corrosion, and some older polybutylene pipes are known for premature failure. However, even modern materials like copper or PEX can leak due to improper installation, high water pressure or foundation movement.

    Older homes may have galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes, both known for higher leak rates due to corrosion or material breakdown. Copper pipes can develop pinhole leaks over time, while PVC and PEX are usually more reliable but can still leak at fittings or due to installation errors.

    If a gas leak is suspected, immediately evacuate the property and call 911 or your gas provider. Once the area is safe, FKM Plumbing can be called to precisely locate the leak and perform the necessary repairs according to strict safety protocols and codes.
