    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Bellflower, CA

    For high-hazard applications in Bellflower, CA where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is often required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. FKM Plumbing specializes in RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes. Proper installation is critical for effective operation.

    Commercial Backflow Preventer Installation in Bellflower

    Restaurants, salons, dental offices and light industrial buildings often need multiple backflow devices on soda machines, boilers, dishwashers and process water. We survey the building, identify each cross-connection and install containment and isolation assemblies as required.     Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Bellflower

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the design of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Bellflower, CA

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested easily. We carry trusted brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in Bellflower, CA

    Irrigation systems in Bellflower, CA are a common source of backflow, as stagnant water and lawn chemicals can be drawn back into the potable water supply. Installing a dedicated backflow preventer for your sprinkler system is often a local code requirement. We are experienced in selecting and installing the correct device, such as a pressure vacuum breaker or atmospheric vacuum breaker, to protect your home's main water line from contaminants introduced by your landscape irrigation.
