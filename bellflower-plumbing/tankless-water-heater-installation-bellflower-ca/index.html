    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Bellflower, CA

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Around Bellflower, flushing the water heater yearly and cleaning aerators keeps mineral buildup from cutting flow and efficiency. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.
