    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Bellflower

    Most standard gas or electric tank replacements cost $1,400 to $3,000 including the heater, labor and typical code upgrades. Attic installs, larger tanks or relocations can raise the cost to $3,000 to $4,500.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line changes.

    Choosing the right size depends on the number of people in your household, their hot water usage habits and peak demand times. Our technicians will calculate the right GPM (gallons per minute) for tankless or gallon capacity for tank models so you never run out of hot water, especially in multi-bathroom Bellflower homes.

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and A.O. Smith water heaters. Each offers reliable performance and a range of warranties.

    Many households of two to three people do well with a 40 gallon tank, while larger families often need 50 gallons or more. We size according to your bathroom count and usage.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, a metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit or a water heater that is over 10 to 12 years old. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a professional.

    While it might seem tempting, water heater replacement involves gas lines, high voltage electricity, water pressure and heavy equipment. It is a complex job best handled by licensed professionals to ensure safety, proper installation and compliance with local plumbing codes, preventing potential leaks or hazards.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and parts, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. FKM Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, giving you added peace of mind. Specific warranty details vary by brand and unit.
