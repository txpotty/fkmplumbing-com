    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Bellflower

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water around the clock. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand.     Brine Tank Replacement in Bellflower

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore steady brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the right salt type for your system.
