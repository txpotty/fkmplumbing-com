    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Victorville

    When a home with polybutylene pipes sits on a slab foundation, repiping becomes a more complex process. Our team specializes in full home repiping using PEX (cross-linked polyethylene), a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant material. PEX is an strong choice for Southern California homes, as it resists freezing better than rigid pipes and expands to accommodate temperature changes. We carefully plan the repipe to minimize disruption, often running new lines through attics or existing wall cavities, delivering a complete and long-lasting upgrade for your entire plumbing system.

    Manufactured Home Poly Replacement in Victorville, CA

    Manufactured homes from the poly era often have polybutylene lines in the floor or belly. We replace these lines with PEX sized for manufactured homes and secure them properly underneath. We also check heat tape and insulation on exposed lines where winter freezes occur. On an occasional cold night, covering backflow preventers and outdoor valves is usually all it takes to avoid a break. The result is a reliable, modern supply system.

    Some homeowners choose Type L copper for its long history and rigid feel. We solder with lead-free solder or use pressed fittings for faster installation. Copper costs more than PEX and takes a little longer to install. We help you weigh the options for your budget and local water.
