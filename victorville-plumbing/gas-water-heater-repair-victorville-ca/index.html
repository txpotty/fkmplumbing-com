    A malfunctioning burner assembly can result in insufficient heating or unusual noises. We carefully inspect the burner for blockages, corrosion or wear.     Gas Control Valve Replacement in Victorville, CA

    The gas control valve controls temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something fails. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or approved universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Victorville, CA

    Atmospheric gas water heaters rely on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and confirm draft with a test. Proper venting is critical for your family's safety.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Victorville

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it detects vapors or overheating.
