    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Victorville

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light. Once hardened, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Trenchless Pipe Repair in Victorville

    The cost of trenchless pipe repair in Victorville varies widely depending on the pipe's length, diameter, type of damage, and the specific trenchless method used, such as CIPP lining versus pipe bursting. In general, you can expect costs to range from $80 to $250 per linear foot for residential projects. A camera inspection is essential for an accurate estimate from FKM Plumbing.

    A correctly installed epoxy liner is designed to last 50 years or more. HDPE pipe used in bursting has a similar long service life.

    Most cracked, rooted or separated pipes can be lined. Pipes that are fully collapsed or badly bellied may need bursting or traditional repair. A camera inspection tells us which method works best.

    Signs you might need trenchless pipe repair include recurring drain clogs, sewage backups, foul odors from drains, wet spots in your yard or foundation without visible leaks, and unusually lush patches of grass. These often indicate a damaged or leaking underground pipe.

    FKM Plumbing uses trusted, industry-leading brands for our trenchless pipe repair equipment and materials, such as Perma-Liner and Picote. We choose brands known for their durability, effectiveness, and proven track record in providing long-lasting pipe repair solutions.

    While trenchless methods are very versatile, they are not suitable for all types of pipe damage. Severely collapsed pipes, pipes with large offsets, or pipes that have suffered extreme structural failure may still require traditional excavation. A professional camera inspection from FKM Plumbing will confirm what is possible.

    Most trenchless pipe repair projects, especially CIPP lining, can be completed in one to three days. Pipe bursting might take a bit longer, depending on the pipe length and complexity. This is much faster than traditional methods, which can take weeks due to excavation and restoration.
