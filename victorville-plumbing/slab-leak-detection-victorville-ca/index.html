    Slab Leak Reroutes in Victorville

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is a good fit when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Slab Leak Detection in Victorville

    The cost of slab leak detection in Victorville, CA usually ranges from $250 to $700, depending on the complexity of the leak and the time required for detection. This cost covers the specialized equipment and expertise needed to accurately locate the leak, which is crucial before any repair work can begin.

    Common pipe materials prone to slab leaks include older copper pipes susceptible to corrosion from hard water or soil acidity, and sometimes PEX or CPVC if they were improperly installed or exposed to extreme temperatures. Galvanized steel pipes, though less common under slabs today, can also corrode and leak.

    A water line slab leak involves pressurized fresh water escaping from supply pipes, often resulting in higher water bills, warm spots or the sound of running water. A sewer line slab leak involves wastewater leaking from drain pipes, which usually causes foul odors, slow drains or visible sewage seepage, and is not usually pressurized.

    Yes, FKM Plumbing stands behind our work. We offer a warranty on our slab leak detection services, standing behind the accuracy of our findings. Any subsequent repair work performed by us also comes with a full warranty on parts and labor, providing you with peace of mind.

    Common signs of a slab leak include surprisingly high water bills, warm spots on your floor, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, visible cracks in the foundation, damp or wet spots on carpets or flooring and a sudden drop in water pressure. Musty odors can also point to moisture buildup.

    While you can look for the signs, precisely detecting a slab leak requires specialized equipment and expertise that most homeowners don't have. DIY attempts often lead to more damage or misdiagnosis, making professional detection a more affordable and reliable solution in the long run.

    Some homeowner's insurance policies may cover the cost of accessing and repairing the slab leak, particularly if the leak was sudden and accidental. However, coverage often varies, and damage caused by long-term neglect might not be covered. We recommend checking your specific policy details or contacting your insurance provider for clarification.

    For major slab leak repairs, especially those involving excavation or extensive re-piping, a plumbing permit from the local building department is often required.
