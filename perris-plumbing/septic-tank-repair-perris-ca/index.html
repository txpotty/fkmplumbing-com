    Many modern septic systems, especially those on sloped properties or with advanced treatment units, rely on a working septic pump and alarm.     Septic Inlet Line Repair in Perris

    Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Perris

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field quickly. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where appropriate.
