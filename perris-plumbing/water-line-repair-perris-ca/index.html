    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Repair in Perris

    A spot repair on an accessible service line usually runs $350 to $1,500 depending on depth and access. Full replacement from meter to house usually costs $1,800 to $5,500, and trenchless replacement often falls between $3,000 and $7,000. We give you a written price before we start.

    Most spot repairs are finished in two to four hours. A full replacement typically takes one day, and trenchless jobs are often done in a day as well. Water is usually back on the same day.

    In most communities the water provider owns the meter and the pipe on the street side of it. The homeowner is responsible for the service line from the meter to the house. We confirm which side the leak is on before quoting you.

    Trenchless water line repair is a method of fixing or replacing underground pipes without extensive digging. Techniques like pipe bursting involve pulling a new pipe through the old one, while pipe lining inserts a new, cured-in-place pipe inside the existing one. It's an excellent option for preserving landscaping and limiting disruption, especially on properties in Perris with established gardens or driveways.

    We use only quality, reputable brands for all our water line repairs and installations. This includes trusted names in PEX like Uponor and Viega, and durable copper piping from manufacturers known for their reliability. Our fittings are always code-compliant and selected for their longevity in varied soil and water conditions, for a solid repair.

    Many policies do not cover the pipe itself but may cover resulting damage to your home. Some homeowners carry separate service line coverage through their utility or insurer. We provide photos and an itemized invoice to back up your claim.

    Full service line replacement usually requires a plumbing permit and inspection from your city or county. Small spot repairs often do not. We pull the permit and schedule the inspection when needed.

    A homeowner can sometimes tighten a leaking fitting at an exposed hose bib. Buried service line repairs require locating, safe excavation, 811 utility marking and code-approved fittings. Mistakes can cause bigger leaks, so most homeowners call a licensed plumber.
