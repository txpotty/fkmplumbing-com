    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Perris, CA

    Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California install softeners or scale filters. Water in many areas contains minerals that contribute to scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can speed up corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral buildup, ensuring a longer-lasting plumbing system.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Perris

We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to three days.
