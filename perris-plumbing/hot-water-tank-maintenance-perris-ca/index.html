    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Perris

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause noisy popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Perris

    A properly working temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to confirm it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank.     Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Perris

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. High street pressure is not unusual in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.
