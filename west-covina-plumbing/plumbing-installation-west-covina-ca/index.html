    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Installation in West Covina

    Costs depend on the project. A standard tank water heater install usually runs $1,600 to $3,500, a water softener $1,500 to $4,000, and a whole-home PEX repipe $5,000 to $15,000 or more depending on home size.

    Most standard water heater installations, whether tank or tankless, can be completed by FKM Plumbing within 3 to 6 hours. This timeframe can vary based on the complexity of the existing setup, any required modifications to gas or water lines, and if electrical upgrades are required for electric tankless units.

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible plastic tubing used for water supply lines. It's popular for installations in West Covina because it's corrosion-resistant, less prone to bursting from freezing than copper, and often quicker to install. Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings, reducing potential leak points and making it a durable choice for Southern California homes.

    A sump pump is designed to remove clear groundwater that collects in a sump pit, usually in a basement or crawl space, to prevent flooding. A sewage ejector pump, on the other hand, handles wastewater containing solids from plumbing fixtures located below the main sewer line, lifting it up to the main sewer or septic system.

    Tank water heaters cost less upfront, while tankless units offer endless hot water and can last longer. We help you compare based on household size, fuel type and cost.

    You should consider replacing your main water line if you experience consistently low water pressure, recurring leaks, discolored water, or an unusually high water bill that cannot be attributed to other issues. Older homes in West Covina often have aging galvanized or polybutylene pipes that are prone to failure, making replacement a wise investment.
