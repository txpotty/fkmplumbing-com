    Traditional Trench Sewer Line Replacement in West Covina

    For significant damage or certain pipe layouts, traditional trenching may be the most effective method. This involves digging a trench to access and replace the damaged sewer line directly. Our team carefully plans the excavation to minimize disruption to your landscape, using the right equipment for clean, precise digging. We make sure of proper grading and backfilling to prevent future issues and restore your property.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in West Covina

    Cast iron drains in older homes corrode from the inside, particularly along the bottom of the pipe. Over time this leaves cracks, holes and a rough surface that snags waste. Replacing the cast iron sewer outside the home is often the first step. We can also evaluate under-slab cast iron and discuss lining or rerouting options. New PVC or HDPE will not rust or scale the same way.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in West Covina

    Many older homes in West Covina still have sewer lines made from outdated materials like clay or cast iron, which are prone to cracking, corrosion, and root invasion. We offer upgrades to newer, more durable materials such as PVC or HDPE (high-density polyethylene). These materials resist corrosion, roots, and shifting soil, giving your sewer system a much longer lifespan.

    Clay and Orangeburg Line Replacement in West Covina, CA

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, and Orangeburg fiber pipe flattens and blisters with age. Both are near the end of their service life in many older properties. When the camera shows multiple failures, replacement is typically the best value. We remove or burst the old line and install modern pipe with a long expected service life. You get a smooth, root-resistant line to the street.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair and Replacement in West Covina, CA

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive option to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining inserts a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe. Both methods significantly reduce the need for excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in West Covina, CA while providing a long-lasting solution.

    Sewer Line Replacement Cost Assessment in West Covina

    The cost of sewer line replacement varies significantly based on the method used (trenchless or traditional), the length and depth of the pipe, the type of material, and site access. Before any work begins, FKM Plumbing provides a detailed and transparent cost assessment. We explain all potential expenses, including labor, materials, equipment, and permit fees in West Covina.     Partial Sewer Line Repair in West Covina, CA

    Not every damaged sewer needs full replacement. If the camera shows a single break or one root-damaged joint, a spot repair may solve the problem. We locate the exact spot and depth, excavate a small area and replace that section. This costs much less than replacing the whole lateral. We only recommend it when the rest of the line is in sound condition.
