    Direct Slab Leak Repair in West Covina, CA

    When a slab leak is localized and accessible, direct repair is often the most efficient solution. This involves carefully jackhammering a small section of your foundation to expose the damaged pipe, then repairing or replacing the compromised section. Our technicians are skilled in careful concrete cutting and restoration, so the repair is effective and the area is professionally restored. We use durable materials designed to hold up to soil movement under the slab.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in West Covina, CA

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel. Mineral scale and constant recirculation can wear the pipe wall faster. We repair the leak or reroute the hot line, and we can adjust recirculation pumps to limit wear. Warm floors and higher gas or electric bills are common signs.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in West Covina, CA

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a load-bearing wall or in a difficult-to-access area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method. This allows our plumbers to reach the damaged pipe from outside your home, avoiding disruption to your interior floors and finishes. Tunneling spares the interior but calls for skilled excavation and shoring to protect structural integrity throughout the repair process in West Covina, CA.

    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in West Covina, CA

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most efficient repair method. This involves carefully cutting a small section of your concrete slab to access and repair or replace the damaged pipe section. Our team makes sure the repair is fully compliant with Southern California plumbing codes and the slab is properly restored, preventing future issues. We take great care to contain dust and debris during this process in your West Covina home.

    Advanced Leak Detection Technology in West Covina

    Our commitment to effective slab leak repair includes investing in modern diagnostic technology. We use equipment such as thermal imaging cameras to detect temperature changes caused by leaking hot water lines, and very sensitive acoustic sensors to listen for the sound of escaping water. This technology allows us to locate leaks without guesswork, even in the diverse mix of buildings across West Covina.

    Slab Leak Detection in West Covina

    We start by shutting off fixtures and watching the meter to confirm a leak. Then we isolate the hot and cold lines to find out which one is leaking. Using acoustic ground microphones, we listen for the sound of escaping water through the concrete. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor surface.

    Slab Leak Reroute in West Covina

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is easiest. Reroutes end future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in West Covina

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is severely deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an effective long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This eliminates future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in West Covina with aging copper or galvanized pipes.
