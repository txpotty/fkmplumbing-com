    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in West Covina, CA

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in West Covina, CA

    Washing machine hoses, utility sinks and water heaters can all leak into nearby walls. We replace burst-prone rubber hoses with braided stainless hoses and install leak pans and shutoff valves.     Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in West Covina

    Many homes in West Covina have crawl spaces, which are highly susceptible to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting stops ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an effective way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in West Covina

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most effective method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Expansive clay and hillside soils can shift over time, putting stress on slab plumbing and cracking older sewer joints. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also added to divert water away.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in West Covina, CA

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already passed through the foundation walls. This usually involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in West Covina, CA

    Older homes, especially those built before modern waterproofing standards, are particularly prone to water intrusion. Repiping with PEX or copper is a popular upgrade across Southern California, often done to fix low pressure and repeated slab leaks. Many older foundations may lack adequate exterior drainage or proper sealing. We specialize in assessing and upgrading waterproofing systems for these properties, often combining exterior excavation with interior drainage solutions. We respect the existing structure while installing modern, effective waterproofing, with attention to the challenges of older brick and stone foundations.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in West Covina, CA
