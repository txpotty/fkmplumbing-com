    Water Softener Maintenance in West Covina

    Water softeners combat hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale.     Hot Water Tank Maintenance in West Covina, CA

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits greatly from annual maintenance. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to early tank failure.

    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in West Covina

    Postwar tract homes are common in Southern California, and many still have original galvanized or copper supply lines and cast iron drains beneath the slab. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks.     Gas Line Inspection and Repair in West Covina

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility first.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in West Covina

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers require annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.
