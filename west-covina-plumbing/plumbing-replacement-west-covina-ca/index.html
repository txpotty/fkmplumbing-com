    Water Softener Replacement in West Covina, CA

    Hard water leads to mineral buildup in pipes and appliances. Municipal water across much of Southern California is hard, leaving white scale on faucets, showerheads, and inside water heater tanks. If your old water softener is no longer properly removing minerals, or if it is over 15 years old, a replacement will protect your plumbing and improve water quality. We replace all types of water softeners, including salt-based and salt-free systems, sized for your household's water usage and installed correctly on your main water line to provide truly soft water throughout your West Covina home.

    Well Pump and Pressure Tank Replacement in West Covina, CA

    Rural properties rely on well pumps to deliver water, and pump failure means no water at all. Typical signs include pressure that fluctuates, a pump that cycles rapidly or no water at the tap. We replace submersible and jet pumps and waterlogged pressure tanks with properly sized equipment. Pressure switch settings are adjusted for steady pressure throughout the home.

    Water Line Replacement in West Covina, CA

    A leaking main water line can lead to significant water loss, high bills and foundation damage, which is a serious concern for homes on slab foundations. Our water line replacement services address issues like corroded pipes, leaks or low water pressure. We locate the leak, excavate the area if necessary and replace the damaged section or the entire line with durable PEX or copper piping. We make sure of a secure, leak-free connection from the meter to your home, restoring reliable water flow.

    Whole-Home Pipe Replacement in West Covina

    Homes with multiple pinhole leaks, galvanized pipe or polybutylene may benefit from a complete repipe. We replace supply lines with PEX or copper, often rerouting through the attic to avoid future slab leaks. Most repipes take two to four days with water restored each evening whenever we can. Drywall openings are patched when the work is complete.

    Pipe Replacement and Repiping in West Covina

    Corroded, leaking or galvanized pipes can contaminate your water and cause costly damage. FKM Plumbing offers full pipe replacement, or repiping, for homes in West Covina. We replace old plumbing with new PEX or copper, improving water quality and pressure throughout your whole home. This is a crucial upgrade for many older homes.

    Sewer Line Replacement in West Covina

    Persistent sewer backups, foul odors or lush spots in your yard can signal a failing sewer line. Earthquakes are a constant consideration in Southern California, and seismic straps and flexible connectors help keep water and gas lines intact during shaking. We offer complete sewer line replacement using trenchless technology whenever possible, minimizing disruption to your landscaping. For more severe damage, traditional excavation is performed with careful attention. We make sure new lines are properly graded, connected and compliant with local West Covina plumbing codes, preventing future blockages and costly repairs.

    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in West Covina, CA

    Upgrade to endless hot water with a tankless water heater replacement. These units heat water on demand, saving energy and space, which is a great benefit for West Covina homes. We specialize in replacing older tankless units or converting from traditional tank heaters to high-efficiency tankless models from brands like Rinnai and Noritz. Our service includes assessing your home's gas line capacity or electrical service, ensuring proper venting and precise installation to maximize efficiency and longevity.

    Water Heater Replacement in West Covina
