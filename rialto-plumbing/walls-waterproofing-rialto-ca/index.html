    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Rialto, CA

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already penetrated the foundation walls. This usually involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Rialto

    The cost of walls waterproofing in Rialto, CA can vary a great deal based on the method used, the extent of the damage, and the size of your foundation. Basic crack sealing might range from $300 to $800 per crack, while a full exterior foundation waterproofing project could be $8,000 to $20,000 or more. Interior drainage systems with a sump pump usually fall between $4,000 and $12,000. We provide clear, itemized quotes after a thorough inspection.

    We commonly install Schluter Kerdi, Custom RedGard, Laticrete Hydro Ban and similar systems. The right choice depends on your shower design and tile. We follow each manufacturer's installation steps to keep the warranty in force.

    Key signs you need walls waterproofing include damp or wet basement walls, visible mold or mildew growth, a musty odor in your basement or crawl space, efflorescence on concrete walls, cracks in the foundation, or standing water after heavy rains. Don't ignore these indicators, as they often point to ongoing water intrusion.

    Sprinklers and drip lines aimed at the house are the most common cause. Heavy storms and poor grading also send water toward walls. Masonry absorbs that water and leaves mineral deposits as it dries.

    Look for bubbling paint, soft drywall, stained baseboards and musty odors. White crust on block or stucco walls is another sign of moisture moving through. Loose tiles or cracked grout in a shower also mean water is getting behind.

    Damp proofing is a less durable solution, typically a spray-on asphalt coating that resists soil moisture but isn't designed to withstand hydrostatic pressure. Waterproofing uses stronger membranes, coatings, or drainage systems specifically engineered to prevent liquid water from entering, even under pressure. For basements or crawl spaces in areas with high water tables or heavy rainfall, waterproofing is the right choice.

    Walls waterproofing is a primary step in preventing future mold growth by eliminating the moisture source. However, it does not remove existing mold. If mold is already present, it will need to be professionally remediated before or along with the waterproofing process. Once the area is dry and mold-free, waterproofing will help keep it that way.

    The duration of a walls waterproofing project varies a lot. Smaller jobs like interior crack sealing might take a day or two, while extensive exterior waterproofing with excavation and drainage system installation could take several days to over a week. We'll provide a clear timeline during your consultation.
