    Pressure Switch Replacement in Rialto, CA

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop, typically at 30/50 or 40/60 psi. Pitted contacts, insect nests and a clogged sensing nipple are frequent causes of failure in well houses. We replace worn switches with Square D or similar models and clean or replace the nipple below it. A low-pressure cutoff switch can also be added to stop the pump if the well runs dry. We set the switch to suit your new tank's pre-charge.

    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Rialto

    When your well pressure tank shows signs of trouble, it is important to determine if a repair is feasible or if full replacement is necessary. Small air leaks or issues with the air valve might be repairable. However, if the tank itself is corroded, has a ruptured diaphragm, or is waterlogged, replacement is almost always the more economical and reliable option. Our technicians in Rialto provide an straightforward assessment, explaining the pros and cons of repair versus a complete well pressure tank replacement, so you can make an informed decision.

    Bladder-Style Pressure Tank Replacement in Rialto, CA

    Most modern well pressure tanks are bladder-style, separating air and water for better efficiency. When the bladder fails, you'll experience symptoms like short-cycling of the well pump. FKM Plumbing carefully replaces these bladder-style tanks across Rialto, CA, ensuring proper pre-charge pressure and system integration for long-lasting performance.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Rialto

    The tank tee is the manifold that connects the tank, switch, gauge, relief valve and drain. Galvanized tees corrode and restrict flow, especially with sandy or mineral-heavy water. We replace them with brass or stainless steel tank tees that hold up much longer. A new liquid-filled gauge gives reliable readings, and a fresh relief valve protects against a stuck switch.
