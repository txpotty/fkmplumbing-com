    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Rialto, CA

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California install softeners or scale filters. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Rialto

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to deliver hot water to distant fixtures. A recirculation pump or dedicated return line keeps hot water close to faucets, reducing wait time and water waste. Some units have built-in pumps with smart scheduling. We design the system to suit your plumbing layout and usage patterns.
