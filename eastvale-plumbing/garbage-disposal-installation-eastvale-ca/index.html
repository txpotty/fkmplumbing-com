    In most cases, a direct replacement of an existing garbage disposal in Eastvale does not require a permit.     To extend the life of your new garbage disposal, avoid putting hard items like bones, fruit pits, or fibrous vegetables down the drain. Always use plenty of cold water while operating the disposal and run it regularly, even if there's no waste, to prevent rust and keep components moving.

    For light use or single-person households, a 1/3 or 1/2 horsepower unit might be enough. For average families, a 3/4 horsepower unit is a good balance of power and cost. Large families or those who cook frequently might benefit from a 1 horsepower disposal to handle more food waste easily without jamming.

    To maintain your new garbage disposal, always run cold water before, during, and after grinding food waste. Avoid putting hard items like bones, fibrous vegetables, or grease down the drain. Occasionally grind citrus peels or ice cubes to help clean the blades and freshen the unit.
