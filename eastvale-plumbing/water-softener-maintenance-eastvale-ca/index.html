    Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Eastvale, CA

    The brine tank is crucial for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can build up, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a common issue we address in Eastvale homes.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Eastvale

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, dictating regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages. If issues are found, we can often repair or replace worn parts to restore full function, preventing the need for a costly full system replacement in Eastvale.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repair in Eastvale, CA

    A leaking bypass valve can let hard water slip past the softener without you noticing. We test and rebuild bypass valves and replace cracked fittings. We also inspect the drain line for kinks and proper air gap and the brine overflow line for correct routing. These small fixes prevent leaks and backflow problems.
