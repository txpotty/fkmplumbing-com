    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Eastvale, CA

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal.     PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Eastvale

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the distinct advantages of each.
