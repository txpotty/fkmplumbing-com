    The timeline for water damage restoration usually ranges from 3 to 7 days for drying and initial cleanup, but it can extend to several weeks or even months if extensive repairs and reconstruction are needed.     While you can handle minor leaks or spills, professional water damage restoration is highly recommended for anything beyond surface moisture. Professionals have specialized equipment to confirm complete drying and prevent mold, and they can safely handle contaminated water.

    Most homeowner's insurance policies cover sudden and accidental water damage, such as a burst pipe or appliance malfunction.
