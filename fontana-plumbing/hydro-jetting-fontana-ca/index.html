    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Fontana

    Residential hydro jetting in Fontana typically costs $350 to $800, depending on line length, access and how heavy the buildup is. Commercial jetting is often priced by the hour and may run higher.

    Hydro jetting is very effective against a wide range of stubborn clogs that traditional snaking cannot fully clear. This includes heavy grease buildup, mineral scale, sludge, soap scum, hair clogs, and even invasive tree roots that have penetrated your sewer lines. It is designed to scour the entire pipe surface.

    Snaking uses a rotating cable to break through a clog. Jetting uses pressurized water to clean the entire pipe wall and wash debris away.

    While hydro jetting effectively removes existing tree roots from your sewer line, it does not prevent new roots from growing back. For a long-term solution to recurring root intrusion, we might recommend trenchless sewer repair or pipe replacement, which we can go over during our assessment.

    While small, low-pressure drain jetters are available for rent, professional hydro jetting equipment is significantly more powerful and requires training to operate safely and effectively. Improper use can damage pipes, cause injury, or fail to clear the blockage. For severe clogs, it is always best to rely on experienced professionals like FKM Plumbing.

    For most homes in Fontana, hydro jetting every 1 to 3 years can be a good preventative measure, especially if you have recurring drain issues or older pipes. Businesses, particularly restaurants, may benefit from quarterly or twice-yearly hydro jetting to manage grease buildup. We can suggest a schedule based on your needs.

    Most residential jobs take 1 to 3 hours including inspection. Long commercial lines or heavy grease can add time.

    Small consumer jetters exist, but they lack the pressure and flow of professional units. High-pressure water can also injure untrained users and damage fragile pipe.
