    Slab Leak Detection in Fontana

    Slab leaks are a common concern for homes with concrete foundations, especially where soils shift. Our technicians use dedicated acoustic equipment and thermal cameras to pinpoint leaks under your home's foundation without destructive digging. Early detection of a slab leak prevents serious structural damage and mold issues, saving you significant repair costs down the line. We can locate leaks in both hot and cold water lines beneath the slab.

    Water Leak Detection in Walls & Ceilings in Fontana, CA

    Water leaks behind walls or in ceilings can go unnoticed for long periods, leading to extensive drywall damage, wood rot and mold. We use non-invasive methods like moisture meters, thermal cameras and borescopes to precisely pinpoint the source of these leaks. This approach minimizes the need for destructive investigation, saving you time and money on repairs to your home's walls and ceilings.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection in Fontana, CA

    Sewer line leaks can cause foul odors, slow drains and potential health hazards. Our team uses camera inspections to visually identify breaks, cracks or blockages within your sewer lines. This method allows for accurate location of the leak without guesswork, leading to more efficient and targeted repairs. We repair sewer leaks in all types of piping materials common in Fontana homes, from PVC to cast iron.
