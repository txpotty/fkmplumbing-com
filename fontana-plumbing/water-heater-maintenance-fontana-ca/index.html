    Standard Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Fontana

    Our standard tank water heater maintenance includes flushing the tank to remove sediment buildup. Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California install softeners or scale filters. We also inspect the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and check for any leaks or corrosion on the tank and connections. This service helps prevent early failure and improves heating efficiency, keeping your energy bills in check.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Fontana

    Tankless units are especially sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures consistent.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Fontana, CA

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building dangerous pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets swapped out. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge if you have one.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Fontana, CA

    Gas water heater maintenance involves important safety inspections in Fontana, CA. We check for gas leaks, proper venting of exhaust gases, and the function of the pilot light or igniter. We also clean the burner assembly and inspect the thermocouple. These checks are vital to prevent carbon monoxide exposure and keep your gas-fired appliance running safely.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Fontana, CA

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as necessary. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water quickly. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.
