    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Fontana

    The cost for garbage disposal replacement in Fontana, CA typically ranges from $200 to $600. This includes the new unit itself, which can vary from $100 for a basic 1/3 HP model to over $300 for a high-end 1 HP unit, plus labor costs. Factors like the disposal's horsepower, brand, and any necessary plumbing modifications can influence the final price.

    For light use in a single-person household, 1/3 HP might suffice. Most homeowners choose 1/2 HP or 3/4 HP for general use, which handles common food waste well. For large families, frequent cooking, or tougher waste like small bones, a 1 HP unit offers the best performance and durability.

    We install and replace all major brands, including InSinkErator, Moen, Waste King, KitchenAid, and Badger. Our plumbers are familiar with the installation requirements for each brand, for a perfect fit.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from two to ten years, and we guarantee our labor.

    To extend the life of your new disposal, avoid putting fibrous foods like celery, corn husks, or onion skins down it. Also, do not dispose of grease, coffee grounds, or non-food items. Always run cold water before, during, and after use, and grind small amounts of citrus peels or ice cubes now and then to clean the unit.

    Key signs you need a new garbage disposal include persistent leaks from the unit's housing, a humming noise without grinding, frequent clogs despite proper usage, foul odors that won't go away, or if the unit simply won't turn on. These symptoms usually indicate internal motor or component failure.
