    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Chino

    Most standard gas or electric tank replacements cost $1,400 to $3,000 including the heater, labor and common code upgrades. Attic installs, larger tanks or relocations can raise the cost to $3,000 to $4,500.

    Leaks at the base, rusty water, rumbling noises, inconsistent hot water and an age over 10 years are typical signs. A leaking tank should be replaced right away.

    Manufacturer tank warranties typically range from 6 to 12 years depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, a metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit or a water heater that is over 10 to 12 years old. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a professional.

    FKM Plumbing installs and services all major brands, including Bradford White, Rheem, AO Smith, Rinnai, Navien and State Water Heaters. We can help you choose the best brand and model that suits your budget and hot water demands.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line changes.

    New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and parts, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. FKM Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, giving you added peace of mind. Specific warranty details vary by brand and model.
