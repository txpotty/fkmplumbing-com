    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Chino

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin typically costs $900 to $2,000. A new ejector system with basin, venting and floor work typically ranges from $2,500 to $6,000 or more.

    Pump replacements typically take two to four hours. New systems with floor excavation and venting typically take one to two days.

    Avoid flushing wipes, paper towels, feminine products, grease and cat litter. These items clog pumps and cause early failure.

    A sump pump is designed to remove groundwater from a basement or crawl space, preventing flooding. A sewage ejector pump, however, is specifically engineered to handle wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in areas below the main sewer line. They are not interchangeable and serve separate purposes.

    A sump pump removes groundwater or seepage, while an ejector pump handles sewage and wastewater from fixtures. Ejector pumps are designed to pass solids and require a sealed, vented basin.

    Regular maintenance for a sewage ejector pump involves periodically checking the basin for debris, confirming the check valve works correctly and testing the float switch. It is also wise to avoid flushing non-biodegradable items. FKM Plumbing offers maintenance services to keep your pump running efficiently and prevent surprise breakdowns.

    We primarily install high-quality, reputable brands such as Liberty Pumps and Zoeller. These manufacturers are known for their durability, reliability and efficient performance, providing a long-lasting solution for your home. We can discuss the best brand and model for your needs.

    Installing a sewage ejector pump is a complex job that involves plumbing, electrical and sometimes excavation work.
