    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Chino Hills, CA

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel. Mineral scale and constant recirculation can wear the pipe wall faster. We repair the leak or reroute the hot line, and we can adjust recirculation pumps to limit wear. Warm floors and higher gas or electric bills are common signs.

    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Chino Hills, CA

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most practical repair method. This involves carefully cutting a small section of your concrete slab to access and repair or replace the damaged pipe section. Our team makes sure the repair is fully compliant with Southern California plumbing codes and the slab is correctly restored, preventing future issues. We take real care to contain dust and debris during this process in your Chino Hills home.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Chino Hills

    Cold water slab leaks are harder to feel but still waste water and wet the soil. They often show up as a high water bill or a meter that moves with all fixtures off. We isolate the cold line, find the leak and repair or reroute it. Fixing cold leaks quickly helps protect your foundation.

    Advanced Leak Detection Technology in Chino Hills

    Our approach to effective slab leak repair includes investing in modern diagnostic technology. We use equipment such as thermal imaging cameras to detect temperature changes caused by leaking hot water lines, and highly sensitive acoustic sensors to listen for the sound of escaping water. This technology lets us locate leaks without guesswork, even in the varied mix of buildings across Chino Hills.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Chino Hills, CA

    If multiple slab leaks indicate a systemic issue with your home's original plumbing, a full PEX repipe might be the most cost-effective and durable solution. PEX tubing is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and less prone to leaks caused by foundation shifts, making it a good choice for many homes. We can replace all the water supply lines in your Chino Hills home, providing a brand-new, leak-free plumbing system that will last for decades.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Chino Hills

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is simplest. Reroutes end future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Chino Hills, CA

    Whether your home in Chino Hills, CA has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our plumbers are skilled in repairing both. Pinhole leaks are common in older copper due to corrosive soil conditions and water chemistry, while PEX can sometimes suffer from installation issues or rodent damage. We carry a full range of parts and tools for both materials, making for a correct and durable repair no matter your pipe type.
