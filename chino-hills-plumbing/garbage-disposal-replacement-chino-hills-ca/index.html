    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Chino Hills

    The cost for garbage disposal replacement in Chino Hills, CA usually ranges from $200 to $600. This includes the new unit itself, which can vary from $100 for a basic 1/3 HP model to over $300 for a high-end 1 HP unit, plus labor costs. Factors like the disposal's horsepower, brand, and any necessary plumbing modifications can influence the final price.

    For light use, a 1/3 HP or 1/2 HP unit is usually enough. For regular use and tough food scraps, a 3/4 HP or 1 HP unit is recommended. Our plumbers can help you assess your needs and choose the best fit for your Chino Hills home.

    Common signs include frequent clogs, loud grinding noises without processing food, persistent leaks from the unit's housing, or if the disposal simply stops turning on. If you notice these issues, it's likely time for a replacement.

    While it's possible for a handy homeowner to replace a garbage disposal, it involves working with both electricity and plumbing, which can be hazardous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, electrical shorts, and voided warranties. For safety and peace of mind, hiring a professional plumber is the better choice.

    In most cases, a direct replacement of a garbage disposal with a similar unit does not require a plumbing permit in Chino Hills, CA. However, if there are significant modifications to your kitchen plumbing or electrical system involved, a permit might be necessary. Our plumbers are knowledgeable about local codes and will advise you if any permits are needed.

    New garbage disposals typically come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years, depending on the model and brand. FKM Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation services for your peace of mind.

    Most garbage disposal replacements can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. This timeframe includes disconnecting the old unit, installing the new one, and testing it thoroughly. More complex situations, such as significant plumbing modifications or electrical work, might extend the duration slightly.

    To prolong the life of your new disposal, avoid putting fibrous foods like celery, corn husks, or onion skins down it. Also, do not dispose of grease, coffee grounds, or non-food items. Always run cold water before, during, and after use, and grind small amounts of citrus peels or ice cubes periodically to clean the unit.
