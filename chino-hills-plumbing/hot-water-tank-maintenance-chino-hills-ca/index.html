    Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in Chino Hills

    Sediment buildup is a frequent issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high. Our standard flushing service removes these mineral deposits, which can reduce efficiency and lead to premature tank failure. We drain the tank completely, flush out all sediment and refill it, giving you clear water flow and improved heating performance. This routine service is key for extending the life of your unit.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Chino Hills

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause noisy popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Sediment Flushing & Cleaning in Chino Hills, CA

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Chino Hills

High street pressure is not unusual in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.
