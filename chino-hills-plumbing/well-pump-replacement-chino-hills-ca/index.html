    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Chino Hills

    The cost of well pump replacement in Chino Hills, CA usually ranges from $1,500 to $3,500, though it can vary.     It is often a good idea to consider replacing your pressure tank at the same time as your well pump, especially if both are of similar age. A new pressure tank can improve the efficiency and lifespan of your new pump by reducing cycling. It also saves on labor costs to do both jobs at once.

    Most new well pumps come with a manufacturer's warranty, usually ranging from 1 to 5 years, covering defects in materials and workmanship. FKM Plumbing also backs our installation work with our own labor warranty for complete peace of mind.

    Regular maintenance, such as checking your well's water level, keeping the pressure tank air charge correct and fixing any leaks promptly, can extend your pump's life. Avoiding heavy water use and installing a good quality sediment filter can also reduce wear and tear on the pump.

    In many areas, permits may be required for major plumbing work like well pump replacement, especially if it involves changing the well system's electrical or structural components. FKM Plumbing will help you work through any permitting in Chino Hills, CA so your installation is fully compliant with local regulations.

    Common signs of a failing well pump include no water, sputtering faucets, reduced water pressure, discolored water or surprisingly high electricity bills. If your pump runs constantly or cycles on and off too frequently, these are also signs that replacement might be necessary.
