    Sewer Scope for Slow Drains in Chino Hills

    If you are experiencing consistently slow drains or recurring clogs, a sewer scope inspection can pinpoint the exact cause. Instead of guessing, our camera can show whether the issue is a simple blockage, a pipe belly or more serious damage like a collapse. This precise diagnosis in Chino Hills allows us to recommend the most effective and least invasive repair, saving you from needless digging or repeated efforts.

    Root Intrusion Inspection in Chino Hills, CA

    Roots from shade trees, ornamental trees and thirsty shrubs seek out moisture in sewer joints. Over time they can fill the pipe and cause recurring backups. Our camera shows exactly where roots enter and how much of the pipe they block. We then recommend cleaning, jetting or a long-term repair like lining.

    Sewer Line Locating and Depth Marking in Chino Hills, CA

    Before any repair, it helps to know exactly where your sewer line runs and how deep it is. Our camera includes a sonde transmitter that we trace with a receiver above ground.     Cast Iron and Clay Pipe Condition Assessment in Chino Hills

    Many older homes have cast iron drain lines under the floor and clay sewer laterals outside. Cast iron can corrode and develop scale, cracks and channeling along the bottom of the pipe. Clay joints shift and let roots in. Our camera inspection shows how much life the pipe has left so you can plan repairs on your timeline.
