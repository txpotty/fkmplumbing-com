    Expansion Tank Replacement in Chino Hills, CA

    Expansion tanks wear out over time as the internal bladder or diaphragm fails and the tank fills with water. A waterlogged tank feels heavy, and tapping it produces a dull sound all the way up instead of a hollow top. Pressing the air valve and getting water instead of air is a sure sign it has failed. We replace failed tanks with a correctly sized new unit and check the precharge before installation.

    Brands and Tank Materials in Chino Hills, CA

    We install potable-rated thermal expansion tanks from brands such as Watts, Amtrol and A.O. Smith. Potable tanks use a butyl diaphragm and a lined interior so water never touches bare steel. Municipal water across much of Southern California is hard, leaving chalky scale on faucets, showerheads, and inside water heater tanks.
