    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Chino Hills

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Earthquakes are a constant consideration in Southern California, and seismic straps and flexible connectors help keep water and gas lines intact during shaking. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Chino Hills, CA

    Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California install softeners or scale filters. Water in many areas contains minerals that cause scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can speed up corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral accumulation, ensuring a longer-lasting plumbing system.
