    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Chino Hills

    Submersible sump pumps sit directly inside the sump pit, running quietly and taking up less space. They are typically more powerful and durable, making them a popular choice for many homeowners. We handle the complete installation of submersible units, including proper sealing and electrical connections, matched to the particular needs of your Chino Hills property.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Chino Hills, CA

    Sump pumps often sit idle for long stretches between storms, which can lead to stuck floats and seized motors. We replace old pumps with new models and inspect the pit, check valve and discharge line. Upgrading to a higher capacity pump may help if your old one could not keep up. We test the new pump by filling the pit with water. Replacement before storm season is a smart move.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Chino Hills, CA

    Strong storms can knock out power right when your pump is needed most. Battery backup pumps take over automatically when the main pump fails or power goes out. We install backup systems with sealed batteries and charging units. Many include alarms that alert you to problems. Backups add an extra layer of protection for finished basements.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Chino Hills

    Some homes collect water in sunken patios, below-grade driveways or window wells rather than a basement. We install exterior sump basins with pumps that move storm water to a safe discharge point. These systems are useful for lots that slope toward the house. We route discharge lines so water drains away from the foundation and neighboring lots. This helps prevent siding damage and foundation problems.

    Many older homes in Chino Hills, CA have crawl spaces that are prone to moisture issues and standing water.
