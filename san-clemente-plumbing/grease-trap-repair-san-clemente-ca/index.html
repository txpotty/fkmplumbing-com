    Flow Control Device Repair in San Clemente

    Hydromechanical traps rely on a flow control fitting to keep water moving at the rated speed. If this fitting is missing or clogged, the trap can be overwhelmed and grease escapes. We inspect, clean or replace flow control devices during repair visits. We also check the air intake that allows the device to work properly. Correct flow control is essential for a trap to pass inspection.

    Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in San Clemente

    Beyond the trap itself, the drain lines leading to and from the grease trap can also suffer damage or blockages. These lines are particularly susceptible to grease build-up. In San Clemente, we diagnose and repair issues within these connecting pipes, including clearing FOG accumulation, fixing collapsed sections, or repairing root intrusions.     Baffle Repair and Replacement in San Clemente

    Baffles slow incoming water so grease can rise and solids can settle. When baffles crack, corrode or break loose, grease passes straight through the trap. We remove damaged baffles and install new ones made for your trap model. In concrete interceptors, we can repair or rebuild baffle walls. Restoring baffles brings back proper separation and helps you stay up to code.

    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in San Clemente, CA

    A leaking grease trap can cause significant property damage, foul odors, and potential health code violations. Leaks often stem from corroded tanks, cracked seams, or failing pipe connections. We use leak detection techniques to locate the source and provide targeted repairs, which may involve patching the tank, sealing connections, or replacing damaged sections of the trap or associated plumbing. Addressing leaks promptly is essential to maintain a sanitary environment and avoid costly remediation in San Clemente, CA.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in San Clemente, CA

    Grease that hardens in the lines before or after the trap can cause backups even when the trap itself is clean. We use hydro jetting to clear the pipe and remove buildup. A camera inspection helps us confirm the line is clear and check for damage. If we find broken or sagging pipe, we can repair the section. This keeps water and waste moving smoothly.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in San Clemente, CA

    Tees direct flow into and out of the trap at the correct depth. Missing or broken tees allow floating grease to escape into the sewer line. We replace damaged tees with durable PVC or other approved materials. We also check that they are set at the right depth for your unit. This is one of the most common and important grease trap repairs.
