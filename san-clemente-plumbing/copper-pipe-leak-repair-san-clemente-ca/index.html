    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in San Clemente

    Pinhole leaks are tiny holes that form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. They usually appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher. We cut out the affected section and replace it with new Type L copper or a PEX transition. While the wall is open, we inspect nearby pipe for discoloration or thinning.

    Hot Water Copper Line Repair in San Clemente, CA

    Hot water lines see more corrosion than cold lines because heat speeds up chemical reactions. Recirculation pumps that run constantly can also wear copper from the inside. We repair leaking hot lines and can adjust or add timers to your recirculation system. That reduces wear and lowers energy use.

    Copper Repair vs Repipe Cost in San Clemente, CA

    A single copper leak repair is affordable and fast. If you have had two or more pinhole leaks in a year, the copper is likely thinning throughout the system. At that point a PEX or Type L copper repipe can cost less over time than repeated repairs and water damage. We help you compare both options with real numbers.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in San Clemente

    Copper pipes often run under concrete slabs in San Clemente homes. A slab leak can be particularly challenging to detect, and shifting soil can put extra stress on those pipes. Expansive clay and hillside soils can shift over time, putting stress on slab plumbing and cracking older sewer joints. We use acoustic and thermal imaging technology to locate these hidden leaks without invasive demolition. Once found, we offer direct access repair or re-routing options to fix the damaged copper section, minimizing disruption to your home.
