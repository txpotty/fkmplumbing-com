    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in San Clemente

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water.     Whole-Home Copper Repiping in San Clemente

    Copper piping offers excellent durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in San Clemente. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Our team meticulously solders each joint, ensuring a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that can withstand the test of time, including changing temperatures and local water conditions. Copper also inherently inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.
