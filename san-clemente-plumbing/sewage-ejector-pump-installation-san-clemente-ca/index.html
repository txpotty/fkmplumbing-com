    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in San Clemente

    While both sump pumps and sewage ejector pumps remove water, their applications are different. FKM Plumbing provides clear guidance and installation for both in San Clemente. A sewage ejector pump, however, is designed for wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in below-grade plumbing.
