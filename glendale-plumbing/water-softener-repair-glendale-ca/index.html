    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Glendale, CA

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, regulating the flow of water and starting regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water, regenerate too often, or leak. We diagnose issues like stuck pistons, worn out seals, motor failures, or programming errors. Our plumbers carry a range of replacement parts for popular brands like Fleck, Clack, and Autotrol, ensuring a quick and effective repair to restore proper operation.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Glendale, CA

    A salt bridge, a crust of salt that forms in the brine tank, can prevent your water softener from regenerating properly. This leads to hard water throughout your home. Our team can effectively break up and remove salt bridges, clean the brine tank, and advise on best practices to prevent future occurrences. We also repair leaks or damage to the brine tank itself, common in older systems.

    Hard Water Symptoms & Diagnostics in Glendale

    If you're noticing scale buildup on faucets, cloudy dishes, dry skin and hair, or clothes that feel stiff after washing, your water softener likely needs attention. FKM Plumbing offers complete diagnostic services to identify the root cause of these hard water symptoms.     Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Glendale

    A frequent issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to inefficient regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and ensuring the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering targeted repairs or replacements.
