    Commercial and Rental Tank Maintenance in Glendale

    Property managers, restaurants and small offices often run several tanks that work much harder than a home unit.     Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Glendale

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. We test each element with a meter, check the thermostats and look for scorched wires at the terminal block. If an element has failed we replace it with the matching wattage and voltage. Plenty of families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Glendale

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause loud popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Glendale, CA

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is vital for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it changes the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's significantly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, saving you from a full tank replacement.
