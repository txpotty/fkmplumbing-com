    Dependable Grease Trap Repair Services in Glendale, CA

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Glendale

    Baffles slow incoming water so grease can rise and solids can settle. When baffles crack, corrode or break loose, grease passes straight through the trap. We remove damaged baffles and install new ones made for your trap model. In concrete interceptors, we can repair or rebuild baffle walls. Restoring baffles brings back proper separation and helps you stay compliant.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Glendale, CA

    Grease that hardens in the lines before or after the trap can cause backups even when the trap itself is clean. We use hydro jetting to clear the pipe and remove buildup. A camera inspection helps us confirm the line is clear and check for damage. If we find broken or sagging pipe, we can repair the section. This keeps water and waste moving smoothly.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Glendale, CA

    The internal baffles of a grease trap are crucial for separating FOG from wastewater. If these are damaged or dislodged, the trap becomes inefficient, allowing grease to escape into the sewer system. We repair or replace broken baffles and cracked lids in Glendale, CA, ensuring your trap functions as designed. A secure lid also prevents odors and keeps pests out, maintaining hygiene around the unit.

    Grease Interceptor Clog Removal in Glendale

    The most common grease trap issue is a severe clog caused by accumulated fats, oils, and grease. Our plumbers in Glendale use specialized hydro-jetting equipment to fully break down and flush out stubborn blockages from grease traps and their connecting drain lines.     Even residential properties in Glendale, CA can benefit from a grease trap, especially those with septic systems or older plumbing.     Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Glendale, CA

    Tees direct flow into and out of the trap at the correct depth. Missing or broken tees allow floating grease to escape into the sewer line. We replace damaged tees with durable PVC or other approved materials. We also check that they are set at the right depth for your unit. This is one of the most common and important grease trap repairs.

    Flow Control Device Repair in Glendale

    Hydromechanical traps rely on a flow control fitting to keep water moving at the rated speed. If this fitting is missing or clogged, the trap can be overwhelmed and grease escapes. We inspect, clean or replace flow control devices during repair visits. We also check the air intake that allows the device to work properly. Correct flow control is necessary for a trap to pass inspection.
