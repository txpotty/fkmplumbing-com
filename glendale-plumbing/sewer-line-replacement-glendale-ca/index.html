    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair and Replacement in Glendale, CA

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive option to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining installs a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe.
