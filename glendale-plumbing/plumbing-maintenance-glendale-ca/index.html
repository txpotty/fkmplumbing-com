    Repiping with PEX or copper is a popular upgrade across Southern California, often done to fix low pressure and recurring slab leaks.     Tree roots invade sewer lines during dry months, so the first big rain of the season often exposes a cracked or blocked lateral.     Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Glendale

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work.     Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Glendale, CA

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits greatly from annual maintenance.
