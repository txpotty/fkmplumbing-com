    Brine Tank Replacement in Glendale

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore consistent brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the right salt type for your system.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Glendale, CA

    For homeowners in Glendale, CA looking for an alternative to traditional salt-based systems, salt-free water conditioners offer a different approach. These units, sometimes called descalers, condition water to prevent mineral scale without removing the minerals or adding salt. While they do not produce soft feeling water, they reliably protect pipes and appliances from scale. We can replace your existing salt-free system or help you switch from a salt-based unit.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Glendale, CA

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more economical than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes better sense.

    Softener Loop Installation in Glendale, CA

We also route a drain line to a laundry box, floor drain or approved location. A good loop keeps outdoor hose bibs on hard water to save salt. This makes future service simpler.
