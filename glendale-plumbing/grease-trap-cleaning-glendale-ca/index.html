    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Cleaning in Glendale

    The cost of grease trap cleaning in Glendale, CA varies based on the size of the trap, its accessibility, and the amount of accumulated FOG. Typically, prices can range from $200 for smaller under-sink units to over $1,000 for large outdoor interceptors. We provide transparent, upfront quotes after assessing your specific needs.

    FOG stands for Fats, Oils, and Grease. When poured down drains, FOG cools and solidifies, sticking to pipe walls and eventually leading to severe clogs. In grease traps, it separates from wastewater, but if not regularly removed, it overflows into the sewer system, causing blockages and environmental issues.

    The terms are often used interchangeably, but 'grease interceptor' usually refers to larger, outdoor units with capacities over 500 gallons, while 'grease trap' usually describes smaller, indoor units under 100 gallons, such as under-sink models. Both serve the same function of separating FOG from wastewater.

    Neglecting commercial grease trap cleaning in Glendale can lead to severe drain blockages, sewage backups, foul odors impacting your business, and potential fines from the local health department or wastewater authority. It can also cause expensive plumbing emergencies and business downtime.

    No, enzymes and additives can move grease downstream where it hardens again. Many cities discourage or prohibit their use in place of pumping, and regular cleaning remains the dependable option.

    Indoor traps typically take 30 minutes to an hour. Outdoor interceptors can take one to two hours depending on size and how much buildup is present.

    FKM Plumbing services all common types of grease traps and interceptors, including passive hydromechanical grease interceptors (HGIs), automatic grease removal units (AGRUs), and large gravity grease interceptors (GGIs). We handle both indoor under-sink units and outdoor ground-level tanks.

    Generally, you do not need a permit to have your existing grease trap cleaned by a professional. However, commercial establishments in Glendale are often required to maintain records of their grease trap cleaning for compliance audits. New grease trap installations may require plumbing permits.
