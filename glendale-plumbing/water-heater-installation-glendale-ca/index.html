    Expansion Tank Installation in Glendale, CA

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and pipes.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Glendale

    The cost of water heater installation in Glendale, CA can range from $1,200 to $4,000 or more, depending on the type of unit (traditional tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed plumbing or electrical upgrades. Tankless units generally have higher installation costs due to more complex venting and utility requirements. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your particular needs.

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve or check valve, an expansion tank is typically required. It protects your water heater and pipes from pressure spikes.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. FKM Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, ensuring peace of mind. We will discuss exact warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from 6 to 12 years on the tank and parts. We also warranty our installation labor.

    A standard tank replacement typically takes two to four hours. Tankless installs or relocations can take most of a day.

    To improve efficiency, ensure your new water heater is properly sized for your home, consider a tankless model for on-demand heating, insulate hot water pipes and set the thermostat to 120°F (49°C) to prevent scalding and reduce energy consumption.

    Many tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years, though hard water can shorten that. Regular flushing and anode rod checks help lengthen life.
