    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in Glendale

    The cost of gas water heater installation in Glendale, CA usually ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the unit type, capacity and complexity of the installation, such as converting from electric or upgrading gas lines.     Most standard gas water heater installations can be completed within 3 to 5 hours. However, more complex jobs, such as converting to a tankless unit or significant gas line modifications, might take a full day.

    Choosing the right size depends on your household's peak hot water demand, determined by the number of residents and hot water fixtures. Our technicians will assess your needs during the consultation to recommend the right gallon capacity or flow rate for your Glendale home.

    Rusty water, leaks, pilot problems and slow recovery are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement may be the better option.
