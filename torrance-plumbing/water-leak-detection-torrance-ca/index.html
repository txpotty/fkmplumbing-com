    Water Heater and Softener Leak Detection in Torrance

    Water heaters and softeners can leak at fittings, valves and drain lines. We check these units carefully and repair or replace failed parts. That often explains a mystery bill in homes with water softeners.

    Pinhole Leak Detection in Torrance, CA

    Pinhole leaks, often found in copper or PEX piping, are small but can cause significant damage over time, especially behind walls or under floors. We employ non-invasive moisture meters to detect elevated humidity levels and thermal cameras to identify cold spots that indicate water. Our plumbers are skilled at tracing these elusive leaks without tearing apart your entire home. Swift action on pinhole leaks prevents extensive drywall damage, mold growth and compromised structural integrity. Intense sun breaks down exposed plastic pipe, sprinkler parts, and outdoor fittings, so they wear out faster than indoor plumbing.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Torrance

    Leaks in underground water lines, from your meter to your home, can go unnoticed for extended periods, wasting water and causing yard erosion. Signs include unusually green patches in your lawn, persistent wet spots or a constantly running water meter. FKM Plumbing employs ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection methods to accurately map out and pinpoint leaks in buried pipes, allowing for targeted excavation and repair rather than digging up your entire yard.
