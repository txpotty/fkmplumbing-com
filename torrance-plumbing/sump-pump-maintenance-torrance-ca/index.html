    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Torrance

    Our team is skilled in maintaining submersible sump pumps, a popular choice in Torrance due to their quiet operation and compact design.     Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Torrance

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last years longer.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Torrance, CA

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil near the slab or foundation.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Torrance, CA

    Many Torrance, CA homeowners rely on battery backup sump pumps for protection during power outages. FKM Plumbing provides essential maintenance for these critical systems, including testing the battery's charge and health, inspecting the backup pump's operation and checking the charging system. This keeps your home protected even when the primary power source fails, a real concern during severe storms.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Torrance, CA

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.
