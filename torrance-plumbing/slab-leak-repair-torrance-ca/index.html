    Slab Leak Detection in Torrance

    We start by shutting off fixtures and watching the meter to verify a leak. Then we isolate the hot and cold lines to determine which one is leaking. Using acoustic ground microphones, we listen for the sound of escaping water through the concrete. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor surface.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Torrance

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is badly deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an effective long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This removes future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Torrance with aging copper or galvanized pipes.

    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Torrance, CA

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most practical repair method. This involves carefully cutting a small section of your concrete slab to access and repair or replace the damaged pipe section. Our team makes sure the repair is fully compliant with Southern California plumbing codes and the slab is properly restored, preventing future issues. We take great care to contain dust and debris during this process in your Torrance home.

    Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Torrance, CA

    A large slab leak can push water up through flooring or into walls quickly. We help you shut off the water and send a technician to isolate the leaking line. Often we can restore water to the rest of the house while we plan the repair. Prompt action limits flooring damage and soil saturation.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Torrance

    Cold water slab leaks are harder to feel but still waste water and wet the soil. They often show up as a high water bill or a meter that moves with all fixtures off. We isolate the cold line, find the leak and repair or reroute it. Fixing cold leaks promptly helps protect your foundation.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Torrance, CA

    If multiple slab leaks indicate a systemic issue with your home's original plumbing, a full PEX repipe might be the most cost-effective and durable solution. PEX tubing is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and less prone to leaks caused by foundation shifts, making it a strong choice for many homes. We can replace all the water supply lines in your Torrance home, providing a brand-new, leak-free plumbing system that will last for decades.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Torrance, CA

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a load-bearing wall or in a difficult-to-access area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method. This lets our plumbers reach the damaged pipe from outside your home, avoiding disruption to your interior floors and finishes. Tunneling spares the interior but requires skilled excavation and shoring to protect structural integrity throughout the repair process in Torrance, CA.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Torrance

    Locating a hidden slab leak takes specialized tools. Our plumbers use sensitive electronic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to pinpoint the exact location of the leak under your concrete foundation. This precision detection reduces the need for extensive concrete removal, saving you time and money. We can detect leaks in both hot and cold water lines, as well as sewer lines, for a complete diagnosis of your Torrance home.
