    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Torrance, CA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies usually form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain.     Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Repair in Torrance

    The cost of sewer repair in Torrance, CA can vary a lot, typically ranging from $500 for minor spot repairs to $3,000 for extensive trenchless work, and up to $10,000 or more for full trench-based sewer line replacement. Factors like the extent of damage, type of repair, pipe material, depth and accessibility all affect the final price. We provide a detailed estimate after a full inspection.

    Roots typically enter through existing gaps at joints, then expand as they grow. Over time they can crack clay pipe, separate joints and create a full blockage. Cutting roots helps short-term, but sealing or replacing the joint is the lasting fix.

    Usually not. Camera locating lets us dig only at the damaged spot, and many problems can be fixed through a single pit or with trenchless methods. We protect landscaping and restore the area when we are finished.

    The duration of a sewer repair depends heavily on the scope of work. Simple spot repairs or hydro-jetting might take a few hours. Trenchless repairs like pipe lining can often be completed in one to two days. Full sewer line replacements involving excavation can take anywhere from two to five days, depending on the length and complexity of the line.

    Trenchless sewer repair is a method that allows for pipe repair or replacement without major digging. Techniques like pipe lining, also called cured-in-place pipe, or pipe bursting minimize disruption to your yard and landscaping. It's an excellent option for preserving your property while fixing underground pipes.

    Sewer repair is complex and best left to licensed professionals due to the specialized equipment required, potential health hazards and strict local plumbing codes. Attempting DIY repairs can lead to further damage, costly mistakes and even safety risks. Always consult with a professional plumber.
