    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Drain Repair in Torrance

    Clearing a single sink, tub or shower drain typically costs $125 to $300. Harder clogs that need a larger machine or access through a roof vent run $250 to $450. Hydro jetting a heavily scaled or greasy line usually costs $350 to $800.

    Most common household clogs, like those in sinks or showers, can be cleared within one to two hours. More severe blockages, such as main sewer line clogs requiring camera inspection and hydro-jetting, might take a few hours to a full day, depending on the complexity.

    Caustic drain cleaners can damage ABS fittings, corrode older metal pipes and sit in the trap if they do not clear the clog. They also create a hazard for anyone working on the drain afterward. Mechanical clearing is safer and more effective.

    You should call a professional if a plunger or simple home remedies don't work, if you experience multiple clogged drains, sewage smells, or water backing up. These symptoms often point to a deeper, more serious issue in your main sewer line that requires expert diagnosis and specialized tools.

    Drain snaking uses a flexible auger to physically break through or pull out blockages. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of pipes, effectively removing scale, grease, and roots, providing a much more complete cleaning. Hydro-jetting is often used for tough clogs or preventative maintenance.

    While chemical drain cleaners are readily available, FKM Plumbing generally advises against them for homes in Torrance. They often provide only temporary relief, can damage pipes over time, and pose environmental and health risks. Professional tools like augers or hydro-jetting are safer and more reliable.

    Enzyme or bacterial cleaners can help reduce organic buildup in kitchen and bathroom drains over time. They are gentler than caustic products and safe for most pipes. They will not clear a full clog but can help head off one.

    Gurgling sounds, water backing up into a different fixture and frequent clogs in several drains point to a deeper issue. Sewage odors or water around a floor drain are also warning signs. These often mean the main line needs attention.
