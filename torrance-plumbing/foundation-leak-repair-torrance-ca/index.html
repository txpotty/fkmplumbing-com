    Water Line Reroute in Torrance

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This avoids breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for simple patching.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Torrance

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs with steel cables under high tension. Cutting into these slabs takes care and scanning to avoid hitting a cable. We use ground-penetrating radar or other scanning methods before opening the floor. When the risk is too great, rerouting the plumbing is usually the safer option.
