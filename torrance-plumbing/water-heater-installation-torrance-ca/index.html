    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Torrance

    The cost of water heater installation in Torrance, CA typically ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of water heater (tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any necessary plumbing or electrical modifications. We provide a written, upfront quote after assessing your specific needs.

    New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. FKM Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, giving you peace of mind. We will discuss specific warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    The decision to repair or replace often depends on the age of your unit, the cost of the repair and its energy efficiency. If your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down, or the repair cost is more than 50% of a new unit, replacement is often the more cost-effective choice. Newer models also offer much better energy efficiency.

    A traditional tank water heater typically lasts between 8 and 12 years with proper maintenance. Tankless water heaters often have a longer lifespan, ranging from 15 to 20 years, thanks to their durable components and on-demand operation.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. For tank units, a 40 to 50 gallon tank is common for 2 to 4 people, while larger families might need 60 to 80 gallons. For tankless units, it is about flow rate (GPM) and temperature rise. Our plumbers will assess your needs to recommend the ideal size.

    The typical lifespan of a traditional tank water heater is about 10 to 15 years, while tankless water heaters can last 20 years or more with proper maintenance. Factors like local water quality, maintenance frequency and usage habits can all affect the actual lifespan of your unit. Regular flushing can help extend its life.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for water heater replacement. We pull permits when required and confirm the install meets code.

    Common signs that you might need a new water heater include a lack of hot water, rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit and an age of 10 to 15 years or more. If you notice any of these issues, it is best to have a professional inspection.
