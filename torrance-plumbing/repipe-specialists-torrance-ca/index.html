    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Torrance, CA

    Many older homes in Torrance, CA still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to corrosion, rust, and mineral buildup. This leads to low water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. Our team specializes in the complete removal and replacement of these outdated systems with modern PEX or copper piping. This upgrade not only resolves existing problems but also greatly improves water quality and flow, boosting your home's value and plumbing reliability for decades to come.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Torrance, CA

    Repiping requires small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures. Our crews cut clean openings, protect surrounding areas and patch every hole when the plumbing is done. We match common textures like knockdown and orange peel. Your walls are ready for paint when we leave.

    Emergency Repipe and Leak Repair in Torrance

    While a full repipe is a planned project, sometimes a major pipe burst or multiple severe leaks can call for immediate action. Our repipe specialists in Torrance are equipped to handle emergency situations, providing temporary fixes and assessing the need for a more comprehensive repiping solution. We can promptly isolate damaged sections and offer options for immediate repair or a phased repiping approach to restore your water service safely and efficiently, preventing extensive water damage.

    Drain Line Repiping in Torrance

    While often overlooked, your home's drain lines are just as important as your supply lines. Old cast iron or corroded drain pipes can lead to frequent clogs, slow drainage, and even sewage backups. FKM Plumbing provides comprehensive drain line repiping in Torrance, replacing failing waste pipes with new, smooth PVC or ABS lines. This supports efficient waste removal and prevents future drainage problems, protecting your home from foul odors and unsanitary conditions.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Torrance

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab. This is a good option for newer homes or a first-time leak. If the home has had multiple leaks, we will explain why a full repipe may save money.

    Copper Repiping in Torrance, CA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Torrance, CA

    Polybutylene pipes, installed in many homes between the 1970s and 1990s, are known for premature failure due to chemical reactions with disinfectants in the water supply. If your home in Torrance, CA has polybutylene, a full replacement is essential to prevent catastrophic water damage. FKM Plumbing offers specialized polybutylene plumbing replacement, carefully removing the old, brittle pipes and installing a dependable new system.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Torrance

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to four days.
