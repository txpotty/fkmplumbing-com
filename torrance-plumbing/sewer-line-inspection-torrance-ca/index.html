    Detecting Pipe Damage and Corrosion in Torrance, CA

    Sewer lines can suffer from many forms of damage, including cracks, breaks, corrosion and sagging sections due to shifting soil. Our camera inspections provide a detailed visual assessment of the pipe's interior, identifying these structural issues. We can determine the severity and exact location of damage, helping you decide between spot repair, relining or full pipe replacement. Early detection is essential to preventing catastrophic failures.

    Sewer Line Locating and Depth Marking in Torrance, CA

    Before any repair, it helps to know exactly where your sewer line runs and how deep it is. Our camera includes a sonde transmitter that we trace with a receiver above ground. We mark the line and problem spots with paint or flags. This makes excavation or trenchless repair faster and more precise.

    Clay and Cast Iron Pipe Inspections in Torrance, CA

    Many older homes in Torrance, CA feature clay or cast iron sewer pipes, which are prone to specific issues like cracking, corrosion and joint separation over time. Our sewer camera equipment is designed to navigate these older pipe materials effectively. We can identify signs of wear and tear, rust or weak points, providing accurate assessments for potential repairs or replacements, which is crucial for maintaining historic properties.

    Pre-Purchase Sewer Line Inspection in Torrance

    Standard home inspections typically do not include a camera look inside the sewer line. A pre-purchase inspection shows whether the line has roots, cracks, bellies or other problems before you close. That information can help you negotiate repairs or price with the seller. We provide a video and written report that fits typical real estate timelines.

    Post-Clearing Sewer Camera Inspection in Torrance

    After a backup is cleared, a camera inspection shows why it happened. It might be grease, roots, a broken section or an object stuck in the line. Knowing the cause helps you avoid repeat backups. We often recommend an inspection after any main line clearing.

    Post-Repair Sewer Line Verification in Torrance

    After any major sewer line repair or cleaning, it's good practice to verify the work. FKM Plumbing offers post-repair inspections to confirm that the problem has been fully resolved and that the pipe is in good working order.     Belly and Offset Detection in Torrance, CA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds water and waste. Offsets happen when pipe sections shift out of alignment, often because of soil movement. Earthquakes are a constant consideration in Southern California, and seismic straps and flexible connectors help keep water and gas lines intact during shaking. The camera shows these defects clearly, and we mark their location for repair.
