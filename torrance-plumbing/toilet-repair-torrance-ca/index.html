    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Torrance, CA

    If your toilet needs two flushes or clogs often, the problem may be scale in the rim jets, a partial blockage in the trapway or a flush valve that does not open fully. We auger the trap, check the vent if gurgling is present and clean the jets with a descaling treatment. Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you plainly if replacement is the better fix.

    Toilet Flush Mechanism Repair in Torrance, CA

    If your toilet isn't flushing properly, it could be due to issues with the flush chain, handle, flapper, or fill valve. A weak flush may mean the tank isn't filling correctly, while a loose handle might not be engaging the flapper. Our plumbers are skilled at diagnosing these internal tank problems, replacing worn or broken components like flush valves, fill valves, and toilet handles to restore full and effective flushing power.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Torrance, CA

    Water around the base typically means the wax ring has failed or the toilet has been rocking on a loose flange. On slab foundations, the flange often sits on concrete and can crack when bolts are overtightened. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange, install a repair ring or new flange if needed and reset the bowl on a fresh seal. We also check for soft flooring so you know if any water damage needs attention.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Torrance

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank usually points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet much quieter.
