    Advanced Trenchless Pipe Repair Services in Torrance, CA

    Pipe Bursting in Torrance, CA

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, fracturing it outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind. The new pipe is fused into one continuous length with no joints. Bursting needs an entry and exit pit but avoids digging the full length.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Torrance

    We use proven trenchless technologies and brands like Perma-Liner for reliable pipe repair in Torrance. Perma-Liner systems are known for their durability and effectiveness in creating long-lasting, structural pipe repairs. Their CIPP products are designed to hold up to harsh conditions, offering a robust solution for your underground plumbing.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Torrance, CA

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing badly damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is well suited for completely replacing a pipe segment with a new, stronger material, such as HDPE. It's often chosen when the existing pipe is collapsed or too damaged for lining. FKM Plumbing uses specialized bursting heads and winches for a smooth and efficient replacement, reducing the overall project time and cost for homeowners in Torrance, CA.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Torrance

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light. Once hardened, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.
