    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Torrance

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a good fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.
