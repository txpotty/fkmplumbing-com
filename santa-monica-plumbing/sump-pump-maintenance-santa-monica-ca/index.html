    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Santa Monica

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to early wear. We confirm these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Santa Monica residence.

    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Santa Monica

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last years longer.
