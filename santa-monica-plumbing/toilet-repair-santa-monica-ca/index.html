    Leaking Toilet Repair in Santa Monica

    Toilet leaks can cause serious water damage to your bathroom floor, subfloor, and even the ceiling below. Leaks can originate from the base of the toilet, the tank, or the supply line. Common culprits include a worn-out wax ring, loose bolts, or cracked porcelain. We thoroughly inspect all potential leak points, replacing seals, tightening connections, or recommending bowl replacement if the porcelain is damaged, preventing further water intrusion.

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Santa Monica, CA

    If your toilet needs two flushes or clogs often, the problem could be scale in the rim jets, a partial blockage in the trapway or a flush valve that does not open fully. We auger the trap, check the vent if gurgling is present and clean the jets with a descaling treatment. Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you honestly if replacement is the better fix.

    Clogged Toilet Solutions in Santa Monica, CA

    Stubborn toilet clogs are a common nuisance in Santa Monica, CA homes, often caused by non-flushable items or excessive toilet paper. While a plunger can clear minor blockages, persistent clogs indicate a deeper issue in the trap, drain line, or even the main sewer line. Our plumbers utilize professional-grade augers and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the toughest clogs without damaging your toilet or pipes. We also inspect for potential causes like root intrusion or pipe corrosion, which are sometimes seen in older homes.

    Running Toilet Repair in Santa Monica

    A running toilet is not just annoying, it can waste hundreds of gallons of water a day, leading to much higher water bills. This common issue is often caused by a faulty flapper, a misaligned fill valve, or an improperly adjusted float. Our plumbers promptly identify the exact cause, whether it's mineral buildup from hard water affecting the flapper seal or a worn-out component, and replace the necessary parts to stop the continuous flow and restore efficiency.

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Santa Monica, CA

    The gaskets and seals within your toilet tank and at its base are essential for preventing leaks. Over time, these rubber components can degrade, crack, or become brittle due to age and exposure to water, especially where the water is hard. We specialize in replacing worn-out flapper seals, tank-to-bowl gaskets, and the wax ring at the toilet's base, which is essential for preventing leaks onto your bathroom floor and maintaining a watertight seal.

    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Santa Monica

    Pressure-assist toilets from Sloan Flushmate and dual-flush models from TOTO and American Standard use parts that hardware stores rarely stock. We carry cartridges, flush buttons and seals for the common models and can order specialty parts quickly. These systems need correct pressure settings to work well, so we test incoming water pressure as part of the repair.

    Toilet Flush Mechanism Repair in Santa Monica, CA

    If your toilet isn't flushing properly, it could be due to issues with the flush chain, handle, flapper, or fill valve. A weak flush might mean the tank isn't filling correctly, while a loose handle might not be engaging the flapper. Our plumbers are skilled at diagnosing these internal tank problems, replacing worn or broken components like flush valves, fill valves, and toilet handles to restore full and effective flushing power.
