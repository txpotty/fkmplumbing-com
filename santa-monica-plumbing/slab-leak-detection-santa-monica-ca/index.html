    Electronic Leak Detection in Santa Monica

    For elusive leaks, our electronic leak detection equipment sends a signal through your pipes and measures changes that indicate a breach.     Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in Santa Monica, CA

    Sewer line issues under a slab can also look like slab leak symptoms.     Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Santa Monica

    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in Santa Monica

    Our team uses electronic listening devices to pinpoint the precise location of a slab leak. These highly sensitive microphones can detect the faint sound of water escaping from a pressurized pipe under your foundation. This non-invasive method helps us avoid unnecessary concrete breaking, saving you time and money on repairs. It's particularly effective for copper and PEX lines commonly found in Santa Monica homes.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Santa Monica

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs nonstop and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before flagging the spot.

    Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Santa Monica, CA

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most economical repair. We cut a small opening in the floor at the marked spot, repair the pipe and pressure test it. Then we patch the concrete and leave the area ready for flooring. Accurate detection keeps that opening as small as possible.
