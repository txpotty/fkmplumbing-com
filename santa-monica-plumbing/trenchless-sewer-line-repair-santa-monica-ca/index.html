    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Santa Monica, CA

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Southern California homeowners.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Santa Monica

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot get into. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their shape.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Santa Monica, CA

    Sewer lines in Santa Monica, CA can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Expansive clay and hillside soils can shift over time, putting stress on slab plumbing and cracking older sewer joints. Tree root intrusion is another major culprit, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also add to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.
