    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Santa Monica, CA

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive option. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from major disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an effective and popular choice for preserving property appearance in Santa Monica.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Santa Monica, CA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies usually form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Most homes in Southern California sit on concrete slabs, so slab leaks often show up as warm floors, damp carpet, or an unexplained jump in the water bill. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Santa Monica, CA

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Santa Monica.
