    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Santa Monica, CA

    PEX and HDPE are flexible, which means fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. HDPE is especially durable for long runs and resists cracking in expansive clay. We use approved compression or fusion fittings and add tracer wire so the line can be found with a locator later. PEX must stay out of sunlight before burial, so we store and lay it with care on every job site.

    Commercial Water Service Installation in Santa Monica

    Restaurants, retail suites and small commercial buildings need service lines sized for higher demand and often require backflow protection at the meter. We install larger diameter copper or HDPE lines and coordinate with your water utility on tap and meter requirements. Our plumbers schedule work to minimize downtime for tenants and customers. Inspection and backflow test reports are handled before the line is put into service.

    Upsizing Water Lines for Better Pressure in Santa Monica, CA

    Many homes built decades ago have a 3/4 inch line that cannot keep up with modern fixture loads, irrigation and pools. If your pressure drops when two showers run or the sprinklers are on, an undersized line may be the cause. We test static and flowing pressure, then calculate whether a larger line will make a real difference. Upsizing is often paired with a new pressure reducing valve and main shutoff.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Installation in Santa Monica

    Most residential water line installations run $1,800 to $5,500 depending on length, soil and material. Long runs through rocky ground, trenchless boring or copper pipe can push costs to $6,000 to $10,000 or more.

    Depth requirements vary by jurisdiction and climate. On an occasional cold night, covering backflow preventers and outdoor valves is typically enough to avoid a break. Lines must be buried below the local frost line, so colder areas require deeper trenches than mild ones.

    FKM Plumbing stands behind the quality of our work. We offer a full warranty on our water line installations, covering both parts and labor. Specific warranty details will be provided with your estimate, giving you peace of mind that your investment is protected.

    In most communities the homeowner owns the line from the meter to the home, while the utility owns the meter and the line on the street side. We confirm responsibility with your provider before work begins.
