    Metered Softener Replacement in Santa Monica

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly used up. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first month.

    Traditional Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Santa Monica

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and proven solution for hard water in Santa Monica. These systems use ion exchange to remove calcium and magnesium minerals, which are well known for causing scale buildup. We replace old, inefficient salt-based units with modern, high-efficiency models that use less salt and water. Our plumbers ensure proper brine tank installation and regeneration settings for strong performance.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Santa Monica, CA

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more cost effective than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes better sense.
