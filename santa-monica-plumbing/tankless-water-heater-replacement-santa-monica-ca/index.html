    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Santa Monica, CA

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can wear out plastic covers and wiring over time. On an occasional cold night, covering backflow preventers and outdoor valves is usually all it takes to avoid a break. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to shield the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the easiest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Santa Monica

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by recovering heat from exhaust gases. They vent through inexpensive PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Intense sun breaks down exposed plastic pipe, sprinkler parts, and exterior fittings, so they wear out faster than indoor plumbing. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth the price.
