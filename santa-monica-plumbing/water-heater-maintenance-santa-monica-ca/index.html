    Tank Water Heater Flushing in Santa Monica

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clean. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs more quietly.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Santa Monica

    For electric water heaters, our maintenance service focuses on checking heating elements, thermostats, and electrical connections. We verify proper wiring and test for any short circuits or power problems that could affect performance. Sediment flushing is also performed, just like with gas models, to prevent buildup and protect the lower heating element from burning out prematurely in Santa Monica.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Santa Monica

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than expected. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is key for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.

    Water Heater Leak Detection and Prevention in Santa Monica, CA

    Leaks are a frequent issue that can lead to significant water damage if not caught early. Our maintenance service includes a thorough inspection of all water heater connections, pipes, and the tank itself for any signs of leaks or corrosion. We examine the drain pan and surrounding area for moisture. Early detection and repair of small leaks can prevent larger, more destructive problems, protecting your home's floors and interior from water damage.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Santa Monica, CA

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building unsafe pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets replaced. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge if you have one.
