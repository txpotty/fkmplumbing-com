    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Santa Monica, CA

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Hard water shortens the life of tankless units, valves, and appliances, which is why many homes in Southern California add softeners or scale filters. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides extra protection.

    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Santa Monica, CA

    Making the switch from a traditional tank water heater to a tankless system involves more than just swapping units. It often requires changes to gas lines, electrical wiring and venting. FKM Plumbing manages all aspects of this conversion, making sure all upgrades meet current codes and your new tankless unit performs at its best, giving you a modern hot water solution in Santa Monica, CA.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Santa Monica

    Converting from a tank requires more than swapping units. A 40 gallon tank heater uses roughly 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need up to 199,000 BTU, so the gas line usually needs to be upsized. Venting changes from a metal flue to PVC or stainless, and the unit moves to a wall location. We remove and dispose of your old tank and patch the old flue penetration.
