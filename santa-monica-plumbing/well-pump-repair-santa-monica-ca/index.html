    Pressure Switch Replacement in Santa Monica

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.

    Jet Pump Repair in Santa Monica, CA

    Jet pumps, typically installed above ground, are often used for shallower wells or as booster pumps. Common issues include loss of prime, seal leaks, or motor wear. We troubleshoot problems like air in the lines, clogged foot valves, or faulty pressure switches. Our team in Santa Monica is skilled at restoring prime and replacing worn components to get your jet pump operating efficiently again, a frequent need where water levels rise and fall.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Santa Monica, CA

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Tree roots invade sewer lines during dry months, so the first big rain of the season often exposes a cracked or blocked lateral. Lightning and power surges often damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help prevent repeat failures.

    Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Santa Monica

    Low water pressure from a well system can be a frustrating problem, often indicating issues beyond just the pump itself. This could be due to a failing pressure switch, a partially clogged well screen, a leak in the supply line, or even a pump that is no longer meeting demand. Our team conducts thorough diagnostics to pinpoint the exact cause of low pressure in your Santa Monica home, ensuring a targeted and effective repair solution.

    Well Pump Pressure Tank Issues in Santa Monica

    The pressure tank works in conjunction with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. A waterlogged tank, a ruptured diaphragm, or a faulty air valve can cause rapid pump cycling, fluctuating water pressure, or even no water at all. Our team accurately diagnoses pressure tank problems and performs necessary repairs or replacements. We make sure your tank is properly sized and charged for optimal system performance in Santa Monica.
