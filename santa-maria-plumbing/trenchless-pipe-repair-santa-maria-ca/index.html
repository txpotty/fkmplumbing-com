    Pipe Bursting Services in Santa Maria, CA

    For severely damaged or collapsed pipes, pipe bursting offers a full replacement solution. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, breaking apart the old pipe at the same time. This provides a completely new, durable pipe, often HDPE (high-density polyethylene), which resists corrosion and leaks. It's an effective long-term fix for widespread issues in Santa Maria, CA.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Santa Maria

    CIPP lining is a popular trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is excellent for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Santa Maria.

We make sure all materials meet or exceed local plumbing codes for durability and performance.

    CIPP (cured-in-place pipe) lining involves inserting a new liner inside the existing pipe to create a pipe within a pipe. Pipe bursting, on the other hand, involves breaking apart the old pipe while pulling a new pipe into its place, which leaves you with a completely new pipe.

    A correctly installed epoxy liner is designed to last 50 years or more. HDPE pipe used in bursting has a similar long service life.

    FKM Plumbing uses trusted, industry-leading brands for our trenchless pipe repair equipment and materials, such as Perma-Liner and Picote. We choose brands known for their durability, effectiveness, and proven track record in providing long-lasting pipe repair solutions.
