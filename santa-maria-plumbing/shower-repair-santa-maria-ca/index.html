    Shower Diverter Repair in Santa Maria, CA

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This restores full flow to the showerhead.

    Shower Temperature Problem Repair in Santa Maria

    If your shower turns cold when someone flushes, the pressure-balance spool may be stuck. If you cannot get enough hot water, the anti-scald limit stop may be set too low or the cartridge may be worn. We repair or replace pressure-balance and thermostatic valves and adjust limit stops. Your shower will hold a steady, comfortable temperature.

    Clogged Shower Drain Solutions in Santa Maria, CA

    Slow or standing water in your shower is a clear sign of a clogged drain. These clogs are usually caused by a buildup of hair, soap scum, and sometimes mineral deposits. Our team uses professional drain snakes and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the most stubborn blockages without damaging your pipes. We also offer advice on preventive measures to keep your shower drains flowing freely.

    Hot Water Pressure Issues in Santa Maria, CA

    Lack of hot water or weak hot water pressure in your shower can be incredibly frustrating. This can stem from several issues, including a failing water heater, sediment buildup in the pipes, or a problem with the shower mixing valve itself. Our technicians will diagnose whether the issue is with your water heater, a blocked pipe, or a faulty component within the shower. We provide effective solutions to restore consistent hot water flow and pressure.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Santa Maria, CA

    Leaks under the shower pan or behind tile can cause rot, mold and stains on ceilings below. We use moisture meters and flood tests to find failed grout, cracked pans or leaking pipe joints. Then we repair the source, whether that means resealing a drain, replacing a valve or rebuilding a pan. On slab homes, these leaks can damage flooring in adjoining rooms.

    Shower Faucet Leak Fixes in Santa Maria

    Beyond the shower head, leaks can occur at the faucet handles or behind the wall. These leaks can be harder to spot but can cause significant damage if left unaddressed. We use modern diagnostic tools to pinpoint the exact location of the leak and provide precise repairs, often involving O-ring replacement or tightening connections, preventing expensive water damage to your home's structure.

    Showerhead and Arm Repair in Santa Maria

    Dripping showerheads, leaking arm connections and broken handheld hoses are frequent problems. We clean or replace showerheads, reseal shower arms and install new handheld hoses and holders. We can also add a slide bar for a handheld shower. Hard water clogs nozzles, so we recommend heads with flexible rubber nozzles that are easy to clean.

    Leaky Shower Faucet Repair in Santa Maria

    A dripping shower faucet can waste hundreds of gallons of water a year and drive up your utility bill. Our plumbers promptly identify the source of the leak, which is often a worn-out O-ring, washer, or cartridge within the faucet assembly. We carry a wide range of replacement parts for common brands like Delta, Moen, Kohler, and Price Pfister, allowing for same-day repair. We will make sure the new components are correctly installed, stopping the drip and saving you water.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Repair in Santa Maria

    The cost of shower repair in Santa Maria, CA varies widely depending on the issue. A simple O-ring replacement might cost between $100 and $250, while a full shower valve replacement could range from $350 to $800, including parts and labor. We provide a detailed estimate before any work begins.

    Cleaning or replacing a showerhead is an easy DIY task. Valve cartridge replacements and leak repairs behind tile are best handled by a plumber to avoid damage.

    A stuck or worn pressure-balance spool often causes temperature swings when other fixtures are used. Replacing the cartridge or valve typically solves it.

    We repair Moen, Delta, Kohler, Pfister, American Standard, Symmons and many others. We carry common cartridges on our service vans.

    Minor repairs like replacing a showerhead or cartridge usually do not require a permit. However, extensive work such as relocating plumbing lines, replacing major sections of piping, or a full bathroom remodel might require one. FKM Plumbing can advise you on permit requirements for your specific repair.

    A dripping shower typically has a worn cartridge or seals in the valve. Hard water speeds up wear, so drips are common in many homes.

    Low shower water pressure can be caused by several factors, including mineral buildup in the showerhead, a faulty pressure balancing valve, sediment in your water heater, or even a leak in your water supply lines. Our plumbers can diagnose the exact cause and recommend the appropriate repair.

    Most cartridge and diverter repairs take 1 to 2 hours. Leak repairs that require opening walls or rebuilding pans can take more time.
