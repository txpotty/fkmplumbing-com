    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Jetting in Santa Maria

    Drain jetting for an interior branch line in Santa Maria usually costs $250 to $550. Commercial kitchen lines and multiple drains may cost more and are quoted on site.

    Drain snaking usually involves pushing an auger through a clog to create a small opening for water to pass. Drain jetting, or hydro jetting, uses high-pressure water to scour the entire pipe wall, removing buildup and leaving the pipe much cleaner. Jetting is more effective for stubborn blockages and preventative maintenance.

    A typical residential drain jetting service can take about 1 to 3 hours, depending on the complexity and location of the clog. More severe blockages or main sewer line jetting may require additional time for preparation and thorough cleaning.

    We strongly advise against using a home pressure washer for drain jetting. These machines lack the power, specialized nozzles, and safety features of professional equipment. Improper use can damage your pipes, cause severe injury, or push clogs deeper. Always rely on trained professionals for drain jetting.

    For most homes, preventative drain jetting every 1 to 2 years is enough, especially if you experience frequent minor clogs. Businesses, particularly those in food service, may benefit from quarterly or semi-annual jetting to prevent grease buildup. We can recommend a schedule based on your specific needs.
